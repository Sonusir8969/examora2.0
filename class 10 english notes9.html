NCERT Solutions for Class 10 English Chapter 9 Madam Rides the Bus

NCERT Solutions for Class 10 English First Flight Chapter 9 Madam Rides the Bus covers the story of Valli's first solo bus journey, the theme of childhood curiosity and independence, and important questions and answers.

  • 1. Story Summary
  • 2. Character Sketch
  • 3. Theme & Message

1. Story Summary

Short Note:

• Valli is an eight-year-old girl who has always been fascinated by the town bus that passes by her village and dreams of taking a solo ride on it someday.

• She saves money secretly and, when the opportunity finally arises, boards the bus alone, carefully observing everything around her with sharp curiosity, including the conductor, passengers and the scenery outside.

• Though the journey brings a slightly frightening moment when she sees what she believes to be a dead cow on the road, she quickly regains her composure, and by the time she returns home, she has grown noticeably in maturity and confidence.

2. Character Sketch

Short Note:

• Valli: A clever, independent and observant young girl whose small adventure of riding the bus alone reflects her growing curiosity about the world and her budding sense of independence.

• The Bus Conductor: Initially amused by Valli's serious, business-like manner, he grows fond of her by the end of the journey, admiring her intelligence and composure.

3. Theme & Message

Short Note:

• The story captures the innocent excitement and wonder of childhood, particularly a child's desire for small acts of independence and adventure.

• It also subtly portrays how a single meaningful experience can contribute to a child's emotional growth and self-confidence.

Reading With Insight (Textbook Questions)

Q1: Why did Valli want to travel on the town bus?
Answer: Valli had always admired the town bus passing through her village and was fascinated by the idea of the journey and destinations beyond her village, so she developed a strong desire to experience a solo bus ride to see the wider world for herself.
Q2: How did Valli prepare for her bus journey, and what does this show about her character?
Answer: Valli carefully saved money over time and firmly refused offers of help or company from her neighbours, insisting on managing everything herself, which shows her determination, independence, and thoughtful planning despite her young age.
Q3: How did Valli react when she thought she saw a dead cow on the road, and what does this reveal about her personality?
Answer: Valli initially felt a flash of fear and sadness upon seeing what appeared to be a dead cow, but she quickly regained her composure and cleverly argued with the other passengers, refusing to simply accept assumptions, which shows her sharp, independent-minded and resilient nature.

Extract-Based / Short Answer Questions

Q4: How does the bus conductor's attitude towards Valli change during the journey?
Answer: The bus conductor is initially amused and slightly teasing towards Valli because of her serious, adult-like manner and refusal of a discounted 'child' fare status, but by the end of the journey, he grows genuinely fond of her, admiring her sharp intelligence and composed nature.
Q5: What does the story suggest about children's need for independence?
Answer: The story suggests that even young children have a deep desire for small experiences of independence and self-reliance, and that allowing them such opportunities, within safe limits, can significantly contribute to their confidence and personal growth.
