Ch-10: Biomolecules 12 Questions

1. Carbohydrates that cannot be hydrolyzed further into simpler polyhydroxy compounds are called:

  • A. Monosaccharides
  • B. Disaccharides
  • C. Polysaccharides
  • D. Oligosaccharides

Answer: A. Monosaccharides

Explanation: Monosaccharides such as glucose and fructose are the simplest carbohydrate units and cannot be broken down further by hydrolysis.

2. In which disaccharide are glucose and fructose linked together through a glycosidic bond?

  • A. Sucrose
  • B. Maltose
  • C. Lactose
  • D. Starch

Answer: A. Sucrose

Explanation: Sucrose is formed by the glycosidic linkage between the C1 of glucose and C2 of fructose.

3. Proteins are essentially polymers built from monomer units of:

  • A. Amino acids
  • B. Glucose units
  • C. Fatty acids
  • D. Nucleotides

Answer: A. Amino acids

Explanation: Proteins are polypeptides formed by condensation polymerization of various amino acids linked by peptide bonds.

4. The secondary structure of a protein mainly refers to:

  • A. Regular structural patterns such as the α-helix and β-pleated sheet
  • B. The linear sequence of amino acids
  • C. The overall three-dimensional folding of the whole chain
  • D. The aggregation of multiple protein subunits

Answer: A. Regular structural patterns such as the α-helix and β-pleated sheet

Explanation: Secondary structure describes local, regular folding patterns of the polypeptide backbone stabilized by hydrogen bonds.

5. Denaturation of a protein primarily leads to the loss of its:

  • A. Biological activity, due to disruption of secondary and tertiary structure
  • B. Primary structure (amino acid sequence)
  • C. Molecular formula
  • D. Amino acid composition

Answer: A. Biological activity, due to disruption of secondary and tertiary structure

Explanation: Denaturing agents disrupt hydrogen bonds and other interactions that maintain higher-order structure, without breaking the peptide bonds of the primary structure.

6. A key structural difference between DNA and RNA is that DNA contains the sugar:

  • A. Deoxyribose
  • B. Ribose
  • C. Glucose
  • D. Fructose

Answer: A. Deoxyribose

Explanation: DNA contains 2-deoxyribose (lacking an oxygen at the 2' position), whereas RNA contains ribose.

7. Which nitrogenous base is found in DNA but is replaced by a different base in RNA?

  • A. Thymine
  • B. Cytosine
  • C. Adenine
  • D. Guanine

Answer: A. Thymine

Explanation: DNA contains thymine, while RNA contains uracil in its place.

8. A deficiency of which vitamin causes the disease scurvy?

  • A. Vitamin C
  • B. Vitamin A
  • C. Vitamin D
  • D. Vitamin B12

Answer: A. Vitamin C

Explanation: Scurvy, characterized by bleeding gums and poor wound healing, results from a deficiency of vitamin C (ascorbic acid).

9. Enzymes are best described as:

  • A. Biological catalysts, mostly made of protein
  • B. Simple carbohydrates
  • C. Nucleic acids
  • D. Lipid molecules

Answer: A. Biological catalysts, mostly made of protein

Explanation: Enzymes are typically globular proteins that catalyze specific biochemical reactions by lowering activation energy.

10. The zwitterionic form of an amino acid arises because it contains:

  • A. Both a basic -NH2 group and an acidic -COOH group
  • B. Only a -COOH group
  • C. Only an -NH2 group
  • D. No ionizable functional groups

Answer: A. Both a basic -NH2 group and an acidic -COOH group

Explanation: In aqueous solution, the -COOH group donates a proton to the -NH2 group, forming an internal salt (zwitterion) with both positive and negative charges.

11. Amino acids are joined together in a protein chain through:

  • A. Peptide bonds
  • B. Glycosidic bonds
  • C. Phosphodiester bonds
  • D. Hydrogen bonds alone

Answer: A. Peptide bonds

Explanation: A peptide bond forms between the -COOH group of one amino acid and the -NH2 group of the next, with loss of a water molecule.

12. Insulin, which regulates blood glucose levels, is an example of a:

  • A. Protein hormone
  • B. Carbohydrate
  • C. Nucleic acid
  • D. Vitamin

Answer: A. Protein hormone

Explanation: Insulin is a small protein hormone composed of two polypeptide chains linked by disulfide bonds.
