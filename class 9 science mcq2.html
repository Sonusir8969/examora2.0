Ch-2: Is Matter Around Us Pure 10 Questions

1. Which of the following is considered a pure substance in chemistry?

  • A. Milk
  • B. Distilled water
  • C. Air
  • D. Fruit juice

Answer: B. Distilled water

Explanation: A pure substance consists of a single type of particle. Distilled water contains only H₂O molecules, whereas milk, air, and juice are mixtures.

2. A mixture of salt and water is an example of a:

  • A. Homogeneous mixture
  • B. Heterogeneous mixture
  • C. Compound
  • D. Suspension

Answer: A. Homogeneous mixture

Explanation: When salt dissolves completely in water, it forms a uniform composition throughout, making it a homogeneous mixture.

3. The scattering of a beam of light by colloidal particles is called:

  • A. Brownian motion
  • B. Tyndall effect
  • C. Reflection
  • D. Dispersion

Answer: B. Tyndall effect

Explanation: The Tyndall effect is the phenomenon in which colloidal particles scatter light passing through the mixture, making the path of light visible.

4. Which technique is used to separate butter from curd (or cream from milk)?

  • A. Evaporation
  • B. Centrifugation
  • C. Chromatography
  • D. Sublimation

Answer: B. Centrifugation

Explanation: Centrifugation works on the principle that denser particles are forced to the bottom and lighter particles stay at the top when spun rapidly.

5. Which of the following is a chemical change?

  • A. Melting of ice
  • B. Rusting of iron
  • C. Dissolving salt in water
  • D. Cutting a piece of wood

Answer: B. Rusting of iron

Explanation: Rusting creates a new chemical compound (iron oxide) with completely different chemical properties, making it a chemical change.

6. Tincture of iodine is a solution formed by dissolving iodine in:

  • A. Water
  • B. Alcohol
  • C. Acetone
  • D. Benzene

Answer: B. Alcohol

Explanation: Tincture of iodine, widely used as an antiseptic, has iodine (solid) as the solute and alcohol (liquid) as the solvent.

7. Brass is an alloy made up of which two metals?

  • A. Copper and Zinc
  • B. Copper and Tin
  • C. Iron and Carbon
  • D. Aluminum and Copper

Answer: A. Copper and Zinc

Explanation: Brass is a solid-in-solid homogeneous mixture (alloy) containing approximately 70% Copper and 30% Zinc.

8. Which of the following is an example of a suspension?

  • A. Salt solution
  • B. Chalk powder in water
  • C. Milk
  • D. Air

Answer: B. Chalk powder in water

Explanation: Chalk powder in water is a heterogeneous mixture where solute particles do not dissolve and remain suspended visible to the naked eye.

9. Two immiscible liquids having different densities can be separated using a:

  • A. Filter paper
  • B. Separating funnel
  • C. Fractional distillation column
  • D. Centrifuge

Answer: B. Separating funnel

Explanation: A separating funnel separates immiscible liquids (like oil and water) into distinct layers based on their densities.

10. What is the mass percentage of a solution containing 40 g of salt in 320 g of water?

  • A. 10%
  • B. 11.1%
  • C. 12.5%
  • D. 15%

Answer: B. 11.1%

Explanation: Mass of solution = 40g + 320g = 360g. Mass percentage = (40 / 360) × 100 = 11.11%.
