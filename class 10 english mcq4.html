Ch-4: From the Diary of Anne Frank 30 Questions

1. Who is the author of 'From the Diary of Anne Frank'?

  • A. Anne Frank
  • B. Margot Frank
  • C. Otto Frank
  • D. Edith Frank

Answer: A. Anne Frank

Explanation: The chapter consists of excerpts written directly by Anne Frank in her personal diary.

2. What name did Anne give to her diary?

  • A. Kitty
  • B. Edith
  • C. Margot
  • D. Betty

Answer: A. Kitty

Explanation: Anne called her diary 'Kitty' because she wanted it to act as her true friend.

3. What was Anne Frank's full name?

  • A. Annelies Marie Frank
  • B. Anna Maria Frank
  • C. Annabella Margaret Frank
  • D. Anne Mary Frank

Answer: A. Annelies Marie Frank

Explanation: Anne Frank's full name was Annelies Marie Frank.

4. Why did writing in a diary feel like a strange experience for Anne?

  • A. She had never written anything before and felt no one would be interested in a 13-year-old's thoughts
  • B. She didn't know how to write properly
  • C. Her parents forced her to write
  • D. She didn't have a quiet place

Answer: A. She had never written anything before and felt no one would be interested in a 13-year-old's thoughts

Explanation: Anne felt writing was strange because she hadn't written before and thought neither she nor anyone else would care about a schoolgirl's musings.

5. Complete the famous saying mentioned by Anne: "Paper has more ______ than people."

  • A. Patience
  • B. Power
  • C. Knowledge
  • D. Silence

Answer: A. Patience

Explanation: Anne wrote that "Paper has more patience than people," meaning a diary listens without judgment or impatience.

6. Why did Anne decide to keep a diary despite having family and friends?

  • A. She lacked a true friend with whom she could share her innermost thoughts
  • B. She was completely isolated without any relatives
  • C. She wanted to become a famous writer
  • D. Her teacher asked her to write daily notes

Answer: A. She lacked a true friend with whom she could share her innermost thoughts

Explanation: Though she had loving parents and about 30 friends, she felt she couldn't confide deeply in any of them.

7. When was Anne Frank born?

  • A. 12 June 1929
  • B. 12 July 1930
  • C. 15 May 1928
  • D. 20 August 1931

Answer: A. 12 June 1929

Explanation: Anne Frank was born on 12 June 1929 in Frankfurt, Germany.

8. Where was Anne Frank born?

  • A. Frankfurt, Germany
  • B. Amsterdam, Holland
  • C. Berlin, Germany
  • D. Paris, France

Answer: A. Frankfurt, Germany

Explanation: Anne Frank was born in Frankfurt, Germany, before her family emigrated to Holland.

9. To which city did Anne's father emigrate in 1933?

  • A. Holland (Amsterdam)
  • B. London
  • C. Brussels
  • D. New York

Answer: A. Holland (Amsterdam)

Explanation: Her father Otto Frank emigrated to Holland in 1933 to escape Nazi persecution.

10. Where were Anne and her sister Margot sent to live with their grandmother initially?

  • A. Aachen
  • B. Munich
  • C. Cologne
  • D. Hamburg

Answer: A. Aachen

Explanation: Anne and Margot were sent to Aachen to stay with their grandmother while their parents went to Holland.

11. What birthday present did Margot receive in December that Anne joined in February?

  • A. Margot was sent to Holland as a birthday present for Anne
  • B. A bike
  • C. A watch
  • D. A new school bag

Answer: A. Margot was sent to Holland as a birthday present for Anne

Explanation: Margot flew to Holland in December, and Anne was "plunked down on the table" as a birthday present for Margot in February.

12. Who was Anne's headmistress at the Montessori nursery school?

  • A. Mrs. Kuperus
  • B. Mrs. Frank
  • C. Mrs. Mr. Keesing
  • D. Mrs. van Daan

Answer: A. Mrs. Kuperus

Explanation: Mrs. Kuperus was the headmistress of the Montessori nursery school who wept when bidding farewell to Anne.

13. In which year did Anne's beloved grandmother pass away?

  • A. 1942
  • B. 1940
  • C. 1941
  • D. 1944

Answer: A. 1942

Explanation: Grandma fell ill in the summer of 1941 and died in January 1942.

14. What date marks the first entry of Anne's diary included in this lesson?

  • A. 20 June 1942
  • B. 12 June 1942
  • C. 20 July 1942
  • D. 1 January 1943

Answer: A. 20 June 1942

Explanation: The entry provided in the text begins on Saturday, 20 June 1942.

15. Why was the entire class shaking in their boots on the entry day?

  • A. Teachers were holding a meeting to decide who would move to the next form
  • B. An earthquake occurred
  • C. A surprise exam was scheduled
  • D. The principal resigned

Answer: A. Teachers were holding a meeting to decide who would move to the next form

Explanation: The class was nervous because teachers were about to decide who would move up and who would stay back.

16. What subject was Anne most worried about passing?

  • A. Maths
  • B. English
  • C. History
  • D. Science

Answer: A. Maths

Explanation: Anne was confident about most subjects except Maths, where she had trouble with Mr. Keesing.

17. Who was Mr. Keesing?

  • A. Her Maths teacher
  • B. Her History teacher
  • C. Her Headmaster
  • D. Her English teacher

Answer: A. Her Maths teacher

Explanation: Mr. Keesing was an old-fashioned teacher who taught Maths to Anne's class.

18. Why was Mr. Keesing annoyed with Anne?

  • A. Because she talked too much in class
  • B. Because she did not complete her homework
  • C. Because she was late to school
  • D. Because she failed tests

Answer: A. Because she talked too much in class

Explanation: Mr. Keesing was annoyed with Anne because she was extremely talkative during lessons.

19. What topic was given to Anne for her first punishment essay?

  • A. 'A Chatterbox'
  • B. 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox'
  • C. 'Quack, Quack, Quack'
  • D. 'My School Life'

Answer: A. 'A Chatterbox'

Explanation: As punishment for talking, Mr. Keesing assigned her an essay titled 'A Chatterbox'.

20. What justification did Anne give for talking in her first essay?

  • A. It was an inherited trait from her mother and a student's feature
  • B. She argued that talking kept her awake
  • C. She claimed the teacher was boring
  • D. She promised never to speak again

Answer: A. It was an inherited trait from her mother and a student's feature

Explanation: Anne argued that talking was a student trait and that she inherited it from her mother, so she couldn't cure it easily.

21. What topic was assigned to Anne as her second punishment essay?

  • A. 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox'
  • B. 'A Chatterbox'
  • C. 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterback'
  • D. 'The Noise in Class'

Answer: A. 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox'

Explanation: When she didn't stop talking, Mr. Keesing assigned 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox'.

22. What was the title of the third essay assigned by Mr. Keesing?

  • A. 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterback'
  • B. 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox'
  • C. 'Silent Ducks'
  • D. 'The Story of Three Ducklings'

Answer: A. 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterback'

Explanation: The third title given as punishment was 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterback'.

23. Who helped Anne write her third essay in verse form?

  • A. Her friend Sanne
  • B. Her sister Margot
  • C. Her mother
  • D. Mrs. Kuperus

Answer: A. Her friend Sanne

Explanation: Sanne, who was good at poetry, offered to help Anne write the whole essay from start to finish in verse.

24. What happened to the three baby ducklings in Anne's poem?

  • A. They were bitten to death by their father swan because they quacked too much
  • B. They flew away into the wild
  • C. They learned to swim gracefully
  • D. They were adopted by a farmer

Answer: A. They were bitten to death by their father swan because they quacked too much

Explanation: In her satirical poem, the father swan bit the ducklings to death because they quacked too much.

25. How did Mr. Keesing react to Anne's third essay written in verse?

  • A. He took the joke well and read the poem aloud to the class
  • B. He got furious and suspended her
  • C. He gave her extra homework
  • D. He called her parents

Answer: A. He took the joke well and read the poem aloud to the class

Explanation: Mr. Keesing appreciated the humor, read the poem to the class with his comments, and stopped giving extra homework.

26. Who was the only surviving member of Anne Frank's family after World War II?

  • A. Her father, Otto Frank
  • B. Her mother, Edith Frank
  • C. Her sister, Margot Frank
  • D. Her grandmother

Answer: A. Her father, Otto Frank

Explanation: Otto Frank was the sole survivor among the family members after the war ended.

27. In which language was Anne Frank's diary originally written?

  • A. Dutch
  • B. German
  • C. English
  • D. French

Answer: A. Dutch

Explanation: The diary was originally written in Dutch before being translated into many languages worldwide.

28. What was the published title of Anne Frank's diary?

  • A. The Diary of a Young Girl
  • B. Anne Frank's Secret Secret
  • C. Life in Hiding
  • D. A Girl's Musings

Answer: A. The Diary of a Young Girl

Explanation: The diary was published internationally under the title 'The Diary of a Young Girl'.

29. What does the word 'plunked down' mean as used in the lesson?

  • A. Put down abruptly or casually
  • B. Thrown away
  • C. Lifted up carefully
  • D. Pushed into water

Answer: A. Put down abruptly or casually

Explanation: 'Plunked down' means placed down casually or abruptly.

30. What change came over Mr. Keesing after Anne submitted her final poem?

  • A. He allowed Anne to talk and started making jokes in class himself
  • B. He quit teaching Maths
  • C. He gave her a medal
  • D. He became stricter with other students

Answer: A. He allowed Anne to talk and started making jokes in class himself

Explanation: After reading the poem, Mr. Keesing allowed Anne to speak in class and started cracking jokes himself.
