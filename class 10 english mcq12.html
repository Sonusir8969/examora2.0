Ch-12: English Grammar Practice Set 25 Questions

1. Identify the correct tense: "The Sun ______ in the east."

  • A. rises
  • B. is rising
  • C. rose
  • D. has risen

Answer: A. rises

Explanation: Universal facts and habitual truths take Simple Present Tense.

2. "She ______ working in this school since 2018." Choose the correct verb form.

  • A. has been
  • B. is
  • C. was
  • D. will be

Answer: A. has been

Explanation: Time expressions with 'since' or 'for' representing an action continuing from the past into present require Present Perfect Continuous Tense ('has been').

3. Choose the appropriate modal: "______ God bless you!"

  • A. May
  • B. Can
  • C. Might
  • D. Should

Answer: A. May

Explanation: 'May' is used to express prayers, wishes, or blessings in optative sentences.

4. "When I reached the station, the train ______." Choose the correct tense.

  • A. had left
  • B. left
  • C. has left
  • D. was leaving

Answer: A. had left

Explanation: When two past actions occur sequentially, the earlier completed action takes the Past Perfect Tense ('had left').

5. "You ______ obey your parents and teachers." Select the correct modal verb.

  • A. should
  • B. may
  • C. can
  • D. might

Answer: A. should

Explanation: 'Should' is used to express moral obligation, advice, or social duty.

6. Select the correct passive voice: "He wrote a letter."

  • A. A letter was written by him.
  • B. A letter is written by him.
  • C. A letter had been written by him.
  • D. A letter has written by him.

Answer: A. A letter was written by him.

Explanation: Simple Past Tense ('wrote') changes to 'was/were + V3' ('was written') in passive voice.

7. "Look! The baby ______." Complete with the appropriate verb form.

  • A. is sleeping
  • B. sleeps
  • C. slept
  • D. had slept

Answer: A. is sleeping

Explanation: Exclamatory markers like 'Look!' denote an action happening right now, requiring Present Continuous Tense.

8. "He is ______ Honest man." Choose the correct article/determiner.

  • A. an
  • B. a
  • C. the
  • D. no article required

Answer: A. an

Explanation: 'Honest' begins with a silent 'h' and a vowel sound ('on-ist'), so 'an' is required.

9. "If it rains, we ______ stay inside." Choose the suitable modal.

  • A. will
  • B. would
  • C. must have
  • D. could have

Answer: A. will

Explanation: In a first conditional sentence (If + Simple Present), the main clause takes 'will + main verb'.

10. Choose the correct passive sentence for: "Who broke this mirror?"

  • A. By whom was this mirror broken?
  • B. By whom is this mirror broken?
  • C. Who was broken this mirror?
  • D. By whom this mirror was broken?

Answer: A. By whom was this mirror broken?

Explanation: Questions starting with 'Who' in Simple Past transform to 'By whom + was/were + Object + V3?'.

11. "I ______ drive a car when I was only twelve years old." Select the correct modal.

  • A. could
  • B. can
  • C. may
  • D. must

Answer: A. could

Explanation: 'Could' expresses general ability in the past.

12. "They ______ the project before the deadline yesterday."

  • A. completed
  • B. have completed
  • C. complete
  • D. are completing

Answer: A. completed

Explanation: Specific past time indicators like 'yesterday' require Simple Past Tense ('completed').

13. Choose the indirect speech for: He said, "I am busy now."

  • A. He said that he was busy then.
  • B. He said that I was busy now.
  • C. He says that he is busy then.
  • D. He told that he is busy now.

Answer: A. He said that he was busy then.

Explanation: Present tense 'am' changes to 'was', and time indicator 'now' changes to 'then' in reported speech.

14. "Neither he nor his friends ______ present at the meeting."

  • A. were
  • B. was
  • C. is
  • D. have

Answer: A. were

Explanation: When subjects are joined by 'neither... nor', the verb agrees with the closer subject ('his friends' = plural -> 'were').

15. "It ______ rain tonight; the sky is covered with dark clouds."

  • A. might / may
  • B. must not
  • C. ought to
  • D. cannot

Answer: A. might / may

Explanation: 'May' or 'might' denotes possibility in future weather conditions based on observation.

16. "Slow and steady ______ the race." Choose the correct subject-verb agreement.

  • A. wins
  • B. win
  • C. winning
  • D. have won

Answer: A. wins

Explanation: When two singular nouns joined by 'and' express a single idea/proverb, they take a singular verb ('wins').

17. "There isn't ______ water left in the pitcher." Choose the correct determiner.

  • A. much
  • B. many
  • C. few
  • D. several

Answer: A. much

Explanation: 'Water' is an uncountable noun, so 'much' is used in negative expressions.

18. Choose the correct passive transformation: "They are playing football."

  • A. Football is being played by them.
  • B. Football was played by them.
  • C. Football has been played by them.
  • D. Football is played by them.

Answer: A. Football is being played by them.

Explanation: Present continuous tense ('are playing') transforms into 'is/are + being + V3' in passive voice.

19. "The patient ______ before the doctor arrived." Fill in the blank.

  • A. had died
  • B. died
  • C. has died
  • D. was dying

Answer: A. had died

Explanation: Action completed prior to another past action takes Past Perfect Tense ('had died').

20. "Walk carefully lest you ______ stumble." Select the correct modal.

  • A. should
  • B. may
  • C. must
  • D. would

Answer: A. should

Explanation: The conjunction 'lest' is grammatically paired with 'should'.

21. "Bread and butter ______ his favourite breakfast."

  • A. is
  • B. are
  • C. were
  • D. have been

Answer: A. is

Explanation: 'Bread and butter' forms a singular combination/idea representing a single meal item, taking a singular verb ('is').

22. Change to reported speech: She said to me, "Where do you live?"

  • A. She asked me where I lived.
  • B. She asked me where did I live.
  • C. She told me where I live.
  • D. She asked me that where I lived.

Answer: A. She asked me where I lived.

Explanation: Wh-questions do not use 'that'; the question form converts to assertive order ('where I lived').

23. "He has ______ knowledge of the topic, so he kept quiet."

  • A. little
  • B. a little
  • C. the little
  • D. few

Answer: A. little

Explanation: 'Little' carries a negative meaning (almost none), explaining why he stayed quiet.

24. "We ______ follow traffic rules strictly." Choose the correct modal.

  • A. must
  • B. can
  • C. may
  • D. might

Answer: A. must

Explanation: 'Must' expresses strong necessity, law enforcement, or compulsion.

25. "By next month, we ______ in this house for ten years."

  • A. will have been living
  • B. will live
  • C. are living
  • D. lived

Answer: A. will have been living

Explanation: 'By next month' combined with a duration ('for ten years') indicates Future Perfect Continuous Tense.
