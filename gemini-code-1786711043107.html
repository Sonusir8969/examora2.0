Class 9 Social Science Exam Preparation Strategy

Key Preparation Tips:

• History: Focus on chronological timelines, key political figures, social classes, and cause-effect relationships of major revolutions.

• Geography: Practice map pointing regularly for mountain ranges, rivers, climate zones, national parks, and population density regions.

• Political Science: Memorize definition characteristics of Democracy, key features of the Indian Constitution, and functions of Parliament, President, and Judiciary.

• Economics: Understand basic economic concepts such as factors of production, human capital formation, poverty line criteria, and government food schemes (PDS, FCI).
