Ch-6: Mother's Day 30 Questions

1. Who is the author of the play 'Mother's Day'?

  • A. J. B. Priestley
  • B. Marga Minco
  • C. William Saroyan
  • D. A. R. Williams

Answer: A. J. B. Priestley

Explanation: 'Mother's Day' is a satirical play written by English playwright J. B. Priestley.

2. What theme does the play 'Mother's Day' primary address?

  • A. The unappreciated position and status of mothers in suburban families
  • B. The benefits of magic tricks and fortune-telling
  • C. The importance of children taking up part-time jobs
  • D. Political disputes in British households

Answer: A. The unappreciated position and status of mothers in suburban families

Explanation: The play highlights how housewives/mothers are taken for granted as unpaid servants by their families.

3. Who is the central female protagonist facing mistreatment by her family?

  • A. Mrs. Annie Pearson
  • B. Mrs. Fitzgerald
  • C. Doris Pearson
  • D. Mrs. S

Answer: A. Mrs. Annie Pearson

Explanation: Mrs. Annie Pearson is a loving, gentle woman treated like a servant by her husband and children.

4. Who is Mrs. Pearson's neighbour with a strong, domineering personality?

  • A. Mrs. Fitzgerald
  • B. Mrs. Dorling
  • C. Mrs. S
  • D. Aunt Arak

Answer: A. Mrs. Fitzgerald

Explanation: Mrs. Fitzgerald is Mrs. Pearson's bold neighbour who knows fortune-telling and eastern magic.

5. Where did Mrs. Fitzgerald learn the art of fortune-telling and magic spells?

  • A. In the East, where her husband served in the army for twelve years
  • B. At a university in London
  • C. From an old book she bought
  • D. From her grandmother

Answer: A. In the East, where her husband served in the army for twelve years

Explanation: Her husband was stationed in the East (Asia) as a quartermaster, where she picked up esoteric skills.

6. What is Mrs. Pearson's main character flaw that allows her family to take advantage of her?

  • A. She is overly fond, timid, and afraid of asserting herself or causing unpleasantness
  • B. She spends all her time sleeping
  • C. She is lazy and ignores household work
  • D. She complains constantly without taking action

Answer: A. She is overly fond, timid, and afraid of asserting herself or causing unpleasantness

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson loves her family too much to stand up to them or enforce respect.

7. What solution does Mrs. Fitzgerald propose to teach Mrs. Pearson's family a lesson?

  • A. Temporarily swapping bodies/personalities using magic spell
  • B. Calling the police to report harassment
  • C. Leaving the house forever
  • D. Refusing to cook dinner for a year

Answer: A. Temporarily swapping bodies/personalities using magic spell

Explanation: Mrs. Fitzgerald offers to swap bodies with Annie so she can handle the family with her strong persona.

8. What spell words does Mrs. Fitzgerald chant while holding hands with Mrs. Pearson?

  • A. "Arshtatta dum... arshtatta lam..."
  • B. "Abracadabra hocus pocus..."
  • C. "Sim salabim bam..."
  • D. "Open sesame..."

Answer: A. "Arshtatta dum... arshtatta lam..."

Explanation: She recites "Arshtatta dum, arshtatta lam, arshtatta locum, tmobhn" to exchange personalities.

9. After the body swap, whose personality resides in Mrs. Pearson's body?

  • A. Mrs. Fitzgerald's bold and aggressive personality
  • B. Mrs. Pearson's gentle personality
  • C. Doris's personality
  • D. George's personality

Answer: A. Mrs. Fitzgerald's bold and aggressive personality

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson's body now carries the strong, assertive, and outspoken spirit of Mrs. Fitzgerald.

10. Who is the first family member to enter the house after the personality transformation?

  • A. Doris Pearson, the daughter
  • B. Cyril Pearson, the son
  • C. George Pearson, the husband
  • D. Charlie Spence

Answer: A. Doris Pearson, the daughter

Explanation: Doris comes home first, demanding her yellow silk dress be ironed and tea be served.

11. What was Mrs. Pearson doing when Doris walked into the living room?

  • A. Smoking a cigarette and playing cards
  • B. Ironing Doris's yellow dress
  • C. Cooking dinner in the kitchen
  • D. Cleaning the room with a mop

Answer: A. Smoking a cigarette and playing cards

Explanation: Doris is shocked to see her mother smoking a cigarette, drinking, and playing patience with cards.

12. What work did Doris ask her mother to do immediately upon coming home?

  • A. Iron her yellow silk dress for her evening date
  • B. Wash her shoes
  • C. Pack her lunchbox
  • D. Polish her purse

Answer: A. Iron her yellow silk dress for her evening date

Explanation: Doris orders her mother to iron her dress because she is going out with Charlie Spence.

13. Who was Doris planning to go out with that evening?

  • A. Charlie Spence
  • B. George Pearson
  • C. Cyril's friend
  • D. A colleague from work

Answer: A. Charlie Spence

Explanation: Doris was going out on a date with Charlie Spence, whom her mother strongly criticizes.

14. What insult did Mrs. Pearson level against Charlie Spence?

  • A. "Buck-toothed and half-witted"
  • B. "Lazy and uneducated"
  • C. "Rude and arrogant"
  • D. "Greedy and ugly"

Answer: A. "Buck-toothed and half-witted"

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson mocks Charlie Spence as buck-toothed and half-witted, driving Doris to tears.

15. Why does Doris burst into tears and run out of the room?

  • A. Because her mother refused to iron her dress, made no tea, and criticized Charlie Spence
  • B. Because she lost her job
  • C. Because she fell down the stairs
  • D. Because Charlie Spence canceled their date

Answer: A. Because her mother refused to iron her dress, made no tea, and criticized Charlie Spence

Explanation: The sudden, harsh, and blunt behavior of her mother deeply hurt and confused Doris.

16. Who enters the house second after Doris?

  • A. Cyril Pearson, the son
  • B. George Pearson, the father
  • C. Mrs. Fitzgerald
  • D. Charlie Spence

Answer: A. Cyril Pearson, the son

Explanation: Cyril arrives home next, making similar demands for tea and checking on his clothes.

17. What demand does Cyril make as soon as he steps into the house?

  • A. Is tea ready and have his clothes been put out?
  • B. Where is his bicycle key?
  • C. Has dinner been served?
  • D. Can his mother lend him money?

Answer: A. Is tea ready and have his clothes been put out?

Explanation: Cyril expects his mother to have tea served and his outfit prepped for his night out.

18. How does Mrs. Pearson respond to Cyril's expectation of working 8 hours a day?

  • A. She tells him she will also work an 8-hour day and 40-hour week, taking weekends off
  • B. She promises to work 16 hours a day
  • C. She asks him to pay her a monthly wage
  • D. She agrees that housewives should work without rest

Answer: A. She tells him she will also work an 8-hour day and 40-hour week, taking weekends off

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson demands equal treatment, stating she will work a 40-hour week just like the rest of them.

19. What do Doris and Cyril think has happened to their mother's mind?

  • A. They suspect she suffered a concussion or hit her head
  • B. They think she is joking with them
  • C. They think she won a lottery
  • D. They think she is practicing for a theatre play

Answer: A. They suspect she suffered a concussion or hit her head

Explanation: Whispering together, the siblings wonder if she hit her head on something and got a concussion.

20. Who arrives home third in the family?

  • A. George Pearson, the husband
  • B. Mrs. Fitzgerald
  • C. Charlie Spence
  • D. The local grocer

Answer: A. George Pearson, the husband

Explanation: George Pearson enters third, described as slow, solemn, and self-important.

21. What does George Pearson inform his wife upon coming home?

  • A. He doesn't want any tea because he is drinking at the Club
  • B. He bought flowers for her
  • C. He wants a heavy meal right away
  • D. He invited his friends home for dinner

Answer: A. He doesn't want any tea because he is drinking at the Club

Explanation: George announces he won't drink tea, but gets offended when told tea wasn't made anyway.

22. What embarrassing secret nickname do members at the Club use for George Pearson behind his back?

  • A. "Pompey-Ompey Pearson"
  • B. "Jolly-Golly George"
  • C. "Silly-Billy Pearson"
  • D. "Lazy-Dazy George"

Answer: A. "Pompey-Ompey Pearson"

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson exposes that people at the Social Club laugh at him and call him "Pompey-Ompey Pearson" because he is pompous and slow.

23. Why do people at the Club call George by that nickname?

  • A. Because they think he is pompous and slow
  • B. Because he is bad at playing billiards
  • C. Because he wears strange clothes
  • D. Because he spends too much money

Answer: A. Because they think he is pompous and slow

Explanation: They mock his self-important, slow manner behind his back while he believes he is highly respected there.

24. What drink is Mrs. Pearson sipping in front of her shocked husband?

  • A. Stout (beer)
  • B. Tea with lemon
  • C. Whiskey
  • D. Wine

Answer: A. Stout (beer)

Explanation: She drinks a glass of stout from a bottle, further shocking George's conservative expectations.

25. What happens when real Mrs. Fitzgerald (in Mrs. Pearson's body) comes to check on the situation?

  • A. She becomes horrified seeing her family so upset and begs to change back
  • B. She joins in beating up George
  • C. She laughs at the children
  • D. She leaves the house in anger

Answer: A. She becomes horrified seeing her family so upset and begs to change back

Explanation: Gentle Annie Pearson (now inside Mrs. Fitzgerald's body) cannot bear to see her husband and children crying/distressed.

26. How do they reverse the body swap magic spell?

  • A. By holding hands and repeating the spell chant in reverse
  • B. By drinking a special potion
  • C. By sleeping overnight
  • D. By washing their hands in cold water

Answer: A. By holding hands and repeating the spell chant in reverse

Explanation: They sit face to face, hold hands, and repeat the magical incantation to restore their original bodies.

27. What final warning/advice does Mrs. Fitzgerald give Annie before leaving the house?

  • A. Keep a firm hand and don't go soft on them again, or all the effort will be wasted
  • B. Apologize to everyone for the bad behavior
  • C. Cook them a grand feast to celebrate
  • D. Go for a holiday alone

Answer: A. Keep a firm hand and don't go soft on them again, or all the effort will be wasted

Explanation: Mrs. Fitzgerald warns her to remain firm so her family doesn't slip back into their selfish old habits.

28. What game does Mrs. Pearson suggest playing with her family at the end of the play?

  • A. A game of Rummy
  • B. Chess
  • C. Monopoly
  • D. Bridge

Answer: A. A game of Rummy

Explanation: She proposes a nice family game of rummy while the children prepare supper.

29. Who is assigned to prepare the supper at the end of the play?

  • A. Doris and Cyril, while George helps
  • B. Mrs. Pearson alone
  • C. Mrs. Fitzgerald
  • D. They order food from outside

Answer: A. Doris and Cyril, while George helps

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson instructs the children to get the meal ready while she rests and talks with George.

30. What is the ultimate outcome/transformation at the end of the play 'Mother's Day'?

  • A. The family members become respectful, obedient, and appreciative of Mrs. Pearson
  • B. The family decides to throw Mrs. Pearson out
  • C. The children leave home permanently
  • D. Mrs. Pearson becomes a full-time fortune teller

Answer: A. The family members become respectful, obedient, and appreciative of Mrs. Pearson

Explanation: The shock treatment successfully restores order, equality, and genuine respect for the mother in the Pearson household.
