Ch-1: Matter in Our Surroundings 10 Questions

1. Which of the following is considered as matter?

  • A. Chair
  • B. Air
  • C. Cold drink
  • D. All of the above

Answer: D. All of the above

Explanation: Anything that occupies space and has mass is defined as matter. Chair, air, and cold drink all have mass and volume.

2. What is the SI unit of density?

  • A. kg/m³
  • B. g/cm³
  • C. kg/L
  • D. g/L

Answer: A. kg/m³

Explanation: Density is defined as mass per unit volume. Mass is measured in kg and volume in m³, so its SI unit is kg/m³.

3. What is the physical state of water at 100°C?

  • A. Solid
  • B. Liquid
  • C. Gaseous (Steam) / Both Liquid and Gas
  • D. Plasma

Answer: C. Gaseous (Steam) / Both Liquid and Gas

Explanation: At 100°C (the boiling point of water), liquid water transitions into vapor/steam, existing in both liquid and gaseous states during boiling.

4. The process of change of a solid directly into gas without passing through the liquid state is called:

  • A. Evaporation
  • B. Sublimation
  • C. Condensation
  • D. Fusion

Answer: B. Sublimation

Explanation: Sublimation is the direct conversion of a solid substance into its gaseous state upon heating (e.g., Camphor, Ammonium Chloride).

5. Which factor increases the rate of evaporation?

  • A. Increase in surface area
  • B. Increase in temperature
  • C. Increase in wind speed
  • D. All of the above

Answer: D. All of the above

Explanation: Increasing surface area, temperature, and wind speed all enhance the rate at which liquid molecules escape into the air.

6. What is the value of 0°C on the Kelvin scale of temperature?

  • A. 273.15 K
  • B. 373 K
  • C. 100 K
  • D. 0 K

Answer: A. 273.15 K

Explanation: Temperature in Kelvin = Temperature in °C + 273.15 (commonly taken as 273 K).

7. Dry Ice is the solid form of which gas?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Carbon dioxide
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Hydrogen

Answer: B. Carbon dioxide

Explanation: Solid Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) is stored under high pressure and is commonly known as Dry Ice.

8. The phenomenon of intermixing of particles of two different types of matter on their own is known as:

  • A. Diffusion
  • B. Osmosis
  • C. Evaporation
  • D. Transpiration

Answer: A. Diffusion

Explanation: Diffusion is the spontaneous intermixing of particles of two different substances due to their continuous motion.

9. Which state of matter has a fixed volume but no fixed shape?

  • A. Solid
  • B. Liquid
  • C. Gas
  • D. Plasma

Answer: B. Liquid

Explanation: Liquids take the shape of the container they are poured into, giving them a indefinite shape, but their volume remains fixed.

10. Why does water stored in an earthen pot (matka) remain cool during summer?

  • A. Due to sublimation
  • B. Due to evaporation
  • C. Due to condensation
  • D. Due to precipitation

Answer: B. Due to evaporation

Explanation: Water seeps through the tiny pores of the earthen pot and evaporates, causing cooling of the remaining water inside.
