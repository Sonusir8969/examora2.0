Ch-11: Constructions 30 Questions

1. Which geometric instruments are allowed in geometric constructions using Euclidean principles?

  • A. Protractor and Ruler
  • B. Graduated ruler and Compass
  • C. Un-graduated ruler (straightedge) and Compass
  • D. Set squares and Ruler

Answer: C. Un-graduated ruler (straightedge) and Compass

Explanation: Classical geometrical construction strictly uses an un-marked ruler (straightedge) to draw lines and a compass to draw arcs/circles.

2. The angle bisector divides a given angle into how many equal parts?

  • A. 3
  • B. 2
  • C. 4
  • D. 5

Answer: B. 2

Explanation: An angle bisector divides an angle into two equal parts.

3. Which of the following angles CANNOT be constructed using a ruler and compass only?

  • A. 60°
  • B. 45°
  • C. 85°
  • D. 105°

Answer: C. 85°

Explanation: Angles that are multiples of 7.5° (or 15°) can be constructed using ruler and compass. 85° is not a multiple of 7.5°.

4. Which of the following angles CAN be constructed using a compass and ruler?

  • A. 37.5°
  • B. 40°
  • C. 50°
  • D. 70°

Answer: A. 37.5°

Explanation: 37.5° is half of 75° (bisecting 75°), which is a standard constructible angle (multiple of 7.5°).

5. The perpendicular bisector of a line segment divides the line segment at an angle of:

  • A. 45°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 180°

Answer: C. 90°

Explanation: "Perpendicular" means the line forms right angles (90°) at the point of intersection.

6. To construct a perpendicular bisector of a line segment AB of length 8 cm, the compass radius taken should be:

  • A. Exactly 4 cm
  • B. Less than 4 cm
  • C. More than 4 cm
  • D. Any radius

Answer: C. More than 4 cm

Explanation: The compass radius must be greater than half the length of the line segment (> 4 cm) so that the arcs intersect on both sides.

7. To construct an angle of 60° at the initial point of a ray, the arc radius must be equal to:

  • A. The length of the ray
  • B. Half the length of the ray
  • C. Any convenient radius
  • D. 10 cm

Answer: C. Any convenient radius

Explanation: Any convenient radius can be used, provided the same radius is maintained when cutting the initial arc.

8. A triangle ABC can be constructed if its perimeter and ________ base angles are given.

  • A. One
  • B. Two
  • C. Three
  • D. Zero

Answer: B. Two

Explanation: A triangle can be constructed given its perimeter and two base angles.

9. To construct a triangle ABC, given BC = 6 cm, ∠B = 60°, which of the following conditions is required for the sum AB + AC?

  • A. AB + AC = BC
  • B. AB + AC < BC
  • C. AB + AC > BC
  • D. AB + AC = 0

Answer: C. AB + AC > BC

Explanation: By the triangle inequality theorem, the sum of any two sides must be strictly greater than the third side (AB + AC > 6 cm).

10. To construct a triangle given base BC, ∠B, and difference AB - AC, construction is possible only when AB - AC is:

  • A. Equal to BC
  • B. Greater than BC
  • C. Less than BC
  • D. Double of BC

Answer: C. Less than BC

Explanation: The difference between two sides of a triangle is always less than the third side (|AB - AC| < BC).

11. An angle of 22.5° can be constructed by bisecting an angle of:

  • A. 30°
  • B. 45°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 90°

Answer: B. 45°

Explanation: 22.5° is half of 45° (45° / 2 = 22.5°).

12. An angle of 135° can be obtained by adding 90° to:

  • A. 30°
  • B. 45°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 15°

Answer: B. 45°

Explanation: 90° + 45° = 135°. It can also be constructed by bisecting the region between 90° and 180°.

13. Bisecting an angle of 120° gives an angle of:

  • A. 30°
  • B. 45°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 90°

Answer: C. 60°

Explanation: 120° / 2 = 60°.

14. Which angle is formed by bisecting the angle between 60° and 120°?

  • A. 75°
  • B. 90°
  • C. 105°
  • D. 80°

Answer: B. 90°

Explanation: The midpoint between 60° and 120° is (60° + 120°) / 2 = 180° / 2 = 90°.

15. The angle 75° can be constructed by bisecting the angle between:

  • A. 60° and 90°
  • B. 0° and 90°
  • C. 90° and 120°
  • D. 60° and 120°

Answer: A. 60° and 90°

Explanation: The midpoint between 60° and 90° is (60° + 90°) / 2 = 150° / 2 = 75°.

16. The angle 105° is constructed by bisecting the angle between:

  • A. 60° and 90°
  • B. 90° and 120°
  • C. 90° and 180°
  • D. 120° and 150°

Answer: B. 90° and 120°

Explanation: The midpoint between 90° and 120° is (90° + 120°) / 2 = 210° / 2 = 105°.

17. Is it possible to construct a triangle with sides 3 cm, 4 cm, and 8 cm?

  • A. Yes
  • B. No
  • C. Depends on the angles
  • D. Data insufficient

Answer: B. No

Explanation: Here, 3 + 4 = 7 cm, which is NOT greater than 8 cm. A triangle cannot be formed.

18. Every point on the perpendicular bisector of a line segment is ________ from the endpoints of the segment.

  • A. Unequal
  • B. Equidistant
  • C. Double distance
  • D. Half distance

Answer: B. Equidistant

Explanation: By property of perpendicular bisectors, any point on it is equidistant from both endpoints of the segment.

19. To construct an equilateral triangle of side 5 cm, we need to construct base of 5 cm and base angles of:

  • A. 45° each
  • B. 60° each
  • C. 90° each
  • D. 30° each

Answer: B. 60° each

Explanation: All internal angles of an equilateral triangle are equal to 60°.

20. The angle bisector of an angle of 150° gives two angles of:

  • A. 75° each
  • B. 60° each
  • C. 50° each
  • D. 90° each

Answer: A. 75° each

Explanation: Bisecting 150° yields 150° / 2 = 75° on each side.

21. Is it possible to construct a triangle ABC where BC = 5 cm, ∠B = 45° and AB + AC = 4 cm?

  • A. Yes
  • B. No
  • C. Only if ∠C = 90°
  • D. Cannot be determined

Answer: B. No

Explanation: Sum of two sides (AB + AC = 4 cm) must be greater than third side (BC = 5 cm). Since 4 < 5, construction is impossible.

22. To construct a right triangle whose base is 4 cm and sum of its hypotenuse and other side is 8 cm, the base angle at the right vertex is:

  • A. 45°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 30°

Answer: C. 90°

Explanation: In a right triangle, the angle between the base and the vertical leg is 90°.

23. Construction of a triangle ABC with AB = 4 cm, BC = 5 cm is NOT possible if AC is equal to:

  • A. 6 cm
  • B. 7 cm
  • C. 8 cm
  • D. 9 cm

Answer: D. 9 cm

Explanation: AB + BC = 4 + 5 = 9 cm. For a valid triangle, AC must be strictly LESS than 9 cm. So AC = 9 cm is not possible.

24. Bisecting a 90° angle yields an angle of:

  • A. 30°
  • B. 45°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 15°

Answer: B. 45°

Explanation: 90° / 2 = 45°.

25. Bisecting a 45° angle gives an angle of:

  • A. 22.5°
  • B. 15°
  • C. 30°
  • D. 12.5°

Answer: A. 22.5°

Explanation: 45° / 2 = 22.5° (or 22½°).

26. Which angle is constructed by bisecting the angle between 0° and 60°?

  • A. 15°
  • B. 30°
  • C. 45°
  • D. 20°

Answer: B. 30°

Explanation: The midpoint between 0° and 60° is 60° / 2 = 30°.

27. Which angle is constructed by bisecting the angle between 0° and 30°?

  • A. 15°
  • B. 10°
  • C. 20°
  • D. 25°

Answer: A. 15°

Explanation: 30° / 2 = 15°.

28. Which of the following angle measures CANNOT be constructed with compass and ruler?

  • A. 15°
  • B. 30°
  • C. 35°
  • D. 45°

Answer: C. 35°

Explanation: 35° is not a multiple of 7.5° and cannot be obtained by bisecting standard constructible angles.

29. The locus of points equidistant from two fixed points A and B is the:

  • A. Angle bisector
  • B. Circle with diameter AB
  • C. Perpendicular bisector of AB
  • D. Parallel line to AB

Answer: C. Perpendicular bisector of AB

Explanation: The perpendicular bisector contains all points that are equal distances from point A and point B.

30. When constructing a triangle ABC given base BC, base angle ∠B, and difference of sides (AC - AB) where AC > AB, the ray BX is drawn and segment cut on the ray is in direction:

  • A. Same direction of ray BX
  • B. Opposite direction of ray BX (extended downwards)
  • C. Perpendicular to BX
  • D. Parallel to BC

Answer: B. Opposite direction of ray BX (extended downwards)

Explanation: When AC > AB (i.e. AB < AC), the line segment equal to (AC - AB) is cut on the extension of ray BX in the opposite direction.
