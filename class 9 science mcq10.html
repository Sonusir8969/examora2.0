Ch-10: Gravitation 10 Questions

1. What is the value of Universal Gravitational Constant (G)?

  • A. 9.8 m/s²
  • B. 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²
  • C. 6.67 × 10¹¹ N·m²/kg²
  • D. 9.8 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²

Answer: B. 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²

Explanation: The accepted value of G was measured by Henry Cavendish and is 6.673 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg².

2. How does the gravitational force between two objects change if the distance between them is doubled?

  • A. Doubles
  • B. Quadruples
  • C. Becomes one-half
  • D. Becomes one-fourth

Answer: D. Becomes one-fourth

Explanation: Gravitational force follows inverse-square law (F ∝ 1/d²). If distance d is doubled (2d), F becomes 1/(2)² = 1/4th of its initial value.

3. What is the average acceleration due to gravity (g) near the surface of the Earth?

  • A. 9.8 m/s²
  • B. 6.67 m/s²
  • C. 9.8 N·m²/kg²
  • D. 0 m/s²

Answer: A. 9.8 m/s²

Explanation: Standard acceleration due to gravity on Earth's surface is approximately 9.8 m/s².

4. The weight of an object on the Moon is approximately how much of its weight on Earth?

  • A. 1/2
  • B. 1/4
  • C. 1/6
  • D. Same as on Earth

Answer: C. 1/6

Explanation: Because the mass and radius of the Moon are smaller, acceleration due to gravity on the Moon (g_moon) is about 1/6th of g_earth.

5. Which of the following statements about Mass and Weight is correct?

  • A. Mass changes with location, weight remains constant
  • B. Mass is a vector quantity, weight is a scalar quantity
  • C. Mass remains constant everywhere, weight varies with gravity
  • D. Both mass and weight have the same SI unit

Answer: C. Mass remains constant everywhere, weight varies with gravity

Explanation: Mass is the amount of matter in a body and stays constant. Weight (W = mg) is a force that depends on local acceleration due to gravity (g).

6. What is the SI unit of Pressure?

  • A. Newton (N)
  • B. Pascal (Pa)
  • C. Joule (J)
  • D. kg/m³

Answer: B. Pascal (Pa)

Explanation: Pressure is force per unit area (N/m²). One Newton per square meter is defined as 1 Pascal (Pa).

7. According to Archimedes' Principle, when a body is immersed fully or partially in a fluid, it experiences an upward buoyant force equal to:

  • A. Total volume of the fluid
  • B. Mass of the body immersed
  • C. Weight of the fluid displaced by it
  • D. Total surface area of the body

Answer: C. Weight of the fluid displaced by it

Explanation: Archimedes' Principle states that buoyant force acting on a submerged object equals the weight of the liquid displaced by that object.

8. An object will float in a liquid if its density is:

  • A. Greater than the density of the liquid
  • B. Less than or equal to the density of the liquid
  • C. Double the density of the liquid
  • D. Independent of liquid density

Answer: B. Less than or equal to the density of the liquid

Explanation: If an object has a density less than (or equal to) the liquid, the buoyant force balances its weight, allowing it to float.

9. What is Relative Density?

  • A. Density of substance / Density of water
  • B. Density of water / Density of substance
  • C. Mass of substance × Volume of water
  • D. Density of substance × Density of water

Answer: A. Density of substance / Density of water

Explanation: Relative density is the ratio of the density of a substance to the density of water at 4°C. It has no units.

10. If an object weighs 60 N on Earth, what would be its weight on the surface of the Moon?

  • A. 60 N
  • B. 360 N
  • C. 10 N
  • D. 6 N

Answer: C. 10 N

Explanation: Weight on Moon = 1/6 × Weight on Earth = 1/6 × 60 N = 10 N.
