Ch-13: Statistics 30 Questions

1. Range of a given set of observations is defined as:

  • A. Maximum value - Minimum value
  • B. Maximum value + Minimum value
  • C. (Maximum value - Minimum value) / 2
  • D. Sum of observations / Total number

Answer: A. Maximum value - Minimum value

Explanation: Range is the simplest measure of dispersion, calculated as Maximum value minus Minimum value.

2. The mean deviation about mean for ungrouped data is given by:

  • A. (1/n) Σ |xᵢ - x̄|
  • B. (1/n) Σ (xᵢ - x̄)²
  • C. Σ |xᵢ - x̄|
  • D. √[(1/n) Σ (xᵢ - x̄)²]

Answer: A. (1/n) Σ |xᵢ - x̄|

Explanation: Mean deviation about mean is arithmetic mean of absolute deviations from mean.

3. Find the range of the data: 6, 7, 10, 12, 13, 4, 8, 12.

  • A. 9
  • B. 8
  • C. 7
  • D. 10

Answer: A. 9

Explanation: Maximum value = 13, Minimum value = 4. Range = 13 - 4 = 9.

4. Mean deviation about median is minimum compared to mean deviation about any other value. This statement is:

  • A. True
  • B. False
  • C. True only for symmetric data
  • D. Cannot be determined

Answer: A. True

Explanation: The sum of absolute deviations is minimum when measured about the median.

5. Variance (σ²) of n observations x₁, x₂, ..., xₙ is given by:

  • A. (1/n) Σ (xᵢ - x̄)²
  • B. (1/n) Σ |xᵢ - x̄|
  • C. √[(1/n) Σ (xᵢ - x̄)²]
  • D. Σ (xᵢ - x̄)²

Answer: A. (1/n) Σ (xᵢ - x̄)²

Explanation: Variance is defined as mean of squared deviations from mean.

6. Standard Deviation (σ) is equal to:

  • A. Square root of Variance
  • B. Square of Variance
  • C. Mean Deviation
  • D. Half of Variance

Answer: A. Square root of Variance

Explanation: Standard deviation σ = √(Variance) = √σ².

7. The formula for Coefficient of Variation (C.V.) is:

  • A. (σ / x̄) × 100
  • B. (x̄ / σ) × 100
  • C. (σ² / x̄) × 100
  • D. (Mean Deviation / Mean) × 100

Answer: A. (σ / x̄) × 100

Explanation: C.V. measures relative dispersion and equals (Standard Deviation / Mean) × 100.

8. Between two series, the series with greater Coefficient of Variation is:

  • A. More variable (less consistent)
  • B. Less variable (more consistent)
  • C. More consistent
  • D. Has higher mean

Answer: A. More variable (less consistent)

Explanation: Higher C.V. indicates greater variability and lesser consistency.

9. Variance of first n natural numbers is given by:

  • A. (n² - 1) / 12
  • B. (n² + 1) / 12
  • C. (n - 1) / 6
  • D. n² / 12

Answer: A. (n² - 1) / 12

Explanation: Standard formula for variance of 1, 2, ..., n is (n² - 1) / 12.

10. Standard deviation of first n natural numbers is:

  • A. √[(n² - 1) / 12]
  • B. (n² - 1) / 12
  • C. √[(n² + 1) / 12]
  • D. (n + 1) / 2

Answer: A. √[(n² - 1) / 12]

Explanation: Standard deviation is square root of variance: √[(n² - 1) / 12].

11. If each observation is multiplied by a constant k, then the new standard deviation becomes:

  • A. |k| × original σ
  • B. k² × original σ
  • C. original σ
  • D. original σ + k

Answer: A. |k| × original σ

Explanation: Scaling observations by k multiplies standard deviation by absolute value |k|.

12. If each observation is multiplied by a constant k, the new variance becomes:

  • A. k² × original variance
  • B. k × original variance
  • C. original variance
  • D. original variance + k²

Answer: A. k² × original variance

Explanation: Since variance is square of S.D., multiplying data by k multiplies variance by k².

13. If a constant k is added to each observation, the new standard deviation is:

  • A. Same as original σ
  • B. original σ + k
  • C. k × original σ
  • D. original σ - k

Answer: A. Same as original σ

Explanation: Standard deviation is independent of change of origin (adding/subtracting constant).

14. Find mean deviation about mean for data: 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 17.

  • A. 3
  • B. 3.25
  • C. 4
  • D. 2.5

Answer: A. 3

Explanation: Mean x̄ = 80/8 = 10. Deviations |xᵢ - 10| = 6, 3, 2, 1, 0, 2, 3, 7. Sum = 24. Mean Dev = 24/8 = 3.

15. Sum of deviations of observations from their actual mean is always:

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. Positive
  • D. Negative

Answer: A. 0

Explanation: Fundamental algebraic property of arithmetic mean: Σ(xᵢ - x̄) = 0.

16. Calculate variance for data: 6, 7, 10, 12, 13, 4, 8, 12.

  • A. 9.25
  • B. 8.5
  • C. 10
  • D. 9.75

Answer: A. 9.25

Explanation: Mean x̄ = 72/8 = 9. Squared deviations: 9, 4, 1, 9, 16, 25, 1, 9. Sum = 74. Variance = 74/8 = 9.25.

17. Standard deviation of 5 observations is 4. If each observation is increased by 2, new S.D. is:

  • A. 4
  • B. 6
  • C. 2
  • D. 8

Answer: A. 4

Explanation: S.D. does not change when a constant is added to each observation.

18. Standard deviation of 5 observations is 3. If each observation is multiplied by 2, new S.D. is:

  • A. 6
  • B. 3
  • C. 12
  • D. 9

Answer: A. 6

Explanation: New S.D. = |2| × original S.D. = 2 × 3 = 6.

19. Variance of 10 observations is 5. If each observation is multiplied by 3, new variance is:

  • A. 45
  • B. 15
  • C. 25
  • D. 135

Answer: A. 45

Explanation: New Variance = 3² × original variance = 9 × 5 = 45.

20. If mean of a distribution is 25 and standard deviation is 5, its Coefficient of Variation is:

  • A. 20%
  • B. 5%
  • C. 25%
  • D. 125%

Answer: A. 20%

Explanation: C.V. = (σ / x̄) × 100 = (5 / 25) × 100 = 20%.

21. Find median of data: 13, 17, 16, 14, 11, 13, 10, 16, 11, 18, 12.

  • A. 13
  • B. 14
  • C. 12
  • D. 13.5

Answer: A. 13

Explanation: Ordered data (11 items): 10, 11, 11, 12, 13, 13, 14, 16, 16, 17, 18. Median = 6th term = 13.

22. Variance of constant sequence 5, 5, 5, 5, 5 is:

  • A. 0
  • B. 5
  • C. 25
  • D. 1

Answer: A. 0

Explanation: All observations are identical, so there is no dispersion (variance = 0).

23. For grouped discrete frequency distribution, variance is given by:

  • A. (1/N) Σ fᵢ(xᵢ - x̄)²
  • B. (1/N) Σ fᵢ|xᵢ - x̄|
  • C. Σ fᵢxᵢ² / N
  • D. (1/N) Σ (xᵢ - x̄)²

Answer: A. (1/N) Σ fᵢ(xᵢ - x̄)²

Explanation: Standard frequency-weighted formula for variance where N = Σ fᵢ.

24. Variance of first 5 natural numbers (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) is:

  • A. 2
  • B. 2.5
  • C. 3
  • D. 1.5

Answer: A. 2

Explanation: Formula = (n² - 1) / 12 = (25 - 1) / 12 = 24 / 12 = 2.

25. Which of the following is NOT a measure of dispersion?

  • A. Mean
  • B. Range
  • C. Standard Deviation
  • D. Variance

Answer: A. Mean

Explanation: Mean is a measure of central tendency, whereas Range, S.D., and Variance measure dispersion.

26. If C.V. of a distribution is 60% and standard deviation is 21, then mean is:

  • A. 35
  • B. 30
  • C. 40
  • D. 25

Answer: A. 35

Explanation: C.V. = (σ / x̄) × 100 ⇒ 60 = (21 / x̄) × 100 ⇒ x̄ = 2100 / 60 = 35.

27. Sum of squares of deviations of 10 observations from their mean 15 is 250. Variance is:

  • A. 25
  • B. 250
  • C. 5
  • D. 15

Answer: A. 25

Explanation: Variance = Σ(xᵢ - x̄)² / n = 250 / 10 = 25.

28. Two series with means x̄₁ and x̄₂ have same S.D. σ. Which series is more consistent?

  • A. Series with greater mean
  • B. Series with smaller mean
  • C. Both are equally consistent
  • D. Cannot be determined

Answer: A. Series with greater mean

Explanation: C.V. = (σ / x̄) × 100. Larger mean gives smaller C.V., which implies higher consistency.

29. Shortcut formula for calculating variance with step-deviation yᵢ = (xᵢ - A)/h is:

  • A. h² / N² × [N Σ fᵢyᵢ² - (Σ fᵢyᵢ)²]
  • B. h / N × [N Σ fᵢyᵢ² - (Σ fᵢyᵢ)²]
  • C. h² / N × [Σ fᵢyᵢ² - Σ fᵢyᵢ]
  • D. [Σ fᵢyᵢ² - (Σ fᵢyᵢ)²] / N²

Answer: A. h² / N² × [N Σ fᵢyᵢ² - (Σ fᵢyᵢ)²]

Explanation: Standard step-deviation formula for variance in grouped frequency distribution.

30. Mean deviation about mean is always:

  • A. Less than or equal to Standard Deviation
  • B. Greater than Standard Deviation
  • C. Equal to Variance
  • D. Double of Standard Deviation

Answer: A. Less than or equal to Standard Deviation

Explanation: Mathematically, Mean Deviation ≤ Standard Deviation (M.D. ≈ 0.8 × S.D. for normal distributions).
