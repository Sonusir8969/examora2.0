NCERT Solutions for Class 10 English Chapter 6 The Hundred Dresses II

NCERT Solutions for Class 10 English First Flight Chapter 6 The Hundred Dresses II covers the continuation of Wanda's story, the reveal of her artistic talent, and important questions and answers.

Overview of Topics & Subtopics (Click to Jump)

  • 1. Story Summary
  • 2. Character Sketch
  • 3. Theme & Message

1. Story Summary

Short Note:

• This chapter continues from where Part I left off, revealing that Wanda had actually drawn a hundred different, beautiful dress designs, each based on herself, which she had kept secret from her taunting classmates.

• The class is stunned when these wonderful drawings are displayed at school as part of a drawing contest, which Wanda wins, revealing her hidden talent to everyone, including those who had mocked her.

• Peggy and Maddie feel deep regret for their behaviour and try to write a letter of apology to Wanda, but by then her family has already moved to a new city, leaving them unable to properly make amends.

2. Character Sketch

Short Note:

• Wanda Petronski: Revealed to be a genuinely talented artist whose quiet dignity throughout the teasing makes her ultimate triumph even more poignant.

• Peggy and Maddie: Both girls undergo a moral transformation, moving from thoughtlessness and silent complicity to genuine remorse and a desire to change their behaviour towards others in the future.

3. Theme & Message

Short Note:

• The chapter emphasises themes of regret, redemption, and the value of kindness, showing how guilt can lead to genuine personal growth and a resolve to treat others better.

• It reinforces the idea that everyone has hidden depths and talents, and that judging others too quickly can cause us to overlook their true worth.

Reading With Insight (Textbook Questions)

Q1: How did the class react when they saw Wanda's hundred dress drawings?
Answer: The class, including Peggy and Maddie, was amazed and deeply moved by the beauty and creativity of Wanda's hundred dress designs, realising for the first time the extraordinary talent that lay behind her claim, which they had previously mocked as a lie.
Q2: Why did Peggy and Maddie feel guilty after seeing Wanda's drawings?
Answer: They felt guilty because they finally understood that Wanda had been telling a kind of truth all along through her imaginative designs, and they realised how much pain their thoughtless teasing must have caused her, especially since she never had a chance to properly explain herself before moving away.
Q3: What did Maddie resolve to do differently in the future?
Answer: Maddie resolved that she would never again remain a silent bystander while someone was being bullied or mistreated, promising herself to speak up and defend others even if it meant risking her own popularity or friendships.

Extract-Based / Short Answer Questions

Q4: Why couldn't Peggy and Maddie properly apologise to Wanda?
Answer: By the time the girls tried to send Wanda a letter of apology and appreciation for her drawings, her family had already moved away to a new city, leaving the girls without a way to make things right or express their regret to her directly.
Q5: What is the overall moral message conveyed by the two parts of 'The Hundred Dresses'?
Answer: The overall moral message is that unkindness and thoughtless teasing can cause deep, lasting hurt, and that true courage lies in standing up against injustice rather than staying silent, while also reminding readers to value people for their true worth rather than superficial judgments.
