Ch-12: Surface Areas and Volumes 30 Questions

1. Two cubes each of volume 64 cm³ are joined end to end. The surface area of the resulting cuboid is:

  • A. 160 cm²
  • B. 128 cm²
  • C. 144 cm²
  • D. 120 cm²

Answer: A. 160 cm²

Explanation: Side of cube = ∛64 = 4 cm. For resulting cuboid: l = 8 cm, b = 4 cm, h = 4 cm. Total surface area = 2(lb + bh + hl) = 2(32 + 16 + 32) = 160 cm².

2. Total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius r is:

  • A. 2πr²
  • B. 3πr²
  • C. 4πr²
  • D. (2/3)πr³

Answer: B. 3πr²

Explanation: Total surface area of solid hemisphere = Curved surface area + Base area = 2πr² + πr² = 3πr².

3. Curved surface area of a right circular cone with radius r and slant height l is:

  • A. πr²h
  • B. πrl
  • C. 2πrl
  • D. πr(l + r)

Answer: B. πrl

Explanation: The formula for curved surface area (CSA) of a right circular cone is πrl.

4. Volume of a sphere of radius r is given by:

  • A. (4/3)πr³
  • B. (2/3)πr³
  • C. 4πr²
  • D. (1/3)πr³

Answer: A. (4/3)πr³

Explanation: Standard formula for the volume of a sphere of radius r is (4/3)πr³.

5. If the radius of a sphere is doubled, then its volume becomes how many times?

  • A. 2 times
  • B. 4 times
  • C. 8 times
  • D. 16 times

Answer: C. 8 times

Explanation: Volume varies with r³. If radius is doubled (2r)³, new volume = 2³ = 8 times original volume.

6. Relationship between height (h), radius (r), and slant height (l) of a cone is:

  • A. l² = h² + r²
  • B. l = h + r
  • C. h² = l² + r²
  • D. r² = l² + h²

Answer: A. l² = h² + r²

Explanation: By Pythagorean theorem in right-angled triangle formed inside the cone: l = √(r² + h²) ⇒ l² = h² + r².

7. A metallic sphere of radius 3 cm is melted and recast into a wire of radius 0.2 cm. Length of wire is:

  • A. 900 cm
  • B. 450 cm
  • C. 90 cm
  • D. 360 cm

Answer: A. 900 cm

Explanation: Volume of sphere = Volume of cylinder. (4/3)π(3³) = π(0.2²)h ⇒ 36 = 0.04h ⇒ h = 900 cm.

8. The curved surface area of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm is:

  • A. 440 cm²
  • B. 220 cm²
  • C. 1540 cm²
  • D. 880 cm²

Answer: A. 440 cm²

Explanation: CSA = 2πrh = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 10 = 440 cm².

9. If volumes of two spheres are in ratio 64 : 27, then ratio of their surface areas is:

  • A. 4 : 3
  • B. 16 : 9
  • C. 9 : 16
  • D. 3 : 4

Answer: B. 16 : 9

Explanation: Ratio of volumes = r1³/r2³ = 64/27 ⇒ r1/r2 = 4/3. Ratio of surface areas = r1²/r2² = (4/3)² = 16/9.

10. Volume of a cone of height 12 cm and base radius 7 cm is:

  • A. 616 cm³
  • B. 1848 cm³
  • C. 308 cm³
  • D. 1232 cm³

Answer: A. 616 cm³

Explanation: Volume = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3) × (22/7) × 7 × 7 × 12 = 22 × 7 × 4 = 616 cm³.

11. Total surface area of a cube of edge length 'a' is:

  • A. 4a²
  • B. 6a²
  • C. a³
  • D. 12a

Answer: B. 6a²

Explanation: A cube has 6 square faces, each of area a². Total surface area = 6a².

12. Volume of a hemisphere of radius 3 cm is:

  • A. 18π cm³
  • B. 36π cm³
  • C. 54π cm³
  • D. 9π cm³

Answer: A. 18π cm³

Explanation: Volume = (2/3)πr³ = (2/3) × π × 3³ = (2/3) × 27π = 18π cm³.

13. Slant height of a cone having radius 5 cm and height 12 cm is:

  • A. 13 cm
  • B. 17 cm
  • C. 15 cm
  • D. 10 cm

Answer: A. 13 cm

Explanation: l = √(r² + h²) = √(5² + 12²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13 cm.

14. Ratio of total surface area to curved surface area of a solid hemisphere is:

  • A. 3 : 2
  • B. 2 : 3
  • C. 4 : 3
  • D. 1 : 2

Answer: A. 3 : 2

Explanation: TSA = 3πr², CSA = 2πr². Ratio TSA / CSA = 3πr² / 2πr² = 3/2.

15. Length of diagonal of a cube of edge 'a' is:

  • A. a√2
  • B. a√3
  • C. 2a
  • D. a√6

Answer: B. a√3

Explanation: Space diagonal of a cube = √(a² + a² + a²) = √(3a²) = a√3.

16. If height and radius of a cone are doubled, then its volume becomes:

  • A. 2 times
  • B. 4 times
  • C. 8 times
  • D. 16 times

Answer: C. 8 times

Explanation: Original Vol = (1/3)πr²h. New Vol = (1/3)π(2r)²(2h) = (1/3)π(4r²)(2h) = 8 × Original Vol.

17. A solid piece of iron in form of a cuboid of dimensions 49cm × 33cm × 24cm is melted to form a solid sphere. Radius of sphere is:

  • A. 21 cm
  • B. 14 cm
  • C. 28 cm
  • D. 7 cm

Answer: A. 21 cm

Explanation: Volume sphere = Volume cuboid ⇒ (4/3) × (22/7) × r³ = 49 × 33 × 24 ⇒ r³ = 9261 ⇒ r = 21 cm.

18. The total surface area of a hollow hemisphere of outer radius R and inner radius r is:

  • A. 2π(R² + r²)
  • B. π(3R² + r²)
  • C. 3π(R² - r²)
  • D. 2π(R² - r²)

Answer: B. π(3R² + r²)

Explanation: TSA = Outer CSA + Inner CSA + Ring Area = 2πR² + 2πr² + π(R² - r²) = π(3R² + r²).

19. Total surface area of a cylinder of radius r and height h is:

  • A. 2πr(r + h)
  • B. 2πrh
  • C. πr(r + h)
  • D. 2πr²h

Answer: A. 2πr(r + h)

Explanation: TSA of cylinder = Curved surface area + 2 × Base area = 2πrh + 2πr² = 2πr(r + h).

20. The radius of a sphere whose surface area is 154 cm² is:

  • A. 3.5 cm
  • B. 7 cm
  • C. 14 cm
  • D. 2.5 cm

Answer: A. 3.5 cm

Explanation: 4πr² = 154 ⇒ 4 × (22/7) × r² = 154 ⇒ r² = 12.25 ⇒ r = 3.5 cm.

21. Volume of the largest right circular cone that can be cut out of a cube of edge 7 cm is:

  • A. 89.83 cm³
  • B. 179.66 cm³
  • C. 44.91 cm³
  • D. 343 cm³

Answer: A. 89.83 cm³

Explanation: Diameter = 7 cm (r = 3.5 cm), Height = 7 cm. Vol = (1/3) × (22/7) × (3.5)² × 7 = 89.83 cm³.

22. If radius of base of a cylinder is doubled and height is halved, its volume becomes:

  • A. Same
  • B. Doubled
  • C. Halved
  • D. Quadrupled

Answer: B. Doubled

Explanation: New Vol = π(2r)²(h/2) = π(4r²)(h/2) = 2(πr²h) = 2 × Original Vol.

23. Number of spherical balls of diameter 2 cm that can be made by melting a solid sphere of radius 8 cm is:

  • A. 512
  • B. 64
  • C. 256
  • D. 128

Answer: A. 512

Explanation: Ball radius = 1 cm. Number = Volume large sphere / Volume small sphere = (4/3)π(8³) / [(4/3)π(1³)] = 512.

24. Lateral surface area of a cube is 256 cm². Edge of the cube is:

  • A. 8 cm
  • B. 16 cm
  • C. 64 cm
  • D. 4 cm

Answer: A. 8 cm

Explanation: LSA of cube = 4a² = 256 ⇒ a² = 64 ⇒ a = 8 cm.

25. Total surface area of a cone of base radius r and slant height l is:

  • A. πr(l + r)
  • B. πrl
  • C. 2πr(l + r)
  • D. πr²l

Answer: A. πr(l + r)

Explanation: Total surface area = Curved surface area + Base area = πrl + πr² = πr(l + r).

26. Total surface area of a solid wooden toy in the shape of a hemisphere surmounted by a cone is:

  • A. πr(l + 2r)
  • B. πr(l + r)
  • C. 2πr(l + r)
  • D. πr²(h + r)

Answer: A. πr(l + 2r)

Explanation: TSA = Cone CSA + Hemisphere CSA = πrl + 2πr² = πr(l + 2r).

27. Ratio of volumes of a cylinder, cone, and sphere having same radius and same height (height = diameter = 2r) is:

  • A. 3 : 1 : 2
  • B. 1 : 2 : 3
  • C. 3 : 2 : 1
  • D. 2 : 1 : 3

Answer: A. 3 : 1 : 2

Explanation: Cylinder = 2πr³, Cone = (2/3)πr³, Sphere = (4/3)πr³. Ratio = 2 : (2/3) : (4/3) = 6 : 2 : 4 = 3 : 1 : 2.

28. A cone and a cylinder have equal bases and equal heights. Ratio of their volumes is:

  • A. 1 : 3
  • B. 3 : 1
  • C. 1 : 2
  • D. 2 : 1

Answer: A. 1 : 3

Explanation: Volume Cone / Volume Cylinder = [(1/3)πr²h] / [πr²h] = 1/3 = 1 : 3.

29. The capacity of a cylindrical tank of radius 2.1 m and height 5 m is:

  • A. 69.3 m³
  • B. 34.65 m³
  • C. 138.6 m³
  • D. 693 m³

Answer: A. 69.3 m³

Explanation: Vol = πr²h = (22/7) × (2.1)² × 5 = (22/7) × 4.41 × 5 = 69.3 m³.

30. Surface area of a sphere of diameter 14 cm is:

  • A. 616 cm²
  • B. 308 cm²
  • C. 154 cm²
  • D. 1232 cm²

Answer: A. 616 cm²

Explanation: Radius r = 7 cm. Area = 4πr² = 4 × (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 616 cm².
