Ch-3: Coordinate Geometry 10 Questions

1. The point of intersection of the horizontal axis (x-axis) and vertical axis (y-axis) in a Cartesian plane is called:

  • A. Abscissa
  • B. Ordinate
  • C. Origin
  • D. Quadrant

Answer: C. Origin

Explanation: The point where the x-axis and y-axis intersect perpendicular to each other is called the origin, denoted by O(0, 0).

2. The x-coordinate of a point is also known as its:

  • A. Ordinate
  • B. Abscissa
  • C. Origin
  • D. Quadrant

Answer: B. Abscissa

Explanation: In an ordered pair (x, y), the x-coordinate is called the abscissa and the y-coordinate is called the ordinate.

3. In which quadrant does the point (-3, 5) lie?

  • A. Quadrant I
  • B. Quadrant II
  • C. Quadrant III
  • D. Quadrant IV

Answer: B. Quadrant II

Explanation: Points in Quadrant II have a negative x-coordinate and a positive y-coordinate (- , +).

4. The sign of points lying in Quadrant IV is of the form:

  • A. (+ , +)
  • B. (- , +)
  • C. (- , -)
  • D. (+ , -)

Answer: D. (+ , -)

Explanation: Quadrant IV lies to the right of the y-axis (x is positive) and below the x-axis (y is negative).

5. Any point lying on the x-axis has coordinates of the form:

  • A. (0, y)
  • B. (x, 0)
  • C. (x, y)
  • D. (0, 0)

Answer: B. (x, 0)

Explanation: The perpendicular distance of any point on the x-axis from the x-axis itself is zero, so its ordinate (y) is always 0.

6. What is the perpendicular distance of the point P(4, 3) from the x-axis?

  • A. 4 units
  • B. 3 units
  • C. 7 units
  • D. 5 units

Answer: B. 3 units

Explanation: The perpendicular distance of a point from the x-axis is given by the absolute value of its y-coordinate (ordinate), which is 3.

7. The point (0, -7) lies:

  • A. On the positive x-axis
  • B. In Quadrant III
  • C. On the negative y-axis
  • D. In Quadrant IV

Answer: C. On the negative y-axis

Explanation: Since the x-coordinate is 0 and the y-coordinate is negative (-7), the point lies directly on the negative y-axis.

8. If the abscissa of a point is positive and its ordinate is negative, the point lies in:

  • A. Quadrant I
  • B. Quadrant II
  • C. Quadrant III
  • D. Quadrant IV

Answer: D. Quadrant IV

Explanation: Abscissa (x) > 0 and Ordinate (y) < 0 corresponds to signs (+, -), which belongs to Quadrant IV.

9. Coordinates of the origin are:

  • A. (0, 1)
  • B. (1, 0)
  • C. (0, 0)
  • D. (1, 1)

Answer: C. (0, 0)

Explanation: At the origin, both the x-coordinate and y-coordinate are zero, represented as (0, 0).

10. The Cartesian plane is divided into how many quadrants by the axes?

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 8

Answer: C. 4

Explanation: The two perpendicular coordinate axes divide the Cartesian plane into four equal regions called quadrants (I, II, III, and IV).
