Ch-9: Force and Laws of Motion 10 Questions

1. Newton's First Law of Motion is also known as:

  • A. Law of Conservation of Momentum
  • B. Law of Inertia
  • C. Law of Gravitation
  • D. Law of Action-Reaction

Answer: B. Law of Inertia

Explanation: Newton's first law states that an object remains in a state of rest or uniform motion unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force. This property is inertia.

2. What is the SI unit of Force?

  • A. Joule
  • B. Watt
  • C. Newton
  • D. Pascal

Answer: C. Newton

Explanation: The SI unit of force is Newton (N), which is equivalent to 1 kg·m/s².

3. Inertia of a body depends directly on its:

  • A. Velocity
  • B. Shape
  • C. Mass
  • D. Volume

Answer: C. Mass

Explanation: Mass is a measure of inertia. A heavier object with greater mass has larger inertia, offering higher resistance to change in its state of motion.

4. Mathematical formulation of Newton's Second Law of Motion gives the formula:

  • A. F = m / a
  • B. F = m × a
  • C. F = m × v
  • D. F = m × t

Answer: B. F = m × a

Explanation: According to the second law, force is the rate of change of momentum: F = dp/dt = m(v - u)/t = ma.

5. What is the SI unit of Momentum (p = mv)?

  • A. kg·m/s²
  • B. kg·m/s
  • C. N·m
  • D. m/s²

Answer: B. kg·m/s

Explanation: Momentum = Mass × Velocity = kg × (m/s) = kg·m/s.

6. A fielder pulls his hands backwards while catching a fast-moving cricket ball to:

  • A. Decrease the time taken to stop the ball
  • B. Increase the rate of change of momentum
  • C. Increase time interval, thereby reducing force on hands
  • D. Exert higher force on the ball

Answer: C. Increase time interval, thereby reducing force on hands

Explanation: By pulling hands back, time (t) increases, which lowers the rate of change of momentum (F = Δp/t) and reduces impact force on hands.

7. According to Newton's Third Law of Motion, action and reaction forces act on:

  • A. The same body in the same direction
  • B. Different bodies in opposite directions
  • C. The same body in opposite directions
  • D. Different bodies in the same direction

Answer: B. Different bodies in opposite directions

Explanation: Action and reaction forces are always equal in magnitude and opposite in direction, acting simultaneously on two different interacting bodies.

8. Recoil velocity of a gun after firing a bullet is an application of:

  • A. Law of Conservation of Energy
  • B. Law of Conservation of Mass
  • C. Law of Conservation of Momentum
  • D. Newton's Law of Gravitation

Answer: C. Law of Conservation of Momentum

Explanation: Total momentum before firing (0) equals total momentum after firing (bullet momentum + recoil gun momentum = 0).

9. When a bus suddenly starts moving, passengers tend to fall backwards due to:

  • A. Inertia of motion
  • B. Inertia of rest
  • C. Inertia of direction
  • D. Gravitational pull

Answer: B. Inertia of rest

Explanation: The lower body in contact with the bus floor comes into motion, while the upper body resists movement due to inertia of rest, causing a backward fall.

10. Calculate the force required to produce an acceleration of 2 m/s² in an object of mass 5 kg.

  • A. 2.5 N
  • B. 7 N
  • C. 10 N
  • D. 0.4 N

Answer: C. 10 N

Explanation: F = m × a = 5 kg × 2 m/s² = 10 N.
