Ch-11: The Proposal 30 Questions

1. Who is the author of the play 'The Proposal'?

  • A. Anton Chekhov
  • B. William Shakespeare
  • C. Guy de Maupassant
  • D. Leo Tolstoy

Answer: A. Anton Chekhov

Explanation: 'The Proposal' (originally titled 'A Marriage Proposal') is a famous one-act farce by Russian playwright Anton Chekhov.

2. What type of drama/play is 'The Proposal'?

  • A. One-act farce
  • B. Historical tragedy
  • C. Romantic musical
  • D. Detective thriller

Answer: A. One-act farce

Explanation: The play is a humorous one-act farce that satirizes the upper-class Russian matchmaking traditions.

3. What is the full name of Lomov in the play?

  • A. Ivan Vassilevitch Lomov
  • B. Stepan Stepanovitch Lomov
  • C. Natalya Stepanovna Lomov
  • D. Vassily Petrovitch Lomov

Answer: A. Ivan Vassilevitch Lomov

Explanation: Lomov's full name is Ivan Vassilevitch Lomov, a 35-year-old landowner.

4. What is Chubukov's full name?

  • A. Stepan Stepanovitch Chubukov
  • B. Ivan Vassilevitch Chubukov
  • C. Alexey Nikolayevitch Chubukov
  • D. Mikhail Stepanovitch Chubukov

Answer: A. Stepan Stepanovitch Chubukov

Explanation: The father's full name in the play is Stepan Stepanovitch Chubukov.

5. How old is Natalya Stepanovna?

  • A. 25 years old
  • B. 20 years old
  • C. 30 years old
  • D. 35 years old

Answer: A. 25 years old

Explanation: Natalya is described as a twenty-five-year-old woman, an excellent housekeeper, and educated.

6. How old is Ivan Vassilevitch Lomov?

  • A. 35 years old
  • B. 25 years old
  • C. 40 years old
  • D. 30 years old

Answer: A. 35 years old

Explanation: Lomov mentions that he is already 35 years old, which he considers a critical age to settle down.

7. Why did Lomov visit Chubukov's house dressed formally in evening clothes?

  • A. To ask for Natalya's hand in marriage
  • B. To borrow money from Chubukov
  • C. To invite Chubukov for a party
  • D. To settle a land boundary dispute

Answer: A. To ask for Natalya's hand in marriage

Explanation: Lomov dressed in formal evening dress with gloves to propose marriage to Chubukov's daughter, Natalya.

8. What did Chubukov initialy assume Lomov came for?

  • A. To borrow money
  • B. To ask for help in hunting
  • C. To sell his property
  • D. To propose to his daughter

Answer: A. To borrow money

Explanation: Chubukov privately thought Lomov came to borrow money and secretly resolved not to give him any.

9. How did Chubukov react when he learned Lomov wanted to marry Natalya?

  • A. He was overjoyed, hugged and kissed Lomov
  • B. He rejected the offer immediately
  • C. He got angry and threw him out
  • D. He asked for time to think about it

Answer: A. He was overjoyed, hugged and kissed Lomov

Explanation: Chubukov was extremely happy, embraced Lomov with tears of joy, stating he had always wished for this.

10. What health issue does Lomov suffer from in the play?

  • A. Palpitations, excitability, and insomnia/sleep twitching
  • B. Severe asthma attacks
  • C. High blood pressure only
  • D. Loss of memory

Answer: A. Palpitations, excitability, and insomnia/sleep twitching

Explanation: Lomov complains of palpitations, becoming easily upset, a twitching eyebrow, and sudden spasms when trying to sleep.

11. What was the first topic of argument between Lomov and Natalya?

  • A. Ownership of Oxen Meadows
  • B. Superiority of their hunting dogs
  • C. The wedding date and venue
  • D. Financial status of their families

Answer: A. Ownership of Oxen Meadows

Explanation: While attempting to propose, Lomov casually mentioned "my Oxen Meadows," sparking a heated debate about who actually owned the land.

12. Where are the Oxen Meadows located according to the text?

  • A. Wedged between Chubukov's Birchwoods and the Burnt Marsh
  • B. Near the river bank in the center of the village
  • C. Adjacent to Lomov's residential house
  • D. On the outskirts near the public forest

Answer: A. Wedged between Chubukov's Birchwoods and the Burnt Marsh

Explanation: Lomov describes Oxen Meadows as touching Chubukov's birchwoods and lying between them and the Burnt Marsh.

13. According to Lomov, who originally gave the free use of Oxen Meadows to Natalya's great-grandfather's peasants?

  • A. Lomov's aunt's grandmother
  • B. The local Russian government
  • C. Lomov's father
  • D. A wealthy noble merchant

Answer: A. Lomov's aunt's grandmother

Explanation: Lomov claimed his aunt's grandmother gave the temporary use of the Meadows to Chubukov's peasants in return for making bricks.

14. For how many years had Natalya's family allegedly been using the Oxen Meadows?

  • A. Nearly 300 years
  • B. 40 years
  • C. 100 years
  • D. 50 years

Answer: A. Nearly 300 years

Explanation: Natalya claimed her family had owned the land for nearly 300 years and was shocked by Lomov's assertion.

15. What is the financial worth of Oxen Meadows mentioned by Lomov?

  • A. Around 300 roubles
  • B. Around 500 roubles
  • C. Around 100 roubles
  • D. Around 1000 roubles

Answer: A. Around 300 roubles

Explanation: Lomov says the meadows are worth very little—about 300 roubles—and only about 5 dessiatins, but it's the principle that matters.

16. Whose side did Chubukov take during the Oxen Meadows argument?

  • A. His daughter Natalya's side
  • B. Lomov's side
  • C. He remained completely neutral
  • D. He asked a judge to settle it

Answer: A. His daughter Natalya's side

Explanation: Chubukov joined in and aggressively supported Natalya, claiming the meadows belonged to them.

17. How did the first argument end?

  • A. Lomov suffered palpitations and was insulted and driven out of the house
  • B. They signed a land agreement paper
  • C. Natalya agreed to give up the land
  • D. Chubukov called the police

Answer: A. Lomov suffered palpitations and was insulted and driven out of the house

Explanation: Overwhelmed by health attacks and insults from Chubukov and Natalya, Lomov rushed out of the house in a rage.

18. What did Chubukov reveal to Natalya after Lomov left the house during the first fight?

  • A. That Lomov had come to propose marriage to her
  • B. That Lomov was planning to sue them
  • C. That Lomov was bankrupt
  • D. That Lomov was selling his estate

Answer: A. That Lomov had come to propose marriage to her

Explanation: Chubukov blurted out that the "fool" had come to propose marriage to her, which shocked Natalya.

19. How did Natalya react when she learned Lomov had come to propose to her?

  • A. She panicked, wept hysterically, and demanded her father bring him back immediately
  • B. She laughed and refused the proposal
  • C. She remained indifferent
  • D. She scolded her father for inviting him

Answer: A. She panicked, wept hysterically, and demanded her father bring him back immediately

Explanation: She cried "Bring him back! Bring him back! Ah! Bring him back!" and blamed her father for driving him away.

20. What was the name of Lomov's dog?

  • A. Guess
  • B. Squeezer
  • C. Ugai
  • D. Tracker

Answer: A. Guess

Explanation: Lomov's pet hunting dog was named Guess.

21. What was the name of Natalya's dog?

  • A. Squeezer
  • B. Guess
  • C. Volchanetsky
  • D. Countess

Answer: A. Squeezer

Explanation: Natalya's family hunting dog was named Squeezer.

22. How much did Lomov pay for his dog, Guess?

  • A. 125 roubles
  • B. 85 roubles
  • C. 200 roubles
  • D. 50 roubles

Answer: A. 125 roubles

Explanation: Lomov paid 125 roubles to Mironov for Guess, which he considered cheap for such a good dog.

23. How much did Chubukov buy Squeezer for?

  • A. 85 roubles
  • B. 125 roubles
  • C. 100 roubles
  • D. 150 roubles

Answer: A. 85 roubles

Explanation: Natalya mentions that her father gave 85 roubles for Squeezer.

24. What defect did Lomov point out in Natalya's dog, Squeezer?

  • A. He is undershot (his lower jaw is shorter than upper jaw)
  • B. He is blind in one eye
  • C. He is too old and slow
  • D. He has a limp leg

Answer: A. He is undershot (his lower jaw is shorter than upper jaw)

Explanation: Lomov repeatedly argues that Squeezer is undershot, which means he is a poor grasper in hunting.

25. What defect did Natalya point out regarding Lomov's dog, Guess?

  • A. He is old and short in the muzzle
  • B. He cannot run at all
  • C. He bites people
  • D. He is deaf

Answer: A. He is old and short in the muzzle

Explanation: Natalya calls Guess old, ugly, worn out, and short in the muzzle.

26. What happens to Lomov during the heated second argument over their dogs?

  • A. He collapses onto an armchair and faints/loses consciousness
  • B. He attacks Chubukov physically
  • C. He breaks furniture in anger
  • D. He runs out to bring his dog to prove his point

Answer: A. He collapses onto an armchair and faints/loses consciousness

Explanation: Due to extreme stress and excitement, Lomov collapses and falls unconscious into an armchair.

27. What feared thought crosses Chubukov's mind when Lomov appears dead/fainted?

  • A. He feels so miserable that he calls for a doctor and threatens to cut his own throat
  • B. He fears going to prison
  • C. He regrets selling his land
  • D. He worries about hiding the body

Answer: A. He feels so miserable that he calls for a doctor and threatens to cut his own throat

Explanation: Chubukov dramatically laments his fate, calling for a doctor, a pistol, or a knife to end his own life.

28. How does Chubukov finalize the engagement as soon as Lomov regains consciousness?

  • A. He puts Natalya's hand into Lomov's hand and urges them to kiss and be done with it
  • B. He organizes a grand feast immediately
  • C. He writes a formal marriage contract
  • D. He takes them to church right away

Answer: A. He puts Natalya's hand into Lomov's hand and urges them to kiss and be done with it

Explanation: As soon as Lomov revives, Chubukov hastily joins their hands, gives his blessing, and asks them to kiss.

29. What happens immediately after Lomov and Natalya get engaged?

  • A. They immediately resume arguing about their dogs
  • B. They embrace lovingly and stop quarreling
  • C. Lomov leaves for his home happily
  • D. They sit down to have dinner together peacefully

Answer: A. They immediately resume arguing about their dogs

Explanation: Even while accepting the proposal and kissing, Natalya insists Squeezer is better, restarting the petty quarrel.

30. What central message/theme does Chekhov convey through 'The Proposal'?

  • A. Satire on how wealthy families prioritize material interests and petty pride over genuine love in marriage
  • B. The importance of land boundary documentation
  • C. The superiority of noble Russian hunting dogs
  • D. How to cure heart ailments through arguments

Answer: A. Satire on how wealthy families prioritize material interests and petty pride over genuine love in marriage

Explanation: The farce highlights how landed gentry enter marriages of economic convenience while constantly arguing over trivial, absurd pride.
