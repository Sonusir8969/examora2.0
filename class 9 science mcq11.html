Ch-11: Work and Energy 10 Questions

1. Work is said to be done on an object when:

  • A. A force acts on it without moving it
  • B. A force acts on it and causes displacement in the direction of force
  • C. The object moves without any applied force
  • D. Force and displacement are perpendicular to each other

Answer: B. A force acts on it and causes displacement in the direction of force

Explanation: Work (W = F × s) requires both an applied force and a non-zero displacement in the direction of the force.

2. What is the SI unit of Work and Energy?

  • A. Watt
  • B. Newton
  • C. Joule
  • D. Pascal

Answer: C. Joule

Explanation: Both work and energy are measured in Joules (J). 1 Joule = 1 Newton × 1 meter.

3. When the force applied on a body is perpendicular to its direction of motion, the work done is:

  • A. Positive
  • B. Negative
  • C. Maximum
  • D. Zero

Answer: D. Zero

Explanation: W = F × s × cos(θ). When angle θ = 90°, cos(90°) = 0, so work done is zero (e.g., a coolie carrying a load walking horizontally).

4. Kinetic Energy of an object of mass m moving with velocity v is given by the formula:

  • A. mgh
  • B. ½ mv²
  • C. mv²
  • D. ½ m²v

Answer: B. ½ mv²

Explanation: Kinetic energy possesses by a moving body is proportional to its mass and the square of its velocity (E_k = ½ mv²).

5. Gravitational potential energy of an object raised to a height h is equal to:

  • A. mgh
  • B. ½ mgh
  • C. mg / h
  • D. m / gh

Answer: A. mgh

Explanation: Work done against gravity to raise an object to height h is W = F × h = (mg) × h = mgh, stored as potential energy.

6. According to the Law of Conservation of Energy, energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it can only be:

  • A. Increased
  • B. Transformed from one form to another
  • C. Completely eliminated
  • D. Decreased

Answer: B. Transformed from one form to another

Explanation: The total mechanical energy (Potential Energy + Kinetic Energy) remains constant during energy transformations in an isolated system.

7. What is Power defined as?

  • A. Force per unit area
  • B. Rate of doing work (or rate of energy consumption)
  • C. Product of force and velocity
  • D. Energy multiplied by time

Answer: B. Rate of doing work (or rate of energy consumption)

Explanation: Power = Work / Time (P = W / t). Its SI unit is Watt (W), where 1 Watt = 1 Joule/second.

8. What is the commercial unit of electrical energy?

  • A. Joule (J)
  • B. Watt-hour (Wh)
  • C. Kilowatt-hour (kWh)
  • D. Megawatt (MW)

Answer: C. Kilowatt-hour (kWh)

Explanation: 1 unit of electricity equals 1 kWh. It represents energy consumed by a 1 kW appliance running for 1 hour.

9. How many Joules are there in 1 Kilowatt-hour (1 kWh)?

  • A. 3.6 × 10⁵ J
  • B. 3.6 × 10⁶ J
  • C. 36 × 10⁶ J
  • D. 1000 J

Answer: B. 3.6 × 10⁶ J

Explanation: 1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3,600,000 Joules = 3.6 × 10⁶ J.

10. An electric bulb of 60 W is used for 5 hours per day. Calculate the energy consumed in one day in units (kWh).

  • A. 0.3 units
  • B. 3 units
  • C. 0.03 units
  • D. 300 units

Answer: A. 0.3 units

Explanation: Power = 60 W = 0.06 kW. Time = 5 h. Energy = Power × Time = 0.06 kW × 5 h = 0.3 kWh (0.3 units).
