Ch-1: Chemical Reactions and Equations 30 Questions

1. Burning of a magnesium ribbon in air is an example of which type of chemical reaction?

  • A. Combination reaction
  • B. Decomposition reaction
  • C. Displacement reaction
  • D. Double displacement reaction

Answer: A. Combination reaction

Explanation: Magnesium combines with oxygen to form magnesium oxide (2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO).

2. Which gas is evolved when dilute hydrochloric acid is added to reactive metals like zinc?

  • A. Oxygen gas
  • B. Nitrogen gas
  • C. Hydrogen gas
  • D. Carbon dioxide gas

Answer: C. Hydrogen gas

Explanation: Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂↑. Hydrogen gas burns with a pop sound.

3. What is the chemical formula of rust formed on iron?

  • A. FeO
  • B. Fe₃O₄
  • C. Fe₂O₃ · xH₂O
  • D. Fe(OH)₃

Answer: C. Fe₂O₃ · xH₂O

Explanation: Rust is hydrated ferric oxide formed by exposure of iron to air and moisture.

4. The reaction in which a single reactant breaks down into two or more simpler products is called:

  • A. Combination reaction
  • B. Decomposition reaction
  • C. Displacement reaction
  • D. Neutralization reaction

Answer: B. Decomposition reaction

Explanation: Breaking down of one compound into simpler substances is a decomposition reaction.

5. Which of the following is an endothermic process?

  • A. Respiration
  • B. Decomposition of calcium carbonate
  • C. Slaking of lime
  • D. Combustion of methane

Answer: B. Decomposition of calcium carbonate

Explanation: Decomposition reactions absorb heat, making thermal decomposition endothermic.

6. A substance that undergoes gain of oxygen or loss of hydrogen during a reaction is said to be:

  • A. Reduced
  • B. Oxidized
  • C. Decomposed
  • D. Neutralized

Answer: B. Oxidized

Explanation: Oxidation involves addition of oxygen or removal of hydrogen.

7. What is observed when lead nitrate powder is heated in a test tube?

  • A. Yellow gas evolved
  • B. Brown fumes of NO₂ evolved
  • C. White precipitate formed
  • D. Black residue left

Answer: B. Brown fumes of NO₂ evolved

Explanation: Heating lead nitrate produces brown fumes of nitrogen dioxide gas (NO₂).

8. Chemically, quick lime is known as:

  • A. Calcium carbonate (CaCO₃)
  • B. Calcium hydroxide (Ca(OH)₂)
  • C. Calcium oxide (CaO)
  • D. Calcium sulfate (CaSO₄)

Answer: C. Calcium oxide (CaO)

Explanation: Quick lime is calcium oxide (CaO).

9. When calcium oxide reacts vigorously with water, it produces slaked lime along with heat. This reaction is:

  • A. Endothermic
  • B. Exothermic
  • C. Displacement
  • D. Photochemical

Answer: B. Exothermic

Explanation: Exothermic reactions release heat into the surroundings.

10. The process of respiration in human beings is an example of an:

  • A. Exothermic reaction
  • B. Endothermic reaction
  • C. Reduction reaction
  • D. Displacement reaction

Answer: A. Exothermic reaction

Explanation: Glucose reacts with oxygen to release energy, making respiration exothermic.

11. Electrolysis of water is a decomposition reaction. The mole ratio of hydrogen and oxygen gases liberated is:

  • A. 1 : 1
  • B. 2 : 1
  • C. 1 : 2
  • D. 4 : 1

Answer: B. 2 : 1

Explanation: Water decomposes into 2H₂ + O₂, so the volume ratio of H₂ to O₂ is 2 : 1.

12. Silver chloride turns grey when exposed to sunlight due to:

  • A. Thermal decomposition
  • B. Photolytic decomposition
  • C. Oxidation
  • D. Displacement

Answer: B. Photolytic decomposition

Explanation: 2AgCl → 2Ag + Cl₂ in the presence of sunlight (photolytic decomposition).

13. Which chemical reaction is utilized in black and white photography?

  • A. Decomposition of AgBr
  • B. Combination of CaO and H₂O
  • C. Displacement of Cu by Fe
  • D. Oxidation of Fe

Answer: A. Decomposition of AgBr

Explanation: Silver bromide (AgBr) decomposes in light and is used in black and white photography.

14. When iron nails are dipped in blue copper sulfate solution, the solution turns light green due to formation of:

  • A. Fe₂O₃
  • B. FeSO₄
  • C. Cu(NO₃)₂
  • D. FeCl₂

Answer: B. FeSO₄

Explanation: Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu. Iron displaces copper forming green iron sulfate.

15. The reaction Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe is an example of a:

  • A. Combination reaction
  • B. Double displacement reaction
  • C. Decomposition reaction
  • D. Displacement reaction

Answer: D. Displacement reaction

Explanation: Aluminium is more reactive than iron and displaces iron from its oxide.

16. The chemical equation must be balanced to satisfy which fundamental law?

  • A. Law of Definite Proportions
  • B. Law of Conservation of Mass
  • C. Law of Multiple Proportions
  • D. Avogadro's Law

Answer: B. Law of Conservation of Mass

Explanation: Mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.

17. A reaction in which exchange of ions takes place between reactants is called:

  • A. Displacement reaction
  • B. Double displacement reaction
  • C. Combination reaction
  • D. Oxidation reaction

Answer: B. Double displacement reaction

Explanation: Reactions in which there is an exchange of ions between reactants are double displacement reactions.

18. What is formed when sodium sulfate solution reacts with barium chloride solution?

  • A. White precipitate of BaSO₄
  • B. Yellow precipitate of BaSO₄
  • C. Red precipitate of NaCl
  • D. No precipitate

Answer: A. White precipitate of BaSO₄

Explanation: Na₂SO₄ + BaCl₂ → BaSO₄↓ (white ppt) + 2NaCl.

19. In the reaction CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O, which substance acts as the reducing agent?

  • A. CuO
  • B. H₂
  • C. Cu
  • D. H₂O

Answer: B. H₂

Explanation: H₂ gains oxygen to become H₂O, so H₂ is oxidized and acts as the reducing agent.

20. The shiny brown element 'X' on heating in air turns black and forms compound 'Y'. Name 'X' and 'Y'.

  • A. X = Fe, Y = Fe₂O₃
  • B. X = Cu, Y = CuO
  • C. X = Mg, Y = MgO
  • D. X = Ag, Y = Ag₂O

Answer: B. X = Cu, Y = CuO

Explanation: Copper (Cu) is a shiny brown metal that oxidizes in air to form black copper oxide (CuO).

21. Prevention of oxidation of fat and oil-containing food items is achieved by adding:

  • A. Oxidizing agents
  • B. Antioxidants
  • C. Catalysts
  • D. Acids

Answer: B. Antioxidants

Explanation: Antioxidants slow down or prevent oxidation, preventing rancidity.

22. Which gas is flushed into bags of potato chips to prevent rancidity?

  • A. Oxygen
  • B. Hydrogen
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Carbon dioxide

Answer: C. Nitrogen

Explanation: Nitrogen is an unreactive inert gas that prevents oxygen from reacting with oils and fats.

23. The green coating formed on copper vessels when exposed to moist air is due to formation of:

  • A. Copper carbonate
  • B. Copper oxide
  • C. Copper sulfate
  • D. Copper hydroxide

Answer: A. Copper carbonate

Explanation: Corrosion of copper forms a green layer of basic copper carbonate [CuCO₃·Cu(OH)₂].

24. Black coating on silver objects on exposure to air is due to the formation of:

  • A. Silver nitrate
  • B. Silver sulfide
  • C. Silver oxide
  • D. Silver carbonate

Answer: B. Silver sulfide

Explanation: Silver reacts with H₂S in the air to form a black coating of silver sulfide (Ag₂S).

25. What type of reaction occurs when dilute hydrochloric acid is added to marble chips (calcium carbonate)?

  • A. Evolution of CO₂ gas
  • B. Evolution of H₂ gas
  • C. Formation of green precipitate
  • D. No reaction

Answer: A. Evolution of CO₂ gas

Explanation: CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂↑.

26. Redux reactions involve simultaneous occurrence of:

  • A. Oxidation and Neutralization
  • B. Oxidation and Reduction
  • C. Combination and Decomposition
  • D. Corrosion and Rancidity

Answer: B. Oxidation and Reduction

Explanation: Reactions where oxidation and reduction occur simultaneously are redox reactions.

27. Which condition is necessary for photo-decomposition reactions?

  • A. Electricity
  • B. Heat
  • C. Sunlight
  • D. Catalyst

Answer: C. Sunlight

Explanation: Photo-decomposition reactions take place in the presence of light/sunlight energy.

28. Which of the following is a physical change and NOT a chemical change?

  • A. Ripening of fruits
  • B. Souring of milk
  • C. Melting of candle wax
  • D. Digestion of food

Answer: C. Melting of candle wax

Explanation: Melting is a reversible physical change in state; no new substance is formed.

29. What is the coefficient of O₂ in the balanced chemical equation: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO?

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 3
  • D. 4

Answer: A. 1

Explanation: In 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO, the stoichiometric coefficient of O₂ molecule is 1.

30. Any reaction that produces an insoluble solid in water is known as a:

  • A. Precipitation reaction
  • B. Neutralization reaction
  • C. Oxidation reaction
  • D. Thermal reaction

Answer: A. Precipitation reaction

Explanation: An insoluble solid product formed during a chemical reaction is called a precipitate.
