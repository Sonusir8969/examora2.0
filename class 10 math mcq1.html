Ch-1: Real Numbers 30 Questions

1. The exponent of 2 in the prime factorisation of 144 is:

  • A. 2
  • B. 4
  • C. 1
  • D. 6

Answer: B. 4

Explanation: Prime factorisation of 144 = 2⁴ × 3². The exponent of 2 is 4.

2. The HCF of two numbers a and b is 5 and their LCM is 200. What is the product of a and b?

  • A. 205
  • B. 1000
  • C. 40
  • D. 100

Answer: B. 1000

Explanation: Product of two numbers = HCF × LCM = 5 × 200 = 1000.

3. The total number of prime factors of a prime number is:

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 0
  • D. Infinite

Answer: A. 1

Explanation: A prime number has only itself as its prime factor (it has 2 factors overall: 1 and itself, but only 1 prime factor).

4. Which of the following is an irrational number?

  • A. √4
  • B. √9
  • C. √7
  • D. 22/7

Answer: C. √7

Explanation: Square root of a non-perfect square integer is always an irrational number.

5. The LCM of two co-prime numbers x and y is always equal to:

  • A. 1
  • B. x + y
  • C. x / y
  • D. x × y

Answer: D. x × y

Explanation: For co-prime numbers, HCF = 1. Since HCF × LCM = x × y, LCM = x × y.

6. If two positive integers p and q can be expressed as p = a²b³ and q = a³b, where a and b are prime numbers, then HCF(p, q) is:

  • A. ab
  • B. a²b
  • C. a³b³
  • D. a²b²

Answer: B. a²b

Explanation: HCF takes the lowest power of common prime factors: min(a², a³) = a², min(b³, b¹) = b. So HCF = a²b.

7. Referring to Question 6, what is LCM(p, q)?

  • A. a²b
  • B. a³b³
  • C. a³b
  • D. a²b³

Answer: B. a³b³

Explanation: LCM takes the highest power of each prime factor: max(a², a³) = a³, max(b³, b) = b³. Thus, LCM = a³b³.

8. The smallest composite number and the smallest prime number have an HCF of:

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 4
  • D. 0

Answer: B. 2

Explanation: Smallest composite number = 4, smallest prime number = 2. HCF(4, 2) = 2.

9. What is the LCM of the smallest prime number and the smallest composite number?

  • A. 2
  • B. 4
  • C. 8
  • D. 6

Answer: B. 4

Explanation: Smallest prime = 2, Smallest composite = 4. LCM(2, 4) = 4.

10. The fundamental theorem of arithmetic states that every composite number can be uniquely factorized as a product of:

  • A. Odd numbers
  • B. Even numbers
  • C. Prime numbers
  • D. Rational numbers

Answer: C. Prime numbers

Explanation: Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic asserts that every composite number can be factorized uniquely into prime factors, apart from the order.

11. If HCF(306, 657) = 9, then LCM(306, 657) is equal to:

  • A. 22338
  • B. 22383
  • C. 22833
  • D. 306 × 657

Answer: A. 22338

Explanation: LCM = (a × b) / HCF = (306 × 657) / 9 = 22338.

12. The product of a non-zero rational number and an irrational number is always:

  • A. Always rational
  • B. Always irrational
  • C. Rational or irrational
  • D. Zero

Answer: B. Always irrational

Explanation: Product of any non-zero rational number with an irrational number always yields an irrational result.

13. The sum or difference of a rational number and an irrational number is:

  • A. An integer
  • B. A rational number
  • C. An irrational number
  • D. A whole number

Answer: C. An irrational number

Explanation: Adding or subtracting a rational number to an irrational number always gives an irrational number.

14. If p is a prime number and p divides a² (where a is a positive integer), then p also divides:

  • A. a/2
  • B. a
  • C. 2a
  • D. a³

Answer: B. a

Explanation: By NCERT Theorem 1.2, if a prime number p divides a², then p divides a.

15. What is the value of x in the factor tree: 2 × (3 × x) = 42?

  • A. 5
  • B. 7
  • C. 9
  • D. 14

Answer: B. 7

Explanation: 6x = 42 ⇒ x = 42 / 6 = 7.

16. Express 140 as a product of its prime factors:

  • A. 2² × 5 × 7
  • B. 2 × 5² × 7
  • C. 2³ × 5
  • D. 2 × 3 × 5 × 7

Answer: A. 2² × 5 × 7

Explanation: 140 = 2 × 70 = 2 × 2 × 35 = 2² × 5 × 7.

17. Express 156 as a product of prime factors:

  • A. 2² × 3 × 13
  • B. 2 × 3² × 13
  • C. 2² × 3² × 13
  • D. 2 × 3 × 17

Answer: A. 2² × 3 × 13

Explanation: 156 = 2 × 78 = 2 × 2 × 39 = 2² × 3 × 13.

18. Three bells toll together at intervals of 9, 12, and 15 minutes respectively. If they toll together now, after how much time will they toll together next?

  • A. 60 minutes
  • B. 90 minutes
  • C. 180 minutes
  • D. 360 minutes

Answer: C. 180 minutes

Explanation: Required time = LCM(9, 12, 15) = 180 minutes (or 3 hours).

19. If n is a natural number, then 6ⁿ always ends with the digit:

  • A. 0
  • B. 2
  • C. 6
  • D. 4

Answer: C. 6

Explanation: 6¹ = 6, 6² = 36, 6³ = 216... For any positive integer n, 6ⁿ always ends with digit 6.

20. Can the number 4ⁿ, where n is a natural number, end with the digit zero?

  • A. Yes, for n = 0
  • B. Yes, for all even n
  • C. No, because prime factorisation of 4ⁿ does not contain 5
  • D. Yes, for n = 5

Answer: C. No, because prime factorisation of 4ⁿ does not contain 5

Explanation: To end with digit 0, a number must have both 2 and 5 as prime factors. Prime factorization 4ⁿ = (2²ⁿ) contains only 2.

21. The set of real numbers consists of:

  • A. Only rational numbers
  • B. Only irrational numbers
  • C. Both rational and irrational numbers
  • D. Only integers

Answer: C. Both rational and irrational numbers

Explanation: Real numbers comprise the complete collection of all rational and irrational numbers.

22. What is the greatest number that divides 70 and 125, leaving remainders 5 and 8 respectively?

  • A. 13
  • B. 65
  • C. 875
  • D. 1750

Answer: A. 13

Explanation: Required number = HCF(70 - 5, 125 - 8) = HCF(65, 117) = 13.

23. Which of the following is NOT a prime factor of 3825?

  • A. 3
  • B. 5
  • C. 17
  • D. 7

Answer: D. 7

Explanation: 3825 = 3² × 5² × 17. The prime factors are 3, 5, and 17. 7 is not a prime factor.

24. Given that HCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = 1200 and a = 30, what is the value of b?

  • A. 30
  • B. 40
  • C. 60
  • D. 120

Answer: B. 40

Explanation: a × b = HCF × LCM ⇒ 30 × b = 1200 ⇒ b = 1200 / 30 = 40.

25. If (3 + √5) is a root, then (3 - √5) is also a root and their sum is:

  • A. Irrational
  • B. Rational (6)
  • C. Zero
  • D. 2√5

Answer: B. Rational (6)

Explanation: Sum = (3 + √5) + (3 - √5) = 6, which is a rational number.

26. The product of (2 + √3) and (2 - √3) is:

  • A. An irrational number
  • B. A rational number equal to 1
  • C. A rational number equal to 7
  • D. 4

Answer: B. A rational number equal to 1

Explanation: Using (a+b)(a-b) = a² - b² ⇒ 2² - (√3)² = 4 - 3 = 1 (rational).

27. How many prime factors does the number 5005 have?

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 5

Answer: C. 4

Explanation: 5005 = 5 × 7 × 11 × 13. It has 4 distinct prime factors.

28. HCF of any two consecutive even natural numbers is always:

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 4
  • D. 0

Answer: B. 2

Explanation: Two consecutive even numbers (e.g., 2 and 4, or 10 and 12) always have a common factor of 2 as their highest common factor.

29. HCF of any two consecutive natural numbers is always:

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 0
  • D. Depends on the numbers

Answer: A. 1

Explanation: Consecutive integers are always co-prime, so their HCF is 1.

30. Is π (Pi) a rational or an irrational number?

  • A. Rational number
  • B. Irrational number
  • C. Integer
  • D. Natural number

Answer: B. Irrational number

Explanation: π is an irrational number because its decimal representation is non-terminating and non-repeating (22/7 is only an approximation).
