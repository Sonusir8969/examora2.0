Ch-10: Conic Sections 30 Questions

1. The equation of a circle centered at origin (0, 0) with radius r is:

  • A. x² + y² = r²
  • B. x² - y² = r²
  • C. (x - h)² + (y - k)² = r²
  • D. x + y = r

Answer: A. x² + y² = r²

Explanation: Standard equation of a circle centered at origin is x² + y² = r².

2. The center of the circle (x - 3)² + (y + 4)² = 25 is:

  • A. (3, -4)
  • B. (-3, 4)
  • C. (3, 4)
  • D. (-3, -4)

Answer: A. (3, -4)

Explanation: Comparing with (x - h)² + (y - k)² = r², h = 3 and k = -4.

3. What is the radius of the circle x² + y² - 4x - 8y - 5 = 0?

  • A. 5
  • B. 25
  • C. √20
  • D. 4

Answer: A. 5

Explanation: g = -2, f = -4, c = -5. Radius r = √(g² + f² - c) = √(4 + 16 - (-5)) = √25 = 5.

4. Focus of parabola y² = 4ax is located at:

  • A. (a, 0)
  • B. (-a, 0)
  • C. (0, a)
  • D. (0, -a)

Answer: A. (a, 0)

Explanation: Standard parabola y² = 4ax opens to the right with focus at (a, 0).

5. Directrix of parabola y² = -4ax is given by line:

  • A. x = a
  • B. x = -a
  • C. y = a
  • D. y = -a

Answer: A. x = a

Explanation: Since focus is (-a, 0), the directrix is on opposite side: x = a.

6. Length of latus rectum of parabola x² = 4ay is:

  • A. 4a
  • B. 2a
  • C. a
  • D. a/2

Answer: A. 4a

Explanation: Length of latus rectum for standard parabola is always 4a.

7. Find focus of parabola y² = 12x:

  • A. (3, 0)
  • B. (0, 3)
  • C. (-3, 0)
  • D. (6, 0)

Answer: A. (3, 0)

Explanation: 4a = 12 ⇒ a = 3. Focus is (a, 0) = (3, 0).

8. The eccentricity 'e' of a circle is equal to:

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. < 1
  • D. > 1

Answer: A. 0

Explanation: A circle is a special case of an ellipse with eccentricity e = 0.

9. The eccentricity 'e' of a parabola is equal to:

  • A. 1
  • B. 0
  • C. < 1
  • D. > 1

Answer: A. 1

Explanation: Parabola has eccentricity e = 1 by definition (distance to focus equals distance to directrix).

10. The eccentricity 'e' of an ellipse satisfies:

  • A. 0 < e < 1
  • B. e = 1
  • C. e > 1
  • D. e = 0

Answer: A. 0 < e < 1

Explanation: For an ellipse, eccentricity is always strictly between 0 and 1.

11. The eccentricity 'e' of a hyperbola satisfies:

  • A. e > 1
  • B. e = 1
  • C. 0 < e < 1
  • D. e = 0

Answer: A. e > 1

Explanation: For any hyperbola, eccentricity e is strictly greater than 1.

12. Length of major axis of ellipse x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 (where a > b) is:

  • A. 2a
  • B. 2b
  • C. a
  • D. b

Answer: A. 2a

Explanation: When a > b, major axis lies on x-axis and has total length 2a.

13. Length of minor axis of ellipse x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 (where a > b) is:

  • A. 2b
  • B. 2a
  • C. b
  • D. a

Answer: A. 2b

Explanation: Minor axis lies along y-axis with total length 2b.

14. Relation between a, b, and c in an ellipse x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 (a > b) is:

  • A. c² = a² - b²
  • B. c² = a² + b²
  • C. a² = b² - c²
  • D. b² = a² + c²

Answer: A. c² = a² - b²

Explanation: In an ellipse, semi-focal length c satisfies c² = a² - b².

15. Relation between a, b, and c in a hyperbola x²/a² - y²/b² = 1 is:

  • A. c² = a² + b²
  • B. c² = a² - b²
  • C. a² = b² + c²
  • D. b² = a² - c²

Answer: A. c² = a² + b²

Explanation: In hyperbola, focal length is greater than transverse axis semi-length, c² = a² + b².

16. Length of latus rectum of ellipse x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 (a > b) is:

  • A. 2b²/a
  • B. 2a²/b
  • C. b²/a
  • D. a²/b

Answer: A. 2b²/a

Explanation: Standard formula for latus rectum of ellipse (a > b) is 2b²/a.

17. Foci of hyperbola x²/a² - y²/b² = 1 are given by:

  • A. (±c, 0) where c = √(a² + b²)
  • B. (0, ±c) where c = √(a² + b²)
  • C. (±a, 0)
  • D. (0, ±b)

Answer: A. (±c, 0) where c = √(a² + b²)

Explanation: Standard horizontal hyperbola has foci along x-axis at (±c, 0).

18. Foci of ellipse x²/25 + y²/9 = 1 are:

  • A. (±4, 0)
  • B. (0, ±4)
  • C. (±3, 0)
  • D. (±5, 0)

Answer: A. (±4, 0)

Explanation: a² = 25, b² = 9 ⇒ c = √(25 - 9) = √16 = 4. Foci = (±4, 0).

19. Eccentricity of hyperbola x²/16 - y²/9 = 1 is:

  • A. 5/4
  • B. 4/5
  • C. 3/4
  • D. 5/3

Answer: A. 5/4

Explanation: a² = 16, b² = 9 ⇒ c = √(16 + 9) = 5. Eccentricity e = c/a = 5/4.

20. Directrix of parabola x² = -8y is:

  • A. y = 2
  • B. y = -2
  • C. x = 2
  • D. x = -2

Answer: A. y = 2

Explanation: 4a = 8 ⇒ a = 2. Parabola opens downwards, so directrix line is y = a ⇒ y = 2.

21. Length of transverse axis of hyperbola x²/9 - y²/16 = 1 is:

  • A. 6
  • B. 8
  • C. 3
  • D. 4

Answer: A. 6

Explanation: a² = 9 ⇒ a = 3. Transverse axis length = 2a = 2(3) = 6.

22. Length of conjugate axis of hyperbola x²/9 - y²/16 = 1 is:

  • A. 8
  • B. 6
  • C. 4
  • D. 10

Answer: A. 8

Explanation: b² = 16 ⇒ b = 4. Conjugate axis length = 2b = 2(4) = 8.

23. Equation of circle with center (0,0) passing through point (3, 4) is:

  • A. x² + y² = 25
  • B. x² + y² = 7
  • C. x² + y² = 12
  • D. x² + y² = 5

Answer: A. x² + y² = 25

Explanation: Radius r = √(3² + 4²) = 5. Equation: x² + y² = r² = 25.

24. A hyperbola in which a = b is called a:

  • A. Rectangular hyperbola
  • B. Elliptic hyperbola
  • C. Conjugate hyperbola
  • D. Parabolic hyperbola

Answer: A. Rectangular hyperbola

Explanation: When transverse axis equals conjugate axis (a = b), hyperbola is called rectangular hyperbola.

25. Eccentricity of a rectangular hyperbola is equal to:

  • A. √2
  • B. 2
  • C. 1
  • D. 1/√2

Answer: A. √2

Explanation: For rectangular hyperbola, a = b. e = √(1 + b²/a²) = √(1 + 1) = √2.

26. Vertices of ellipse x²/16 + y²/25 = 1 are:

  • A. (0, ±5)
  • B. (±4, 0)
  • C. (±5, 0)
  • D. (0, ±4)

Answer: A. (0, ±5)

Explanation: Here b² = 25 > a² = 16, so major axis is along y-axis. Vertices are (0, ±b) = (0, ±5).

27. Length of latus rectum of hyperbola x²/a² - y²/b² = 1 is:

  • A. 2b²/a
  • B. 2a²/b
  • C. b²/a
  • D. a²/b

Answer: A. 2b²/a

Explanation: Latus rectum formula for standard hyperbola x²/a² - y²/b² = 1 is 2b²/a.

28. Equation of parabola with vertex at (0,0) and focus at (0, -2) is:

  • A. x² = -8y
  • B. y² = -8x
  • C. x² = 8y
  • D. y² = 8x

Answer: A. x² = -8y

Explanation: Focus on negative y-axis means form x² = -4ay. Here a = 2, so x² = -8y.

29. Area of circle x² + y² = 16 is:

  • A. 16π
  • B. 4π
  • C. 8π
  • D. 32π

Answer: A. 16π

Explanation: r² = 16 ⇒ Area = πr² = 16π.

30. Position of point (2, 3) relative to circle x² + y² = 9 is:

  • A. Outside the circle
  • B. Inside the circle
  • C. On the circle
  • D. At center

Answer: A. Outside the circle

Explanation: Substitute (2, 3): 2² + 3² = 4 + 9 = 13 > 9. Since value > 9, point lies outside.
