NCERT Solutions for Class 9 English Chapter 2 The Sound of Music

NCERT Solutions for Class 9 English Beehive Chapter 2 The Sound of Music covers both parts of the chapter - Evelyn Glennie, the deaf percussionist, and Bismillah Khan, the legendary shehnai player - along with important questions and answers.

Overview of Topics & Subtopics (Click to Jump)

  • 1. Part I: Evelyn Glennie Listens to Sound Without Hearing It
  • 2. Part II: Bismillah Khan, the Shehnai Maestro
  • 3. Theme & Message

1. Part I: Evelyn Glennie Listens to Sound Without Hearing It

Short Note:

• Evelyn Glennie is a Scottish musician who began losing her hearing at the age of eight and was profoundly deaf by twelve, yet became a world-famous percussionist.

• She learned to 'hear' music through the vibrations of sound that she felt through different parts of her body, and she often performs barefoot to feel these vibrations better.

• With the help of her percussion teacher Ron Forbes, she trained herself to sense pitch and rhythm without relying on her ears, proving that determination can overcome physical limitations.

2. Part II: Bismillah Khan, the Shehnai Maestro

Short Note:

• Ustad Bismillah Khan was a legendary Indian musician who made the shehnai, once a simple folk instrument, a respected classical instrument.

• He learned to play the shehnai from his uncle Ali Bux at the Vishwanath Temple in Varanasi and remained deeply devoted to his art and his city all his life.

• He famously refused offers to settle abroad, saying he could never leave his beloved Banaras, the Ganga and the temple where he practised.

3. Theme & Message

Short Note:

• Both parts of the chapter show that true achievement in music comes from hard work, dedication and passion rather than perfect physical conditions.

• The chapter inspires readers to overcome obstacles, whether physical disability or humble beginnings, through consistent practice and love for one's art.

Reading With Insight (Textbook Questions)

Q1: How does Evelyn Glennie hear music?
Answer: Evelyn feels music through vibrations that travel through her body - she can sense different pitches through different parts of her body, such as her hands, arms, cheekbones and even her hair, and she plays barefoot on stage to feel these vibrations more clearly through the floor.
Q2: What is Bismillah Khan's contribution to the shehnai?
Answer: Bismillah Khan elevated the shehnai from being a simple instrument played at weddings and rituals to a respected classical instrument that could be performed on the concert stage, and his mastery brought it national and international recognition.
Q3: How is Bismillah Khan's devotion to Benaras and the river Ganga reflected in his life?
Answer: Bismillah Khan considered Benaras his spiritual home and the Ganga essential to his art; he believed his music flourished because of his connection to the temple, the river, and the culture of the city, and he turned down opportunities abroad to stay rooted there.

Extract-Based / Short Answer Questions

Q4: What obstacles did Evelyn Glennie face and how did she overcome them?
Answer: Evelyn faced the challenge of near-total deafness from a young age, which could have ended her musical dreams, but with her teacher's guidance and her own perseverance she trained her body to sense sound, eventually becoming one of the finest percussionists in the world.
Q5: Why can Bismillah Khan be called a symbol of India's cultural and religious harmony?
Answer: Though a devout Muslim, Bismillah Khan played the shehnai at the Vishwanath Temple every day and considered music above religious boundaries, showing that art can unite people of different faiths and traditions.
