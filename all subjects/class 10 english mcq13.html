Ch-1: A Triumph of Surgery 25 Questions

1. Who is the author of the story 'A Triumph of Surgery'?

  • A. James Herriot
  • B. Ruskin Bond
  • C. Robert Arthur
  • D. H.G. Wells

Answer: A. James Herriot

Explanation: James Herriot, a British veterinary surgeon and writer, wrote 'A Triumph of Surgery'.

2. Who was Tricki in the story?

  • A. A small dog
  • B. A veterinary doctor
  • C. A pet cat
  • D. A servant

Answer: A. A small dog

Explanation: Tricki was the pamper-spoiled pet dog belonging to Mrs. Pumphrey.

3. Who was Mrs. Pumphrey?

  • A. A wealthy and loving owner of Tricki
  • B. The veterinary surgeon's assistant
  • C. A dog trainer
  • D. James Herriot's housekeeper

Answer: A. A wealthy and loving owner of Tricki

Explanation: Mrs. Pumphrey was a rich lady who overfed her pet dog Tricki out of excessive love and concern.

4. What main flaw or problem did Tricki have?

  • A. Greed for food
  • B. Extreme laziness by birth
  • C. Aggressive behavior
  • D. Chronic skin disease

Answer: A. Greed for food

Explanation: Tricki's only fault was greed; he never refused food at any hour of the day or night.

5. How did Tricki look when Mr. Herriot saw him in the street?

  • A. Like a bloated sausage with a leg at each corner
  • B. Active and energetic
  • C. Thin and malnourished
  • D. Fierce and aggressive

Answer: A. Like a bloated sausage with a leg at each corner

Explanation: Tricki had become enormously fat, resembling a bloated sausage with bloodshot, rheumy eyes.

6. What extra snacks did Mrs. Pumphrey feed Tricki between meals, believing he suffered from malnutrition?

  • A. Malt, cod-liver oil, and a bowl of Horlicks
  • B. Green vegetables and boiled eggs
  • C. Dry dog biscuits and fresh milk only
  • D. Raw meat and bones

Answer: A. Malt, cod-liver oil, and a bowl of Horlicks

Explanation: Mrs. Pumphrey gave him malt, cod-liver oil, and Horlicks at night to build him up.

7. What was Tricki's favorite sweet treat that Mrs. Pumphrey couldn't refuse to give him?

  • A. Cream cakes and chocolates
  • B. Ice cream and fruit cakes
  • C. Sweet biscuits and pudding
  • D. Candies and pastries

Answer: A. Cream cakes and chocolates

Explanation: Tricki adored cream cakes and chocolates, and Mrs. Pumphrey could not bear to refuse them to him.

8. Who was Hodgson in the story?

  • A. The gardener
  • B. The driver
  • C. The cook
  • D. The vet assistant

Answer: A. The gardener

Explanation: Hodgson was Mrs. Pumphrey's gardener, who was down with lumbago and couldn't play ring-throw with Tricki.

9. What strict warning did Mr. Herriot give to Mrs. Pumphrey regarding Tricki's health?

  • A. To cut down his sweets and give him plenty of exercise
  • B. To give him antibiotics immediately
  • C. To hire a personal dog trainer
  • D. To keep him in a cold room

Answer: A. To cut down his sweets and give him plenty of exercise

Explanation: Herriot warned that unless she cut his food right down and exercised him, he would be really ill.

10. What happened to Tricki a few days after Herriot's warning?

  • A. He refused to eat anything and had bouts of vomiting
  • B. He ran away from home
  • C. He bit the gardener
  • D. He became extremely active

Answer: A. He refused to eat anything and had bouts of vomiting

Explanation: Tricki stopped eating even his favorite dishes, had continuous vomiting bouts, and lay panting on a rug.

11. For how long did Mr. Herriot decide to hospitalize Tricki?

  • A. A fortnight (14 days)
  • B. One month
  • C. One week
  • D. Two days

Answer: A. A fortnight (14 days)

Explanation: Mr. Herriot realized the only way to save Tricki was to remove him from the house and keep him under observation for a fortnight.

12. What was Mrs. Pumphrey's reaction when Herriot took Tricki away to the surgery?

  • A. She wailed and almost fainted with sorrow
  • B. She became furious with the doctor
  • C. She was happy that Tricki would get medical treatment
  • D. She hired a private ambulance

Answer: A. She wailed and almost fainted with sorrow

Explanation: Mrs. Pumphrey broke into a despairing wail as she parted with her beloved dog.

13. What kind of treatment did Dr. Herriot give Tricki during the first two days at the surgery?

  • A. Plenty of water and no food
  • B. High doses of medicine and injections
  • C. High-protein diet and milk
  • D. Surgical operation on his stomach

Answer: A. Plenty of water and no food

Explanation: For the first two days, Dr. Herriot gave Tricki no food at all, only plenty of fresh water.

14. On which day did Tricki start showing interest in his surroundings and whimper at the door?

  • A. On the third day
  • B. On the first day
  • C. On the tenth day
  • D. On the seventh day

Answer: A. On the third day

Explanation: On the third day, Tricki began to whimper when he heard the other dogs in the yard.

15. Who was Tristan in the story?

  • A. Herriot's veterinary assistant/partner
  • B. Mrs. Pumphrey's butler
  • C. The leader of the greyhounds
  • D. A neighbor

Answer: A. Herriot's veterinary assistant/partner

Explanation: Tristan was Dr. Herriot's colleague/assistant who helped feed and care for the dogs at the surgery.

16. What was the name of the greyhound at the surgery?

  • A. Joe
  • B. Jack
  • C. Rocky
  • D. Bruno

Answer: A. Joe

Explanation: Joe was the greyhound who inspected Tricki along with other pack dogs in the yard.

17. What did Mrs. Pumphrey start sending to the surgery when she heard Tricki was convalescing?

  • A. Fresh eggs, wine, and brandy
  • B. Meat bowls and bones
  • C. Cakes and chocolates
  • D. Dog toys and new cushions

Answer: A. Fresh eggs, wine, and brandy

Explanation: To enrich Tricki's blood, Mrs. Pumphrey sent two dozen fresh eggs daily, along with bottles of wine and brandy.

18. Who actually consumed the eggs, wine, and brandy sent for Tricki?

  • A. Dr. Herriot and his partners/staff
  • B. Tricki and the other hospital dogs
  • C. The hospital patients
  • D. The local shelter workers

Answer: A. Dr. Herriot and his partners/staff

Explanation: Dr. Herriot and his staff enjoyed the luxury items for breakfast, lunch, and dinner since Tricki didn't need them.

19. Why was Dr. Herriot tempted to keep Tricki as a permanent guest?

  • A. Because of the daily supply of eggs, wine, and brandy sent by Mrs. Pumphrey
  • B. Because he grew attached to Tricki
  • C. Because Tricki guarded his clinic well
  • D. Because Mrs. Pumphrey paid a huge daily fee

Answer: A. Because of the daily supply of eggs, wine, and brandy sent by Mrs. Pumphrey

Explanation: The luxurious food supplies made Dr. Herriot playfully wish to keep Tricki permanently.

20. How did Tricki transform physically during his 14-day stay at the surgery?

  • A. He became a lithe, hard-muscled animal
  • B. He became weak and skinny
  • C. He lost his eyesight
  • D. He remained bloated and fat

Answer: A. He became a lithe, hard-muscled animal

Explanation: Regular exercise and playing with other dogs transformed Tricki into a active, hard-muscled dog.

21. How did Tricki react when he saw Mrs. Pumphrey in her car upon her arrival?

  • A. He took a tremendous leap into her lap and licked her face
  • B. He barked at her and ran away
  • C. He ignored her and stayed with Dr. Herriot
  • D. He growled softly in fear

Answer: A. He took a tremendous leap into her lap and licked her face

Explanation: Recognized his mistress, Tricki bounded out of Herriot's arms into Mrs. Pumphrey's lap with joyful excitement.

22. What did Mrs. Pumphrey call Tricki's recovery at the end of the story?

  • A. "A Triumph of Surgery!"
  • B. "A Medical Miracle!"
  • C. "A Divine Blessing!"
  • D. "A Great Victory!"

Answer: A. "A Triumph of Surgery!"

Explanation: Overwhelmed with gratitude and joyful tears, Mrs. Pumphrey called it "A Triumph of Surgery!".

23. Was any actual surgical operation performed on Tricki?

  • A. No medical operation was performed
  • B. Yes, a minor stomach operation
  • C. Yes, a throat surgery
  • D. Yes, an eye surgery

Answer: A. No medical operation was performed

Explanation: Tricki was cured purely through controlled diet, hydration, and natural physical exercise without any medicine or surgery.

24. What ironical element is highlighted by the phrase 'A Triumph of Surgery'?

  • A. The dog was cured without any actual surgery or medicine
  • B. The surgery failed completely
  • C. The doctor charged no fees
  • D. The dog refused to return home

Answer: A. The dog was cured without any actual surgery or medicine

Explanation: The title is ironical because Mrs. Pumphrey thought a complex surgery saved him, whereas simple diet control cured him.

25. What key moral lesson does 'A Triumph of Surgery' teach us?

  • A. Excess of pampering and overindulgence can be harmful to health
  • B. Pets should always be given expensive food
  • C. Veterinary doctors always perform unnecessary surgeries
  • D. Dogs do not need exercise

Answer: A. Excess of pampering and overindulgence can be harmful to health

Explanation: The story illustrates how misplaced affection and overfeeding can harm pets, and how self-discipline and exercise restore health.
