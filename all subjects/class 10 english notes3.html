NCERT Solutions for Class 10 English Chapter 3 Two Stories about Flying

NCERT Solutions for Class 10 English First Flight Chapter 3 Two Stories about Flying covers both His First Flight by Liam O'Flaherty and The Black Aeroplane by Frederick Forsyth, along with important questions and answers.

Overview of Topics & Subtopics (Click to Jump)

  • 1. Part I: His First Flight
  • 2. Part II: The Black Aeroplane
  • 3. Theme & Message

1. Part I: His First Flight

Short Note:

• This story follows a young seagull who is afraid to fly, watching enviously as his brothers and sister confidently take to the air while he remains hesitant on the ledge.

• His parents eventually stop bringing him food to force him to overcome his fear, and out of sheer hunger and desperation, he finally leaps off the ledge.

• To his own surprise, he discovers that flying comes naturally to him once he overcomes his initial fear, and he soon joins his family soaring joyfully above the sea.

2. Part II: The Black Aeroplane

Short Note:

• This story is narrated by a pilot flying alone at night over France, who suddenly encounters a dangerous storm with poor visibility and no way to navigate safely through it.

• A mysterious black aeroplane appears beside him and guides him safely through the storm, though the pilot cannot make radio contact with it or its pilot.

• After landing, the narrator learns from air traffic control that no other aircraft was recorded in that area at the time, leaving the identity of the mysterious guiding pilot unexplained and adding an element of mystery to the story.

3. Theme & Message

Short Note:

• Both stories explore the theme of overcoming fear, whether the young seagull's fear of flying or the pilot's fear of a life-threatening storm.

• They also highlight the importance of courage, trust and the sometimes unexpected sources of help that guide us through difficult moments in life.

Reading With Insight (Textbook Questions)

Q1: Why was the young seagull afraid to fly at first?
Answer: The young seagull was afraid to fly because he doubted his own wings and courage, fearing that he would fall into the sea below rather than being able to soar like his siblings, despite watching them fly successfully.
Q2: How did the seagull's parents help him overcome his fear?
Answer: The seagull's parents stopped feeding him and instead flew around him with food just out of reach, and eventually his mother left a piece of fish floating on the sea below, forcing him through hunger to finally dive off the ledge and discover his natural ability to fly.
Q3: How did the black aeroplane help the narrator survive the storm?
Answer: The black aeroplane appeared suddenly beside the narrator's plane during a dangerous storm and led him safely along a clear path through the clouds until they reached safer skies, effectively guiding him away from the danger of crashing.

Extract-Based / Short Answer Questions

Q4: What is the significance of the seagull's first successful flight?
Answer: The seagull's first flight symbolises the triumph over fear through necessity and instinct, showing that we are often capable of far more than we believe, and that sometimes a push, even an uncomfortable one, is needed to realise our potential.
Q5: Why does the story of The Black Aeroplane end on a mysterious note?
Answer: The story ends mysteriously because air traffic control confirms no other plane was in the area during the storm, leaving the reader to wonder whether the mysterious black aeroplane and its pilot were real, a hallucination, or something more supernatural, adding intrigue to the narrative.
