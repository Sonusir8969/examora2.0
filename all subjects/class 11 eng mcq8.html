Eng-08: The Browning Version (Play) 25 Questions

1. Who is the author of the play 'The Browning Version'?

  • A. Terence Rattigan
  • B. Nick Middleton
  • C. A.R. Williams
  • D. Gordon Cook

Answer: A. Terence Rattigan

Explanation: 'The Browning Version' is a famous one-act play written by British playwright Terence Rattigan.

2. Who is Taplow in the play?

  • A. A 16-year-old school student
  • B. A young science teacher
  • C. The headmaster of the school
  • D. A senior Classics teacher

Answer: A. A 16-year-old school student

Explanation: Taplow is a 16-year-old student studying in the lower fifth form.

3. Who is Frank in 'The Browning Version'?

  • A. A young science master (teacher)
  • B. Taplow's classmate
  • C. The school principal
  • D. Mrs. Crocker-Harris's brother

Answer: A. A young science master (teacher)

Explanation: Frank is a young science teacher working at the same school as Mr. Crocker-Harris.

4. Why did Taplow come to school on the last day of term?

  • A. To do extra work for Mr. Crocker-Harris
  • B. To attend his final exams
  • C. To collect his prizes
  • D. To play cricket with friends

Answer: A. To do extra work for Mr. Crocker-Harris

Explanation: Taplow had missed one day during the term due to illness, so Mr. Crocker-Harris called him for extra work on the last day.

5. Which subject does Taplow wish to specialize in next year?

  • A. Science
  • B. Classics (Greek/Latin)
  • C. History
  • D. Mathematics

Answer: A. Science

Explanation: Taplow wants to opt for Science if he gets promoted to the remove (higher form).

6. Which book/play was Taplow translating for Mr. Crocker-Harris?

  • A. Agamemnon by Aeschylus
  • B. Hamlet by Shakespeare
  • C. The Odyssey by Homer
  • D. Julius Caesar

Answer: A. Agamemnon by Aeschylus

Explanation: Taplow was translating the Greek play 'Agamemnon', which he referred to as "muck" because of its dull teaching style.

7. Why did Taplow refer to 'Agamemnon' as "muck"?

  • A. Because of the way it was taught, focusing on hard Greek words and punishment
  • B. Because the plot was boring
  • C. Because the book was physically dirty
  • D. Because he hated reading books

Answer: A. Because of the way it was taught, focusing on hard Greek words and punishment

Explanation: Taplow clarified that the story was good, but teaching fifty Greek words for getting one wrong made it feel like muck.

8. What is unique about Mr. Crocker-Harris compared to other teachers?

  • A. He does not declare results before the official day
  • B. He gives extra marks to favorites
  • C. He beats students severely
  • D. He never takes extra work

Answer: A. He does not declare results before the official day

Explanation: Unlike other masters who tell results early, Mr. Crocker-Harris strictly follows rules and announces results only on the designated day.

9. What nickname do students use for Mr. Crocker-Harris?

  • A. The Crock
  • B. The Boss
  • C. The Professor
  • D. The Old Man

Answer: A. The Crock

Explanation: Taplow and other boys informally refer to Mr. Crocker-Harris as "the Crock".

10. What advice did Frank give to Taplow while waiting for Mr. Crocker-Harris?

  • A. To cut (leave) the extra work and go play golf
  • B. To study harder
  • C. To complain to the Headmaster
  • D. To go home and sleep

Answer: A. To cut (leave) the extra work and go play golf

Explanation: Frank suggested that Taplow could leave since Mr. Crocker-Harris was ten minutes late, but Taplow was too terrified to do so.

11. How does Taplow describe Mr. Crocker-Harris's personality?

  • A. All shrivelled up inside like a nut, with no feelings
  • B. Hot-tempered and loud
  • C. Friendly and humorous
  • D. Unfair and corrupt

Answer: A. All shrivelled up inside like a nut, with no feelings

Explanation: Taplow describes him as a person who hates being liked and seems emotionally dry or "shrivelled up".

12. What happened when Mr. Crocker-Harris cracked a classical Latin joke in class?

  • A. Only Taplow laughed out of politeness, even though he didn't understand it
  • B. All students laughed loudly
  • C. No one paid attention
  • D. Mr. Crocker-Harris got angry

Answer: A. Only Taplow laughed out of politeness, even though he didn't understand it

Explanation: Taplow laughed out of ordinary good manners, and in response, Mr. Crocker-Harris asked Taplow to explain the joke to the rest of the class.

13. Who is Millie in the play?

  • A. Mrs. Crocker-Harris, wife of Mr. Crocker-Harris
  • B. Taplow's sister
  • C. Frank's colleague
  • D. The school maid

Answer: A. Mrs. Crocker-Harris, wife of Mr. Crocker-Harris

Explanation: Millie Crocker-Harris is the thin, smart-looking wife of Mr. Crocker-Harris, in her late thirties.

14. What was Millie carrying when she entered the room?

  • A. A shopping basket
  • B. A stack of test papers
  • C. A tray of tea
  • D. A suitcase

Answer: A. A shopping basket

Explanation: Millie enters carrying a shopping basket and wearing smart clothes.

15. Why was Taplow terrified when Millie arrived?

  • A. He feared she might have overheard him imitating Mr. Crocker-Harris
  • B. She was very strict with students
  • C. She had caught him cheating
  • D. He owed her money

Answer: A. He feared she might have overheard him imitating Mr. Crocker-Harris

Explanation: Taplow was imitating Crocker-Harris's voice in front of Frank just as Millie entered, fearing she might tell her husband.

16. Where had Mr. Crocker-Harris gone when Taplow was waiting for him?

  • A. To the Bursar's office
  • B. To the Headmaster's house
  • C. To the hospital
  • D. To the station

Answer: A. To the Bursar's office

Explanation: Millie told Taplow that her husband was at the Bursar's and might be delayed.

17. How does Millie help Taplow to leave and go free?

  • A. She gives him a prescription and sends him to the chemist
  • B. She tells him to run away secretly
  • C. She lies to her husband
  • D. She gives him permission to play cricket

Answer: A. She gives him a prescription and sends him to the chemist

Explanation: Millie gives Taplow a prescription to get medicine from the chemist, offering to take the blame if Crocker-Harris comes back early.

18. What quality of Mr. Crocker-Harris makes students respect him despite his strict nature?

  • A. His absolute impartiality, dedication, and adherence to principles
  • B. His generosity with marks
  • C. His sense of humor
  • D. His lenient attitude

Answer: A. His absolute impartiality, dedication, and adherence to principles

Explanation: Taplow admits that despite everything, he still respects Crocker-Harris because he is completely honest, fair, and non-sadistic.

19. What term does Taplow use for teachers who get pleasure from inflicting pain?

  • A. Sadist
  • B. Egoist
  • C. Pessimist
  • D. Tyrant

Answer: A. Sadist

Explanation: Taplow defines a 'sadist' as someone who gets pleasure out of giving pain, but notes Crocker-Harris is not even a sadist.

20. What feeling does Frank express towards Mr. Crocker-Harris?

  • A. Envy/Jealousy of the effect Crocker-Harris has on students
  • B. Pure hatred
  • C. Deep affection
  • D. Fear

Answer: A. Envy/Jealousy of the effect Crocker-Harris has on students

Explanation: Frank admits he envies Crocker-Harris because students remain terrified and obedient to him despite his strictness.

21. What reason does Mr. Crocker-Harris give for leaving the school?

  • A. He is retiring/taking up a position at another school
  • B. He was fired
  • C. He is unwell
  • D. He wants to pursue writing

Answer: A. He is retiring/taking up a position at another school

Explanation: Mr. Crocker-Harris is leaving the school at the end of the term, which is why he is settling all accounts and results.

22. What title 'The Browning Version' refers to in literary context?

  • A. Robert Browning's English translation of Aeschylus's Agamemnon
  • B. A famous textbook
  • C. A poem by Elizabeth Browning
  • D. A version of the school syllabus

Answer: A. Robert Browning's English translation of Aeschylus's Agamemnon

Explanation: 'The Browning Version' refers to the English translation of the Greek tragedy 'Agamemnon' done by Robert Browning.

23. What kind of relationship exists between Frank and Millie Crocker-Harris?

  • A. An informal, intimate, and friendly rapport
  • B. Distant and formal
  • C. Hostile and aggressive
  • D. Teacher and student relationship

Answer: A. An informal, intimate, and friendly rapport

Explanation: The play hints at an underlying familiarity and closeness between Frank and Millie.

24. What setting is the entire scene of the play placed in?

  • A. The school sitting-room of Mr. Crocker-Harris's house
  • B. A classroom
  • C. The school playground
  • D. The Headmaster's office

Answer: A. The school sitting-room of Mr. Crocker-Harris's house

Explanation: The play takes place entirely in the living room / sitting-room of Crocker-Harris's residence in the school grounds.

25. What is the overall theme of the play 'The Browning Version'?

  • A. The complex human emotions, teacher-student dynamics, and repressed feelings
  • B. The benefits of science education over arts
  • C. The harsh realities of school examinations
  • D. The importance of sports in student life

Answer: A. The complex human emotions, teacher-student dynamics, and repressed feelings

Explanation: The play explores loneliness, strictness vs empathy, human relationships, and underlying respect despite outward rigidness.
