Chapter 7: Reach for the Top 30 Questions

1. "Reach for the Top" consists of profiles of how many women achievers?

  • A. One
  • B. Two
  • C. Three
  • D. Four

Answer: B. Two

Explanation: The chapter presents the inspiring life stories of two successful women achievers, Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova.

2. Who is Santosh Yadav, as described in the first part of the chapter?

  • A. A famous tennis player
  • B. A mountaineer known for climbing Mount Everest
  • C. A well-known singer
  • D. A politician

Answer: B. A mountaineer known for climbing Mount Everest

Explanation: Santosh Yadav is celebrated in the chapter as a determined mountaineer notable for her Everest expeditions.

3. What remarkable achievement did Santosh Yadav accomplish related to Mount Everest?

  • A. She became the youngest woman to climb Everest twice
  • B. She was the first woman in the world to climb Everest twice
  • C. She never actually reached the summit
  • D. She climbed Everest without any team support

Answer: B. She was the first woman in the world to climb Everest twice

Explanation: Santosh Yadav made history as the first woman in the world to scale Mount Everest twice.

4. What challenge did Santosh Yadav face during her second Everest expedition regarding a fellow climber?

  • A. She helped save the life of a companion who was in critical condition due to exhaustion and lack of oxygen
  • B. She abandoned a fellow climber in trouble
  • C. She had a serious argument with her team leader
  • D. She lost all her equipment midway

Answer: A. She helped save the life of a companion who was in critical condition due to exhaustion and lack of oxygen

Explanation: During her expedition, Santosh Yadav showed great courage and compassion by helping save a fellow climber who was in a dangerously weak condition.

5. Where did Santosh Yadav grow up, and what challenges did she face regarding education?

  • A. She grew up in Haryana and had to fight for her right to pursue higher education against family expectations
  • B. She grew up abroad with no educational obstacles
  • C. She never attended school at all
  • D. She grew up in a wealthy family with no struggles

Answer: A. She grew up in Haryana and had to fight for her right to pursue higher education against family expectations

Explanation: Santosh Yadav grew up in Haryana and had to assert herself against traditional family expectations to continue her education.

6. What personal quality helped Santosh Yadav succeed as a mountaineer, according to the chapter?

  • A. Her strong determination, willpower, and refusal to give up
  • B. Her wealth and access to expensive equipment alone
  • C. Pure luck
  • D. Support from a large team with no personal effort

Answer: A. Her strong determination, willpower, and refusal to give up

Explanation: The chapter emphasises Santosh Yadav's remarkable determination and willpower as key to overcoming the immense physical and social challenges she faced.

7. Who is the second achiever profiled in the chapter?

  • A. Maria Sharapova
  • B. Serena Williams
  • C. Steffi Graf
  • D. Venus Williams

Answer: A. Maria Sharapova

Explanation: The second part of the chapter profiles the life and achievements of tennis star Maria Sharapova.

8. In which country was Maria Sharapova born?

  • A. United States
  • B. Russia
  • C. France
  • D. Germany

Answer: B. Russia

Explanation: Maria Sharapova was born in Nyagan, Russia, before her family later moved to pursue her tennis career.

9. At what young age did Maria Sharapova move to the United States to pursue tennis training?

  • A. Around the age of seven
  • B. Around the age of fifteen
  • C. Around the age of eighteen
  • D. She never moved abroad for training

Answer: A. Around the age of seven

Explanation: Maria Sharapova moved to the United States at a very young age, around seven, to train seriously at a tennis academy.

10. What major tournament victory brought Maria Sharapova international fame at a young age?

  • A. Winning the Wimbledon Championship at seventeen
  • B. Winning an Olympic gold medal
  • C. Winning the US Open as a junior player only
  • D. Winning a national school tournament

Answer: A. Winning the Wimbledon Championship at seventeen

Explanation: Maria Sharapova achieved worldwide fame when she won the Wimbledon Championship at the young age of seventeen.

11. What sacrifices did Maria Sharapova and her family make to support her tennis career?

  • A. They moved away from home, faced financial struggles, and lived apart from other family members for years
  • B. They had no struggles as they were already wealthy
  • C. They gave up on the idea of her playing tennis altogether
  • D. They stayed in Russia the entire time

Answer: A. They moved away from home, faced financial struggles, and lived apart from other family members for years

Explanation: Maria's family made significant sacrifices, including financial hardship and prolonged family separation, to support her tennis training in America.

12. What personal qualities of Maria Sharapova does the chapter highlight as key to her success?

  • A. Her discipline, hard work, and single-minded focus on her sport
  • B. Her reliance purely on natural talent without effort
  • C. Her disinterest in tennis
  • D. Her preference for a leisurely lifestyle

Answer: A. Her discipline, hard work, and single-minded focus on her sport

Explanation: The chapter attributes Sharapova's success to her intense discipline, rigorous training, and unwavering focus on excelling in tennis.

13. What emotional challenge did Maria Sharapova face due to being away from her mother during her early training years?

  • A. She experienced homesickness and had to cope with being separated from her mother for a long period
  • B. She never missed her family at all
  • C. She was accompanied by her entire family throughout
  • D. She had no emotional struggles during this period

Answer: A. She experienced homesickness and had to cope with being separated from her mother for a long period

Explanation: Due to visa restrictions, Maria's mother could not join her immediately in the US, causing young Maria significant emotional hardship from the separation.

14. What overarching theme connects the stories of both Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova?

  • A. Achieving greatness through determination, hard work, and overcoming significant obstacles
  • B. The importance of inherited wealth in achieving success
  • C. The idea that success comes without effort
  • D. The unimportance of family support in achieving goals

Answer: A. Achieving greatness through determination, hard work, and overcoming significant obstacles

Explanation: Both women's stories illustrate how relentless determination, sacrifice, and hard work can lead to extraordinary achievements despite considerable challenges.

15. What mountain-related danger nearly cost the life of Santosh Yadav's fellow climber during her second Everest ascent?

  • A. Severe exhaustion combined with lack of oxygen at high altitude
  • B. A sudden avalanche burying the entire team
  • C. A severe storm that destroyed all equipment
  • D. An attack by wild animals

Answer: A. Severe exhaustion combined with lack of oxygen at high altitude

Explanation: A fellow climber's condition worsened dangerously due to extreme exhaustion and insufficient oxygen at high altitude, requiring urgent assistance.

16. How did Santosh Yadav's family initially react to her ambitions of pursuing mountaineering and higher education?

  • A. They were initially resistant, expecting her to follow more traditional paths
  • B. They fully supported her from the very beginning
  • C. They had no opinion on her choices
  • D. They actively encouraged her to abandon education

Answer: A. They were initially resistant, expecting her to follow more traditional paths

Explanation: Santosh Yadav had to overcome initial resistance from her family, who held more traditional expectations for her future.

17. What does the title "Reach for the Top" symbolise in relation to both women's stories?

  • A. Striving for excellence and achieving the highest possible goals in one's chosen field
  • B. Literally climbing physical mountains only
  • C. Reaching the top of a career ladder in business
  • D. Achieving fame without any hard work

Answer: A. Striving for excellence and achieving the highest possible goals in one's chosen field

Explanation: The title metaphorically represents both women's pursuit of excellence and their achievement of top honours in their respective fields, mountaineering and tennis.

18. What does the chapter suggest about the role of sacrifice in achieving extraordinary success?

  • A. Significant personal and family sacrifices are often necessary to reach the pinnacle of success
  • B. Success can be achieved without any sacrifice at all
  • C. Sacrifice guarantees failure rather than success
  • D. Only financial sacrifice matters, not personal ones

Answer: A. Significant personal and family sacrifices are often necessary to reach the pinnacle of success

Explanation: Both stories illustrate that reaching extraordinary heights of achievement often demands considerable personal and familial sacrifice.

19. Which sport is central to Maria Sharapova's story in the chapter?

  • A. Tennis
  • B. Swimming
  • C. Gymnastics
  • D. Athletics

Answer: A. Tennis

Explanation: Maria Sharapova's profile focuses on her journey to becoming a world-class tennis champion.

20. What activity is central to Santosh Yadav's profile in the chapter?

  • A. Mountaineering
  • B. Swimming
  • C. Cricket
  • D. Painting

Answer: A. Mountaineering

Explanation: Santosh Yadav's story centres on her extraordinary achievements as a determined and record-breaking mountaineer.

21. How did Sharapova's coach and training environment contribute to her development as a player?

  • A. Rigorous, disciplined coaching at a specialised tennis academy helped hone her skills
  • B. She had no formal coaching at all
  • C. She trained entirely on her own without professional guidance
  • D. Her training environment was casual and unstructured

Answer: A. Rigorous, disciplined coaching at a specialised tennis academy helped hone her skills

Explanation: Sharapova's structured and rigorous training at a professional tennis academy in the United States played a crucial role in developing her skills.

22. What broader message about gender and achievement does the chapter convey through these two women's stories?

  • A. Women can achieve extraordinary success in traditionally challenging fields through determination and hard work
  • B. Women should avoid pursuing demanding careers
  • C. Achievement is only possible for men in these fields
  • D. Success in sports and adventure is unrelated to gender at all, a point not addressed in the chapter

Answer: A. Women can achieve extraordinary success in traditionally challenging fields through determination and hard work

Explanation: Through the inspiring examples of Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova, the chapter conveys that women can excel and break barriers in demanding fields with grit and dedication.

23. What challenges related to extreme weather and terrain did Santosh Yadav have to face while climbing Everest?

  • A. Harsh cold, thin air, and dangerous, unpredictable mountain conditions
  • B. Warm and pleasant weather throughout the climb
  • C. Flat, easy terrain with no risks
  • D. No physical challenges at all

Answer: A. Harsh cold, thin air, and dangerous, unpredictable mountain conditions

Explanation: Climbing Mount Everest involves facing extreme cold, low oxygen levels, and hazardous terrain, all of which Santosh Yadav had to endure and overcome.

24. What can readers learn about goal-setting from the experiences of both women profiled in the chapter?

  • A. Setting ambitious goals and working persistently towards them can lead to remarkable achievements
  • B. Goals should always be modest and easily achievable
  • C. Goal-setting has no real impact on success
  • D. It is better to avoid setting any goals at all

Answer: A. Setting ambitious goals and working persistently towards them can lead to remarkable achievements

Explanation: The chapter's overall message emphasises that ambitious goal-setting paired with persistent effort and resilience is key to reaching extraordinary heights of success.

25. What does the chapter suggest about handling failure or setbacks on the path to success?

  • A. Resilience and continued effort despite setbacks are essential to eventually achieving one's goals
  • B. One should give up immediately after any setback
  • C. Setbacks always guarantee eventual failure
  • D. Setbacks are not addressed in either woman's story

Answer: A. Resilience and continued effort despite setbacks are essential to eventually achieving one's goals

Explanation: Both stories underline the importance of resilience, showing that persistence through difficulties ultimately paves the way to success.

26. What role did family support eventually play in the success of both women, despite initial hardships?

  • A. Family support, once secured, became a crucial pillar in helping them achieve their dreams
  • B. Family support had no role in their success
  • C. Their families actively prevented their success throughout
  • D. They achieved success entirely without any family involvement

Answer: A. Family support, once secured, became a crucial pillar in helping them achieve their dreams

Explanation: Despite initial obstacles, family support eventually played a significant role in enabling both Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova to pursue and achieve their ambitions.

27. What can be inferred about the sacrifices young Maria Sharapova had to make in her personal and social life due to her rigorous training?

  • A. She had to give up much of a normal childhood, including time with family and typical social experiences
  • B. She had an entirely typical, carefree childhood with no sacrifices
  • C. She never trained seriously as a child
  • D. Her training required no time commitment at all

Answer: A. She had to give up much of a normal childhood, including time with family and typical social experiences

Explanation: The demands of intense early training meant Maria Sharapova missed out on many typical childhood experiences, illustrating the personal cost of her pursuit of excellence.

28. Why might the stories of Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova be considered especially motivating for young readers?

  • A. Because they show that with hard work and determination, seemingly impossible goals can be achieved at a young age
  • B. Because they emphasise that success requires no effort
  • C. Because they discourage young people from pursuing their dreams
  • D. Because they focus solely on financial success

Answer: A. Because they show that with hard work and determination, seemingly impossible goals can be achieved at a young age

Explanation: Both women achieved remarkable feats at a young age through dedication and hard work, making their stories especially inspiring for young readers facing their own challenges.

29. What common trait do Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova share, despite pursuing entirely different fields?

  • A. Exceptional determination and willingness to push through hardship to reach the top of their respective fields
  • B. A shared love for the same sport
  • C. Growing up in identical family circumstances
  • D. Achieving success without any personal effort

Answer: A. Exceptional determination and willingness to push through hardship to reach the top of their respective fields

Explanation: Despite excelling in very different pursuits, both women share the common trait of unwavering determination and resilience in the face of significant challenges.

30. What is the overall message the chapter conveys to young readers about achieving one's dreams?

  • A. With determination, discipline, and perseverance, one can overcome obstacles and reach great heights of success
  • B. Success is purely a matter of luck and circumstance
  • C. Dreams should be abandoned if they seem too difficult
  • D. Only those born into privilege can achieve their dreams

Answer: A. With determination, discipline, and perseverance, one can overcome obstacles and reach great heights of success

Explanation: The chapter's central message, illustrated through both women's inspiring journeys, is that determination, discipline, and perseverance can help anyone reach for and achieve the top of their chosen field.
