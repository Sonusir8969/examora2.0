Chapter 6: India - Size and Location 30 Questions

1. What is the total land area of India?

  • A. 2.4 million sq km
  • B. 3.28 million sq km
  • C. 4.5 million sq km
  • D. 1.2 million sq km

Answer: B. 3.28 million sq km

Explanation: India's total geographical area is about 3.28 million square kilometers, making it the 7th largest country in the world.

2. India lies entirely in which hemisphere?

  • A. Northern and Eastern
  • B. Northern and Western
  • C. Southern and Eastern
  • D. Southern and Western

Answer: A. Northern and Eastern

Explanation: The mainland of India extends between 8°4' N to 37°6' N latitudes and 68°7' E to 97°25' E longitudes.

3. Which latitude divides India into two almost equal halves?

  • A. Equator
  • B. Tropic of Cancer
  • C. Tropic of Capricorn
  • D. Arctic Circle

Answer: B. Tropic of Cancer

Explanation: The Tropic of Cancer (23°30'N) passes almost midway through India, dividing it into two climatic zones.

4. The total length of India's coastline is approximately how much?

  • A. 6,100 km
  • B. 7,516.6 km
  • C. 8,200 km
  • D. 5,000 km

Answer: B. 7,516.6 km

Explanation: The total length of India's coastline, including the mainland and island territories, is about 7,516.6 kilometers.

5. Which is the southernmost point of the Indian mainland?

  • A. Indira Point
  • B. Kanyakumari
  • C. Port Blair
  • D. Chennai

Answer: B. Kanyakumari

Explanation: The southernmost tip of the Indian mainland is Kanyakumari (Cape Comorin) located at 8°4' N latitude.

6. Which is the southernmost point of the entire Indian territory?

  • A. Kanyakumari
  • B. Indira Point
  • C. Lakshadweep
  • D. Andaman Islands

Answer: B. Indira Point

Explanation: Indira Point is the southernmost tip of the Great Nicobar Island in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

7. Which country is to the north-west of India?

  • A. China
  • B. Pakistan
  • C. Bangladesh
  • D. Myanmar

Answer: B. Pakistan

Explanation: Pakistan shares its border with India on the north-western side, separated by the Radcliffe Line.

8. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are located in which sea?

  • A. Arabian Sea
  • B. Bay of Bengal
  • C. Indian Ocean
  • D. Pacific Ocean

Answer: B. Bay of Bengal

Explanation: The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are located in the Bay of Bengal, to the south-east of the Indian mainland.

9. Which island group is located in the Arabian Sea?

  • A. Andaman Islands
  • B. Nicobar Islands
  • C. Lakshadweep Islands
  • D. Maldives

Answer: C. Lakshadweep Islands

Explanation: The Lakshadweep Islands, a group of 36 islands, are located in the Arabian Sea off the coast of Kerala.

10. Which country shares the longest land border with India?

  • A. Pakistan
  • B. China
  • C. Bangladesh
  • D. Nepal

Answer: C. Bangladesh

Explanation: India shares a 4,096.7 km long border with Bangladesh, which is the longest land border India has with any country.

11. India shares its eastern border with which two countries?

  • A. Bangladesh and Myanmar
  • B. China and Nepal
  • C. Pakistan and Afghanistan
  • D. Sri Lanka and Bhutan

Answer: A. Bangladesh and Myanmar

Explanation: To the east, India shares borders with Bangladesh and Myanmar (Burma).

12. The Palk Strait separates India from which country?

  • A. Maldives
  • B. Sri Lanka
  • C. Bangladesh
  • D. Myanmar

Answer: B. Sri Lanka

Explanation: The Palk Strait, along with the Gulf of Mannar, separates the Indian mainland from Sri Lanka.

13. Which country is to the north of India?

  • A. Pakistan
  • B. China
  • C. Bangladesh
  • D. Myanmar

Answer: B. China

Explanation: The Tibet Autonomous Region of China lies to the north of India, separated by the mighty Himalayan mountain range.

14. The Standard Meridian of India passes through which state?

  • A. Uttar Pradesh
  • B. Madhya Pradesh
  • C. Andhra Pradesh
  • D. Tamil Nadu

Answer: A. Uttar Pradesh

Explanation: The 82°30' E longitude, which serves as India's Standard Meridian, passes through the town of Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh.

15. Why is the Standard Meridian of India located at 82°30' E?

  • A. To avoid time zone confusion across the country
  • B. To match the time of London
  • C. To align with the Pacific Ocean
  • D. To match the time zone of China

Answer: A. To avoid time zone confusion across the country

Explanation: India has a longitudinal spread of nearly 30 degrees, so the 82°30' E meridian is chosen as the standard time to keep a uniform time (IST) for the whole country.

16. The latitude of the mainland of India extends from ________.

  • A. 8°4' N to 37°6' N
  • B. 10°N to 40°N
  • C. 25°N to 40°N
  • D. 15°N to 30°N

Answer: A. 8°4' N to 37°6' N

Explanation: The mainland of India is situated between 8°4' N (Kanyakumari) and 37°6' N (Kashmir) latitudes.

17. The total length of the land border of India is approximately how much?

  • A. 7,516.6 km
  • B. 15,200 km
  • C. 12,000 km
  • D. 10,000 km

Answer: B. 15,200 km

Explanation: The total length of India's land border with its neighboring countries is approximately 15,200 kilometers.

18. Which water body is to the west of India?

  • A. Bay of Bengal
  • B. Arabian Sea
  • C. Indian Ocean
  • D. Red Sea

Answer: B. Arabian Sea

Explanation: The Arabian Sea forms the western coastline of India, extending from Gujarat down to Kerala.

19. Which of the following is a union territory of India located on the western coast?

  • A. Lakshadweep
  • B. Andaman and Nicobar
  • C. Puducherry
  • D. Goa

Answer: A. Lakshadweep

Explanation: Lakshadweep is a union territory located on the western coast of India in the Arabian Sea.

20. The Tropic of Cancer passes through how many Indian states?

  • A. 6
  • B. 8
  • C. 9
  • D. 10

Answer: B. 8

Explanation: The Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, and Mizoram.

21. Which country is to the south-east of India?

  • A. Pakistan
  • B. Sri Lanka
  • C. Bangladesh
  • D. China

Answer: B. Sri Lanka

Explanation: Sri Lanka, separated by the Gulf of Mannar and the Palk Strait, is located to the south-east of India.

22. Which one of the following is NOT a neighboring country of India?

  • A. Myanmar
  • B. Thailand
  • C. Nepal
  • D. Bhutan

Answer: B. Thailand

Explanation: Thailand does not share a land border with India. India's neighbors are Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar, and Sri Lanka (maritime).

23. India is the ________ largest country in the world in terms of area.

  • A. 3rd
  • B. 5th
  • C. 7th
  • D. 10th

Answer: C. 7th

Explanation: By total area, India is the 7th largest country globally, after Russia, Canada, China, USA, Brazil, and Australia.

24. The Indian landmass has a central location between which two regions?

  • A. East and West Asia
  • B. North and South America
  • C. Europe and Africa
  • D. Australia and Antarctica

Answer: A. East and West Asia

Explanation: India stands as a major strategic center between East and West Asia, due to its central location across the Indian Ocean trade routes.

25. The Deccan Peninsula is surrounded by which water bodies on three sides?

  • A. Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea, Indian Ocean
  • B. Pacific Ocean, Atlantic Ocean, Indian Ocean
  • C. Red Sea, Arabian Sea, Gulf of Oman
  • D. Bay of Bengal, Gulf of Khambhat, Gulf of Kutch

Answer: A. Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea, Indian Ocean

Explanation: The Deccan Peninsula is bounded by the Bay of Bengal on the east, the Arabian Sea on the west, and the Indian Ocean to the south.

26. Why is Indian Ocean named after India?

  • A. India has a long coastline
  • B. India has a central location in the Indian Ocean
  • C. India has a large navy
  • D. India ruled the ocean in ancient times

Answer: B. India has a central location in the Indian Ocean

Explanation: The Indian Ocean is the only ocean named after a country due to India's central, prominent position at the head of the ocean.

27. The longitudinal extent of India is ________.

  • A. 68°7' E to 97°25' E
  • B. 70°E to 100°E
  • C. 60°E to 90°E
  • D. 80°E to 120°E

Answer: A. 68°7' E to 97°25' E

Explanation: India lies longitudinally between 68°7' East and 97°25' East meridians.

28. Which of these countries does not share a land boundary with India?

  • A. Bhutan
  • B. Nepal
  • C. Maldives
  • D. Bangladesh

Answer: C. Maldives

Explanation: The Maldives is a maritime neighbor of India and does not share a land border with the country.

29. The 'Silk Route' connected India to which region historically?

  • A. Europe and Central Asia
  • B. Australia
  • C. South Africa
  • D. South America

Answer: A. Europe and Central Asia

Explanation: The ancient Silk Route linked India to Central Asia and Europe, facilitating the exchange of goods, culture, and ideas.

30. Which state of India touches the borders of Pakistan, China, and Afghanistan?

  • A. Punjab
  • B. Jammu and Kashmir
  • C. Himachal Pradesh
  • D. Uttarakhand

Answer: B. Jammu and Kashmir

Explanation: Jammu and Kashmir historically shares international borders with Pakistan, China, and a small territory with Afghanistan (through the Wakhan Corridor).
