Ch-3: Two Stories about Flying 30 Questions

1. Who wrote the story 'His First Flight'?

  • A. Liam O'Flaherty
  • B. Frederick Forsyth
  • C. G.L. Fuentes
  • D. Roald Dahl

Answer: A. Liam O'Flaherty

Explanation: 'His First Flight' was authored by Irish novelist Liam O'Flaherty.

2. Who wrote the story 'The Black Aeroplane'?

  • A. Frederick Forsyth
  • B. Liam O'Flaherty
  • C. Robert Frost
  • D. Ruskin Bond

Answer: A. Frederick Forsyth

Explanation: 'The Black Aeroplane' was written by Frederick Forsyth.

3. Why was the young seagull afraid to fly?

  • A. He believed his wings would never support him
  • B. He had injured his wings
  • C. He was too lazy
  • D. His parents forbade him to fly

Answer: A. He believed his wings would never support him

Explanation: The young seagull felt certain that his wings would not support him over the vast sea.

4. How many brothers and sisters did the young seagull have?

  • A. Two brothers and one sister
  • B. One brother and two sisters
  • C. Three brothers and one sister
  • D. Two brothers and two sisters

Answer: A. Two brothers and one sister

Explanation: He had two brothers and one sister who had already learned to fly.

5. What threat did the young seagull's parents give him if he didn't fly?

  • A. He would starve on his ledge
  • B. They would push him off the cliff
  • C. They would abandon him forever
  • D. They would peck him

Answer: A. He would starve on his ledge

Explanation: His parents scolded him and threatened to let him starve on his ledge unless he flew away.

6. For how long had the young seagull been left alone without food on the ledge?

  • A. 24 hours
  • B. 12 hours
  • C. 48 hours
  • D. 6 hours

Answer: A. 24 hours

Explanation: Nobody had come near him for twenty-four hours (since the previous day).

7. Who took pity on the young seagull and finally enticed him to dive for food?

  • A. His mother
  • B. His father
  • C. His brother
  • D. His sister

Answer: A. His mother

Explanation: His mother tore a piece of fish and flew across to him, dangling it near his beak to lure him off the ledge.

8. What maddened the young seagull into diving off the cliff edge?

  • A. The sight of food
  • B. The heat of the sun
  • C. Fear of his father
  • D. Loud noises of the sea

Answer: A. The sight of food

Explanation: Maddened by hunger and the sight of fish in his mother's beak, he dived towards the food.

9. What food was the mother seagull tearing with her beak?

  • A. A piece of fish
  • B. An insect
  • C. Small crabs
  • D. Seaweed

Answer: A. A piece of fish

Explanation: The mother was tearing at a piece of fish that lay at her feet.

10. What did the young seagull's family give him as a reward when he landed safely on the sea?

  • A. Scraps of dog-fish
  • B. Herring
  • C. Fresh worms
  • D. Insects

Answer: A. Scraps of dog-fish

Explanation: His family praised him and their beaks were offering him scraps of dog-fish.

11. What plane was the pilot flying in 'The Black Aeroplane'?

  • A. Old Dakota DS 088
  • B. Boeing 747
  • C. Cessna 172
  • D. Air India Airbus

Answer: A. Old Dakota DS 088

Explanation: The author was flying his old Dakota DS 088 airplane across France to England.

12. Where was the pilot traveling from and where was he going?

  • A. From France back to England
  • B. From England to France
  • C. From Germany to England
  • D. From France to Spain

Answer: A. From France back to England

Explanation: He was flying back home from France to England to join his family for breakfast.

13. What was the pilot dreaming about during his night flight?

  • A. A good English breakfast with his family
  • B. Receiving a promotion
  • C. Landing safely at a new airport
  • D. Buying a new plane

Answer: A. A good English breakfast with his family

Explanation: He was looking forward to his holiday and having a big English breakfast with family.

14. Which control station did the pilot call during his flight?

  • A. Paris Control
  • B. London Control
  • C. Berlin Control
  • D. Dover Control

Answer: A. Paris Control

Explanation: The pilot called Paris Control to confirm his direction and route to England.

15. How far was Paris behind the pilot when he first saw the storm clouds?

  • A. 150 kilometers
  • B. 100 kilometers
  • C. 200 kilometers
  • D. 50 kilometers

Answer: A. 150 kilometers

Explanation: Paris was about 150 kilometers behind him when he saw the huge storm clouds.

16. Why didn't the pilot turn back to Paris when he saw the storm clouds?

  • A. He desperately wanted to go home for breakfast
  • B. He didn't have enough fuel to go back
  • C. He lost communication with Paris Control
  • D. He thought the storm was mild

Answer: A. He desperately wanted to go home for breakfast

Explanation: He decided to take the risk because he was eager to get home and have breakfast.

17. What instruments stopped working inside the storm clouds?

  • A. Compass and radio
  • B. Altimeter and lights
  • C. Engine throttle
  • D. Speedometer only

Answer: A. Compass and radio

Explanation: Inside the dark clouds, his compass was dead and spinning round, and the radio failed as well.

18. What peculiar detail did the pilot notice about the strange plane in the storm?

  • A. It had no lights on its wings
  • B. It was glowing in the dark
  • C. It was flying backward
  • D. It was missing a wing

Answer: A. It had no lights on its wings

Explanation: The author noticed that the black aeroplane had no lights on its wings, yet he could see the pilot's face.

19. How did the pilot of the black aeroplane signal the narrator?

  • A. Waved his hand and signaled to "Follow me"
  • B. Flashed his headlights
  • C. Used radio signals
  • D. Dropped flares

Answer: A. Waved his hand and signaled to "Follow me"

Explanation: The mysterious pilot lifted one hand, waved, and gestured for the narrator to follow him.

20. How long did the narrator follow the black aeroplane like an obedient child?

  • A. Half an hour
  • B. One hour
  • C. Fifteen minutes
  • D. Two hours

Answer: A. Half an hour

Explanation: The narrator followed the black plane through the storm for about half an hour.

21. How much fuel was left in the Dakota's last tank before landing?

  • A. Enough for 5 to 10 minutes of flight
  • B. Enough for 30 minutes
  • C. Completely empty
  • D. Enough for 1 hour

Answer: A. Enough for 5 to 10 minutes of flight

Explanation: The fuel gauge showed only enough fuel remaining to fly for five or ten minutes more.

22. What did the narrator see after coming out of the storm clouds?

  • A. Two straight lines of lights of a runway
  • B. The ocean surface
  • C. Paris city lights
  • D. A mountain range

Answer: A. Two straight lines of lights of a runway

Explanation: Emerging from the dark clouds, he saw two long straight lines of lights showing an airport runway.

23. What did the woman in the control center say when asked about the black aeroplane?

  • A. No other aeroplane was flying in the storm that night
  • B. The black plane landed on runway 2
  • C. It crashed near the coast
  • D. It was a military stealth plane

Answer: A. No other aeroplane was flying in the storm that night

Explanation: She looked at him strangely and laughed, saying his plane was the only one on the radar screen.

24. What primary theme is conveyed through the young seagull's story?

  • A. Overcoming fear and gaining self-confidence
  • B. Obeying parents without question
  • C. Importance of searching for food
  • D. Dangers of living near oceans

Answer: A. Overcoming fear and gaining self-confidence

Explanation: 'His First Flight' emphasizes that initial fear must be conquered to unlock one's true potential.

25. How did the young seagull feel when he finally began to fly?

  • A. He was no longer afraid and soared joyfully
  • B. He felt terrified throughout
  • C. He wished to return to his ledge
  • D. He felt angry at his mother

Answer: A. He was no longer afraid and soared joyfully

Explanation: After a moment of terror, his wings spread naturally and he soared outwards, completely forgetting his fear.

26. What was the reaction of the seagull's family when he successfully landed on the water?

  • A. They flew around him, screaming with joy and praise
  • B. They ignored him completely
  • C. They scolded him for delaying
  • D. They flew away to hunt alone

Answer: A. They flew around him, screaming with joy and praise

Explanation: They circled around him, screaming praise and offering him food in delight.

27. What time was it when the pilot checked his watch in 'The Black Aeroplane'?

  • A. 1:30 in the morning
  • B. 3:00 in the morning
  • C. 12:00 midnight
  • D. 5:00 in the morning

Answer: A. 1:30 in the morning

Explanation: The narrator looked at his watch and saw it was 1:30 am in the morning.

28. What mysterious element remains unsolved in 'The Black Aeroplane'?

  • A. The identity and presence of the pilot in the black plane
  • B. The location of the airport
  • C. The fuel capacity of Dakota
  • D. The direction to England

Answer: A. The identity and presence of the pilot in the black plane

Explanation: The story ends as a mystery as no second plane appeared on the radar screen.

29. What does the word 'brink' mean in 'His First Flight'?

  • A. The extreme edge of a steep place
  • B. The center of a flat land
  • C. The highest peak of a mountain
  • D. Deep sea bottom

Answer: A. The extreme edge of a steep place

Explanation: 'Brink' refers to the outer edge of the cliff or ledge.

30. Why was the young seagull's family calling to him 'shrilly' and 'upbraiding' him?

  • A. To scold and encourage him to overcome fear and fly
  • B. To scare him into jumping
  • C. To play a game with him
  • D. To warn him of predators

Answer: A. To scold and encourage him to overcome fear and fly

Explanation: They used tough love (scolding and calling shrilly) to motivate him to take his first flight.
