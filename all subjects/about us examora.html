About Us

Who We Are

Examora is a dedicated online learning platform designed specifically for students preparing for the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC) board examinations. We provide comprehensive test preparation resources including chapter-wise practice tests, subject MCQs, and previous year question papers.

Our mission is to make quality education accessible to every student in Jharkhand. We believe that consistent practice and proper guidance are the keys to academic success. Examora bridges the gap between students and their exam goals by offering a structured and user-friendly learning environment.

What We Offer

  • Chapter-wise practice tests for Class 9 to Class 12
  • Subject-specific MCQ practice for Science, Mathematics, English, Hindi, and Social Science
  • Previous year question papers for JAC board exams
  • Performance tracking and progress analysis
  • Mobile-friendly platform for learning on the go

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and effective test preparation platform for JAC board students across Jharkhand. We aim to empower every student with the tools and resources they need to achieve their academic goals and build a successful future.
