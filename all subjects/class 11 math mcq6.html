Ch-6: Permutations and Combinations 30 Questions

1. The value of 0! is equal to:

  • A. 1
  • B. 0
  • C. Infinity
  • D. Not defined

Answer: A. 1

Explanation: By definition in mathematics, 0! = 1.

2. Compute the value of 7! / 5! :

  • A. 42
  • B. 35
  • C. 49
  • D. 21

Answer: A. 42

Explanation: 7! / 5! = (7 × 6 × 5!) / 5! = 7 × 6 = 42.

3. If 1/8! + 1/9! = x/10!, then x is equal to:

  • A. 100
  • B. 90
  • C. 81
  • D. 10

Answer: A. 100

Explanation: Divide by 1/8!: 1 + 1/9 = x/(10 × 9) ⇒ 10/9 = x/90 ⇒ x = 100.

4. The value of ⁿPᵣ is given by the formula:

  • A. n! / (n - r)!
  • B. n! / [r! (n - r)!]
  • C. (n - r)! / n!
  • D. n! / r!

Answer: A. n! / (n - r)!

Explanation: By standard formula, permutation ⁿPᵣ = n! / (n - r)!.

5. The value of ⁿCᵣ is given by the formula:

  • A. n! / [r! (n - r)!]
  • B. n! / (n - r)!
  • C. n! / r!
  • D. r! / (n - r)!

Answer: A. n! / [r! (n - r)!]

Explanation: By standard formula, combination ⁿCᵣ = n! / [r! (n - r)!].

6. The relation between ⁿPᵣ and ⁿCᵣ is:

  • A. ⁿPᵣ = r! × ⁿCᵣ
  • B. ⁿCᵣ = r! × ⁿPᵣ
  • C. ⁿPᵣ = ⁿCᵣ / r!
  • D. ⁿPᵣ = n! × ⁿCᵣ

Answer: A. ⁿPᵣ = r! × ⁿCᵣ

Explanation: Since ⁿCᵣ = ⁿPᵣ / r!, rearranging gives ⁿPᵣ = r! × ⁿCᵣ.

7. If ⁿC₈ = ⁿC₂, then the value of n is:

  • A. 10
  • B. 6
  • C. 16
  • D. 8

Answer: A. 10

Explanation: Property: If ⁿCₐ = ⁿC_b, then a = b or a + b = n. Here 8 + 2 = n ⇒ n = 10.

8. If ⁿC₈ = ⁿC₂, then the value of ⁿC₂ is:

  • A. 45
  • B. 90
  • C. 10
  • D. 20

Answer: A. 45

Explanation: n = 8 + 2 = 10. Thus, ¹⁰C₂ = (10 × 9) / 2 = 45.

9. How many 3-digit numbers can be formed from the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 if repetition of digits is allowed?

  • A. 125
  • B. 60
  • C. 24
  • D. 120

Answer: A. 125

Explanation: Each of the 3 places can be filled in 5 ways. Total ways = 5 × 5 × 5 = 125.

10. How many 3-digit numbers can be formed from the digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 if repetition of digits is not allowed?

  • A. 60
  • B. 125
  • C. 120
  • D. 20

Answer: A. 60

Explanation: ⁵P₃ = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60.

11. How many 4-digit code numbers can be formed using the first 10 letters of the English alphabet, if no letter can be repeated?

  • A. 5040
  • B. 10000
  • C. 210
  • D. 720

Answer: A. 5040

Explanation: ¹⁰P₄ = 10 × 9 × 8 × 7 = 5040.

12. Find the number of permutations of the letters of the word 'ALLAHABAD':

  • A. 7560
  • B. 9!
  • C. 362880
  • D. 15120

Answer: A. 7560

Explanation: Word has 9 letters: A=4, L=2. Total permutations = 9! / (4! × 2!) = 362880 / (24 × 2) = 7560.

13. In how many ways can a committee of 3 persons be chosen from a group of 2 men and 3 women?

  • A. 10
  • B. 12
  • C. 15
  • D. 20

Answer: A. 10

Explanation: Total people = 2 + 3 = 5. Ways to choose 3 = ⁵C₃ = (5 × 4 × 3) / (3 × 2 × 1) = 10.

14. The value of ⁿC₀ + ⁿC₁ + ⁿC₂ + ... + ⁿCₙ is equal to:

  • A. 2ⁿ
  • B. 2ⁿ - 1
  • C. n²
  • D. 2n

Answer: A. 2ⁿ

Explanation: The sum of all binomial coefficients in the expansion of (1 + x)ⁿ when x = 1 is equal to 2ⁿ.

15. The value of ⁿCₙ is always:

  • A. 1
  • B. n
  • C. 0
  • D. n!

Answer: A. 1

Explanation: ⁿCₙ = n! / (n! × 0!) = 1.

16. The value of ⁿC₁ is always:

  • A. n
  • B. 1
  • C. 0
  • D. n!

Answer: A. n

Explanation: ⁿC₁ = n! / (1! × (n-1)!) = n.

17. Pascal's identity states that ⁿCᵣ + ⁿCᵣ₋₁ is equal to:

  • A. ⁿ⁺¹Cᵣ
  • B. ⁿ⁺¹Cᵣ₊₁
  • C. ⁿCᵣ₊₁
  • D. ⁿ⁺²Cᵣ

Answer: A. ⁿ⁺¹Cᵣ

Explanation: Pascal's rule for combinations states ⁿCᵣ + ⁿCᵣ₋₁ = ⁿ⁺¹Cᵣ.

18. The number of diagonals in a polygon of 'n' sides is:

  • A. n(n - 3) / 2
  • B. n(n - 1) / 2
  • C. n(n - 2) / 2
  • D. n(n + 1) / 2

Answer: A. n(n - 3) / 2

Explanation: Total line segments joining n vertices is ⁿC₂ = n(n-1)/2. Subtracting n sides gives n(n-3)/2.

19. How many diagonals are there in a hexagon (6 sides)?

  • A. 9
  • B. 12
  • C. 15
  • D. 6

Answer: A. 9

Explanation: Formula = 6(6 - 3) / 2 = 18 / 2 = 9.

20. The number of ways to arrange n distinct objects in a circle is:

  • A. (n - 1)!
  • B. n!
  • C. (n + 1)!
  • D. n! / 2

Answer: A. (n - 1)!

Explanation: Circular permutations of n distinct objects is (n - 1)!.

21. In how many ways can 5 people sit around a circular table?

  • A. 24
  • B. 120
  • C. 60
  • D. 12

Answer: A. 24

Explanation: Circular arrangement formula = (5 - 1)! = 4! = 24.

22. What is the value of ⁿP₀?

  • A. 1
  • B. 0
  • C. n
  • D. n!

Answer: A. 1

Explanation: ⁿP₀ = n! / (n - 0)! = n! / n! = 1.

23. What is the value of ⁿPₙ?

  • A. n!
  • B. 1
  • C. n
  • D. 0

Answer: A. n!

Explanation: ⁿPₙ = n! / (n - n)! = n! / 0! = n!.

24. If ⁿP₅ = 42 × ⁿP₃ (n > 4), then n is:

  • A. 10
  • B. 12
  • C. 9
  • D. 8

Answer: A. 10

Explanation: n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)(n-4) = 42 × n(n-1)(n-2) ⇒ (n-3)(n-4) = 42 ⇒ n² - 7n - 30 = 0 ⇒ (n-10)(n+3) = 0 ⇒ n = 10.

25. Out of 7 consonants and 4 vowels, how many words of 3 consonants and 2 vowels can be formed?

  • A. 25200
  • B. 210
  • C. 2400
  • D. 5040

Answer: A. 25200

Explanation: Selection = ⁷C₃ × ⁴C₂ = 35 × 6 = 210. Arrangement of 5 chosen letters = 5! = 120. Total = 210 × 120 = 25200.

26. How many chords can be drawn through 21 points on a circle?

  • A. 210
  • B. 420
  • C. 105
  • D. 21

Answer: A. 210

Explanation: A chord needs 2 points. Number of chords = ²¹C₂ = (21 × 20) / 2 = 210.

27. How many 3-digit even numbers can be made using digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 if no digit is repeated?

  • A. 60
  • B. 120
  • C. 30
  • D. 90

Answer: A. 60

Explanation: Unit place can be 2, 4, or 6 (3 ways). Remaining 2 places filled from remaining 5 digits in ⁵P₂ = 20 ways. Total = 3 × 20 = 60.

28. The number of ways in which 5 boys and 3 girls can be seated in a row so that no two girls are together is:

  • A. 14400
  • B. 7200
  • C. 2880
  • D. 1200

Answer: A. 14400

Explanation: Arrange 5 boys in 5! = 120 ways. 6 gaps created. Choose 3 gaps for girls in ⁶P₃ = 120 ways. Total = 120 × 120 = 14400.

29. The total number of sub-sets of a set containing 5 elements is:

  • A. 32
  • B. 25
  • C. 16
  • D. 64

Answer: A. 32

Explanation: Total subsets = 2ⁿ = 2⁵ = 32.

30. Find the number of ways of selecting 9 balls from 6 red balls, 5 white balls and 5 blue balls if each selection consists of 3 balls of each colour:

  • A. 2000
  • B. 1000
  • C. 500
  • D. 2500

Answer: A. 2000

Explanation: ⁶C₃ × ⁵C₃ × ⁵C₃ = 20 × 10 × 10 = 2000.
