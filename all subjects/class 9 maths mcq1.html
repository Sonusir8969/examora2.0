Ch-1: Number Systems 10 Questions

1. Which of the following is a rational number?

  • A. √2
  • B. √3
  • C. √0.9
  • D. 0

Answer: D. 0

Explanation: Zero can be written in the form p/q as 0/1, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

2. Between any two rational numbers, how many rational numbers exist?

  • A. Exactly one
  • B. Exactly ten
  • C. Infinitely many
  • D. None

Answer: C. Infinitely many

Explanation: Between any two distinct rational numbers, infinitely many rational and irrational numbers always exist.

3. The decimal expansion of an irrational number is always:

  • A. Terminating
  • B. Non-terminating repeating
  • C. Non-terminating non-repeating
  • D. Terminating repeating

Answer: C. Non-terminating non-repeating

Explanation: Irrational numbers are defined as numbers whose decimal expansions neither end (terminate) nor enter a recurring sequence (repeat).

4. Which of the following is an irrational number?

  • A. √23
  • B. √225
  • C. 0.3796
  • D. 7.478478...

Answer: A. √23

Explanation: 23 is not a perfect square, so √23 is irrational. √225 = 15 (rational), 0.3796 is terminating, and 7.478478... is non-terminating repeating (7.478̅).

5. The value of (3 + √3)(3 - √3) is equal to:

  • A. 0
  • B. 6
  • C. 9
  • D. 3

Answer: B. 6

Explanation: Using the identity (a + b)(a - b) = a² - b²: (3)² - (√3)² = 9 - 3 = 6.

6. Rationalize the denominator of 1 / √7:

  • A. 7 / √7
  • B. √7 / 7
  • C. 1 / 7
  • D. √7

Answer: B. √7 / 7

Explanation: Multiply numerator and denominator by √7: (1 × √7) / (√7 × √7) = √7 / 7.

7. What is the value of (64)^(1/2)?

  • A. 4
  • B. 8
  • C. 16
  • D. 32

Answer: B. 8

Explanation: 64 = 8², so (8²)^(1/2) = 8^(2 × 1/2) = 8^1 = 8.

8. What is the value of (2^(2/3)) × (2^(1/5))?

  • A. 2^(2/15)
  • B. 2^(13/15)
  • C. 2^(3/8)
  • D. 2^(1/15)

Answer: B. 2^(13/15)

Explanation: Using the law of exponents a^m × a^n = a^(m+n): 2^(2/3 + 1/5) = 2^((10+3)/15) = 2^(13/15).

9. Every real number is either:

  • A. Only a rational number
  • B. Only an irrational number
  • C. Rational or Irrational
  • D. A natural number

Answer: C. Rational or Irrational

Explanation: The set of Real Numbers (R) consists of the combination of all Rational (Q) and Irrational (Q') numbers.

10. The value of (1/3³)^7 is equal to:

  • A. 3^(-21)
  • B. 3^21
  • C. 3^4
  • D. 3^(-10)

Answer: A. 3^(-21)

Explanation: (1/3³)^7 = (3^(-3))^7 = 3^(-3 × 7) = 3^(-21).
