NCERT Solutions for Class 9 English Chapter 3 The Little Girl

NCERT Solutions for Class 9 English Beehive Chapter 3 The Little Girl covers the relationship between Kezia and her father, the theme of fear and misunderstanding within a family, and important questions and answers.

Overview of Topics & Subtopics (Click to Jump)

  • 1. Story Summary
  • 2. Character Sketch
  • 3. Theme & Message

1. Story Summary

Short Note:

• Kezia is a young girl who is afraid of her strict and busy father, whom she rarely sees except at breakfast and dinner.

• She tries to make a pincushion as a birthday gift for him using pages from his important speech, which leads to her being punished.

• One night, when her mother and grandmother are away, Kezia has a nightmare and runs to her father for comfort; he responds with unexpected kindness, and this incident changes her fear into affection.

2. Character Sketch

Short Note:

• Kezia: A sensitive, imaginative young girl who initially fears her father because of his strictness but comes to understand him better by the end of the story.

• Kezia's Father: A busy, authoritative man who seems harsh on the surface but reveals genuine warmth and love for his daughter when she needs him most.

• Grandmother: A comforting presence in Kezia's life who tries to explain her father's behaviour and encourages Kezia to understand him.

3. Theme & Message

Short Note:

• The story explores how fear can arise from misunderstanding, and how a single act of kindness can transform a relationship.

• It reminds readers that people, especially parents, may seem strict outwardly but often care deeply, and true feelings are revealed through actions in moments of need.

Reading With Insight (Textbook Questions)

Q1: Why was Kezia afraid of her father?
Answer: Kezia was afraid of her father because he was strict, always seemed busy and serious, scolded her over small matters, and she rarely had any warm or playful interaction with him, unlike with her mother or grandmother.
Q2: Why did Kezia's father punish her for making the pincushion?
Answer: Kezia used the pages of her father's important speech to stuff the pincushion, not realising their value, and when her father discovered this, he was angry at the loss of his work and punished her without listening to her innocent explanation.
Q3: How did the incident of the nightmare change Kezia's feelings towards her father?
Answer: When Kezia had a frightening dream and ran to her father in the night, he comforted her gently, let her stay close to him, and even warmed her cold feet against his body, showing a caring side she had never seen, which removed her fear and replaced it with love.

Extract-Based / Short Answer Questions

Q4: What does the story suggest about first impressions within families?
Answer: The story suggests that first impressions, especially a child's early fear of a strict parent, can be misleading, and genuine understanding often develops only through shared moments of vulnerability and comfort.
Q5: How does the grandmother influence Kezia's understanding of her father?
Answer: The grandmother often tries to soften Kezia's view of her father by reminding her that he works hard for the family, encouraging patience and empathy even before Kezia experiences his kindness directly.
