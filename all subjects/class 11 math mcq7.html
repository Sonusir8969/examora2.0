Ch-7: Binomial Theorem 30 Questions

1. The total number of terms in the expansion of (x + a)ⁿ is:

  • A. n + 1
  • B. n
  • C. n - 1
  • D. 2ⁿ

Answer: A. n + 1

Explanation: The binomial expansion of (x + a)ⁿ contains exactly (n + 1) terms.

2. The general term (r + 1)th term Tᵣ₊₁ in the expansion of (a + b)ⁿ is:

  • A. ⁿCᵣ aⁿ⁻ʳ bʳ
  • B. ⁿCᵣ aʳ bⁿ⁻ʳ
  • C. ⁿCᵣ⁻₁ aⁿ⁻ʳ bʳ
  • D. ⁿCᵣ₊₁ aⁿ⁻ʳ bʳ

Answer: A. ⁿCᵣ aⁿ⁻ʳ bʳ

Explanation: By definition, the (r + 1)th term is Tᵣ₊₁ = ⁿCᵣ aⁿ⁻ʳ bʳ.

3. The sum of all binomial coefficients in the expansion of (1 + x)ⁿ is:

  • A. 2ⁿ
  • B. 2ⁿ⁻¹
  • C. 2ⁿ⁺¹
  • D. n²

Answer: A. 2ⁿ

Explanation: Substituting x = 1 in (1 + x)ⁿ = ⁿC₀ + ⁿC₁x + ... + ⁿCₙxⁿ gives 2ⁿ = ⁿC₀ + ⁿC₁ + ... + ⁿCₙ.

4. The sum of coefficients of even terms ⁿC₀ + ⁿC₂ + ⁿC₄ + ... is equal to:

  • A. 2ⁿ⁻¹
  • B. 2ⁿ
  • C. 2ⁿ⁺¹
  • D. 2ⁿ - 1

Answer: A. 2ⁿ⁻¹

Explanation: The sum of even-indexed coefficients equals the sum of odd-indexed coefficients, each equal to 2ⁿ / 2 = 2ⁿ⁻¹.

5. Number of terms in the expansion of (x + 2y)⁹ is:

  • A. 10
  • B. 9
  • C. 8
  • D. 18

Answer: A. 10

Explanation: Number of terms = n + 1 = 9 + 1 = 10.

6. If n is even, the middle term in the expansion of (a + b)ⁿ is:

  • A. (n/2 + 1)th term
  • B. (n/2)th term
  • C. ((n+1)/2)th term
  • D. (n/2 + 2)th term

Answer: A. (n/2 + 1)th term

Explanation: When n is even, there is only one middle term, which is the (n/2 + 1)th term.

7. If n is odd, the middle terms in the expansion of (a + b)ⁿ are:

  • A. ((n+1)/2)th and ((n+3)/2)th terms
  • B. (n/2)th and (n/2 + 1)th terms
  • C. (n+1)th and (n+2)th terms
  • D. ((n-1)/2)th and ((n+1)/2)th terms

Answer: A. ((n+1)/2)th and ((n+3)/2)th terms

Explanation: When n is odd, there are two middle terms: the ((n+1)/2)th and ((n+3)/2)th terms.

8. The 4th term in the expansion of (x - 2y)¹² is:

  • A. -1760 x⁹ y³
  • B. 1760 x⁹ y³
  • C. -220 x⁹ y³
  • D. -1760 x⁸ y⁴

Answer: A. -1760 x⁹ y³

Explanation: T₄ = ¹²C₃ x⁹ (-2y)³ = 220 × x⁹ × (-8y³) = -1760 x⁹ y³.

9. The coefficient of x⁵ in the expansion of (x + 3)⁸ is:

  • A. 1512
  • B. 56
  • C. 27
  • D. 1120

Answer: A. 1512

Explanation: Term with x⁵ is T₄ = ⁸C₃ x⁸⁻³ 3³ = 56 × x⁵ × 27 = 1512 x⁵. Coefficient is 1512.

10. Find the middle term in the expansion of (x + 1/x)¹⁰:

  • A. 252
  • B. 210
  • C. 120
  • D. 252 x

Answer: A. 252

Explanation: n = 10 (even). Middle term = (10/2 + 1) = 6th term. T₆ = ¹⁰C₅ x⁵ (1/x)⁵ = 252.

11. The term independent of x in the expansion of (x + 1/x)²ⁿ is:

  • A. ²ⁿCₙ
  • B. ²ⁿCₙ₊₁
  • C. ²ⁿCₙ⁻¹
  • D. ⁿCₙ

Answer: A. ²ⁿCₙ

Explanation: Tᵣ₊₁ = ²ⁿCᵣ x²ⁿ⁻ʳ (1/x)ʳ = ²ⁿCᵣ x²ⁿ⁻²ʳ. For term independent of x, 2n - 2r = 0 ⇒ r = n. So term is ²ⁿCₙ.

12. What is the value of ⁿC₀ - ⁿC₁ + ⁿC₂ - ⁿC₃ + ... + (-1)ⁿ ⁿCₙ?

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. 2ⁿ
  • D. -1

Answer: A. 0

Explanation: Put x = -1 in (1 + x)ⁿ = (1 - 1)ⁿ = 0ⁿ = 0.

13. In the expansion of (a - b)ⁿ, the signs of the terms are:

  • A. Alternately positive and negative
  • B. All positive
  • C. All negative
  • D. Dependent on n only

Answer: A. Alternately positive and negative

Explanation: Due to (-b)ʳ, odd powers of b introduce negative signs, resulting in alternating signs.

14. Number of terms in the expansion of (x + a)ⁿ + (x - a)ⁿ when n is even is:

  • A. n/2 + 1
  • B. n/2
  • C. (n + 1)/2
  • D. n + 1

Answer: A. n/2 + 1

Explanation: Odd terms cancel out, leaving (n/2 + 1) even-indexed non-zero terms.

15. Number of terms in the expansion of (x + a)ⁿ - (x - a)ⁿ when n is even is:

  • A. n/2
  • B. n/2 + 1
  • C. (n + 1)/2
  • D. n

Answer: A. n/2

Explanation: Even terms cancel out, leaving exactly n/2 terms.

16. The coefficient of a²b³ in the expansion of (a + b)⁵ is:

  • A. 10
  • B. 5
  • C. 20
  • D. 1

Answer: A. 10

Explanation: Coefficient is ⁵C₃ = (5 × 4 × 3) / (3 × 2 × 1) = 10.

17. Find the coefficient of x⁶ y³ in the expansion of (x + 2y)⁹:

  • A. 672
  • B. 84
  • C. 504
  • D. 252

Answer: A. 672

Explanation: T₄ = ⁹C₃ x⁶ (2y)³ = 84 × x⁶ × 8y³ = 672 x⁶ y³. Coefficient is 672.

18. The number of terms in the expansion of (a + b + c)ⁿ is:

  • A. (n + 1)(n + 2) / 2
  • B. n + 1
  • C. n + 2
  • D. (n + 1)²

Answer: A. (n + 1)(n + 2) / 2

Explanation: For a trinomial expansion, total number of terms is ⁿ⁺²C₂ = (n + 1)(n + 2) / 2.

19. The number of terms in (x + y + z)¹⁰ is:

  • A. 66
  • B. 55
  • C. 11
  • D. 121

Answer: A. 66

Explanation: Formula: (10 + 1)(10 + 2) / 2 = (11 × 12) / 2 = 66.

20. The 13th term in the expansion of (9x - 1/(3√x))¹⁸ is:

  • A. 18564
  • B. 18564 x
  • C. 18564 / x
  • D. 18564 x²

Answer: A. 18564

Explanation: T₁₃ = ¹⁸C₁₂ (9x)⁶ (-1 / (3√x))¹² = 18564 × (9⁶ x⁶) × (1 / (3¹² x⁶)) = 18564 (since 9⁶ = 3¹²).

21. Expanding (101)⁴ using Binomial Theorem gives:

  • A. 104060401
  • B. 104060400
  • C. 104040601
  • D. 104060101

Answer: A. 104060401

Explanation: (100 + 1)⁴ = 100⁴ + 4(100³) + 6(100²) + 4(100) + 1 = 100000000 + 4000000 + 60000 + 400 + 1 = 104060401.

22. Which is larger: (1.1)¹⁰⁰⁰⁰ or 1000?

  • A. (1.1)¹⁰⁰⁰⁰
  • B. 1000
  • C. Both are equal
  • D. Cannot be determined

Answer: A. (1.1)¹⁰⁰⁰⁰

Explanation: (1 + 0.1)¹⁰⁰⁰⁰ = 1 + 10000(0.1) + ... = 1 + 1000 + positive terms > 1000.

23. In Pascal's Triangle, each row starts and ends with:

  • A. 1
  • B. 0
  • C. n
  • D. 2

Answer: A. 1

Explanation: Every row in Pascal's Triangle begins with ⁿC₀ = 1 and ends with ⁿCₙ = 1.

24. The middle term in the expansion of (x/3 + 9y)¹⁰ is:

  • A. 61236 x⁵ y⁵
  • B. 252 x⁵ y⁵
  • C. 61236 x⁶ y⁴
  • D. 61236 x⁴ y⁶

Answer: A. 61236 x⁵ y⁵

Explanation: Middle term is T₆ = ¹⁰C₅ (x/3)⁵ (9y)⁵ = 252 × (x⁵ / 243) × 59049 y⁵ = 61236 x⁵ y⁵.

25. In the binomial expansion of (a + b)ⁿ, the degree of each term is always:

  • A. n
  • B. n - 1
  • C. n + 1
  • D. Variable

Answer: A. n

Explanation: In every term ⁿCᵣ aⁿ⁻ʳ bʳ, the sum of indices is (n - r) + r = n.

26. If the coefficient of x⁷ and x⁸ are equal in (2 + x/3)ⁿ, then n is:

  • A. 55
  • B. 50
  • C. 45
  • D. 60

Answer: A. 55

Explanation: Equating coefficients: ⁿC₇ 2ⁿ⁻⁷ (1/3)⁷ = ⁿC₈ 2ⁿ⁻⁸ (1/3)⁸ ⇒ ⁿC₇ × 2 = ⁿC₈ × (1/3) ⇒ 6 = ⁿC₈ / ⁿC₇ = (n - 7) / 8 ⇒ n - 7 = 48 ⇒ n = 55.

27. The first term in the binomial expansion of (x + a)ⁿ is:

  • A. xⁿ
  • B. aⁿ
  • C. n xⁿ⁻¹
  • D. ⁿC₁ xⁿ

Answer: A. xⁿ

Explanation: T₁ = ⁿC₀ xⁿ a⁰ = 1 × xⁿ × 1 = xⁿ.

28. The last term in the binomial expansion of (x + a)ⁿ is:

  • A. aⁿ
  • B. xⁿ
  • C. n aⁿ⁻¹
  • D. ⁿC₁ aⁿ

Answer: A. aⁿ

Explanation: Tₙ₊₁ = ⁿCₙ x⁰ aⁿ = 1 × 1 × aⁿ = aⁿ.

29. The coefficient of xⁿ⁻¹ in the expansion of (x - 1)(x - 2)(x - 3)...(x - n) is:

  • A. -n(n + 1) / 2
  • B. n(n + 1) / 2
  • C. -n
  • D. (-1)ⁿ n!

Answer: A. -n(n + 1) / 2

Explanation: Sum of roots with negative sign = -(1 + 2 + 3 + ... + n) = -n(n + 1)/2.

30. Find the coefficient of x⁴ in the expansion of (1 + x)⁸:

  • A. 70
  • B. 56
  • C. 28
  • D. 35

Answer: A. 70

Explanation: Coefficient is ⁸C₄ = (8 × 7 × 6 × 5) / (4 × 3 × 2 × 1) = 70.
