Class 11 Science Exam Strategy & Tips

Key Tips for Board & Competitive Exams (JEE / NEET):

• Physics: Focus heavily on vector algebra, free-body diagrams in Mechanics, rotational dynamics derivations, and thermodynamic processes.

• Chemistry: Master mole concepts, quantum numbers, hybridization, IUPAC nomenclature, electronic effects (Inductive, Resonance, Hyperconjugation), and reaction mechanisms.

• Biology: Memorize NCERT diagrams and tables thoroughly, especially for Biological Classification, Plant/Animal Kingdom, Cell Biology, and Human Physiology.
