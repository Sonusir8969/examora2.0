Ch-3: Reproductive Health 12 Questions

1. Amniocentesis is a technique primarily used for:

  • A. Prenatal sex determination and detection of certain genetic disorders
  • B. Contraception
  • C. Increasing fertility
  • D. Treating infertility directly

Answer: A. Prenatal sex determination and detection of certain genetic disorders

Explanation: Amniocentesis analyzes fetal cells present in amniotic fluid to detect chromosomal or genetic abnormalities.

2. The full form of the assisted reproductive technique IVF is:

  • A. In Vitro Fertilization
  • B. Intra Vaginal Fertilization
  • C. In Vivo Fertilization
  • D. Intra Vitro Fusion

Answer: A. In Vitro Fertilization

Explanation: IVF involves fertilizing an egg with sperm outside the body ('in vitro', literally 'in glass').

3. Copper-T is a well-known example of a(n):

  • A. Intrauterine device (IUD)
  • B. Oral contraceptive pill
  • C. Barrier method
  • D. Surgical contraceptive method

Answer: A. Intrauterine device (IUD)

Explanation: Copper-T is inserted into the uterus and prevents pregnancy mainly through the spermicidal action of released copper ions.

4. Saheli, a widely known oral contraceptive in India, is classified as a:

  • A. Non-steroidal oral contraceptive
  • B. Barrier contraceptive
  • C. Type of IUD
  • D. Injectable contraceptive

Answer: A. Non-steroidal oral contraceptive

Explanation: Saheli is notable for being a once-a-week, non-steroidal oral contraceptive pill for women.

5. Vasectomy is a surgical contraceptive procedure performed in males, involving:

  • A. Cutting and tying a small part of the vas deferens
  • B. Tying the fallopian tubes
  • C. Complete removal of the testes
  • D. Removal of the ovaries

Answer: A. Cutting and tying a small part of the vas deferens

Explanation: Vasectomy blocks the passage of sperm by cutting and ligating the vas deferens, without removing the testes.

6. Tubectomy is a surgical contraceptive procedure performed in females, involving:

  • A. Cutting and tying a small part of the fallopian tubes
  • B. Cutting the vas deferens
  • C. Removal of the ovaries
  • D. Removal of the testes

Answer: A. Cutting and tying a small part of the fallopian tubes

Explanation: Tubectomy blocks the fallopian tubes to prevent the egg from meeting sperm, without removing the ovaries.

7. The assisted reproductive technique abbreviated as ZIFT stands for:

  • A. Zygote Intra Fallopian Transfer
  • B. Zonal Intra Fertilization Technique
  • C. Zygote In Vitro Fertilization
  • D. Zonal Insemination Fertility Technique

Answer: A. Zygote Intra Fallopian Transfer

Explanation: In ZIFT, an embryo formed through IVF (up to the 8-celled stage) is transferred into the fallopian tube of the recipient.

8. Amniocentesis for the purpose of sex determination is legally banned in India mainly in order to prevent:

  • A. Female foeticide
  • B. Male infertility
  • C. Twin pregnancies
  • D. Genetic mutations

Answer: A. Female foeticide

Explanation: The misuse of amniocentesis for sex-selective abortions led to its legal restriction under the PCPNDT Act in India.

9. Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP) is generally considered relatively safe when performed up to:

  • A. The end of the first trimester of pregnancy
  • B. The entire duration of pregnancy
  • C. Only after the delivery date
  • D. The third trimester only

Answer: A. The end of the first trimester of pregnancy

Explanation: Termination during the first trimester carries fewer complications compared with termination later in pregnancy.

10. Sexually transmitted infections (STIs) can generally be prevented most reliably through the use of:

  • A. Barrier methods such as condoms
  • B. Avoiding all contraceptives
  • C. Oral pills only
  • D. IUDs only

Answer: A. Barrier methods such as condoms

Explanation: Unlike hormonal or intrauterine methods, barrier methods such as condoms also provide protection against STIs.

11. The assisted reproductive technique abbreviated as GIFT stands for:

  • A. Gamete Intra Fallopian Transfer
  • B. Genetic Infertility Fusion Technique
  • C. Gonadal In Vitro Fertilization
  • D. Gamete Insemination Fertility Test

Answer: A. Gamete Intra Fallopian Transfer

Explanation: In GIFT, gametes (ovum and sperm) collected from a donor are transferred into the fallopian tube of another female unable to produce her own.

12. Infertility in couples can sometimes be successfully treated using assisted reproductive technologies such as:

  • A. IVF (In Vitro Fertilization)
  • B. Tubectomy
  • C. Vasectomy
  • D. Insertion of a Copper-T

Answer: A. IVF (In Vitro Fertilization)

Explanation: IVF and related techniques help infertile couples by assisting fertilization or embryo transfer through external medical intervention.
