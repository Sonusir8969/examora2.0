Ch-10: Circles 30 Questions

1. The center of a circle lies in the ________ of the circle.

  • A. Exterior
  • B. Interior
  • C. Boundary
  • D. None of these

Answer: B. Interior

Explanation: The center of a circle always lies inside (interior) the circle.

2. A point whose distance from the center of a circle is greater than its radius lies in the ________ of the circle.

  • A. Interior
  • B. Exterior
  • C. On the circle
  • D. At the center

Answer: B. Exterior

Explanation: Distance > radius means the point is outside (exterior) the circle.

3. The longest chord of a circle is its:

  • A. Radius
  • B. Diameter
  • C. Secant
  • D. Tangent

Answer: B. Diameter

Explanation: The diameter is the longest chord that passes through the center of the circle.

4. An arc is a ________ when its ends are the ends of a diameter.

  • A. Major arc
  • B. Minor arc
  • C. Semicircle
  • D. Full circle

Answer: C. Semicircle

Explanation: An arc whose endpoints lie on the diameter is half a circle, known as a semicircle.

5. Segment of a circle is the region between an arc and the ________ of the circle.

  • A. Radius
  • B. Chord
  • C. Center
  • D. Tangent

Answer: B. Chord

Explanation: A circular segment is bounded by a chord and the corresponding arc.

6. A circle divides the plane on which it lies into ________ parts.

  • A. Two
  • B. Three
  • C. Four
  • D. Infinite

Answer: B. Three

Explanation: The three parts are: the interior, the circle itself, and the exterior.

7. The perpendicular from the center of a circle to a chord ________ the chord.

  • A. Trisects
  • B. Bisects
  • C. Quadruples
  • D. Does not affect

Answer: B. Bisects

Explanation: Theorem 10.3 states that a line drawn from the center perpendicular to a chord bisects the chord into two equal parts.

8. Equal chords of a circle subtend ________ angles at the center.

  • A. Right
  • B. Equal
  • C. Complementary
  • D. Supplementary

Answer: B. Equal

Explanation: Theorem 10.1 states that equal chords of a circle subtend equal angles at the center.

9. How many circles can pass through three non-collinear points?

  • A. Zero
  • B. Exactly one
  • C. Two
  • D. Infinitely many

Answer: B. Exactly one

Explanation: Theorem 10.5 states that there is one and only one circle passing through three given non-collinear points.

10. The angle subtended by an arc at the center is ________ the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle.

  • A. Equal to
  • B. Half of
  • C. Double
  • D. Triple

Answer: C. Double

Explanation: Theorem 10.8 states that the angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining circumference.

11. Angle subtended in a semicircle is a/an:

  • A. Acute angle
  • B. Right angle
  • C. Obtuse angle
  • D. Straight angle

Answer: B. Right angle

Explanation: Angle in a semicircle is always a right angle (90°).

12. Angles in the same segment of a circle are:

  • A. Equal
  • B. Unequal
  • C. Supplementary
  • D. Complementary

Answer: A. Equal

Explanation: Theorem 10.9 states that angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.

13. The sum of either pair of opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral is:

  • A. 90°
  • B. 180°
  • C. 270°
  • D. 360°

Answer: B. 180°

Explanation: Theorem 10.11 states that the opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary (180°).

14. If a quadrilateral ABCD is cyclic, and ∠A = 70°, find ∠C.

  • A. 70°
  • B. 110°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 120°

Answer: B. 110°

Explanation: In a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles sum to 180°. So ∠C = 180° - 70° = 110°.

15. If the radius of a circle is 5 cm and a chord is at a distance of 3 cm from the center, the length of the chord is:

  • A. 4 cm
  • B. 6 cm
  • C. 8 cm
  • D. 10 cm

Answer: C. 8 cm

Explanation: Half chord = √(5² - 3²) = √(25 - 9) = √16 = 4 cm. Full chord length = 2 × 4 = 8 cm.

16. Chords equidistant from the center of a circle are ________ in length.

  • A. Unequal
  • B. Double
  • C. Equal
  • D. Half

Answer: C. Equal

Explanation: Theorem 10.6 states that chords equidistant from the center are equal in length.

17. If an arc subtends an angle of 80° at the center of a circle, what angle does it subtend at the circumference?

  • A. 160°
  • B. 40°
  • C. 80°
  • D. 20°

Answer: B. 40°

Explanation: Angle at circumference = 1/2 × Angle at center = 1/2 × 80° = 40°.

18. A cyclic parallelogram is always a:

  • A. Rhombus
  • B. Rectangle
  • C. Trapezium
  • D. Kite

Answer: B. Rectangle

Explanation: Opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal. Since it's cyclic, their sum is 180°. So each angle = 90°, making it a rectangle.

19. If the length of a chord of a circle of radius 10 cm is 16 cm, find its distance from the center.

  • A. 6 cm
  • B. 8 cm
  • C. 12 cm
  • D. 5 cm

Answer: A. 6 cm

Explanation: Half chord = 8 cm. Distance from center = √(10² - 8²) = √(100 - 64) = √36 = 6 cm.

20. Sector of a circle is the region between an arc and ________ radii.

  • A. One
  • B. Two
  • C. Three
  • D. Four

Answer: B. Two

Explanation: A sector is bounded by two radii joining the center to the endpoints of an arc.

21. In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, if ∠A = 2x and ∠C = 3x, find x.

  • A. 36°
  • B. 18°
  • C. 45°
  • D. 30°

Answer: A. 36°

Explanation: ∠A + ∠C = 180° ⇒ 2x + 3x = 180° ⇒ 5x = 180° ⇒ x = 36°.

22. Number of circles passing through two given points is:

  • A. One
  • B. Two
  • C. Three
  • D. Infinitely many

Answer: D. Infinitely many

Explanation: An infinite number of circles can be drawn passing through two fixed points.

23. In a circle with center O, AB is a chord. If ∠AOB = 100°, what is ∠OAB?

  • A. 40°
  • B. 80°
  • C. 50°
  • D. 100°

Answer: A. 40°

Explanation: In ΔOAB, OA = OB (radii) ⇒ ∠OAB = ∠OBA. Thus 2(∠OAB) + 100° = 180° ⇒ ∠OAB = 40°.

24. If two circles intersect at two points, the line joining their centers is the ________ bisector of the common chord.

  • A. Parallel
  • B. Perpendicular
  • C. Non-perpendicular
  • D. Angle

Answer: B. Perpendicular

Explanation: The line joining the centers of two intersecting circles is the perpendicular bisector of their common chord.

25. If the diameter of a circle is 14 cm, its radius is:

  • A. 28 cm
  • B. 7 cm
  • C. 14 cm
  • D. 3.5 cm

Answer: B. 7 cm

Explanation: Radius = Diameter / 2 = 14 / 2 = 7 cm.

26. If a line segment joining two points subtends equal angles at two other points on the same side, the four points are:

  • A. Collinear
  • B. Concyclic
  • C. Coplanar only
  • D. None of these

Answer: B. Concyclic

Explanation: Theorem 10.10 states that if equal angles are subtended, all four points lie on the same circle (concyclic).

27. An isosceles trapezium is always a ________ quadrilateral.

  • A. Non-cyclic
  • B. Cyclic
  • C. Rhombus
  • D. Parallelogram

Answer: B. Cyclic

Explanation: In an isosceles trapezium, opposite angles are supplementary, so it is always cyclic.

28. Distance of the chord from the center decreases as the length of the chord:

  • A. Decreases
  • B. Increases
  • C. Remains constant
  • D. Becomes zero

Answer: B. Increases

Explanation: Longer chords are closer to the center, reaching distance = 0 for diameter (longest chord).

29. In a circle, if chord AB = chord CD, then their corresponding arcs are:

  • A. Equal
  • B. Unequal
  • C. Supplementary
  • D. Complementary

Answer: A. Equal

Explanation: Equal chords subtend equal arcs on a circle.

30. In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, if ∠A - ∠C = 60°, find ∠A.

  • A. 120°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 150°
  • D. 90°

Answer: A. 120°

Explanation: ∠A + ∠C = 180° and ∠A - ∠C = 60°. Adding equations gives 2∠A = 240° ⇒ ∠A = 120°.
