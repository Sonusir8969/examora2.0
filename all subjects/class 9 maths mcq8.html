Ch-8: Quadrilaterals 30 Questions

1. The sum of all four interior angles of a quadrilateral is equal to:

  • A. 180°
  • B. 270°
  • C. 360°
  • D. 540°

Answer: C. 360°

Explanation: Angle Sum Property of a Quadrilateral states that the sum of the four interior angles is 360°.

2. Three angles of a quadrilateral are 75°, 90°, and 75°. The fourth angle is:

  • A. 90°
  • B. 95°
  • C. 105°
  • D. 120°

Answer: D. 120°

Explanation: Fourth angle = 360° - (75° + 90° + 75°) = 360° - 240° = 120°.

3. A quadrilateral having both pairs of opposite sides parallel is called a:

  • A. Trapezium
  • B. Parallelogram
  • C. Kite
  • D. Quadrilateral only

Answer: B. Parallelogram

Explanation: By definition, a parallelogram is a quadrilateral in which both pairs of opposite sides are parallel.

4. Which of the following is NOT a property of a parallelogram?

  • A. Opposite sides are equal
  • B. Opposite angles are equal
  • C. Diagonals bisect each other
  • D. Diagonals are always equal

Answer: D. Diagonals are always equal

Explanation: Diagonals of a general parallelogram bisect each other, but they are not necessarily equal in length (unless it is a rectangle or square).

5. A diagonal of a parallelogram divides it into two ________ triangles.

  • A. Equilateral
  • B. Similar only
  • C. Congruent
  • D. Isosceles

Answer: C. Congruent

Explanation: Theorem 8.1 states that a diagonal of a parallelogram divides it into two congruent triangles.

6. A parallelogram having all four sides equal is called a:

  • A. Rectangle
  • B. Rhombus
  • C. Trapezium
  • D. Kite

Answer: B. Rhombus

Explanation: A rhombus is defined as a parallelogram with all sides equal in length.

7. A parallelogram having one of its angles equal to 90° is called a:

  • A. Rhombus
  • B. Trapezium
  • C. Rectangle
  • D. Kite

Answer: C. Rectangle

Explanation: If one angle of a parallelogram is a right angle (90°), all its angles become 90°, making it a rectangle.

8. The diagonals of a rhombus intersect each other at:

  • A. 45°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 120°

Answer: C. 90°

Explanation: The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles (90°).

9. The line segment joining the mid-points of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and equal to ________ of it.

  • A. Double
  • B. Half
  • C. One-third
  • D. Equal

Answer: B. Half

Explanation: Mid-point Theorem states that the segment joining mid-points of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and half of its length.

10. In ΔABC, if D and E are mid-points of AB and AC respectively, and BC = 12 cm, find DE.

  • A. 24 cm
  • B. 6 cm
  • C. 12 cm
  • D. 4 cm

Answer: B. 6 cm

Explanation: By Mid-point Theorem, DE = 1/2 × BC = 1/2 × 12 cm = 6 cm.

11. If the diagonals of a quadrilateral bisect each other at right angles, then it is a:

  • A. Rectangle
  • B. Trapezium
  • C. Rhombus
  • D. Parallelogram only

Answer: C. Rhombus

Explanation: If the diagonals of a quadrilateral bisect each other perpendicularly, the quadrilateral is a rhombus.

12. If the diagonals of a parallelogram are equal, then it is a:

  • A. Rhombus
  • B. Rectangle
  • C. Trapezium
  • D. Kite

Answer: B. Rectangle

Explanation: Theorem 8.4 states that if the diagonals of a parallelogram are equal, then it is a rectangle.

13. The angles of a quadrilateral are in ratio 3 : 5 : 9 : 13. Find the smallest angle.

  • A. 36°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 108°
  • D. 156°

Answer: A. 36°

Explanation: 3x + 5x + 9x + 13x = 360° ⇒ 30x = 360° ⇒ x = 12°. Smallest angle = 3(12°) = 36°.

14. The angles of a quadrilateral are in ratio 3 : 5 : 9 : 13. Find the largest angle.

  • A. 36°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 108°
  • D. 156°

Answer: D. 156°

Explanation: 30x = 360° ⇒ x = 12°. Largest angle = 13(12°) = 156°.

15. A quadrilateral having only one pair of opposite sides parallel is a:

  • A. Parallelogram
  • B. Trapezium
  • C. Rhombus
  • D. Square

Answer: B. Trapezium

Explanation: A quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel opposite sides is called a trapezium.

16. Consecutive adjacent angles of a parallelogram are always:

  • A. Equal
  • B. Complementary
  • C. Supplementary
  • D. Reflex

Answer: C. Supplementary

Explanation: Adjacent interior angles between parallel lines sum up to 180° (supplementary).

17. In a parallelogram ABCD, if ∠A = 70°, find ∠B.

  • A. 70°
  • B. 110°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 120°

Answer: B. 110°

Explanation: Adjacent angles are supplementary: ∠B = 180° - ∠A = 180° - 70° = 110°.

18. In a parallelogram ABCD, if ∠A = 70°, find ∠C.

  • A. 70°
  • B. 110°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 180°

Answer: A. 70°

Explanation: Opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal, so ∠C = ∠A = 70°.

19. The figure formed by joining the mid-points of the sides of a quadrilateral in order is a:

  • A. Square
  • B. Rectangle
  • C. Parallelogram
  • D. Rhombus

Answer: C. Parallelogram

Explanation: Joining the consecutive mid-points of any arbitrary quadrilateral always forms a parallelogram.

20. Which shape has equal diagonals that bisect each other at right angles?

  • A. Rectangle
  • B. Rhombus
  • C. Square
  • D. Parallelogram

Answer: C. Square

Explanation: A square possesses both properties: equal diagonals (like a rectangle) and perpendicular bisecting diagonals (like a rhombus).

21. In a rectangle ABCD, if diagonal AC = 10 cm, then diagonal BD is equal to:

  • A. 5 cm
  • B. 10 cm
  • C. 20 cm
  • D. 15 cm

Answer: B. 10 cm

Explanation: The diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length, so BD = AC = 10 cm.

22. Converse of Mid-point Theorem states that a line drawn through the mid-point of one side of a triangle parallel to another side ________ the third side.

  • A. Doubles
  • B. Bisects
  • C. Cuts at 1:3 ratio
  • D. Is perpendicular to

Answer: B. Bisects

Explanation: Theorem 8.10 (Converse of Mid-point Theorem) states that such a line bisects the third side.

23. In a rhombus ABCD, if side AB = 6 cm, what is the perimeter of the rhombus?

  • A. 12 cm
  • B. 18 cm
  • C. 24 cm
  • D. 36 cm

Answer: C. 24 cm

Explanation: A rhombus has 4 equal sides. Perimeter = 4 × 6 cm = 24 cm.

24. If two adjacent sides of a rectangle are equal, it becomes a:

  • A. Rhombus
  • B. Square
  • C. Trapezium
  • D. Parallelogram only

Answer: B. Square

Explanation: A rectangle has all angles equal to 90°. If its adjacent sides are equal, all four sides become equal, making it a square.

25. In a quadrilateral ABCD, if ∠A + ∠B = 200°, find ∠C + ∠D.

  • A. 100°
  • B. 160°
  • C. 180°
  • D. 200°

Answer: B. 160°

Explanation: ∠A + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360° ⇒ 200° + ∠C + ∠D = 360° ⇒ ∠C + ∠D = 160°.

26. A quadrilateral with two pairs of equal adjacent sides but unequal opposite sides is a:

  • A. Rhombus
  • B. Parallelogram
  • C. Kite
  • D. Trapezium

Answer: C. Kite

Explanation: A kite is defined as a quadrilateral with two distinct pairs of equal adjacent sides.

27. If the diagonals of a rhombus are 6 cm and 8 cm, the length of its side is:

  • A. 5 cm
  • B. 10 cm
  • C. 7 cm
  • D. 14 cm

Answer: A. 5 cm

Explanation: Half-diagonals are 3 cm and 4 cm. Using Pythagoras theorem in right triangle formed by intersecting diagonals: Side = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 cm.

28. Bisectors of any two adjacent angles of a parallelogram intersect at an angle of:

  • A. 45°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 180°

Answer: C. 90°

Explanation: Sum of adjacent angles = 180°. Sum of their halves = 90°. In the triangle formed, third angle = 180° - 90° = 90°.

29. In a isosceles trapezium, the non-parallel sides are:

  • A. Parallel
  • B. Equal
  • C. Perpendicular
  • D. Unequal

Answer: B. Equal

Explanation: By definition, an isosceles trapezium has equal non-parallel sides.

30. Is every square a rhombus?

  • A. Yes
  • B. No
  • C. Only if diagonals are equal
  • D. Cannot be determined

Answer: A. Yes

Explanation: A square satisfies all conditions of a rhombus (all 4 sides equal, opposite sides parallel, perpendicular diagonals), so every square is a rhombus.
