Ch-1: The Rise of Nationalism in Europe 30 Questions

1. Who prepared a series of four prints visualizing his dream of a world made up of 'democratic and social Republics' in 1848?

  • A. Frédéric Sorrieu
  • B. Duke Metternich
  • C. Otto von Bismarck
  • D. Giuseppe Mazzini

Answer: A. Frédéric Sorrieu

Explanation: Frédéric Sorrieu, a French artist, prepared a series of four prints in 1848 visualizing his vision of democratic and social republics.

2. When did the French Revolution take place?

  • A. 1789
  • B. 1815
  • C. 1830
  • D. 1848

Answer: A. 1789

Explanation: The first clear expression of nationalism came with the French Revolution in 1789.

3. What was the main purpose of the Napoleonic Code of 1804?

  • A. To re-establish absolute monarchy
  • B. To abolish privileges based on birth and establish equality before law
  • C. To give voting rights to women
  • D. To annex new territories in Europe

Answer: B. To abolish privileges based on birth and establish equality before law

Explanation: The Civil Code of 1804 (Napoleonic Code) did away with all privileges based on birth, established equality before the law, and secured the right to property.

4. Which secret society was founded by Giuseppe Mazzini in Marseilles?

  • A. Young Italy
  • B. Young Europe
  • C. Carbonari
  • D. Jacobin Club

Answer: A. Young Italy

Explanation: Giuseppe Mazzini founded Young Italy in Marseilles and Young Europe in Berne.

5. Who described Giuseppe Mazzini as 'the most dangerous enemy of our social order'?

  • A. Duke Metternich
  • B. Napoleon Bonaparte
  • C. Cavour
  • D. William I

Answer: A. Duke Metternich

Explanation: Austrian Chancellor Duke Metternich described Mazzini as 'the most dangerous enemy of our social order' due to his relentless opposition to monarchy.

6. What was 'Zollverein' formed in 1834?

  • A. A customs union
  • B. A military alliance
  • C. A political party
  • D. A trade treaty between Britain and France

Answer: A. A customs union

Explanation: Zollverein was a customs union formed in 1834 at the initiative of Prussia to abolish tariff barriers and reduce currencies from over thirty to two.

7. Which treaty recognized Greece as an independent nation in 1832?

  • A. Treaty of Constantinople
  • B. Treaty of Vienna
  • C. Treaty of Versailles
  • D. Treaty of Paris

Answer: A. Treaty of Constantinople

Explanation: The Treaty of Constantinople of 1832 recognized Greece as an independent nation after the Greek War of Independence.

8. Who was proclaimed King of United Italy in 1861?

  • A. Victor Emmanuel II
  • B. Giuseppe Garibaldi
  • C. Count Cavour
  • D. Kaiser William I

Answer: A. Victor Emmanuel II

Explanation: Victor Emmanuel II was proclaimed King of united Italy in 1861.

9. Who held the chief architect role in the unification of Germany?

  • A. Otto von Bismarck
  • B. Kaiser William I
  • C. Metternich
  • D. Giuseppe Mazzini

Answer: A. Otto von Bismarck

Explanation: Prussian Chief Minister Otto von Bismarck was the architect of German unification carried out with the help of the Prussian army and bureaucracy.

10. Allegory of the French Republic was personified as:

  • A. Marianne
  • B. Germania
  • C. Britannia
  • D. Liberty

Answer: A. Marianne

Explanation: Marianne was the female allegory representing the French Republic, underlining the idea of a people's nation.

11. Germania wore a crown of oak leaves because the German oak stands for:

  • A. Heroism
  • B. Peace
  • C. Readiness to fight
  • D. Liberty

Answer: A. Heroism

Explanation: In visual representations, Germania wears a crown of oak leaves as the German oak symbolizes heroism.

12. The Balkan region was under the control of which empire for a long period?

  • A. Ottoman Empire
  • B. Habsburg Empire
  • C. Russian Empire
  • D. British Empire

Answer: A. Ottoman Empire

Explanation: A large part of the Balkans was under the control of the Ottoman Empire before nationalistic tensions escalated in the region.

13. Which European power was NOT involved in the Balkan rivalry prior to World War I?

  • A. Switzerland
  • B. Russia
  • C. Germany
  • D. England

Answer: A. Switzerland

Explanation: Major powers like Russia, Germany, England, and Austro-Hungary were involved in intense rivalries over the Balkans.

14. What happened at the Congress of Vienna in 1815?

  • A. Restoration of Bourbon dynasty in France
  • B. Unification of Italy
  • C. Declaration of German Empire
  • D. Signing of Treaty of Constantinople

Answer: A. Restoration of Bourbon dynasty in France

Explanation: The Congress of Vienna in 1815 restored the Bourbon dynasty to power in France after Napoleon's defeat.

15. What does 'Elle' measure in 19th century Europe?

  • A. Cloth
  • B. Land
  • C. Weight
  • D. Volume

Answer: A. Cloth

Explanation: 'Elle' was the unit used to measure cloth in German territories, varying in length from region to region.

16. Who was famous for leading the 'Expedition of the Thousand' to South Italy?

  • A. Giuseppe Garibaldi
  • B. Count Cavour
  • C. Giuseppe Mazzini
  • D. Victor Emmanuel II

Answer: A. Giuseppe Garibaldi

Explanation: Garibaldi led armed volunteers known as 'Red Shirts' in the Expedition of the Thousand to Southern Italy in 1860.

17. Which Act led to the formation of the 'United Kingdom of Great Britain' in 1707?

  • A. Act of Union
  • B. Reform Act
  • C. Settlement Act
  • D. Independence Act

Answer: A. Act of Union

Explanation: The Act of Union (1707) between England and Scotland resulted in the formation of the 'United Kingdom of Great Britain'.

18. Romanticism was a cultural movement that sought to develop a particular form of:

  • A. Nationalist sentiment
  • B. Economic policy
  • C. Monarchical rule
  • D. Military dominance

Answer: A. Nationalist sentiment

Explanation: Romantic artists and poets focused on emotions, intuition, and mystical feelings to create a sense of a shared collective heritage and nationalist sentiment.

19. Who was Johann Gottfried Herder?

  • A. German philosopher
  • B. French painter
  • C. Italian revolutionary
  • D. British statesman

Answer: A. German philosopher

Explanation: Johann Gottfried Herder was a Romantic German philosopher who claimed true German culture was discovered among the common people ('das Volk').

20. What happened in Frankfurt Parliament in 1848?

  • A. German intellectuals drafted a constitution for a unified Germany under a constitutional monarchy
  • B. Bismarck declared German Unification
  • C. Napoleon was defeated
  • D. Italy was declared a republic

Answer: A. German intellectuals drafted a constitution for a unified Germany under a constitutional monarchy

Explanation: Convened at St. Paul's Church in Frankfurt, 831 elected representatives drafted a constitution for a German nation headed by a monarch subject to parliament.

21. Who rejected the crown offered by the Frankfurt Parliament in 1848?

  • A. Friedrich Wilhelm IV, King of Prussia
  • B. William I
  • C. Victor Emmanuel II
  • D. Louis Philippe

Answer: A. Friedrich Wilhelm IV, King of Prussia

Explanation: Friedrich Wilhelm IV rejected the crown offered by the elected assembly and joined other monarchs to oppose it.

22. What was the main demand of liberal revolutionaries in 1848 across Europe?

  • A. Creation of nation-states based on parliamentary principles
  • B. Complete military rule
  • C. Return to feudalism
  • D. Extension of slavery

Answer: A. Creation of nation-states based on parliamentary principles

Explanation: Liberals demanded creation of nation-states with a constitution, freedom of the press, and freedom of association.

23. In 1806, Napoleon created a confederation of how many German states?

  • A. 39 states
  • B. 25 states
  • C. 52 states
  • D. 12 states

Answer: A. 39 states

Explanation: Napoleon's administrative measures created out of countless small principalities a confederation of 39 states.

24. What does the term 'Utopian' mean in Sorrieu's context?

  • A. A vision of a society so ideal that it is unlikely to actually exist
  • B. A violent political system
  • C. A military alliance
  • D. A realistic economic blueprint

Answer: A. A vision of a society so ideal that it is unlikely to actually exist

Explanation: 'Utopian' refers to an ideal vision of society that is practically too perfect to exist in reality.

25. What was the primary language spoken by the aristocracy in 19th-century Europe?

  • A. French
  • B. English
  • C. German
  • D. Italian

Answer: A. French

Explanation: Members of the landed aristocracy in Europe spoke French for purposes of diplomacy and in high society.

26. Who was forced to flee France during the July Revolution of 1830?

  • A. Bourbon Kings (Charles X)
  • B. Louis Philippe
  • C. Napoleon III
  • D. Louis XVI

Answer: A. Bourbon Kings (Charles X)

Explanation: The Bourbon kings who were restored after 1815 were overthrown in July 1830 by liberal revolutionaries.

27. Which Italian region was ruled by an Italian princely house before unification?

  • A. Sardinia-Piedmont
  • B. Lombardy
  • C. Venetia
  • D. Kingdom of the Two Sicilies

Answer: A. Sardinia-Piedmont

Explanation: Out of seven Italian states, only Sardinia-Piedmont was ruled by an Italian princely house.

28. What was the motto of French Revolution reflected in national symbols?

  • A. Liberty, Equality, Fraternity
  • B. Peace, Order, Progress
  • C. One King, One Nation, One Law
  • D. Freedom and Wealth

Answer: A. Liberty, Equality, Fraternity

Explanation: Liberty, Equality, and Fraternity were the core ideals of the French Revolution.

29. What marked the final inclusion of Ireland into the United Kingdom in 1801?

  • A. Forced incorporation after a failed revolt led by Wolfe Tone
  • B. Peaceful treaty between parliaments
  • C. Referendum held among Irish public
  • D. Conquest by Scottish forces

Answer: A. Forced incorporation after a failed revolt led by Wolfe Tone

Explanation: Ireland was forcibly incorporated into the UK in 1801 after a failed revolt led by Wolfe Tone and his United Irishmen.

30. What was the major outcome of the Unification of Germany in 1871?

  • A. Proclamation of Kaiser William I as German Emperor at Versailles
  • B. Establishment of a French Republic
  • C. Dissolution of the Prussian State
  • D. Treaty of Vienna signing

Answer: A. Proclamation of Kaiser William I as German Emperor at Versailles

Explanation: In January 1871, Prussian King William I was proclaimed German Emperor in a ceremony at Versailles.
