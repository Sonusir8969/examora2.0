Class 9 Annual Exam Preparation Strategy

Key Tips for Class 9 Examinations:

• Physics: Master the equations of motion (v = u + at, s = ut + ½at²), unit conversions, work-energy calculations, and wave formulas (v = λ × ν).

• Chemistry: Practice writing chemical formulas using valency criss-cross method, calculating molecular mass, and atomic models (Rutherford & Bohr).

• Biology: Draw and practice neat, labeled diagrams of Plant vs Animal cells, Neuron (nerve cell), and Plant Tissues (Xylem, Phloem).

• Crop & Food Resources: Focus on differences between Macro and Micro nutrients, Manure vs Fertilizers, and Composite Fish Culture.
