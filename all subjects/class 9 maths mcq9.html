Ch-9: Areas of Parallelograms and Triangles 30 Questions

1. Two congruent figures always have:

  • A. Different areas
  • B. Equal areas
  • C. Double area
  • D. None of these

Answer: B. Equal areas

Explanation: Congruent figures are identical in shape and size, so their areas are always equal.

2. If two figures have equal areas, then they are:

  • A. Always congruent
  • B. Need not be congruent
  • C. Always similar
  • D. Equal in perimeter

Answer: B. Need not be congruent

Explanation: Two figures can have equal areas without having the same shape (e.g., a square and a rectangle of equal area).

3. Parallelograms on the same base and between the same parallels are:

  • A. Equal in area
  • B. Unequal in area
  • C. Always congruent
  • D. Similar

Answer: A. Equal in area

Explanation: Theorem states that parallelograms on the same base and between the same parallel lines are equal in area.

4. The area of a parallelogram is given by the formula:

  • A. Base × Height
  • B. 1/2 × Base × Height
  • C. 2 × Base × Height
  • D. Base + Height

Answer: A. Base × Height

Explanation: Area of a parallelogram = base × corresponding height (altitude).

5. If a triangle and a parallelogram are on the same base and between the same parallels, the area of the triangle is ________ the area of the parallelogram.

  • A. Equal to
  • B. Half of
  • C. Double of
  • D. One-third of

Answer: B. Half of

Explanation: Area(Triangle) = 1/2 × Area(Parallelogram) when both lie on the same base and between the same parallels.

6. Triangles on the same base and between the same parallels are:

  • A. Equal in area
  • B. Congruent
  • C. Unequal in area
  • D. Similar

Answer: A. Equal in area

Explanation: Theorem states that triangles on the same base (or equal bases) and between the same parallels are equal in area.

7. The median of a triangle divides it into two triangles of:

  • A. Equal perimeter
  • B. Equal area
  • C. Congruent shapes
  • D. Unequal area

Answer: B. Equal area

Explanation: A median of a triangle divides it into two triangles of equal areas.

8. Area of a triangle is calculated using which formula?

  • A. Base × Height
  • B. 1/2 × Base × Height
  • C. 2 × (Base + Height)
  • D. Base / Height

Answer: B. 1/2 × Base × Height

Explanation: Standard formula for area of a triangle is 1/2 × base × altitude.

9. ABCD is a parallelogram. If area(ABCD) = 40 cm², then area(ΔABC) is equal to:

  • A. 40 cm²
  • B. 20 cm²
  • C. 80 cm²
  • D. 10 cm²

Answer: B. 20 cm²

Explanation: Diagonal AC divides parallelogram ABCD into two triangles of equal area. Area(ΔABC) = 1/2 × 40 = 20 cm².

10. In ΔABC, AD is a median. If area(ΔABC) = 30 cm², then area(ΔABD) is:

  • A. 30 cm²
  • B. 15 cm²
  • C. 60 cm²
  • D. 10 cm²

Answer: B. 15 cm²

Explanation: A median divides the triangle into two parts of equal area. Area(ΔABD) = 1/2 × 30 = 15 cm².

11. If a rectangle and a parallelogram stand on the same base and between the same parallels, their areas are in the ratio:

  • A. 1 : 2
  • B. 2 : 1
  • C. 1 : 1
  • D. 1 : 4

Answer: C. 1 : 1

Explanation: A rectangle is also a parallelogram. Parallelograms on the same base and between same parallels have equal areas, so ratio is 1 : 1.

12. If the base of a parallelogram is 8 cm and its corresponding altitude is 5 cm, its area is:

  • A. 20 cm²
  • B. 40 cm²
  • C. 13 cm²
  • D. 80 cm²

Answer: B. 40 cm²

Explanation: Area = Base × Altitude = 8 cm × 5 cm = 40 cm².

13. In ΔABC, D, E, F are mid-points of sides BC, CA, and AB respectively. The ratio of area(ΔDEF) to area(ΔABC) is:

  • A. 1 : 2
  • B. 1 : 3
  • C. 1 : 4
  • D. 1 : 8

Answer: C. 1 : 4

Explanation: The four smaller triangles formed by mid-points are equal in area. Hence, Area(ΔDEF) = 1/4 × Area(ΔABC).

14. Parallelogram ABCD and ΔABE lie on the same base AB and between same parallels AB and CD. If area(ΔABE) = 24 cm², find area(ABCD):

  • A. 12 cm²
  • B. 24 cm²
  • C. 48 cm²
  • D. 36 cm²

Answer: C. 48 cm²

Explanation: Area(ABCD) = 2 × Area(ΔABE) = 2 × 24 = 48 cm².

15. If the area of a parallelogram is 60 cm² and its base is 10 cm, then its altitude is:

  • A. 6 cm
  • B. 12 cm
  • C. 30 cm
  • D. 5 cm

Answer: A. 6 cm

Explanation: Altitude = Area / Base = 60 / 10 = 6 cm.

16. The area of a rhombus with diagonals d₁ and d₂ is given by:

  • A. d₁ × d₂
  • B. 1/2 × d₁ × d₂
  • C. 2 × d₁ × d₂
  • D. 1/4 × d₁ × d₂

Answer: B. 1/2 × d₁ × d₂

Explanation: Area of rhombus = 1/2 × product of diagonals = 1/2 × d₁ × d₂.

17. Two triangles having equal areas and a common base on the same side lie between:

  • A. Perpendicular lines
  • B. Intersecting lines
  • C. Same parallel lines
  • D. Concentric circles

Answer: C. Same parallel lines

Explanation: Triangles with equal areas on the same base lie between the same parallels (converse theorem).

18. The area of a trapezium is given by:

  • A. 1/2 × (sum of parallel sides) × height
  • B. (sum of parallel sides) × height
  • C. 1/2 × (product of parallel sides) × height
  • D. 2 × (sum of parallel sides) × height

Answer: A. 1/2 × (sum of parallel sides) × height

Explanation: Area of a trapezium = 1/2 × (a + b) × h, where a and b are parallel sides and h is distance between them.

19. In ΔABC, E is the mid-point of median AD. Then area(ΔBED) is equal to:

  • A. 1/2 area(ΔABC)
  • B. 1/3 area(ΔABC)
  • C. 1/4 area(ΔABC)
  • D. 1/8 area(ΔABC)

Answer: C. 1/4 area(ΔABC)

Explanation: Area(ΔABD) = 1/2 Area(ΔABC). Since BE is median of ΔABD, Area(ΔBED) = 1/2 Area(ΔABD) = 1/4 Area(ΔABC).

20. The diagonals AC and BD of a parallelogram ABCD intersect at O. Then area(ΔAOB) =

  • A. area(ΔBOC)
  • B. 1/4 area(ABCD)
  • C. Both A and B
  • D. None of these

Answer: C. Both A and B

Explanation: Diagonals of a parallelogram divide it into four triangles of equal area, so area(ΔAOB) = area(ΔBOC) = 1/4 area(ABCD).

21. If base of a triangle is doubled and altitude is halved, its area will:

  • A. Double
  • B. Become half
  • C. Remain unchanged
  • D. Quadruple

Answer: C. Remain unchanged

Explanation: New Area = 1/2 × (2b) × (h/2) = 1/2 × b × h = Original Area.

22. Parallelogram ABCD and rectangle ABEF are on the same base AB. Then:

  • A. area(ABCD) > area(ABEF)
  • B. area(ABCD) < area(ABEF)
  • C. area(ABCD) = area(ABEF)
  • D. No relation

Answer: C. area(ABCD) = area(ABEF)

Explanation: Both are parallelograms on the same base AB and between same parallel lines, hence equal in area.

23. In a triangle ABC, if altitude h = 6 cm and area = 24 cm², find base b.

  • A. 4 cm
  • B. 8 cm
  • C. 12 cm
  • D. 16 cm

Answer: B. 8 cm

Explanation: Area = 1/2 × b × h ⇒ 24 = 1/2 × b × 6 ⇒ b = 8 cm.

24. ABCD is a trapezium with AB || CD. Diagonal AC and BD intersect at O. Then:

  • A. area(ΔAOD) = area(ΔBOC)
  • B. area(ΔAOB) = area(ΔCOD)
  • C. area(ΔAOD) = area(ΔAOB)
  • D. None of these

Answer: A. area(ΔAOD) = area(ΔBOC)

Explanation: Area(ΔADC) = Area(ΔBDC) (same base CD, same parallels). Subtracting area(ΔDOC) from both gives area(ΔAOD) = area(ΔBOC).

25. If ABCD is a square of side 6 cm, what is the area of ΔABC?

  • A. 36 cm²
  • B. 18 cm²
  • C. 12 cm²
  • D. 9 cm²

Answer: B. 18 cm²

Explanation: Area of square ABCD = 6² = 36 cm². Diagonal AC bisects area, so Area(ΔABC) = 36 / 2 = 18 cm².

26. Altitude of a triangle divides it into two right triangles of:

  • A. Always equal areas
  • B. Equal areas only if triangle is isosceles/equilateral
  • C. Always congruent
  • D. Never equal areas

Answer: B. Equal areas only if triangle is isosceles/equilateral

Explanation: In an isosceles or equilateral triangle, the altitude is also the median, dividing it into two equal area triangles.

27. If parallel sides of a trapezium are 10 cm and 14 cm and height is 5 cm, its area is:

  • A. 60 cm²
  • B. 120 cm²
  • C. 35 cm²
  • D. 70 cm²

Answer: A. 60 cm²

Explanation: Area = 1/2 × (10 + 14) × 5 = 1/2 × 24 × 5 = 60 cm².

28. Two parallelograms have equal bases and lie between same parallel lines. The ratio of their areas is:

  • A. 1 : 2
  • B. 1 : 1
  • C. 2 : 1
  • D. 1 : 3

Answer: B. 1 : 1

Explanation: Parallelograms on equal bases and between same parallels have equal areas, so ratio is 1 : 1.

29. In ΔABC, D is mid-point of BC and E is mid-point of AD. Then area(ΔAEC) =

  • A. 1/2 area(ΔABC)
  • B. 1/4 area(ΔABC)
  • C. 1/3 area(ΔABC)
  • D. 1/8 area(ΔABC)

Answer: B. 1/4 area(ΔABC)

Explanation: Area(ΔADC) = 1/2 Area(ΔABC). CE is median of ΔADC, so Area(ΔAEC) = 1/2 Area(ΔADC) = 1/4 Area(ΔABC).

30. Can two figures be congruent if their areas are unequal?

  • A. Yes
  • B. No
  • C. Sometimes
  • D. Depends on shape

Answer: B. No

Explanation: Congruent figures must be identical in all respects, including area. Therefore, two figures with unequal areas can never be congruent.
