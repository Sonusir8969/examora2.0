Ch-11: Power Sharing 30 Questions

1. What percentage of Belgium's total population lives in the Flemish region and speaks Dutch?

  • A. 59%
  • B. 40%
  • C. 80%
  • D. 20%

Answer: A. 59%

Explanation: Of the country's total population, 59 per cent lives in the Flemish region and speaks the Dutch language.

2. In Brussels, the capital city of Belgium, what percentage of people speak French?

  • A. 80%
  • B. 20%
  • C. 59%
  • D. 40%

Answer: A. 80%

Explanation: In Brussels, 80 per cent of the people speak French while 20 per cent are Dutch-speaking, which was inverse to the national ratio.

3. Which ethnic community in Sri Lanka constitutes the majority, making up about 74% of the population?

  • A. Sinhala-speakers
  • B. Tamil-speakers
  • C. Christians
  • D. Muslims

Answer: A. Sinhala-speakers

Explanation: The major social groups in Sri Lanka are the Sinhala-speakers (74 per cent) and the Tamil-speakers (18 per cent).

4. An Act was passed in Sri Lanka in 1956 to recognize which language as the sole official language, disregarding Tamil?

  • A. Sinhala
  • B. English
  • C. Hindi
  • D. Pali

Answer: A. Sinhala

Explanation: In 1956, an Act was passed to recognize Sinhala as the only official language, thereby disregarding Tamil.

5. Which religion was fostered and protected by the government under the new constitution of Sri Lanka?

  • A. Buddhism
  • B. Hinduism
  • C. Islam
  • D. Christianity

Answer: A. Buddhism

Explanation: A new constitution stipulated that the state should protect and foster Buddhism, which was practiced by most Sinhalas.

6. A belief that the majority community should be able to rule a country in whichever way it wants, by disregarding the wishes and needs of the minority, is known as:

  • A. Majoritarianism
  • B. Federalism
  • C. Secularism
  • D. Socialism

Answer: A. Majoritarianism

Explanation: Majoritarianism is a concept where the majority community asserts dominance over minorities, leading to alienation as seen in Sri Lanka.

7. How many times was the Constitution of Belgium amended between 1970 and 1993 to accommodate cultural diversity?

  • A. 4 times
  • B. 2 times
  • C. 5 times
  • D. 3 times

Answer: A. 4 times

Explanation: Between 1970 and 1993, the Belgian leaders amended their constitution four times so as to work out an arrangement that would allow everyone to live together.

8. What unique third level of government was created in Belgium to handle cultural, educational, and language-related issues?

  • A. Community Government
  • B. Municipal Corporation
  • C. Provincial Council
  • D. Regional Assembly

Answer: A. Community Government

Explanation: Apart from the Central and State governments, Belgium established a 'Community Government' elected by people belonging to one language community.

9. Which city was chosen as the headquarters when many European countries came together to form the European Union?

  • A. Brussels
  • B. Paris
  • C. Geneva
  • D. Berlin

Answer: A. Brussels

Explanation: When many countries of Europe came together to form the European Union, Brussels was chosen as its headquarters due to its exemplary power-sharing model.

10. Power sharing among different organs of government, such as the Legislature, Executive, and Judiciary, is called:

  • A. Horizontal distribution of power
  • B. Vertical distribution of power
  • C. Federal distribution of power
  • D. Community power sharing

Answer: A. Horizontal distribution of power

Explanation: It is called horizontal distribution because it allows different organs of government placed at the same level to exercise different powers.

11. A system of 'Checks and Balances' is associated with which form of power sharing?

  • A. Horizontal distribution of power
  • B. Vertical division of power
  • C. Power sharing among pressure groups
  • D. Coalition government

Answer: A. Horizontal distribution of power

Explanation: Since each organ checks the others, horizontal distribution results in a balance of power, creating a system of checks and balances.

12. Power sharing involving different levels of government, such as Central, State, and Local governments, is known as:

  • A. Vertical division of power
  • B. Horizontal distribution of power
  • C. Separation of powers
  • D. Checks and balances

Answer: A. Vertical division of power

Explanation: Power shared among governments at different levels (higher and lower) is called vertical division of power, or a federal system.

13. Which reason emphasizes that power sharing is good because it helps to reduce the possibility of conflict between social groups?

  • A. Prudential reason
  • B. Moral reason
  • C. Legal reason
  • D. Historical reason

Answer: A. Prudential reason

Explanation: Prudential reasons stress that power sharing brings out better practical outcomes, such as avoiding civil strife and ensuring political stability.

14. Which reason stresses that power sharing is the very spirit of democracy?

  • A. Moral reason
  • B. Prudential reason
  • C. Political reason
  • D. Economic reason

Answer: A. Moral reason

Explanation: Moral reasons emphasize the intrinsic valuable nature of power sharing as fundamental to democratic governance.

15. When power is shared among two or more political parties forming an alliance to run the government, it is called a:

  • A. Coalition government
  • B. Unitary government
  • C. Community government
  • D. Absolute monarchy

Answer: A. Coalition government

Explanation: When an alliance of different political parties gets elected and forms a joint government, it is called a Coalition Government.

16. What demand was made by Sri Lankan Tamils to protect their political rights and identity?

  • A. An independent Tamil Eelam (state) in northern and eastern Sri Lanka
  • B. Complete expulsion of Sinhalas
  • C. Conversion to Buddhism
  • D. Unification with India

Answer: A. An independent Tamil Eelam (state) in northern and eastern Sri Lanka

Explanation: By the 1980s, several political organizations were formed demanding an independent Tamil Eelam (state) in northern and eastern parts of Sri Lanka.

17. Which continent is Belgium located in?

  • A. Europe
  • B. Asia
  • C. North America
  • D. Africa

Answer: A. Europe

Explanation: Belgium is a small country in Europe, smaller in area than the state of Haryana in India.

18. Which of the following countries does NOT share borders with Belgium?

  • A. Italy
  • B. France
  • C. Netherlands
  • D. Germany

Answer: A. Italy

Explanation: Belgium shares borders with France, the Netherlands, Germany, and Luxembourg (it does not border Italy).

19. What percentage of the population in Belgium lives in the Wallonia region and speaks French?

  • A. 40%
  • B. 59%
  • C. 1%
  • D. 80%

Answer: A. 40%

Explanation: Another 40 per cent of Belgium's population lives in the Wallonia region and speaks French.

20. What percentage of Belgians speak German?

  • A. 1%
  • B. 5%
  • C. 10%
  • D. 18%

Answer: A. 1%

Explanation: Remaining 1 per cent of the Belgians speak German.

21. In Sri Lanka, the Sri Lankan Tamils are concentrated mainly in which regions of the country?

  • A. North and East
  • B. South and West
  • C. Central and South
  • D. West and North

Answer: A. North and East

Explanation: Sri Lankan Tamils are concentrated in the north and east of the island country.

22. What are the descendants of Indian laborers who migrated to Sri Lanka during the colonial period called?

  • A. Indian Tamils
  • B. Sri Lankan Tamils
  • C. Estate Sinhalas
  • D. Anglo-Indians

Answer: A. Indian Tamils

Explanation: Tamils whose forefathers came from India as plantation workers during the colonial period are called 'Indian Tamils'.

23. In Belgium, both Dutch and French-speaking ministers are equal in number in which government body?

  • A. Central Government
  • B. Community Council
  • C. European Parliament
  • D. Municipal Court

Answer: A. Central Government

Explanation: The Constitution prescribes that the number of Dutch and French-speaking ministers shall be equal in the Central Government.

24. Reserved constituencies for women and weaker sections in India's parliament and assemblies are an example of power sharing among:

  • A. Different social groups
  • B. Different organs of government
  • C. Different levels of government
  • D. Political parties and pressure groups

Answer: A. Different social groups

Explanation: Reserved constituencies give space in the government and administration to diverse social groups who otherwise would feel alienated.

25. When was Sri Lanka emerged as an independent country?

  • A. 1948
  • B. 1947
  • C. 1950
  • D. 1956

Answer: A. 1948

Explanation: Sri Lanka emerged as an independent country in 1948, one year after India achieved independence.

26. Modern democracies maintain power sharing because it:

  • A. Sustains political stability and democracy
  • B. Concentrates power in a single authority
  • C. Delays decision-making endlessly without benefit
  • D. Encourages majority domination over all minorities

Answer: A. Sustains political stability and democracy

Explanation: Power sharing ensures stability of political order and respects the diverse aspirations of all citizens.

27. Judges are appointed by the executive, but they can check the functioning of executive or laws made by the legislatures. This system is known as:

  • A. System of checks and balances
  • B. Judicial supremacy
  • C. Direct democracy
  • D. Federal decentralization

Answer: A. System of checks and balances

Explanation: Even though judges are appointed by executive, they can check executive and legislative acts, forming a system of checks and balances.

28. Which of the following is NOT a form of power sharing?

  • A. Dictatorship where all decisions are taken by one ruler
  • B. Power shared among different organs of government
  • C. Power shared among governments at different levels
  • D. Power shared among political parties and pressure groups

Answer: A. Dictatorship where all decisions are taken by one ruler

Explanation: In a dictatorship, power is concentrated in one individual, which is the direct opposite of power sharing.

29. What percentage of Sri Lanka's population are Christians, including both Tamil and Sinhala speakers?

  • A. 7%
  • B. 18%
  • C. 25%
  • D. 12%

Answer: A. 7%

Explanation: About 7 per cent of Sri Lanka's population are Christians, who are both Tamil and Sinhala.

30. Why was the Belgian model of power sharing considered successful despite being complex?

  • A. It avoided civic strife and prevented partition of the country on linguistic lines
  • B. It forced all minorities to adopt Dutch culture
  • C. It handed over all control to European Union delegates
  • D. It converted Belgium into a centralized monarchy

Answer: A. It avoided civic strife and prevented partition of the country on linguistic lines

Explanation: Though complex, the Belgian accommodation model effectively prevented ethnic conflict and maintained national unity.
