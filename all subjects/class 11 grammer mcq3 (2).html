Grammar-1: Tenses and Modals 25 Questions

1. The sun _______ in the east and sets in the west.

  • A. rises
  • B. rising
  • C. rose
  • D. has risen

Answer: A. rises

Explanation: Universal truths and general facts are always expressed using the Simple Present Tense.

2. I _______ football since 2 o'clock this afternoon.

  • A. have been playing
  • B. am playing
  • C. played
  • D. was playing

Answer: A. have been playing

Explanation: The time expression 'since 2 o'clock' indicates an action that started in the past and is still continuing, requiring Present Perfect Continuous Tense.

3. She _______ her homework before her mother arrived.

  • A. had finished
  • B. finishes
  • C. has finished
  • D. will finish

Answer: A. had finished

Explanation: When two actions occurred in the past, the earlier completed action takes Past Perfect Tense ('had finished').

4. Look! The boy _______ across the busy street.

  • A. is running
  • B. runs
  • C. ran
  • D. has run

Answer: A. is running

Explanation: An action happening right now at the time of speaking requires Present Continuous Tense.

5. By next year, Rohan _______ his medical degree.

  • A. will have completed
  • B. completes
  • C. was completing
  • D. completed

Answer: A. will have completed

Explanation: 'By next year' denotes a deadline in the future, requiring Future Perfect Tense ('will have + V3').

6. We _______ respect our elders and teachers.

  • A. ought to
  • B. may
  • C. can
  • D. might

Answer: A. ought to

Explanation: 'Ought to' is used to express moral obligation and duty.

7. _______ I come in, Sir?

  • A. May
  • B. Can
  • C. Might
  • D. Must

Answer: A. May

Explanation: 'May' is the correct formal modal verb used for seeking permission.

8. It is very dark outside; it _______ rain tonight.

  • A. may
  • B. must
  • C. should
  • D. would

Answer: A. may

Explanation: 'May' expresses a good possibility based on current evidence (dark outside).

9. He _______ lift this heavy iron chest when he was young.

  • A. could
  • B. can
  • C. may
  • D. should

Answer: A. could

Explanation: 'Could' is used to express general physical ability in the past.

10. You _______ follow the traffic signals; otherwise, you will be fined.

  • A. must
  • B. might
  • C. can
  • D. could

Answer: A. must

Explanation: 'Must' expresses legal compulsion, rule, or strong necessity.

11. Water _______ at 100 degrees Celsius.

  • A. boils
  • B. boiled
  • C. is boiling
  • D. has boiled

Answer: A. boils

Explanation: Scientific facts are stated in Simple Present Tense.

12. When I arrived at the station, the train _______ already left.

  • A. had
  • B. has
  • C. was
  • D. is

Answer: A. had

Explanation: The train leaving happened before the arrival, so Past Perfect ('had left') is correct.

13. Work hard lest you _______ fail in the examination.

  • A. should
  • B. would
  • C. might
  • D. can

Answer: A. should

Explanation: The conjunction 'lest' is always followed by the modal verb 'should'.

14. She _______ speak five languages fluently.

  • A. can
  • B. must
  • C. might
  • D. ought

Answer: A. can

Explanation: 'Can' denotes present capacity or mental skill/ability.

15. My uncle _______ to Delhi yesterday evening.

  • A. went
  • B. goes
  • C. has gone
  • D. had gone

Answer: A. went

Explanation: Past time adverbs like 'yesterday' demand Simple Past Tense ('went').

16. _______ God bless you with health and prosperity!

  • A. May
  • B. Can
  • C. Must
  • D. Should

Answer: A. May

Explanation: Optative sentences expressing wishes or prayers start with 'May'.

17. While he was reading a book, his friend _______ into the room.

  • A. came
  • B. comes
  • C. was coming
  • D. had come

Answer: A. came

Explanation: A single interrupting past event while another action was ongoing takes Simple Past Tense ('came').

18. You _______ buy a ticket before entering the cinema hall.

  • A. must
  • B. may
  • C. might
  • D. could

Answer: A. must

Explanation: Buying a ticket is a mandatory requirement/rule, expressed by 'must'.

19. If it rains, we _______ cancel the picnic.

  • A. will
  • B. would
  • C. had
  • D. were

Answer: A. will

Explanation: In First Conditional sentences (If + Simple Present), the main clause uses Simple Future ('will + V1').

20. He _______ used to smoke, but he gave it up last year.

  • A. used to
  • B. ought to
  • C. should
  • D. must

Answer: A. used to

Explanation: 'Used to' expresses a past habit or routine that no longer exists in the present.

21. They _______ for the results for two hours now.

  • A. have been waiting
  • B. are waiting
  • C. waited
  • D. were waiting

Answer: A. have been waiting

Explanation: 'For two hours now' indicates Present Perfect Continuous Tense ('have been waiting').

22. There are no clouds in the sky, so it _______ rain today.

  • A. might not
  • B. must
  • C. can
  • D. should

Answer: A. might not

Explanation: 'Might not' indicates weak or remote impossibility based on clear evidence (no clouds).

23. She told me that she _______ help me the following day.

  • A. would
  • B. will
  • C. can
  • D. may

Answer: A. would

Explanation: In indirect speech introduced by a past tense verb ('told'), 'will' changes to 'would'.

24. Drivers _______ slow down when approaching a school zone.

  • A. should
  • B. might
  • C. could
  • D. would

Answer: A. should

Explanation: 'Should' is used to express advice, recommendation, or sensible obligation.

25. Neither of the two boys _______ present in the class yesterday.

  • A. was
  • B. were
  • C. are
  • D. have been

Answer: A. was

Explanation: 'Neither' is singular and takes a singular past verb ('was') when referring to 'yesterday'.
