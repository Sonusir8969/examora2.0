Eng-06: The Voice of the Rain (Poem) 25 Questions

1. Who is the poet of the poem 'The Voice of the Rain'?

  • A. Walt Whitman
  • B. Ted Hughes
  • C. Markus Natten
  • D. Shirley Toulson

Answer: A. Walt Whitman

Explanation: The poem 'The Voice of the Rain' is composed by the American poet Walt Whitman.

2. Whom does the poet ask "And who art thou?" in the poem?

  • A. The soft-falling shower
  • B. The wind
  • C. The sea
  • D. The clouds

Answer: A. The soft-falling shower

Explanation: The poet asks "And who art thou?" (Who are you?) to the gentle falling rain shower.

3. What was strange about the response to the poet's question?

  • A. The rain gave an answer to the poet
  • B. The rain stopped falling immediately
  • C. Thunder started making noise
  • D. The rain turned into snow

Answer: A. The rain gave an answer to the poet

Explanation: The poet says "Which, strange to tell, gave me an answer," as rain personified itself and spoke back.

4. How does the rain identify itself in the poem?

  • A. "I am the Poem of Earth"
  • B. "I am the Daughter of Heaven"
  • C. "I am the Life of Nature"
  • D. "I am the Gift of Clouds"

Answer: A. "I am the Poem of Earth"

Explanation: The rain calls itself "the Poem of Earth" in response to the poet's question.

5. Where does the rain originate from before rising upward?

  • A. From the land and the bottomless sea
  • B. From the mountain peaks
  • C. From the sky directly
  • D. From the autumn leaves

Answer: A. From the land and the bottomless sea

Explanation: Rain rises endlessly in the form of vapor from the land and deep water bodies.

6. What word is used in the poem to show that the origin of rain cannot be touched or caught?

  • A. Impalpable
  • B. Eternal
  • C. Vagabond
  • D. Recked

Answer: A. Impalpable

Explanation: 'Impalpable' means something that cannot be touched or physically grabbed (like invisible water vapor).

7. Where does the rain rise to in the form of vapor?

  • A. Upward to heaven
  • B. Towards the moon
  • C. Into deep underground caves
  • D. Over the ocean waves

Answer: A. Upward to heaven

Explanation: The poem mentions "Upward to heaven, whence, vaguely form'd, altogether changed, and yet the same."

8. What happens to the rain in heaven before it falls back down?

  • A. Its shape changes into clouds, but its essence remains the same
  • B. It freezes completely into ice
  • C. It disappears forever into the atmosphere
  • D. It turns into bright light

Answer: A. Its shape changes into clouds, but its essence remains the same

Explanation: Though vapor turns into clouds ("altogether changed"), its elemental structure as water remains "yet the same".

9. What is the main purpose of the rain descending to the Earth?

  • A. To lave (wash) the droughts, atomies, and dust-layers of the globe
  • B. To create floods in rivers
  • C. To destroy crops
  • D. To cool down the sky

Answer: A. To lave (wash) the droughts, atomies, and dust-layers of the globe

Explanation: Rain descends to wash away dry areas, tiny particles, and dust resting on the earth.

10. What does the word 'lave' mean in the poem?

  • A. Wash or bathe
  • B. Destroy or ruin
  • C. Create or shape
  • D. Love and cherish

Answer: A. Wash or bathe

Explanation: 'Lave' is an archaic poetic word meaning to wash, clean, or soak with water.

11. What would remain hidden and unborn without rain on Earth?

  • A. The seeds inside the earth
  • B. The rocks on mountains
  • C. The fish in the sea
  • D. The birds in the sky

Answer: A. The seeds inside the earth

Explanation: "And all that in them without me were seeds only, latent, unborn" highlights rain's role in germination.

12. What does the rain continuously give back to its own origin?

  • A. Life, purity, and beauty
  • B. Mud and dirty water
  • C. Cold atmosphere
  • D. Darkness and shade

Answer: A. Life, purity, and beauty

Explanation: "For song... I give life back to my own origin, And make pure and beautify it."

13. What scientific process is described poetically in 'The Voice of the Rain'?

  • A. Water Cycle
  • B. Photosynthesis
  • C. Nitrogen Cycle
  • D. Condensation of gases

Answer: A. Water Cycle

Explanation: The journey of rain rising as vapor, forming clouds, and descending as rainfall is the natural Water Cycle.

14. What poetic device is used in "I am the Poem of Earth"?

  • A. Metaphor
  • B. Simile
  • C. Hyperbole
  • D. Oxymoron

Answer: A. Metaphor

Explanation: Comparing rain directly to a poem without using 'like' or 'as' is a metaphor.

15. Which poetic device is used when the rain speaks to the poet?

  • A. Personification
  • B. Alliteration
  • C. Onomatopoeia
  • D. Irony

Answer: A. Personification

Explanation: Giving non-human rain a voice and human speech abilities is personification.

16. What comparison is made in the last two lines placed inside brackets in the poem?

  • A. Rain is compared to a song
  • B. Rain is compared to a river
  • C. Rain is compared to a story
  • D. Rain is compared to a flower

Answer: A. Rain is compared to a song

Explanation: The poet compares the journey of rain to a song issued from its birthplace and returning with love.

17. What does "reck'd or unreck'd" mean in the last line of the poem?

  • A. Whether cared for or not cared for
  • B. Whether wrecked or repaired
  • C. Whether heard or ignored
  • D. Whether hot or cold

Answer: A. Whether cared for or not cared for

Explanation: 'Reck'd or unreck'd' means whether people notice and appreciate the rain/song or not, it completes its loving duty.

18. Why are the last two lines put inside brackets (parenthesis)?

  • A. They contain poet's own commentary/reflection, separate from the rain's direct words
  • B. They were written by a different author
  • C. They are grammatical mistakes
  • D. They are extra non-essential details

Answer: A. They contain poet's own commentary/reflection, separate from the rain's direct words

Explanation: The bracketed lines represent the poet's general reflection comparing rain's cycle to music.

19. What does the word 'latent' mean in the context of the poem?

  • A. Hidden, inactive, or un-germinated
  • B. Growing fast
  • C. Dead forever
  • D. Visible and clear

Answer: A. Hidden, inactive, or un-germinated

Explanation: 'Latent' seeds are dormant or hidden under the soil awaiting rain water to sprout.

20. Which figure of speech is present in "soft-falling shower"?

  • A. Alliteration
  • B. Simile
  • C. Oxymoron
  • D. Anaphora

Answer: A. Alliteration

Explanation: The repetition of the starting 's' consonant sound in "soft-falling shower" is alliteration.

21. What happens when the rain returns to its birthplace with love?

  • A. It purifies and beautifies its own origin
  • B. It vanishes into thin air
  • C. It causes destruction
  • D. It creates heavy storms

Answer: A. It purifies and beautifies its own origin

Explanation: The rain cleanses and beautifies Earth, giving new life back to the place it originated from.

22. What form of poetry is 'The Voice of the Rain' written in?

  • A. Free verse
  • B. Sonnet
  • C. Ballad
  • D. Haiku

Answer: A. Free verse

Explanation: Walt Whitman wrote the poem in free verse without a fixed rhyming scheme or strict rhythm.

23. How does the rain describe its movement upward?

  • A. Eternal and impalpable
  • B. Fast and stormy
  • C. Heavy and noisy
  • D. Dark and fearful

Answer: A. Eternal and impalpable

Explanation: The poem begins: "Eternal I rise impalpable out of the land..."

24. What does the word 'atomies' mean in the poem?

  • A. Tiny particles or dust specs
  • B. Atomic energy
  • C. Ocean creatures
  • D. Small seeds

Answer: A. Tiny particles or dust specs

Explanation: 'Atomies' refers to extremely small particles or dust specks washed by the rain.

25. What is the overall tone of the poem 'The Voice of the Rain'?

  • A. Reflective, appreciative, and soothing
  • B. Angry and melancholic
  • C. Sarcastic and humorous
  • D. Scared and anxious

Answer: A. Reflective, appreciative, and soothing

Explanation: The dialogue between poet and rain carries a gentle, reflective, and nature-appreciating tone.
