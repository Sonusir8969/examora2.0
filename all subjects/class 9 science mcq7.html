Ch-7: Diversity in Living Organisms 10 Questions

1. Who proposed the Five Kingdom Classification system of living organisms?

  • A. Carl Linnaeus
  • B. Robert Whittaker
  • C. Ernst Haeckel
  • D. Carl Woese

Answer: B. Robert Whittaker

Explanation: R.H. Whittaker proposed the five kingdom system in 1969: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, and Animalia.

2. Organisms belonging to Kingdom Monera are characterized by being:

  • A. Unicellular Eukaryotes
  • B. Multicellular Eukaryotes
  • C. Unicellular Prokaryotes
  • D. Multicellular Prokaryotes

Answer: C. Unicellular Prokaryotes

Explanation: Kingdom Monera consists of unicellular organisms that lack a defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles (e.g., bacteria, blue-green algae).

3. Which group of plants is known as the "Amphibians of the Plant Kingdom"?

  • A. Thallophyta
  • B. Bryophyta
  • C. Pteridophyta
  • D. Gymnosperms

Answer: B. Bryophyta

Explanation: Bryophytes (like Moss and Marchantia) live in soil but require water for sexual reproduction, making them amphibians of the plant world.

4. Plants with naked seeds that do not produce flowers belong to which group?

  • A. Angiosperms
  • B. Gymnosperms
  • C. Pteridophytes
  • D. Bryophytes

Answer: B. Gymnosperms

Explanation: 'Gymnos' means naked and 'sperma' means seed. Gymnosperms bear naked seeds, usually in cones (e.g., Pinus, Cycas).

5. Which phylum is the largest group in the Animal Kingdom, characterized by jointed legs?

  • A. Annelida
  • B. Arthropoda
  • C. Mollusca
  • D. Echinodermata

Answer: B. Arthropoda

Explanation: Arthropoda is the largest phylum in animalia, featuring bilateral symmetry, segmented bodies, and jointed appendages (e.g., insects, spiders, crabs).

6. Pores/holes all over the body are a characteristic feature of which animal phylum?

  • A. Porifera
  • B. Coelenterata
  • C. Platyhelminthes
  • D. Nematoda

Answer: A. Porifera

Explanation: Porifera means 'pore-bearing'. These non-motile animals (like Sponges, Sycon) have pores leading to a canal system for circulating water.

7. Which class of vertebrates has a three-chambered heart (except crocodiles) and lays eggs in water?

  • A. Pisces
  • B. Amphibia
  • C. Aves
  • D. Mammalia

Answer: B. Amphibia

Explanation: Amphibians (frogs, toads, salamanders) have a 3-chambered heart, cold-blooded metabolism, and adapt to both land and aquatic environments.

8. Who is known as the "Father of Taxonomy" for introducing binomial nomenclature?

  • A. Charles Darwin
  • B. Gregor Mendel
  • C. Carl Linnaeus
  • D. Aristotle

Answer: C. Carl Linnaeus

Explanation: Carl Linnaeus devised the system of binomial nomenclature (Genus + Species) and laying the groundwork for modern taxonomy.

9. Flatworms (like Planaria and Tapeworm) belong to which phylum?

  • A. Nematoda
  • B. Platyhelminthes
  • C. Annelida
  • D. Coelenterata

Answer: B. Platyhelminthes

Explanation: Platyhelminthes consists of dorsoventrally flattened organisms commonly referred to as flatworms.

10. Warm-blooded animals with a four-chambered heart and mammary glands belong to class:

  • A. Reptilia
  • B. Amphibia
  • C. Aves
  • D. Mammalia

Answer: D. Mammalia

Explanation: Mammals are warm-blooded, have 4-chambered hearts, produce milk for young via mammary glands, and typically have hair on skin.
