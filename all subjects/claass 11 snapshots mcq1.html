Chapter-01: The Summer of the Beautiful White Horse 25 Questions

1. Who is the author of the story 'The Summer of the Beautiful White Horse'?

  • A. William Saroyan
  • B. Marga Minco
  • C. A.R. Williams
  • D. Gordon Cook

Answer: A. William Saroyan

Explanation: 'The Summer of the Beautiful White Horse' is written by Armenian-American author William Saroyan.

2. Who are the two main boys in the story?

  • A. Aram and Mourad
  • B. John and Byro
  • C. Khosrove and Aram
  • D. Uncle and Nephew

Answer: A. Aram and Mourad

Explanation: Aram (9 years old) and Mourad (13 years old) are the two primary Armenian cousins in the story.

3. Which tribe did Aram and Mourad belong to?

  • A. Garoghlanian tribe
  • B. Assyrian tribe
  • C. Kurdish tribe
  • D. Nomad tribe

Answer: A. Garoghlanian tribe

Explanation: They belonged to the Garoghlanian family/tribe, famous for eleven centuries for honesty and integrity.

4. For what hallmark was the Garoghlanian tribe famous?

  • A. Honesty and trust
  • B. Wealth and luxury
  • C. Horse racing
  • D. Cleverness and trickery

Answer: A. Honesty and trust

Explanation: The Garoghlanian family was renowned for being honest, proud, and uncompromising on truth.

5. How old was Aram when the incident of the white horse took place?

  • A. Nine years old
  • B. Thirteen years old
  • C. Eleven years old
  • D. Seven years old

Answer: A. Nine years old

Explanation: Aram states: "I was nine and the world was full of every imaginable kind of grandeur..."

6. What time of the morning did Mourad come to Aram's house with the white horse?

  • A. 4:00 am (four in the morning)
  • B. 6:00 am
  • C. 2:00 am
  • D. 8:00 am

Answer: A. 4:00 am (four in the morning)

Explanation: Mourad came at four in the morning and tapped on Aram's room window.

7. Why couldn't Aram believe his eyes when he saw Mourad outside?

  • A. Mourad was sitting on a beautiful white horse, which they were too poor to buy
  • B. Mourad had a new sports car
  • C. Mourad was wearing royal clothes
  • D. Mourad brought a lion

Answer: A. Mourad was sitting on a beautiful white horse, which they were too poor to buy

Explanation: Aram knew their tribe was poverty-stricken, so buying a horse was impossible, implying it was stolen.

8. How did Aram justify Mourad's act of taking the horse?

  • A. Stealing a horse for riding was not the same as stealing money
  • B. It was their own horse
  • C. Mourad found it abandoned on the road
  • D. John Byro gifted it to them

Answer: A. Stealing a horse for riding was not the same as stealing money

Explanation: Aram rationalized that unless they offered to sell the horse, taking it for joyriding wasn't true stealing.

9. Who was considered the natural descendant of the crazy streak in the family?

  • A. Mourad
  • B. Aram
  • C. Zorab
  • D. John Byro

Answer: A. Mourad

Explanation: Mourad was considered crazy by everyone in the family, inheriting the crazy streak of Uncle Khosrove.

10. What was Uncle Khosrove's famous catchphrase for every problem?

  • A. "It is no harm; pay no attention to it."
  • B. "Do not worry, be happy."
  • C. "Money is everything."
  • D. "Never trust anyone."

Answer: A. "It is no harm; pay no attention to it."

Explanation: Uncle Khosrove roared this phrase whenever anyone complained or raised a concern.

11. Where did the boys hide the horse every day after riding?

  • A. In the barn of a deserted vineyard belonging to Fetvajian
  • B. In Aram's backyard
  • C. In a public park
  • D. In a forest cave

Answer: A. In the barn of a deserted vineyard belonging to Fetvajian

Explanation: They hid the white horse in the barn of a deserted vineyard once owned by farmer Fetvajian.

12. Who was the real owner of the white horse?

  • A. John Byro
  • B. Fetvajian
  • C. Uncle Khosrove
  • D. Dikran Halabian

Answer: A. John Byro

Explanation: John Byro, an Assyrian farmer, was the actual owner whose horse had been missing for over a month.

13. How much money had John Byro paid for his white horse?

  • A. 60 dollars
  • B. 100 dollars
  • C. 50 dollars
  • D. 500 dollars

Answer: A. 60 dollars

Explanation: John Byro mentioned at Aram's house that he bought his horse for sixty dollars.

14. What special ability did Mourad claim to have?

  • A. "I have a way with horses/animals"
  • B. "I can talk to birds"
  • C. "I can predict weather"
  • D. "I can run faster than horses"

Answer: A. "I have a way with horses/animals"

Explanation: Mourad repeatedly claimed, "I have a way with a horse," showing his natural bonding with animals.

15. What bird was Mourad nursing when Aram went to his house?

  • A. A young robin with an injured wing
  • B. A wounded pigeon
  • C. A injured parrot
  • D. A sparrow

Answer: A. A young robin with an injured wing

Explanation: Mourad was repairing the wing of an injured robin and helping it learn to fly.

16. What happened when Aram tried to ride the horse alone?

  • A. The horse ran wildly into a vineyard, threw him off, and ran away
  • B. The horse stood still and didn't move
  • C. Aram rode it smoothly like a expert
  • D. The horse bit Aram's leg

Answer: A. The horse ran wildly into a vineyard, threw him off, and ran away

Explanation: When Aram kicked the horse's muscles, it reared up, leaped over grapevines, unseated him, and bolted.

17. How long had Mourad been keeping the horse before Aram found out?

  • A. One month
  • B. Two days
  • C. One week
  • D. Six months

Answer: A. One month

Explanation: John Byro mentioned losing his horse a month ago, meaning Mourad had been riding it secretly for weeks.

18. What did John Byro do when he ran into the boys walking with the horse?

  • A. Examined the horse tooth by tooth and called it the twin of his horse
  • B. Called the police immediately
  • C. Beat the boys with a stick
  • D. Took the horse back forcibly

Answer: A. Examined the horse tooth by tooth and called it the twin of his horse

Explanation: Because of the family's legendary honesty, Byro refused to believe they stole it and declared it must be his horse's twin.

19. Why didn't John Byro accuse the boys of stealing his horse?

  • A. He trusted the famed honesty of the Garoghlanian family
  • B. He was afraid of Uncle Khosrove
  • C. He wasn't sure if it was his horse
  • D. He wanted to give them a gift

Answer: A. He trusted the famed honesty of the Garoghlanian family

Explanation: He remarked: "A suspicious man would believe his eyes instead of his heart," trusting their family integrity.

20. What prompted the boys to quietly return the horse the next morning?

  • A. Their conscience and respect for their family's reputation for honesty
  • B. Fear of police arrest
  • C. The horse became sick
  • D. Aram got bored of riding

Answer: A. Their conscience and respect for their family's reputation for honesty

Explanation: John Byro's absolute faith in their family's honor touched their conscience, leading them to return it silently.

21. Where did the boys return the horse?

  • A. To John Byro's vineyard and tied it in his barn
  • B. Left it on the main road
  • C. Gave it directly to Uncle Khosrove
  • D. Left it at Aram's garden

Answer: A. To John Byro's vineyard and tied it in his barn

Explanation: Early next morning, they took the horse to John Byro's vineyard and quietly placed it in the barn.

22. What change did John Byro notice in his horse after it was returned?

  • A. It was stronger, better-tempered, and well-cared for
  • B. It had grown weak and thin
  • C. It had turned aggressive
  • D. It became blind

Answer: A. It was stronger, better-tempered, and well-cared for

Explanation: Byro happily told Aram's mother that the horse was stronger and better-behaved than before.

23. What name did Mourad give to the white horse secretly?

  • A. My Heart (Wazire)
  • B. White Lightning
  • C. Silver
  • D. Champion

Answer: A. My Heart (Wazire)

Explanation: When John Byro asked the horse's name, Mourad said he called it "My Heart" in Armenian.

24. What setting/location forms the background of this story?

  • A. San Joaquin Valley, California
  • B. Yerevan, Armenia
  • C. London, England
  • D. New York City

Answer: A. San Joaquin Valley, California

Explanation: The story is set in the Armenian-American settlement in San Joaquin Valley, California.

25. What core lesson does the story convey?

  • A. Integrity and ethics should take priority over childish desires
  • B. Stealing is good if done for fun
  • C. Money can buy every happiness
  • D. Animals are dangerous to keep

Answer: A. Integrity and ethics should take priority over childish desires

Explanation: The story shows how moral conscience and family values guide youth back to truth and honor.
