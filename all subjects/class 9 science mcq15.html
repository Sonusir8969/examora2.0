Ch-15: Improvement in Food Resources 10 Questions

1. Which of the following is an essential macronutrient required by plants in large quantities?

  • A. Iron
  • B. Zinc
  • C. Nitrogen
  • D. Copper

Answer: C. Nitrogen

Explanation: Plants require 16 essential nutrients. Macronutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, Calcium, Magnesium, Sulfur) are needed in large quantities compared to micronutrients like Iron, Zinc, or Copper.

2. Kharif crops are grown during which season in India?

  • A. Winter season (October to March)
  • B. Rainy season (June to October)
  • C. Summer season (March to June)
  • D. Autumn season only

Answer: B. Rainy season (June to October)

Explanation: Kharif crops (e.g., Paddy, Soyabean, Maize, Pigeon pea) are grown during the rainy season, whereas Rabi crops (e.g., Wheat, Gram, Mustard) are grown in winter.

3. Blue Revolution is related to the increase in production of:

  • A. Milk
  • B. Fish
  • C. Food grains
  • D. Eggs and poultry

Answer: B. Fish

Explanation: Blue Revolution refers to the growth and development of aquaculture and fish production. White Revolution relates to milk, and Green Revolution to food grains.

4. Which indigenous cattle breed is commonly known for high milk production?

  • A. Sahiwal
  • B. Jersey
  • C. Holstein-Friesian
  • D. Leghorn

Answer: A. Sahiwal

Explanation: Sahiwal and Red Sindhi are indigenous (local) breeds selected for long lactation periods, while Jersey and Holstein-Friesian are exotic breeds.

5. Growing two or more crops simultaneously in definite row patterns on the same field is called:

  • A. Mixed cropping
  • B. Inter-cropping
  • C. Crop rotation
  • D. Monoculture

Answer: B. Inter-cropping

Explanation: Inter-cropping involves growing two or more crops in a specific row layout (e.g., 1 row of Soyabean + 2 rows of Maize) to maximize yield and prevent pest spread.

6. Which Italian bee variety is commonly used for commercial honey production in India due to high yield?

  • A. Apis dorsata
  • B. Apis mellifera
  • C. Apis cerana indica
  • D. Apis florae

Answer: B. Apis mellifera

Explanation: *Apis mellifera* (Italian bee) has high honey-collection capacity, stings less, and stays in a given beehive for long periods.

7. Compost prepared using earthworms to speed up organic matter decomposition is known as:

  • A. Green Manure
  • B. Bio-fertilizer
  • C. Vermicompost
  • D. Chemical Fertilizer

Answer: C. Vermicompost

Explanation: Vermicompost is rich in organic nutrients produced by breaking down organic waste through the activity of earthworms.

8. Which of the following is an example of a pulse crop rich in proteins?

  • A. Wheat
  • B. Pigeon pea (Gram/Arhar)
  • C. Groundnut
  • D. Mustard

Answer: B. Pigeon pea (Gram/Arhar)

Explanation: Pulses (such as gram, arhar, lentil, and pea) are rich sources of dietary protein. Cereals provide carbohydrates, and oilseeds provide fats.

9. Pasturage available to honeybees directly determines the:

  • A. Size of the beehive
  • B. Taste and quality of honey
  • C. Lifespan of the queen bee
  • D. Number of drones

Answer: B. Taste and quality of honey

Explanation: Pasturage refers to the flowers available to bees for nectar and pollen collection, directly governing the flavor and quality of the honey produced.

10. Marine fish farming is carried out using which of the following techniques?

  • A. Mariculture
  • B. Floriculture
  • C. Sericulture
  • D. Apiculture

Answer: A. Mariculture

Explanation: Culturing marine finfishes and shellfishes (like Pomphret, Mackerel, Tuna, Oysters) in coastal sea waters is called mariculture.
