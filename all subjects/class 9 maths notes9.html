NCERT Solutions for Class 9 Maths Chapter 9 Areas of Parallelograms and Triangles

NCERT Solutions for Class 9 Maths Chapter 9 Areas of Parallelograms and Triangles covers figures on the same base and between the same parallels, and the key theorems relating their areas.

1. Figures on the Same Base and Between the Same Parallels

Short Note:

• Two figures are said to be on the same base and between the same parallels if they have a common side (base) and the vertices opposite to the base lie on a line parallel to the base.

2. Parallelograms on the Same Base and Between the Same Parallels

Short Note:

• Parallelograms on the same base and between the same parallels are equal in area.

• A parallelogram and a rectangle on the same base and between the same parallels are equal in area.

3. Triangles on the Same Base and Between the Same Parallels

Short Note:

• Two triangles on the same base and between the same parallels are equal in area.

• A triangle and a parallelogram on the same base and between the same parallels have area of the triangle equal to half the area of the parallelogram.

• The median of a triangle divides it into two triangles of equal area.

NCERT Textbook Exercise Questions

In the given figure, ABCD is a parallelogram, AE ⊥ DC and CF ⊥ AD. If AB = 16 cm, AE = 8 cm and CF = 10 cm, find AD.
Answer: Area of parallelogram = base × height. Using DC as base: Area = DC × AE = 16 × 8 = 128 cm² (since DC = AB = 16 cm). Using AD as base: Area = AD × CF ⇒ 128 = AD × 10 ⇒ AD = 12.8 cm.
Show that a diagonal of a parallelogram divides it into two triangles of equal area.
Answer: Diagonal AC divides parallelogram ABCD into ΔABC and ΔACD. Since AB = DC and BC = AD (opposite sides) and AC is common, ΔABC ≅ ΔACD by SSS, so they have equal area, each half the area of the parallelogram.
D, E and F are respectively the mid-points of the sides BC, CA and AB of ΔABC. Show that (i) BDEF is a parallelogram, (ii) ar(DEF) = ¼ ar(ABC).
Answer: By the Mid-point Theorem, FE || BD and FE = BD (= ½BC), so BDEF is a parallelogram. Since ΔABC is divided into four congruent triangles by joining the mid-points, each has area equal to one-fourth of ar(ABC), so ar(DEF) = ¼ ar(ABC).
Diagonals AC and BD of a trapezium ABCD with AB || DC intersect each other at O. Prove that ar(AOD) = ar(BOC).
Answer: ΔABD and ΔABC lie on the same base AB and between the same parallels AB and DC, so ar(ABD) = ar(ABC). Subtracting the common area ar(AOB) from both gives ar(AOD) = ar(BOC).
