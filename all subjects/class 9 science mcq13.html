Ch-13: Why Do We Fall Ill 10 Questions

1. Which of the following is defined as a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being?

  • A. Fitness
  • B. Health
  • C. Immunity
  • D. Disease-free state

Answer: B. Health

Explanation: According to the WHO, health is a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being, and not merely the absence of disease.

2. Which of the following diseases is classified as a chronic disease?

  • A. Common Cold
  • B. Tuberculosis (TB)
  • C. Influenza
  • D. Cholera

Answer: B. Tuberculosis (TB)

Explanation: Chronic diseases last for a long time or even a lifetime. TB and Elephantiasis are examples, whereas common cold is an acute disease.

3. Which of the following diseases is transmitted by a protozoan pathogen?

  • A. Malaria
  • B. Typhoid
  • C. Tuberculosis
  • D. Polio

Answer: A. Malaria

Explanation: Malaria is caused by the protozoan *Plasmodium*, transmitted through the bite of an infected female *Anopheles* mosquito.

4. Which organism causes Kala-azar (visceral leishmaniasis)?

  • A. Trypanosoma
  • B. Leishmania
  • C. Ascaris
  • D. Staphylococci

Answer: B. Leishmania

Explanation: *Leishmania* is a protozoan parasite that causes Kala-azar (transmitted by sandflies).

5. Penicillin is an antibiotic that blocks the chemical pathway for making cell walls in:

  • A. Viruses
  • B. Bacteria
  • C. Fungi
  • D. Protozoans

Answer: B. Bacteria

Explanation: Penicillin blocks bacterial cell-wall synthesis. It does not work on viruses, as viruses do not use cell walls or bacterial metabolic pathways.

6. Which vector transmits Dengue and Yellow Fever viruses?

  • A. Female Anopheles Mosquito
  • B. Aedes Mosquito
  • C. Culex Mosquito
  • D. Housefly

Answer: B. Aedes Mosquito

Explanation: The *Aedes aegypti* mosquito acts as a vector for viral diseases such as Dengue, Chikungunya, and Yellow Fever.

7. AIDS (Acquired Immunodeficiency Syndrome) is caused by which pathogen?

  • A. Bacterium
  • B. Protozoan
  • C. HIV (Virus)
  • D. Fungus

Answer: C. HIV (Virus)

Explanation: AIDS is caused by the Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV), which damages the host immune system.

8. Immunization or vaccination works on the principle of:

  • A. Blocking bacterial cell wall formation
  • B. Memory function of the immune system
  • C. Increasing body mass
  • D. Reducing body temperature

Answer: B. Memory function of the immune system

Explanation: Vaccines introduce dead or weakened microbes, training the immune system to recognize and produce specific antibodies for faster response upon future exposures.

9. Which of the following is an airborne disease?

  • A. Cholera
  • B. Typhoid
  • C. Pneumonia
  • D. Hepatitis

Answer: C. Pneumonia

Explanation: Pneumonia, Common Cold, and Tuberculosis spread through droplets released into the air when an infected person coughs or sneezes.

10. What type of disease affects the liver and causes yellowing of the skin and eyes (jaundice)?

  • A. Rabies
  • B. Hepatitis
  • C. Malaria
  • D. Tetanus

Answer: B. Hepatitis

Explanation: Hepatitis is inflammation of the liver caused by viral infection (e.g., Hepatitis A/B) often transmitted via contaminated water or body fluids.
