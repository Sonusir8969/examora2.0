Ch-4: Structure of the Atom 10 Questions

1. Who discovered the subatomic particle called 'Electron'?

  • A. J.J. Thomson
  • B. Ernest Rutherford
  • C. James Chadwick
  • D. Niels Bohr

Answer: A. J.J. Thomson

Explanation: J.J. Thomson discovered the electron in 1897 through his cathode ray tube experiments.

2. Which subatomic particle was discovered by James Chadwick in 1932?

  • A. Electron
  • B. Proton
  • C. Neutron
  • D. Positron

Answer: C. Neutron

Explanation: James Chadwick discovered the neutral particle in the atomic nucleus called the neutron, which has mass nearly equal to a proton.

3. Rutherford's alpha-particle scattering experiment led to the discovery of:

  • A. Electron
  • B. Atomic Nucleus
  • C. Proton
  • D. Neutron

Answer: B. Atomic Nucleus

Explanation: Rutherford used gold foil and alpha particles to discover that most of an atom's mass and positive charge is concentrated in a tiny nucleus.

4. What is the maximum number of electrons that can be accommodated in the 'M' shell?

  • A. 2
  • B. 8
  • C. 18
  • D. 32

Answer: C. 18

Explanation: The maximum capacity of a shell is given by 2n². For the M shell (n = 3), capacity = 2 × 3² = 18 electrons.

5. The valency of Chlorine (Atomic Number = 17) is:

  • A. 7
  • B. 1
  • C. 8
  • D. 2

Answer: B. 1

Explanation: Chlorine's electronic configuration is 2, 8, 7. Since it needs 1 electron to complete its octet, its valency is 8 - 7 = 1.

6. Atoms of the same element having the same atomic number but different mass numbers are called:

  • A. Isobars
  • B. Isotopes
  • C. Isotones
  • D. Isomers

Answer: B. Isotopes

Explanation: Isotopes have identical atomic numbers (same protons) but different mass numbers (different neutrons), e.g., Protium, Deuterium, Tritium.

7. Which isotope of an element is used in the treatment of cancer?

  • A. Uranium-235
  • B. Cobalt-60
  • C. Iodine-131
  • D. Carbon-14

Answer: B. Cobalt-60

Explanation: An isotope of Cobalt (Cobalt-60) emits gamma rays and is widely used in radiotherapy for treating cancer.

8. What are Isobars?

  • A. Atoms with same atomic number and same mass number
  • B. Atoms with different atomic numbers but same mass number
  • C. Atoms with same neutrons but different protons
  • D. Atoms with same chemical properties

Answer: B. Atoms with different atomic numbers but same mass number

Explanation: Isobars are atoms of different elements having different atomic numbers but the same total mass number (e.g., Calcium-40 and Argon-40).

9. An atom has 11 protons and 12 neutrons. What is its mass number?

  • A. 11
  • B. 12
  • C. 23
  • D. 1

Answer: C. 23

Explanation: Mass Number (A) = Number of Protons + Number of Neutrons = 11 + 12 = 23 (Sodium, Na).

10. Who proposed that electrons move in discrete energy orbits (shells) around the nucleus?

  • A. J.J. Thomson
  • B. Ernest Rutherford
  • C. Niels Bohr
  • D. John Dalton

Answer: C. Niels Bohr

Explanation: Niels Bohr overcame Rutherford's model flaws by stating that electrons revolve in non-radiating discrete orbits named K, L, M, N shells.
