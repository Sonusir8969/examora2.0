Poem-05: Father to Son 25 Questions

1. Who is the poet of the poem 'Father to Son'?

  • A. Elizabeth Jennings
  • B. Markus Natten
  • C. Walt Whitman
  • D. Shirley Toulson

Answer: A. Elizabeth Jennings

Explanation: The poem 'Father to Son' is composed by English poet Elizabeth Jennings.

2. What is the main problem depicted in the poem 'Father to Son'?

  • A. Generation gap and communication breakdown between father and son
  • B. Financial crisis in the family
  • C. Death of a family member
  • D. Quarrel between neighbors

Answer: A. Generation gap and communication breakdown between father and son

Explanation: The poem highlights the emotional distance, lack of understanding, and communication gap between father and son.

3. How long have the father and son lived in the same house?

  • A. For years
  • B. For a few months
  • C. For one week
  • D. They live in different houses

Answer: A. For years

Explanation: The poem begins: "I do not understand this child / Though we have lived together now / In the same house for years."

4. Does the father know anything about his son according to the poem?

  • A. No, he knows nothing of him
  • B. Yes, he knows everything
  • C. He knows only his likes
  • D. He knows his friends well

Answer: A. No, he knows nothing of him

Explanation: The father says "I know nothing of him," expressing his helplessness despite living together.

5. What does the father attempt to build with his son?

  • A. A relationship / understanding from his childhood days
  • B. A new house
  • C. A business empire
  • D. A wooden structure

Answer: A. A relationship / understanding from his childhood days

Explanation: The father tries to rebuild a bond starting from when the son was a small child.

6. What metaphor is used in "The seed I spent or sown it where / The land is his and none of mine"?

  • A. Agricultural metaphor for upbringing and emotional disconnect
  • B. Financial metaphor for money spent
  • C. Travel metaphor
  • D. Educational metaphor

Answer: A. Agricultural metaphor for upbringing and emotional disconnect

Explanation: 'Seed' symbolizes father's efforts and love in raising his son, but the 'land' (son's mind) feels completely alien to him.

7. How do father and son talk to each other?

  • A. Like strangers
  • B. Like best friends
  • C. Like strict master and student
  • D. With joy and laughter

Answer: A. Like strangers

Explanation: The poem states "We speak like strangers, there's no sign / Of understanding in the air."

8. Physically, the son was built to his father's design, but what is different?

  • A. What he loves, the father cannot share
  • B. His height and appearance
  • C. His voice
  • D. His eating habits

Answer: A. What he loves, the father cannot share

Explanation: Despite physical resemblance, their tastes, thoughts, and interests do not match at all.

9. What surrounds their relationship in the house?

  • A. Silence
  • B. Music
  • C. Noise
  • D. Anger

Answer: A. Silence

Explanation: "Silence surrounds us" emphasizes the complete lack of meaningful dialogue and connection.

10. How would the father prefer his son to return to him?

  • A. Like the 'Prodigal Son' returning home, rather than making his own world
  • B. As a famous person
  • C. Rich and wealthy
  • D. Only during festivals

Answer: A. Like the 'Prodigal Son' returning home, rather than making his own world

Explanation: The father refers to the biblical reference of the 'prodigal son', ready to forgive him if he returns home.

11. What is the father willing to do if his son returns?

  • A. Forgive him and shape a new love out of sorrow
  • B. Punish him for leaving
  • C. Ignore him completely
  • D. Demand money from him

Answer: A. Forgive him and shape a new love out of sorrow

Explanation: The father states he would forgive him and convert his grief into a renewed relationship.

12. Does the son also feel the same pain of distance?

  • A. Yes, he speaks from his own grief and cannot understand himself
  • B. No, he is completely happy
  • C. No, he hates his father
  • D. He doesn't care at all

Answer: A. Yes, he speaks from his own grief and cannot understand himself

Explanation: The son also expresses sorrow: "Father and son, we both must live / On the same globe and the same land."

13. What do both father and son long for at the end of the poem?

  • A. Something to forgive / Reconciliation
  • B. More wealth and power
  • C. A bigger house
  • D. To move to different countries

Answer: A. Something to forgive / Reconciliation

Explanation: The poem ends with: "We put out an empty hand / Longing for something to forgive."

14. What does "empty hand" symbolize in the last stanza?

  • A. Eagerness to reach out and reconcile, despite emptiness
  • B. Poverty
  • C. Giving up hope
  • D. Begging for food

Answer: A. Eagerness to reach out and reconcile, despite emptiness

Explanation: Extending an 'empty hand' shows a desire to make peace, even when communication has yielded nothing yet.

15. What literary allusion is present in the poem?

  • A. The biblical story of the Prodigal Son
  • B. Shakespeare's Hamlet
  • C. Greek mythology of Icarus
  • D. The story of Cain and Abel

Answer: A. The biblical story of the Prodigal Son

Explanation: "I would have him prodigal, returning to his father's house" is a direct allusion to the Bible.

16. What prevents the father and son from reuniting?

  • A. Egos, lack of understanding, and inability to express love
  • B. Physical distance between states
  • C. Legal disputes
  • D. Interference by relatives

Answer: A. Egos, lack of understanding, and inability to express love

Explanation: Pride, miscommunication, and inability to compromise create an invisible barrier between them.

17. What type of poem is 'Father to Son'?

  • A. Dramatic Monologue / Reflective poem
  • B. Sonnet
  • C. Ballad
  • D. Epic poem

Answer: A. Dramatic Monologue / Reflective poem

Explanation: It is a personal and reflective poem where the father voices his inner sorrow and desire for connection.

18. What emotion dominates the father's tone in the poem?

  • A. Helplessness, grief, and longing
  • B. Joy and optimism
  • C. Extreme anger and hatred
  • D. Sarcasm

Answer: A. Helplessness, grief, and longing

Explanation: The father feels sorrowful and helpless over the barrier between him and his son, yet longs for peace.

19. What does "globe and the same land" imply in the poem?

  • A. Despite living in the same environment, they live in isolated personal worlds
  • B. They love traveling around the globe together
  • C. They own vast properties on Earth
  • D. They are farmers

Answer: A. Despite living in the same environment, they live in isolated personal worlds

Explanation: They share the same physical space/world, but mentally and emotionally they are worlds apart.

20. What poetic device is used in "The seed I spent or sown it where / The land is his and none of mine"?

  • A. Extended Metaphor
  • B. Simile
  • C. Oxymoron
  • D. Onomatopoeia

Answer: A. Extended Metaphor

Explanation: The seed-and-land metaphor represents parenting, upbringing, and emotional alienation.

21. Why does the father say "I cannot understand myself"?

  • A. Because anger grows in him from grief
  • B. Because he lost his memory
  • C. Because he doesn't know his name
  • D. Because he wants to leave the house

Answer: A. Because anger grows in him from grief

Explanation: The father fails to understand why his sadness over the situation manifests as anger towards his son.

22. What does "shaping a new love out of sorrow" mean?

  • A. Transforming mutual grief into forgiveness and a fresh relationship
  • B. Buying new gifts for the son
  • C. Forgetting the son completely
  • D. Finding a new family

Answer: A. Transforming mutual grief into forgiveness and a fresh relationship

Explanation: It means rebuilding their broken bond through forgiveness and mutual understanding.

23. How many stanzas are there in the poem 'Father to Son'?

  • A. Four stanzas
  • B. Three stanzas
  • C. Five stanzas
  • D. Two stanzas

Answer: A. Four stanzas

Explanation: The poem consists of four stanzas of six lines each.

24. What is the rhyme scheme of each stanza in 'Father to Son'?

  • A. ABABDE / ABABCC pattern
  • B. AABBCC
  • C. ABCA BC
  • D. Free verse (No rhyme scheme)

Answer: A. ABABDE / ABABCC pattern

Explanation: The poem follows a structured 6-line stanza pattern with slight variations in end rhymes.

25. What universal message does 'Father to Son' convey to readers?

  • A. Open communication, empathy, and willingness to forgive are essential in family relationships
  • B. Children should always live separately from parents
  • C. Parents should never compromise with children
  • D. Silence is the best solution for family disputes

Answer: A. Open communication, empathy, and willingness to forgive are essential in family relationships

Explanation: The poem conveys that without dialogue and forgiveness, emotional distance ruins precious family ties.
