Ch-6: The Hundred Dresses – II 30 Questions

1. Who brought a letter to Miss Mason's class in 'The Hundred Dresses – II'?

  • A. The principal's monitor
  • B. Wanda's father
  • C. The school janitor
  • D. Peggy

Answer: A. The principal's monitor

Explanation: A monitor brought a note from the principal's office to Miss Mason.

2. Who wrote the letter addressed to Miss Mason?

  • A. Mr. Jan Petronski
  • B. Wanda Petronski
  • C. The Principal
  • D. Jake Petronski

Answer: A. Mr. Jan Petronski

Explanation: The letter was written by Jan Petronski, Wanda's father.

3. What reason did Jan Petronski give for taking Wanda and Jake out of school?

  • A. They were moving to a big city where no one would make fun of their funny names
  • B. Wanda was too sick to attend school
  • C. They could not afford the fees
  • D. They were moving back to Poland

Answer: A. They were moving to a big city where no one would make fun of their funny names

Explanation: Mr. Petronski stated in the letter that in a big city there are plenty of funny names, so no one would pick on them.

4. How did Miss Mason feel after reading Mr. Petronski's letter?

  • A. She was deeply hurt, sad, and disappointed
  • B. She was furious at the class monitor
  • C. She was happy for Wanda's family
  • D. She was indifferent

Answer: A. She was deeply hurt, sad, and disappointed

Explanation: Miss Mason adjusted her glasses, looked grave, and expressed sadness that anyone in Room 13 would hurt someone's feelings.

5. What advice did Miss Mason give to the class after reading the letter?

  • A. Not to hurt anyone's feelings deliberately because of a name or appearance
  • B. To apologize immediately in writing
  • C. To stop drawing pictures in class
  • D. To focus strictly on their studies

Answer: A. Not to hurt anyone's feelings deliberately because of a name or appearance

Explanation: She asked everyone to think about what had happened and hoped no child would deliberately hurt another's feelings.

6. How did Maddie feel about herself after hearing the letter?

  • A. She felt like a coward for standing by silently
  • B. She felt proud of Peggy
  • C. She felt relieved that Wanda left
  • D. She felt angry at Mr. Petronski

Answer: A. She felt like a coward for standing by silently

Explanation: Maddie felt terrible because while Peggy made fun, Maddie had stood by in silence, which she felt was just as bad.

7. What vow or resolution did Maddie make after Wanda left?

  • A. Never to stand by silent again if anyone was being picked on
  • B. Never to talk to Peggy again
  • C. To leave Room 13 forever
  • D. To win the next drawing contest

Answer: A. Never to stand by silent again if anyone was being picked on

Explanation: Maddie promised herself that she would speak up if she ever heard anyone being made fun of for their name or clothes.

8. Where did Peggy and Maddie go right after school ended?

  • A. To Boggins Heights
  • B. To the city center
  • C. To the post office
  • D. To Peggy's house

Answer: A. To Boggins Heights

Explanation: They walked up to Boggins Heights hoping to catch Wanda before her family completely moved away.

9. What did Wanda's house at Boggins Heights look like?

  • A. Little and shabby but clean
  • B. Big and luxurious
  • C. Broken down and dirty
  • D. Overgrown with tall trees

Answer: A. Little and shabby but clean

Explanation: The house looked shabby and small, but neat and clean, reminding Maddie of Wanda's faded blue dress.

10. Did Peggy and Maddie find anyone at Wanda's house?

  • A. No, the house was completely empty and locked
  • B. Yes, they met Wanda's father
  • C. Yes, Wanda was waiting for them
  • D. They met a new neighbor living there

Answer: A. No, the house was completely empty and locked

Explanation: Knocking on the door yielded no response; the Petronskis had already gone.

11. How did Peggy try to justify her behavior towards Wanda?

  • A. She claimed her teasing inspired Wanda to draw the hundred dresses
  • B. She said she was just following other girls
  • C. She claimed Wanda started the argument
  • D. She said she never spoke to Wanda

Answer: A. She claimed her teasing inspired Wanda to draw the hundred dresses

Explanation: Peggy reasoned that asking Wanda about her dresses might have given her the ideas to win the contest.

12. What action did Peggy and Maddie decide to take to contact Wanda?

  • A. Write a friendly letter to Wanda
  • B. Put an advertisement in the newspaper
  • C. Call her father's new workplace
  • D. Visit her new school in the big city

Answer: A. Write a friendly letter to Wanda

Explanation: They decided to write a friendly letter telling her about the contest and asking if she liked her new place.

13. What contents were included in the letter written by Peggy and Maddie?

  • A. Praise for her drawings, news that she won the contest, and friendly greetings
  • B. A formal apology with cash
  • C. Questions about why her father wrote the letter
  • D. Complaints about her sudden departure

Answer: A. Praise for her drawings, news that she won the contest, and friendly greetings

Explanation: Although they intended to apologize, it ended up as a friendly letter praising her drawings and congratulating her.

14. How was the letter addressed so it could reach Wanda?

  • A. Addressed to Boggins Heights with 'Please Forward' written on the envelope
  • B. Sent directly to her new school in Chicago
  • C. Handed to the principal
  • D. Sent to her grandmother's house

Answer: A. Addressed to Boggins Heights with 'Please Forward' written on the envelope

Explanation: Since they didn't know her new address, they mailed it to Boggins Heights marked 'Please Forward'.

15. On what occasion did Miss Mason receive a reply from Wanda?

  • A. Christmas time
  • B. Thanksgiving Day
  • C. Halloween
  • D. New Year's Day

Answer: A. Christmas time

Explanation: On the last day of school before the Christmas holidays, Miss Mason received a letter from Wanda.

16. What gift did Wanda offer to Peggy and Maddie in her letter to Miss Mason?

  • A. Specific dress drawings from her contest collection
  • B. Woolen sweaters for winter
  • C. Pairs of shoes
  • D. Christmas greeting cards

Answer: A. Specific dress drawings from her contest collection

Explanation: Wanda requested Miss Mason to give the green dress drawing to Peggy and the blue dress drawing to Maddie.

17. Which specific drawing was gifted to Peggy by Wanda?

  • A. The green dress with red trimming
  • B. The blue dress with ribbons
  • C. The yellow silk gown
  • D. The red dress

Answer: A. The green dress with red trimming

Explanation: Wanda gifted the drawing of the green dress with red trimmings specifically to Peggy.

18. Which specific drawing was gifted to Maddie by Wanda?

  • A. The blue dress
  • B. The green dress
  • C. The pink dress
  • D. The white gown

Answer: A. The blue dress

Explanation: Wanda gifted the drawing of the blue dress to Maddie.

19. What surprising detail did Maddie discover when she stared closely at her drawing at home?

  • A. The face and head in the drawing looked exactly like Maddie herself
  • B. Wanda had signed Maddie's name at the bottom
  • C. The dress was drawn using real fabric
  • D. There was a hidden note behind the paper

Answer: A. The face and head in the drawing looked exactly like Maddie herself

Explanation: Maddie noticed that the head and face of the picture looked just like her own face.

20. What did Peggy discover about her green dress drawing when Maddie pointed it out?

  • A. The face in the green dress drawing looked like Peggy
  • B. It had a tear at the corner
  • C. Wanda had painted a picture of Peggy's dog
  • D. The colors were fading

Answer: A. The face in the green dress drawing looked like Peggy

Explanation: Peggy looked closely and realized that the head and face in her drawing looked just like Peggy herself.

21. What did the customized faces in the drawings prove to Peggy and Maddie?

  • A. Wanda genuinely liked them despite how they treated her
  • B. Wanda was making fun of them
  • C. Wanda wanted them to buy the drawings
  • D. Wanda was angry at them

Answer: A. Wanda genuinely liked them despite how they treated her

Explanation: They realized that Wanda actually liked them and harbored no malice towards them.

22. What phrase did Peggy repeat upon discovering her face in the drawing?

  • A. "What did I tell you! She really must have liked us anyway!"
  • B. "She cheated us!"
  • C. "I knew she was a genius!"
  • D. "We should write her another letter!"

Answer: A. "What did I tell you! She really must have liked us anyway!"

Explanation: Peggy exclaimed with relief that Wanda really liked them despite the teasing.

23. How did Maddie react emotionally when she realized Wanda truly liked her?

  • A. Tears came to her eyes as she pictured Wanda standing alone in the sunny schoolyard
  • B. She laughed happily and went to sleep
  • C. She decided to destroy the drawing
  • D. She called her mother immediately

Answer: A. Tears came to her eyes as she pictured Wanda standing alone in the sunny schoolyard

Explanation: Maddie blinked away tears as she remembered Wanda standing quietly in the schoolyard claiming she had a hundred dresses.

24. What decoration was placed in Room 13 before the Christmas holidays?

  • A. Wreaths and a Christmas tree
  • B. Paper lanterns and ribbons
  • C. Snowflakes painted on windows
  • D. Candles on desks

Answer: A. Wreaths and a Christmas tree

Explanation: The classroom had a small Christmas tree and wreaths hung around the room.

25. What was the central message of Mr. Petronski's letter?

  • A. Discrimination based on ethnicity and names causes emotional pain
  • B. Moving to big cities provides better job opportunities
  • C. Children should focus more on drawing
  • D. Schools should ban uniform policies

Answer: A. Discrimination based on ethnicity and names causes emotional pain

Explanation: The letter highlighted the prejudice and teasing foreign children face in small-town communities.

26. What was Maddie's night-time habit after Wanda left?

  • A. Making speeches defending Wanda from crowds of mocking girls
  • B. Drawing pictures of dresses
  • C. Writing letters to Wanda
  • D. Reading storybooks

Answer: A. Making speeches defending Wanda from crowds of mocking girls

Explanation: At night, Maddie would dream of defending Wanda from girls asking about her dresses, shouting "Stop!".

27. Where did Maddie pin her drawing in her bedroom?

  • A. Over a torn spot in the wallpaper in her bedroom
  • B. On her study table
  • C. Next to her window
  • D. On the closet door

Answer: A. Over a torn spot in the wallpaper in her bedroom

Explanation: She pinned it over a torn place in the wallpaper, making her shabby room look vivid and bright.

28. What character flaw did Maddie recognize in herself regarding Wanda's situation?

  • A. Cowardice for remaining silent
  • B. Envy of Wanda's drawing skills
  • C. Greed for money
  • D. Dishonesty in exams

Answer: A. Cowardice for remaining silent

Explanation: She realized that standing by silently without stopping Peggy made her a coward.

29. How did Wanda show her forgiveness towards the class and her former classmates?

  • A. By gifting her winning drawings to Peggy and Maddie and wishing everyone Merry Christmas
  • B. By sending a box of chocolates
  • C. By returning to visit them
  • D. By writing an apology letter

Answer: A. By gifting her winning drawings to Peggy and Maddie and wishing everyone Merry Christmas

Explanation: Wanda showed true grace and forgiveness by gifting her prized drawings to Peggy and Maddie with warm Christmas wishes.

30. What is the ultimate lesson 'The Hundred Dresses – II' teaches readers?

  • A. Forgiveness, empathy, and overcoming remorse
  • B. Never write letters to teachers
  • C. Moving cities solves all problems
  • D. Always win competitions

Answer: A. Forgiveness, empathy, and overcoming remorse

Explanation: The story teaches that forgiveness heals regret and that we must stand up against injustice and prejudice.
