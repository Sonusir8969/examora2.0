Ch-11: Introduction to Three Dimensional Geometry 30 Questions

1. Into how many octants do the coordinate planes divide three-dimensional space?

  • A. 8
  • B. 4
  • C. 6
  • D. 12

Answer: A. 8

Explanation: Three mutually perpendicular coordinate planes divide space into 8 regions called octants.

2. The coordinates of origin in 3D space are:

  • A. (0, 0, 0)
  • B. (0, 0)
  • C. (1, 1, 1)
  • D. (0, 1, 0)

Answer: A. (0, 0, 0)

Explanation: The point where all three axes intersect has x=0, y=0, z=0.

3. A point lies on the x-axis. What are its y and z coordinates?

  • A. y = 0, z = 0
  • B. x = 0, y = 0
  • C. x = 0, z = 0
  • D. x = 1, y = 1

Answer: A. y = 0, z = 0

Explanation: Any point on the x-axis has coordinates of the form (x, 0, 0).

4. The point (2, -3, 4) lies in which octant?

  • A. IV octant
  • B. I octant
  • C. II octant
  • D. V octant

Answer: A. IV octant

Explanation: Signs (+, -, +) correspond to Octant IV (X O Y' Z).

5. Distance of point P(x, y, z) from the origin is given by:

  • A. √(x² + y² + z²)
  • B. x + y + z
  • C. √(x² + y²)
  • D. x² + y² + z²

Answer: A. √(x² + y² + z²)

Explanation: Distance from origin O(0,0,0) to P(x,y,z) is √((x-0)² + (y-0)² + (z-0)²).

6. Distance between points P(x₁, y₁, z₁) and Q(x₂, y₂, z₂) is:

  • A. √((x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)² + (z₂ - z₁)²)
  • B. (x₂ - x₁) + (y₂ - y₁) + (z₂ - z₁)
  • C. √((x₂ + x₁)² + (y₂ + y₁)² + (z₂ + z₁)²)
  • D. √((x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²)

Answer: A. √((x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)² + (z₂ - z₁)²)

Explanation: Standard 3D distance formula derived from Pythagorean theorem.

7. Find distance between points (1, 2, 3) and (4, 6, 3):

  • A. 5
  • B. 25
  • C. √10
  • D. 7

Answer: A. 5

Explanation: d = √((4-1)² + (6-2)² + (3-3)²) = √(3² + 4² + 0) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5.

8. Midpoint of line joining (x₁, y₁, z₁) and (x₂, y₂, z₂) is:

  • A. ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2, (z₁+z₂)/2)
  • B. ((x₁-x₂)/2, (y₁-y₂)/2, (z₁-z₂)/2)
  • C. (x₁+x₂, y₁+y₂, z₁+z₂)
  • D. ((x₁+x₂)/3, (y₁+y₂)/3, (z₁+z₂)/3)

Answer: A. ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2, (z₁+z₂)/2)

Explanation: Standard midpoint formula in 3D geometry.

9. Coordinates of point dividing segment in ratio m : n internally are:

  • A. ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m+n), (mz₂ + nz₁)/(m+n))
  • B. ((mx₂ - nx₁)/(m-n), (my₂ - ny₁)/(m-n), (mz₂ - nz₁)/(m-n))
  • C. ((mx₁ + nx₂)/(m+n), (my₁ + ny₂)/(m+n), (mz₁ + nz₂)/(m+n))
  • D. ((x₁ + x₂)/m, (y₁ + y₂)/m, (z₁ + z₂)/m)

Answer: A. ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m+n), (mz₂ + nz₁)/(m+n))

Explanation: Internal section formula for 3D coordinate space.

10. Coordinates of point dividing segment in ratio m : n externally are:

  • A. ((mx₂ - nx₁)/(m-n), (my₂ - ny₁)/(m-n), (mz₂ - nz₁)/(m-n))
  • B. ((mx₂ + nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂ + ny₁)/(m+n), (mz₂ + nz₁)/(m+n))
  • C. ((nx₁ - mx₂)/(m-n), (ny₁ - my₂)/(m-n), (nz₁ - mz₂)/(m-n))
  • D. ((mx₁ - nx₂)/(m-n), (my₁ - ny₂)/(m-n), (mz₁ - nz₂)/(m-n))

Answer: A. ((mx₂ - nx₁)/(m-n), (my₂ - ny₁)/(m-n), (mz₂ - nz₁)/(m-n))

Explanation: External section formula substitutes -n for n in internal division formula.

11. Centroid of a triangle with vertices (x₁, y₁, z₁), (x₂, y₂, z₂), and (x₃, y₃, z₃) is:

  • A. ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3, (z₁+z₂+z₃)/3)
  • B. ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/2, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/2, (z₁+z₂+z₃)/2)
  • C. (x₁+x₂+x₃, y₁+y₂+y₃, z₁+z₂+z₃)
  • D. ((x₁+x₂)/3, (y₁+y₂)/3, (z₁+z₂)/3)

Answer: A. ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3, (z₁+z₂+z₃)/3)

Explanation: Centroid is average of x, y, and z coordinates of three vertices.

12. The point (-3, 1, -2) lies in which octant?

  • A. VI octant
  • B. III octant
  • C. VII octant
  • D. II octant

Answer: A. VI octant

Explanation: Signs (-, +, -) correspond to Octant VI (X' O Y Z').

13. In xy-plane, the z-coordinate of any point is always:

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. Any constant
  • D. Undefined

Answer: A. 0

Explanation: Equation of xy-plane is z = 0, so all points on it have z = 0.

14. In yz-plane, which coordinate is always zero?

  • A. x
  • B. y
  • C. z
  • D. None

Answer: A. x

Explanation: Equation of yz-plane is x = 0.

15. Distance of point P(3, 4, 5) from xy-plane is:

  • A. 5 units
  • B. 3 units
  • C. 4 units
  • D. √(3² + 4²) = 5

Answer: A. 5 units

Explanation: Distance from xy-plane is given by absolute value of z-coordinate, |5| = 5.

16. Distance of point P(3, 4, 5) from x-axis is:

  • A. √(y² + z²) = √41
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 5

Answer: A. √(y² + z²) = √41

Explanation: Distance from x-axis to (x, y, z) is √(y² + z²) = √(16 + 25) = √41.

17. Find midpoint of line joining (2, 3, 4) and (4, 7, 8):

  • A. (3, 5, 6)
  • B. (6, 10, 12)
  • C. (1, 2, 2)
  • D. (2, 4, 4)

Answer: A. (3, 5, 6)

Explanation: Midpoint = ((2+4)/2, (3+7)/2, (4+8)/2) = (3, 5, 6).

18. The octant in which all three coordinates are negative (-, -, -) is:

  • A. VII octant
  • B. VIII octant
  • C. III octant
  • D. V octant

Answer: A. VII octant

Explanation: Octant VII (X' O Y' Z') has negative x, negative y, and negative z.

19. Reflection of point (2, 3, 5) in xy-plane is:

  • A. (2, 3, -5)
  • B. (-2, 3, 5)
  • C. (2, -3, 5)
  • D. (-2, -3, -5)

Answer: A. (2, 3, -5)

Explanation: In xy-plane reflection, x and y remain same while sign of z flips.

20. Reflection of point (2, 3, 5) in yz-plane is:

  • A. (-2, 3, 5)
  • B. (2, -3, 5)
  • C. (2, 3, -5)
  • D. (-2, -3, 5)

Answer: A. (-2, 3, 5)

Explanation: In yz-plane reflection, y and z remain same while sign of x flips.

21. Foot of perpendicular from point (2, 3, 4) on x-axis is:

  • A. (2, 0, 0)
  • B. (0, 3, 0)
  • C. (0, 0, 4)
  • D. (2, 3, 0)

Answer: A. (2, 0, 0)

Explanation: On x-axis, y and z coordinates become 0 while x remains identical.

22. Foot of perpendicular from point (2, 3, 4) on xy-plane is:

  • A. (2, 3, 0)
  • B. (2, 0, 4)
  • C. (0, 3, 4)
  • D. (2, 0, 0)

Answer: A. (2, 3, 0)

Explanation: On xy-plane, z-coordinate is 0 while x and y remain identical.

23. If points (1, 1, 1), (0, 0, 0) and (x, y, z) are collinear, then:

  • A. x = y = z
  • B. x + y + z = 0
  • C. x + y = z
  • D. x = y = -z

Answer: A. x = y = z

Explanation: Line joining origin (0,0,0) and (1,1,1) has direction ratios 1:1:1, so any point on it has x = y = z.

24. Distance between points (0, 0, 0) and (-3, 4, 12) is:

  • A. 13
  • B. 12
  • C. 5
  • D. √169 = 13

Answer: A. 13

Explanation: d = √((-3)² + 4² + 12²) = √(9 + 16 + 144) = √169 = 13.

25. Centroid of triangle with vertices (1, 2, 3), (3, 0, 1), and (-1, 4, 2) is:

  • A. (1, 2, 2)
  • B. (3, 6, 6)
  • C. (1, 3, 2)
  • D. (2, 2, 2)

Answer: A. (1, 2, 2)

Explanation: Centroid = ((1+3-1)/3, (2+0+4)/3, (3+1+2)/3) = (3/3, 6/3, 6/3) = (1, 2, 2).

26. What is the equation of xz-plane?

  • A. y = 0
  • B. x = 0
  • C. z = 0
  • D. x + z = 0

Answer: A. y = 0

Explanation: In xz-plane, y-coordinate of every point is equal to 0.

27. Ratio in which yz-plane divides segment joining (2, 4, 5) and (3, 5, -4) is:

  • A. -2 : 3 (externally)
  • B. 2 : 3 (internally)
  • C. 3 : 2 (internally)
  • D. 1 : 2

Answer: A. -2 : 3 (externally)

Explanation: Ratio = -x₁ : x₂ = -2 : 3. Negative sign indicates external division in 2 : 3.

28. Length of diagonal of a cuboid with sides a, b, c is:

  • A. √(a² + b² + c²)
  • B. a + b + c
  • C. √(a² + b²)
  • D. 2(ab + bc + ca)

Answer: A. √(a² + b² + c²)

Explanation: Space diagonal of cuboid with vertices from (0,0,0) to (a,b,c) is √(a² + b² + c²).

29. Coordinates of point on z-axis at distance 5 from origin are:

  • A. (0, 0, ±5)
  • B. (±5, 0, 0)
  • C. (0, ±5, 0)
  • D. (5, 5, 5)

Answer: A. (0, 0, ±5)

Explanation: Points on z-axis have x=0, y=0. Distance 5 means z = ±5.

30. Signs of coordinates in Octant VIII (X O Y' Z') are:

  • A. (+, -, -)
  • B. (-, -, -)
  • C. (+, +, -)
  • D. (-, +, -)

Answer: A. (+, -, -)

Explanation: Octant VIII corresponds to positive x, negative y, and negative z: (+, -, -).
