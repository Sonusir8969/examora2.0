Suppl-5: Mother's Day 25 Questions

1. Who is the author of the play 'Mother's Day'?

  • A. J.B. Priestley
  • B. Patrick Pringle
  • C. Marga Minco
  • D. William Saroyan

Answer: A. J.B. Priestley

Explanation: The satirical play 'Mother's Day' was written by the famous English playwright J.B. Priestley.

2. What central theme is highlighted in the play 'Mother's Day'?

  • A. The status and respect of a mother/housewife in a family
  • B. Conflict between two neighboring families
  • C. Educational issues of teenagers
  • D. Financial struggles of a middle-class household

Answer: A. The status and respect of a mother/housewife in a family

Explanation: The play focuses on how family members take mothers for granted and ignore their contributions.

3. Who is Mrs. Annie Pearson's neighbor and friend?

  • A. Mrs. Fitzgerald
  • B. Mrs. Dorling
  • C. Mrs. Packletide
  • D. Mrs. Higgins

Answer: A. Mrs. Fitzgerald

Explanation: Mrs. Fitzgerald is Mrs. Pearson's strong-willed neighbor who helps her gain respect at home.

4. Where did Mrs. Fitzgerald learn fortune-telling and magic art?

  • A. In the East (Asia)
  • B. In America
  • C. In London
  • D. In Africa

Answer: A. In the East (Asia)

Explanation: Mrs. Fitzgerald learnt magic and fortune-telling when her husband was stationed in the East for twelve years.

5. How does Mrs. Fitzgerald describe Mrs. Pearson's personality at the beginning?

  • A. Soft-spoken, submissive, and overly fond of pleasing her family
  • B. Arrogant, stubborn, and short-tempered
  • C. Lazy, irresponsible, and careless
  • D. Strict, demanding, and controlling

Answer: A. Soft-spoken, submissive, and overly fond of pleasing her family

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson is gentle and shy, constantly running after her demanding family members.

6. What plan does Mrs. Fitzgerald suggest to solve Mrs. Pearson's problem?

  • A. Swap personalities/bodies temporarily using an Eastern spell
  • B. Call the police on her family
  • C. Leave the house permanently
  • D. Refuse to talk to her husband forever

Answer: A. Swap personalities/bodies temporarily using an Eastern spell

Explanation: Mrs. Fitzgerald proposes swapping personalities so she can teach Mrs. Pearson's family a lesson in her body.

7. What spell does Mrs. Fitzgerald chant to exchange personalities?

  • A. "Arshtatta duht... Arshtatta lam..."
  • B. "Abracadabra hocus pocus..."
  • C. "Simsalabim open sesame..."
  • D. "Alakazam boom..."

Answer: A. "Arshtatta duht... Arshtatta lam..."

Explanation: Mrs. Fitzgerald holds hands with Mrs. Pearson and recites this magic incantation.

8. Who is Doris Pearson?

  • A. Mrs. Pearson's daughter
  • B. Mrs. Pearson's sister
  • C. Mrs. Fitzgerald's daughter
  • D. The domestic maid

Answer: A. Mrs. Pearson's daughter

Explanation: Doris is Mrs. Pearson's spoiled daughter who expects her mother to do all her chores.

9. What did Doris ask her mother to do as soon as she entered the house?

  • A. Iron her yellow silk dress and make tea
  • B. Cook dinner and wash dishes
  • C. Buy movie tickets
  • D. Clean her bedroom

Answer: A. Iron her yellow silk dress and make tea

Explanation: Doris ordered her mother to iron her dress for her date and asked if tea was ready.

10. Who was Charlie Spence?

  • A. Doris Pearson's boyfriend
  • B. Cyril Pearson's best friend
  • C. George Pearson's boss
  • D. The local club president

Answer: A. Doris Pearson's boyfriend

Explanation: Charlie Spence was the young man with whom Doris was going out on a date.

11. How did Mrs. Pearson (with Mrs. Fitzgerald's soul) insult Charlie Spence in front of Doris?

  • A. Called him buck-toothed and half-witted
  • B. Called him a greedy millionaire
  • C. Called him short and lazy
  • D. Said he was untidy and rude

Answer: A. Called him buck-toothed and half-witted

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson mocked Doris's choice by calling Charlie Spence buck-toothed and half-witted.

12. Who is Cyril Pearson?

  • A. Mrs. Pearson's son
  • B. Mrs. Pearson's husband
  • C. Mrs. Fitzgerald's son
  • D. Doris's classmate

Answer: A. Mrs. Pearson's son

Explanation: Cyril is Mrs. Pearson's demanding son who expects his mother to keep everything ready for him.

13. What was Cyril's initial demand when he came back home?

  • A. Asked for tea and if his clothes were put out
  • B. Demanded pocket money
  • C. Asked his mother to cook a lavish dinner
  • D. Wanted his friend to be served drinks

Answer: A. Asked for tea and if his clothes were put out

Explanation: Cyril rushed in expecting tea to be ready and his clothes laid out for him.

14. What was Mrs. Pearson doing when Cyril and Doris saw her at home?

  • A. Drinking stout (beer) and playing cards
  • B. Cooking in the kitchen
  • C. Crying in her bedroom
  • D. Cleaning the living room

Answer: A. Drinking stout (beer) and playing cards

Explanation: She was relaxing, drinking stout from a glass, and playing patience cards.

15. What name was given to Mrs. Pearson's husband?

  • A. George Pearson
  • B. Arthur Pearson
  • C. Henry Pearson
  • D. Richard Pearson

Answer: A. George Pearson

Explanation: Her husband's name was George Pearson, a pompous and self-important man.

16. What mock name did people at the social club call George Pearson behind his back?

  • A. Pompy-Ompy Pearson
  • B. Grumpy George
  • C. King Pearson
  • D. Silly Billy

Answer: A. Pompy-Ompy Pearson

Explanation: Mrs. Pearson reveals that members at the club laugh at him and call him "Pompy-Ompy Pearson" because he is slow and pompous.

17. How did George feel when Mrs. Pearson told him about his nickname at the club?

  • A. Shocked, humiliated, and deeply hurt
  • B. Amused and laughed out loud
  • C. Angry at the club manager
  • D. Indifferent and ignored it

Answer: A. Shocked, humiliated, and deeply hurt

Explanation: George was crushed to learn that people at the club made fun of him behind his back.

18. What principle did Mrs. Pearson state regarding her work hours at home?

  • A. Forty-hour week with weekends off just like everyone else
  • B. Working 24/7 without rest
  • C. Doing chores only on weekdays
  • D. Working 12 hours every day

Answer: A. Forty-hour week with weekends off just like everyone else

Explanation: She announced that she would also work a 40-hour week and do no extra work on weekends unless treated politely.

19. Why did Doris cry in her room?

  • A. Her mother spoke to her harshly and criticized her boyfriend
  • B. She lost her job
  • C. She had a fight with Cyril
  • D. Her yellow dress was ruined

Answer: A. Her mother spoke to her harshly and criticized her boyfriend

Explanation: Doris wept because her mother refused to iron her dress and criticized Charlie Spence fiercely.

20. What happened when the real Mrs. Fitzgerald (in Mrs. Pearson's body) visited the house?

  • A. She was shocked to see Mrs. Pearson treating her family so harshly and begged to switch back
  • B. She cheered and encouraged Mrs. Pearson to be harsher
  • C. She joined George in arguing
  • D. She left immediately in anger

Answer: A. She was shocked to see Mrs. Pearson treating her family so harshly and begged to switch back

Explanation: Seeing her family upset broke the real Mrs. Pearson's soft heart, and she insisted on changing back to original personalities.

21. What condition did Mrs. Fitzgerald set before reversing the body-swap spell?

  • A. Mrs. Pearson must remain firm and not apologize or soften up immediately
  • B. George must pay her money
  • C. Doris and Cyril must leave the house
  • D. They must sign a written contract

Answer: A. Mrs. Pearson must remain firm and not apologize or soften up immediately

Explanation: Mrs. Fitzgerald warned Annie not to back down or apologize, otherwise all the effort would be wasted.

22. What activity did Mrs. Pearson suggest for the family at the end of the play?

  • A. A family game of rummy while the children prepare supper
  • B. Going out to watch a movie together
  • C. Going to the club with George
  • D. Ordering food from a restaurant

Answer: A. A family game of rummy while the children prepare supper

Explanation: At the end, Mrs. Pearson suggests spending a nice evening together playing rummy while Doris and Cyril cook supper.

23. How did the family members react to Mrs. Pearson's final request at the end?

  • A. They eagerly agreed and showed respect and obedience
  • B. They refused and ran out of the house
  • C. George got angry again
  • D. Doris started crying again

Answer: A. They eagerly agreed and showed respect and obedience

Explanation: Realizing the shift in power, George, Doris, and Cyril gladly obeyed her and stayed at home.

24. What message does J.B. Priestley convey through this play?

  • A. Homemakers deserve equal respect, appreciation, and time off from domestic work
  • B. Family members should live separately
  • C. Magic is necessary to solve household problems
  • D. Children should not go on dates

Answer: A. Homemakers deserve equal respect, appreciation, and time off from domestic work

Explanation: The play advocates for gender equality at home and recognition of the unpaid labor done by mothers.

25. What tone is primarily used in the play 'Mother's Day'?

  • A. Humorous and satirical
  • B. Tragic and depressing
  • C. Highly aggressive and violent
  • D. Philosophical and serious

Answer: A. Humorous and satirical

Explanation: The author uses humor, witty dialogue, and satire to address a serious social issue effectively.
