Class 12 Board & Competitive Exam Strategy

Key Tips for Board Examinations & Competitive Entrance Tests (JEE / NEET):

• Physics: Master standard derivations (Gauss Law, Lens Maker Formula, Wavefronts), circuit numericals (Kirchhoff's Laws, AC Circuits), and semiconductor p-n junction characteristics.

• Chemistry: Practice named organic reactions (Aldol, Cannizzaro, Reimer-Tiemann), coordination compound IUPAC/CFT concepts, and physical chemistry formula-based numericals (Nernst equation, rate laws).

• Biology: Focus on NCERT diagrams (embryo sac, human reproductive system, DNA replication fork), genetic pedigree analysis, lac operon mechanism, and recombinant DNA technology steps.
