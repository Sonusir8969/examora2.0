Ch-10: Democracy and Diversity 15 Questions

1. In which year were the Mexico Olympics held?

  • A. 1968
  • B. 1972
  • C. 1964
  • D. 1976

Answer: A. 1968

Explanation: The Mexico Olympics were held in 1968, where Tommie Smith and John Carlos raised international awareness about racial discrimination in the US.

2. Tommie Smith and John Carlos received their medals wearing black socks and no shoes to represent what?

  • A. Black Poverty
  • B. Black Pride
  • C. Civil Rights Movement
  • D. Sports Solidarity

Answer: A. Black Poverty

Explanation: They wore black socks and no shoes to represent Black poverty in the United States.

3. Who was the Australian athlete who supported Tommie Smith and John Carlos during the award ceremony?

  • A. Peter Norman
  • B. Carlos Carlos
  • C. Jesse Owens
  • D. Usain Bolt

Answer: A. Peter Norman

Explanation: Peter Norman was an Australian sprinter who wore a human rights badge on his shirt during the ceremony to show solidarity with the African-American athletes.

4. The Civil Rights Movement in the USA was led by which prominent leader during 1954–1968?

  • A. Martin Luther King Jr.
  • B. Abraham Lincoln
  • C. Nelson Mandela
  • D. Barack Obama

Answer: A. Martin Luther King Jr.

Explanation: The Civil Rights Movement in the USA (1954–1968) refers to a set of events and reform movements aimed at abolishing legal racial discrimination against African-Americans, led by Martin Luther King Jr.

5. Which movement in the US emerged in 1966 and lasted till 1975, advocating militant methods to end racism?

  • A. Black Power Movement
  • B. Civil Rights Movement
  • C. Non-violent Resistance Movement
  • D. Feminist Movement

Answer: A. Black Power Movement

Explanation: The Black Power movement emerged in 1966 and was a more anti-racist, militant movement advocating even violence if necessary to end racism in the US.

6. Social differences arise mainly due to which of the following factors?

  • A. Birth
  • B. Choice
  • C. Education
  • D. Occupation

Answer: A. Birth

Explanation: Most social differences are based on accident of birth. We don't choose to belong to the community we are born into.

7. Which type of social division leads to deep social cleavages and conflicts?

  • A. Overlapping social differences
  • B. Cross-cutting social differences
  • C. Economic differences
  • D. Political differences

Answer: A. Overlapping social differences

Explanation: Overlapping social differences occur when one difference overlaps with another, creating deep social divisions and tension.

8. In Northern Ireland, what are the two main religious sects of Christianity that experienced violent conflict?

  • A. Roman Catholics and Protestants
  • B. Catholics and Orthodox
  • C. Protestants and Baptists
  • D. Anglicans and Lutherans

Answer: A. Roman Catholics and Protestants

Explanation: Northern Ireland experienced a violent ethno-political conflict between Roman Catholics (53%) and Protestants (44%).

9. Which country suffered disintegration into six independent countries due to ethno-religious social divisions?

  • A. Yugoslavia
  • B. Sri Lanka
  • C. Belgium
  • D. Northern Ireland

Answer: A. Yugoslavia

Explanation: Political competition along religious and ethnic lines led to the disintegration of Yugoslavia into six independent countries.

10. Netherlands experiences which type of social differences between Catholics and Protestants?

  • A. Cross-cutting differences
  • B. Overlapping differences
  • C. Deeply hostile differences
  • D. Racial differences

Answer: A. Cross-cutting differences

Explanation: In Netherlands, class and religion tend to cross-cut each other, meaning Catholics and Protestants are about equally likely to be rich or poor, reducing social tension.

11. How many key factors are crucial in deciding the outcome of politics of social divisions?

  • A. Three
  • B. Two
  • C. Four
  • D. Five

Answer: A. Three

Explanation: The three factors are: how people perceive their identities, how political leaders raise demands of any community, and how the government reacts to these demands.

12. What was the silver medalist Peter Norman's action in the 1968 Olympics meant to symbolize?

  • A. Human rights solidarity
  • B. Opposition to Olympic rules
  • C. Support for the US team
  • D. Personal political ambition

Answer: A. Human rights solidarity

Explanation: Peter Norman wore a Human Rights badge on his shirt to demonstrate international solidarity with the anti-racism movement.

13. Which political party in Northern Ireland represented the interest of the Catholic community demanding integration with Republic of Ireland?

  • A. Nationalist parties
  • B. Unionist parties
  • C. Labor party
  • D. Conservative party

Answer: A. Nationalist parties

Explanation: Nationalist parties represented the Catholics who demanded that Northern Ireland be unified with the Republic of Ireland.

14. When does a social difference become a social division?

  • A. When it overlaps with another social difference
  • B. When people start migrating
  • C. When economic conditions improve
  • D. When education levels increase

Answer: A. When it overlaps with another social difference

Explanation: A social difference becomes a social division when it overlaps with other differences, making people feel they belong to different communities.

15. In a democracy, political expression of social division is considered as:

  • A. Normal and healthy, if kept within constitutional limits
  • B. Highly dangerous and always destructive
  • C. Illegal and punishable
  • D. Something that should always be banned

Answer: A. Normal and healthy, if kept within constitutional limits

Explanation: Expressing social divisions in politics allows marginalized groups to express grievances and get the government to attend to these, which is a normal aspect of democracy.
