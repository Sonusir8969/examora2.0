NCERT Solutions for Class 10 English Chapter 1 A Letter to God

NCERT Solutions for Class 10 English First Flight Chapter 1 A Letter to God covers the story of Lencho's unwavering faith, the theme of blind faith versus human dishonesty, and important questions and answers.

1. About the Author and Story Setting

Short Note:

• The story is written by G.L. Fuentes and is set in a small, isolated valley village called San Diego de la Union in Mexico, surrounded by hills and fields.

• Lencho, a hardworking farmer, depends entirely on the rains for a good harvest, and his family's future rests on the success of his crop of corn.

• When a devastating hailstorm destroys his entire crop overnight, Lencho, a man of complete and simple faith in God, decides to write a letter to God asking for a hundred pesos to save his family from hunger.

2. Plot and Key Incident

Short Note:

• Lencho writes his letter and posts it, fully confident that God will answer his prayer directly, showing his deep and literal faith.

• The postmaster, touched by the farmer's faith, decides to collect money from himself and his colleagues to send to Lencho, though they can only gather seventy pesos.

• When Lencho receives the money and finds it short by thirty pesos, he assumes the post office staff must have stolen the rest, and writes an angry second letter to God demanding the remaining money, since he cannot conceive that God himself could have made a mistake.

3. Theme & Message

Short Note:

• The story explores the theme of blind, unquestioning faith and contrasts it with the flawed, sometimes petty behaviour of ordinary human beings.

• It also carries gentle irony: the postmaster's genuine act of kindness and sacrifice is met with suspicion rather than gratitude, showing how deep faith can sometimes blind a person to human goodness.

• The story ultimately celebrates both faith and human compassion, while highlighting how they can clash due to human limitations.

Reading With Insight (Textbook Questions)

Q1: Why did Lencho write a letter to God, and what did he ask for?
Answer: Lencho wrote to God because a hailstorm had destroyed his entire corn crop, leaving his family without food for the year, and he asked for a hundred pesos so he could resow his fields and support his family until the next harvest.
Q2: How did the postmaster react to Lencho's letter, and what did he do?
Answer: The postmaster was moved by Lencho's strong and simple faith in God and decided to collect a donation from himself and other post office employees, gathering seventy pesos to send to Lencho in God's name, though this was less than the full amount requested.
Q3: Why was Lencho angry after receiving the money, and what does this reveal about his character?
Answer: Lencho was angry because thirty pesos were missing from the amount he had asked God for, and he immediately assumed that the post office employees had stolen the rest, revealing his complete inability to doubt God and his willingness to suspect fellow human beings instead, reflecting his deep, unquestioning faith.

Extract-Based / Short Answer Questions

Q4: What does the postmaster's action reveal about human nature?
Answer: The postmaster's willingness to sacrifice his own money to help a stranger reveals genuine kindness, compassion and generosity present in ordinary people, even when their good deed goes unappreciated.
Q5: What is ironic about the ending of the story?
Answer: It is ironic that Lencho, whose faith in God was so strong that he wrote directly to Him, never suspects that his prayer was actually answered by kind human beings, and instead blames those very people for supposedly stealing from him.
