Ch-16: Globalization and the Indian Economy 30 Questions

1. What is the process of rapid integration or interconnection between countries called?

  • A. Globalization
  • B. Urbanization
  • C. Industrialization
  • D. Privatization

Answer: A. Globalization

Explanation: Globalization is the process of rapid integration or interconnection among nations through foreign trade, investment, and movement of technology and people.

2. A company that owns or controls production in more than one nation is called a:

  • A. Multinational Corporation (MNC)
  • B. National Enterprise
  • C. Public Sector Undertaking
  • D. Joint Venture

Answer: A. Multinational Corporation (MNC)

Explanation: MNCs are large corporations that set up production units in multiple countries where labor, raw materials, and other resources are cheap.

3. Why do MNCs set up offices and factories in developing countries like India and China?

  • A. To get cheap labor and lower costs of production
  • B. To pay higher taxes to host countries
  • C. To avoid modern technology
  • D. To sell goods only in foreign countries

Answer: A. To get cheap labor and lower costs of production

Explanation: MNCs relocate production to regions where labor and input costs are low so that they can earn higher profits.

4. Money spent by MNCs to buy assets such as land, building, machines, and other equipment in foreign countries is called:

  • A. Foreign Investment
  • B. Public Expenditure
  • C. Domestic Investment
  • D. Sovereign Debt

Answer: A. Foreign Investment

Explanation: Investment made by a company located in one country into physical assets in another country is termed Foreign Investment.

5. What is the most common route for MNCs to expand production in developing countries?

  • A. To buy up local companies
  • B. To set up government agencies
  • C. To build non-profit schools
  • D. To close domestic markets

Answer: A. To buy up local companies

Explanation: Acquiring established local companies allows MNCs to easily tap into existing production networks and market channels.

6. Cargill Foods, a large American MNC, bought over which famous Indian food company?

  • A. Parakh Foods
  • B. Amul Foods
  • C. Britannia Industries
  • D. Dabur India

Answer: A. Parakh Foods

Explanation: Cargill Foods bought Parakh Foods, which possessed a large marketing network and edible oil refineries across India.

7. Foreign trade results in connecting the markets of different countries by facilitating:

  • A. Integration of markets
  • B. Complete isolation of domestic producers
  • C. Total control by local governments
  • D. Reduction of international competition

Answer: A. Integration of markets

Explanation: Foreign trade creates opportunities for producers to reach beyond domestic markets, leading to market integration across boundaries.

8. Which factor has played a major role in stimulating the process of globalization?

  • A. Rapid improvements in Information and Communication Technology (ICT)
  • B. Increase in trade tariffs
  • C. Strict travel bans between nations
  • D. Discontinuation of internet services

Answer: A. Rapid improvements in Information and Communication Technology (ICT)

Explanation: Technological advances in telecommunications, computers, and internet have drastically sped up communication and trade across borders.

9. Containerization in transportation technology has led to:

  • A. Huge reduction in port handling costs and faster delivery of goods
  • B. Increase in shipping costs
  • C. Destruction of goods during transit
  • D. Slow delivery times for international trade

Answer: A. Huge reduction in port handling costs and faster delivery of goods

Explanation: Goods placed in standardized containers can be easily transferred onto ships, trains, and trucks without manual repacking, reducing cost and time.

10. Tax on imports is a classic example of a:

  • A. Trade Barrier
  • B. Export Subsidy
  • C. Free Trade Agreement
  • D. Foreign Exchange Rate

Answer: A. Trade Barrier

Explanation: Tax on imports is called a trade barrier because it places restrictions on the entry of foreign goods into a country.

11. Why did the Indian government impose barriers on foreign trade and foreign investment after independence?

  • A. To protect domestic producers from foreign competition
  • B. To promote foreign MNCs in India
  • C. To stop agricultural production
  • D. To decrease local employment opportunities

Answer: A. To protect domestic producers from foreign competition

Explanation: After independence, India protected newly emerging domestic industries from established foreign competitors through strict trade barriers.

12. Around which year did India bring about major policy changes by removing trade barriers and introducing New Economic Policy?

  • A. 1991
  • B. 1947
  • C. 2001
  • D. 1975

Answer: A. 1991

Explanation: In 1991, the Indian government introduced economic reforms (LPG policy) to liberalize foreign trade and investment.

13. Removing restrictions or barriers set by the government on foreign trade and foreign investment is called:

  • A. Liberalisation
  • B. Socialisation
  • C. Nationalisation
  • D. Urbanisation

Answer: A. Liberalisation

Explanation: Liberalisation refers to opening up the economy by dismantling government licensing, quotas, and tariff barriers.

14. Which international organisation aims to liberalise international trade and establish rules for global trade?

  • A. World Trade Organisation (WTO)
  • B. International Monetary Fund (IMF)
  • C. World Health Organisation (WHO)
  • D. UNESCO

Answer: A. World Trade Organisation (WTO)

Explanation: The WTO sets and enforces rules for international trade and promotes barrier-free trade among member countries.

15. Where are the headquarters of the World Trade Organisation (WTO) located?

  • A. Geneva, Switzerland
  • B. New York, USA
  • C. London, UK
  • D. Paris, France

Answer: A. Geneva, Switzerland

Explanation: The World Trade Organisation (WTO) is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.

16. What is a common criticism against the World Trade Organisation (WTO)?

  • A. Developed countries unfairly maintain trade barriers while forcing developing nations to remove theirs
  • B. It only supports developing nations
  • C. It forces all countries to ban imports
  • D. It operates only in Asian countries

Answer: A. Developed countries unfairly maintain trade barriers while forcing developing nations to remove theirs

Explanation: Critics point out that wealthy developed nations continue to subsidize their farmers while pressuring developing nations to open their agricultural markets.

17. Special industrial zones set up with world-class infrastructure facilities to attract foreign investments are called:

  • A. Special Economic Zones (SEZs)
  • B. Free Agriculture Zones
  • C. Rural Industrial Estates
  • D. Public Sector Parks

Answer: A. Special Economic Zones (SEZs)

Explanation: SEZs feature top-tier infrastructure (electricity, roads, water, storage) to entice MNCs to set up manufacturing units.

18. Companies setting up production units in Special Economic Zones (SEZs) do not have to pay taxes for an initial period of:

  • A. 5 years
  • B. 10 years
  • C. 1 year
  • D. 15 years

Answer: A. 5 years

Explanation: To attract investment, companies located in SEZs enjoy tax exemptions for the first five years of operation.

19. How have governments amended labour laws to attract foreign investment in recent years?

  • A. Made labor laws flexible so companies can hire workers temporarily
  • B. Made jobs mandatory permanent for all workers
  • C. Banned overtime work in all factories
  • D. Raised minimum wage rates drastically

Answer: A. Made labor laws flexible so companies can hire workers temporarily

Explanation: Labour law flexibility allows companies to employ workers on short contracts during peak demand periods, reducing fixed labour costs.

20. Which section of consumers has benefited the most from globalization in India?

  • A. Well-off urban consumers with greater choice and lower prices
  • B. Poor rural laborers
  • C. Small traditional artisans
  • D. Subsistence farmers

Answer: A. Well-off urban consumers with greater choice and lower prices

Explanation: Urban consumers with high purchasing power enjoy improved product quality, wider choices, and lower prices due to market competition.

21. Which sector in India faced harsh competition and severe losses due to globalization?

  • A. Small-scale manufacturing industries (e.g. capacitors, batteries, plastic toys)
  • B. Large IT service companies
  • C. Automobile manufacturing MNCs
  • D. Petrochemical refineries

Answer: A. Small-scale manufacturing industries (e.g. capacitors, batteries, plastic toys)

Explanation: Small domestic producers were unable to compete against cheap imports and large MNC production, leading to factory shutdowns.

22. Which Indian MNC emerged as a global leader in the IT and software industry?

  • A. Infosys
  • B. Ranbaxy
  • C. Asian Paints
  • D. Sundaram Fasteners

Answer: A. Infosys

Explanation: Infosys, along with TCS and Wipro, expanded globally as premier software and IT services MNCs from India.

23. Which Indian automobile company became a multinational corporation by acquiring foreign automotive brands like Jaguar and Land Rover?

  • A. Tata Motors
  • B. Maruti Suzuki
  • C. Bajaj Auto
  • D. Mahindra & Mahindra

Answer: A. Tata Motors

Explanation: Tata Motors is an Indian multinational automobile manufacturing company that expanded its footprint across global markets.

24. What does 'Fair Globalization' mean?

  • A. Globalization that creates equal opportunities for all and shares benefits fairly
  • B. Globalization that benefits only multinational corporations
  • C. Complete ban on international trade
  • D. Providing subsidies exclusively to foreign companies

Answer: A. Globalization that creates equal opportunities for all and shares benefits fairly

Explanation: Fair globalization ensures that labor rights are protected, small producers are supported, and the gains of trade reach all sections of society.

25. How can the government play a crucial role in bringing about Fair Globalization?

  • A. By enforcing labor laws and negotiating for fairer WTO rules
  • B. By removing all minimum wage laws
  • C. By shutting down all public sector industries
  • D. By banning local small businesses

Answer: A. By enforcing labor laws and negotiating for fairer WTO rules

Explanation: The government can protect workers' rights, support small producers through policy intervention, and form alliances with developing nations at WTO.

26. An entry quota or quantitative limit imposed by a country on imported items is called a:

  • A. Import Quota
  • B. Export Tariff
  • C. Special Economic Grant
  • D. Trade Exemption

Answer: A. Import Quota

Explanation: An import quota fixes a maximum physical limit on the volume of a specific good that can be imported during a given timeframe.

27. Contracting out business activities or services to third-party vendors in foreign countries is known as:

  • A. Outsourcing
  • B. Internalization
  • C. Nationalization
  • D. Import Substitution

Answer: A. Outsourcing

Explanation: In outsourcing, companies hire overseas service providers (such as call centers or software programmers in India) to perform tasks cost-effectively.

28. Which sector has grown rapidly in India due to outsourcing by foreign MNCs?

  • A. Information Technology and Call Center Services
  • B. Jute Textiles
  • C. Handloom Weaving
  • D. Heavy Mining

Answer: A. Information Technology and Call Center Services

Explanation: India's skilled English-speaking workforce and IT engineers made it a top global destination for business process outsourcing (BPO).

29. What effect has globalization had on job security for factory workers in unorganized sectors?

  • A. Employment has become highly flexible and insecure
  • B. Life-long government job guarantees were created
  • C. Pension benefits doubled for all workers
  • D. Work hours were reduced to 4 hours per day

Answer: A. Employment has become highly flexible and insecure

Explanation: Due to fierce competition and flexible labour policies, workers are often employed temporarily without social security benefits.

30. Besides movement of goods, services, and investments, globalization also involves the movement of:

  • A. People between countries for education, employment, and better living
  • B. Land and rivers across borders
  • C. Sovereign territories
  • D. Permanent physical geographic boundaries

Answer: A. People between countries for education, employment, and better living

Explanation: Globalization encompasses the migration of skilled and unskilled labor across nations seeking jobs, education, and better livelihoods.
