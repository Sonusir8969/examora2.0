Chapter 7: Physical Features of India 30 Questions

1. Which is the oldest landmass of India?

  • A. The Himalayas
  • B. The Peninsular Plateau
  • C. The Northern Plains
  • D. The Coastal Plains

Answer: B. The Peninsular Plateau

Explanation: The Peninsular Plateau is the oldest landmass in India, composed of ancient crystalline, igneous, and metamorphic rocks.

2. The Himalayas belong to which type of mountains?

  • A. Block Mountains
  • B. Fold Mountains
  • C. Volcanic Mountains
  • D. Residual Mountains

Answer: B. Fold Mountains

Explanation: The Himalayas are young fold mountains formed by the tectonic collision between the Indian and Eurasian plates.

3. The Himalayan mountain range is divided into how many distinct parallel ranges?

  • A. Two
  • B. Three
  • C. Four
  • D. Five

Answer: B. Three

Explanation: The Himalayas are divided longitudinally into three parallel ranges: the Great Himalayas, the Lesser Himalayas, and the Outer Himalayas.

4. The world's highest peak, Mount Everest, lies in which range?

  • A. The Great Himalayas (Himadri)
  • B. The Lesser Himalayas (Himachal)
  • C. The Outer Himalayas (Shivalik)
  • D. The Purvanchal Hills

Answer: A. The Great Himalayas (Himadri)

Explanation: Mount Everest, the highest peak in the world, is located in the Great Himalayas, also known as the Himadri range.

5. Which is the highest peak in India?

  • A. Mt. Everest
  • B. Kanchenjunga
  • C. Nanda Devi
  • D. Kailash

Answer: B. Kanchenjunga

Explanation: Kanchenjunga, located in Sikkim, is the highest peak in India and the third highest in the world.

6. The famous 'Valley of Flowers' is located in which state of India?

  • A. Himachal Pradesh
  • B. Uttarakhand
  • C. Jammu and Kashmir
  • D. Sikkim

Answer: B. Uttarakhand

Explanation: The Valley of Flowers, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is located in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand.

7. The Northern Plains of India are formed by the sediments deposited by which two major rivers?

  • A. Ganga and Yamuna
  • B. Indus and Ganga
  • C. Godavari and Krishna
  • D. Narmada and Tapi

Answer: B. Indus and Ganga

Explanation: The fertile Northern Plains are primarily formed by the alluvial deposits of the Indus river system (West) and the Ganga river system (East).

8. The western part of the Northern Plains is known as the ________.

  • A. Ganga Plain
  • B. Punjab Plain
  • C. Brahmaputra Plain
  • D. Deccan Plain

Answer: B. Punjab Plain

Explanation: The western part of the Northern Plains, fed by the Indus and its tributaries, is called the Punjab Plain.

9. Which river system forms the eastern part of the Northern Plains?

  • A. Indus System
  • B. Ganga-Brahmaputra System
  • C. Narmada System
  • D. Godavari System

Answer: B. Ganga-Brahmaputra System

Explanation: The Ganga-Brahmaputra river system forms the vast eastern part of the Northern Plains.

10. Which physical feature separates India from China?

  • A. The Deccan Plateau
  • B. The Himalayas
  • C. The Vindhyas
  • D. The Western Ghats

Answer: B. The Himalayas

Explanation: The mighty Himalayan mountain range acts as the natural northern boundary separating India from China.

11. The Western Ghats are also known as the ________.

  • A. Satpura Range
  • B. Sahyadri Range
  • C. Vindhya Range
  • D. Aravalli Range

Answer: B. Sahyadri Range

Explanation: The Western Ghats are locally known as the Sahyadri range in Maharashtra and the Nilgiri hills in Tamil Nadu.

12. Which is the highest peak in the Western Ghats?

  • A. Anamudi
  • B. Kanchenjunga
  • C. Mount Abu
  • D. Dhaulagiri

Answer: A. Anamudi

Explanation: Anamudi, located in the Idukki district of Kerala, is the highest peak in the Western Ghats.

13. Which river flows westward into the Arabian Sea?

  • A. Godavari
  • B. Krishna
  • C. Narmada
  • D. Kaveri

Answer: C. Narmada

Explanation: The Narmada and Tapi are the only major rivers in the Peninsular Plateau that flow westward into the Arabian Sea.

14. The largest delta in the world is formed by which two rivers?

  • A. Ganga and Godavari
  • B. Ganga and Brahmaputra
  • C. Mahanadi and Godavari
  • D. Krishna and Kaveri

Answer: B. Ganga and Brahmaputra

Explanation: The Sundarbans Delta, the largest delta globally, is formed by the Ganga and Brahmaputra rivers in West Bengal and Bangladesh.

15. Which is the oldest mountain range in India?

  • A. Himalayas
  • B. Aravalli Range
  • C. Western Ghats
  • D. Satpura Range

Answer: B. Aravalli Range

Explanation: The Aravalli Range in Rajasthan is the oldest fold mountain range in India.

16. The eastern coastal plain of India is known as the ________.

  • A. Konkan Coast
  • B. Coromandel Coast
  • C. Malabar Coast
  • D. Kannada Coast

Answer: B. Coromandel Coast

Explanation: The eastern coastal plain stretching from the Mahanadi delta to the Kaveri delta is known as the Coromandel Coast.

17. Which mountain pass connects India to the Kailash Mansarovar region?

  • A. Nathu La
  • B. Shipkila
  • C. Lipulekh
  • D. Zojila

Answer: C. Lipulekh

Explanation: The Lipulekh pass in Uttarakhand connects India to Tibet and the Kailash Mansarovar region.

18. The Peninsular Plateau is divided into two main parts by which river?

  • A. Godavari
  • B. Narmada
  • C. Tapi
  • D. Krishna

Answer: B. Narmada

Explanation: The Narmada river flows through the rift valley and divides the Peninsular Plateau into the Malwa Plateau (North) and the Deccan Plateau (South).

19. The Coastal Plains are divided into how many main parts in India?

  • A. One
  • B. Two
  • C. Three
  • D. Four

Answer: B. Two

Explanation: The coastal plains of India are divided into two main sections: the Western Coastal Plain and the Eastern Coastal Plain.

20. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are believed to be an extension of which mountain range?

  • A. Himalayas
  • B. Aravallis
  • C. Western Ghats
  • D. Satpuras

Answer: A. Himalayas

Explanation: The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are geologically considered an extension of the Himalayan mountain range.

21. Which of the following is NOT a physical division of India?

  • A. The Himalayas
  • B. The Northern Plains
  • C. The Deccan Plateau
  • D. The Thar Desert

Answer: D. The Thar Desert

Explanation: The Thar Desert is a sub-region within the Northern Plains. The main physical divisions are Mountains, Plains, Plateaus, and Coastal/Islands.

22. The highest peak of the Eastern Ghats is ________.

  • A. Mahendragiri
  • B. Anamudi
  • C. Kanchenjunga
  • D. Nanda Devi

Answer: A. Mahendragiri

Explanation: Mahendragiri is the highest peak of the Eastern Ghats, located in the state of Odisha.

23. The 'Purvanchal Hills' are located in which part of India?

  • A. North-eastern India
  • B. Southern India
  • C. Western India
  • D. Northern India

Answer: A. North-eastern India

Explanation: The Purvanchal hills extend in the north-eastern part of India, covering states like Nagaland, Manipur, and Mizoram.

24. The famous Chilika Lake is situated on which coast of India?

  • A. Western Coast
  • B. Eastern Coast
  • C. Northern Coast
  • D. Southern Coast

Answer: B. Eastern Coast

Explanation: Chilika Lake, the largest brackish water lake in Asia, is located on the eastern coast of India in Odisha.

25. The river Mahanadi flows into which water body?

  • A. Arabian Sea
  • B. Bay of Bengal
  • C. Indian Ocean
  • D. Gulf of Kutch

Answer: B. Bay of Bengal

Explanation: The Mahanadi river originates in Chhattisgarh and flows eastwards to empty into the Bay of Bengal.

26. The river Krishna originates from which mountain range?

  • A. Western Ghats
  • B. Eastern Ghats
  • C. Himalayas
  • D. Satpura

Answer: A. Western Ghats

Explanation: The Krishna river originates from the Western Ghats near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra and flows eastward.

27. The 'Malwa Plateau' lies to the north of which river?

  • A. Godavari
  • B. Narmada
  • C. Tapi
  • D. Krishna

Answer: B. Narmada

Explanation: The Malwa Plateau lies to the north of the Narmada river and to the south of the Bundelkhand region.

28. The Himalayan range extends for about ________ km in length.

  • A. 1,200 km
  • B. 2,400 km
  • C. 4,000 km
  • D. 500 km

Answer: B. 2,400 km

Explanation: The length of the Himalayan mountain range is approximately 2,400 kilometers from the Indus gorge in the west to the Brahmaputra gorge in the east.

29. The 'Rann of Kutch' is a famous ________.

  • A. River
  • B. Mountain
  • C. Salt marsh
  • D. Lake

Answer: C. Salt marsh

Explanation: The Great Rann of Kutch in Gujarat is a vast seasonal salt marsh that is flooded during monsoons.

30. The Nilgiri hills are situated at the meeting point of which two Ghats?

  • A. Western and Eastern Ghats
  • B. Himalayas and Aravalis
  • C. Satpura and Vindhyas
  • D. Shivaliks and Himadri

Answer: A. Western and Eastern Ghats

Explanation: The Nilgiri hills mark the junction where the Western Ghats and the Eastern Ghats meet in the southern part of India.
