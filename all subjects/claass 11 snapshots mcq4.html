Suppl-4: Albert Einstein at School 25 Questions

1. Who is the author of the story 'Albert Einstein at School'?

  • A. Patrick Pringle
  • B. Masti Venkatesha Iyengar
  • C. Marga Minco
  • D. William Saroyan

Answer: A. Patrick Pringle

Explanation: The chapter is an extract from Patrick Pringle's book 'The Young Einstein'.

2. In which city was Albert Einstein attending high school?

  • A. Munich
  • B. Berlin
  • C. Frankfurt
  • D. Vienna

Answer: A. Munich

Explanation: Einstein was studying at a gymnasium school in Munich, Germany.

3. What was the name of Albert Einstein's History teacher?

  • A. Mr. Braun
  • B. Mr. Koch
  • C. Dr. Ernest Weil
  • D. Mr. Yuri

Answer: A. Mr. Braun

Explanation: Mr. Braun was the History teacher who argued with Albert over learning dates of historical battles.

4. Why did Albert argue with his History teacher?

  • A. He believed learning facts and dates was useless compared to understanding ideas
  • B. He didn't like History as a subject at all
  • C. He failed in the previous history examination
  • D. The teacher scolded him for coming late

Answer: A. He believed learning facts and dates was useless compared to understanding ideas

Explanation: Albert argued that dates could be looked up in books; learning the reasons behind wars was far more important.

5. According to Albert, what was true education?

  • A. Learning ideas, reasons, and concepts rather than memorizing facts
  • B. Getting top marks in all examinations
  • C. Memorizing textbook definitions line by line
  • D. Following all school regulations blindly

Answer: A. Learning ideas, reasons, and concepts rather than memorizing facts

Explanation: Albert stated that ideas, not facts, constituted real education.

6. Who was Albert's best and only friend in Munich?

  • A. Yuri
  • B. Ernest Weil
  • C. Mr. Koch
  • D. Hans

Answer: A. Yuri

Explanation: Yuri was Albert's loyal friend who supported him and introduced him to Dr. Ernest Weil.

7. Who was Elsa?

  • A. Albert's cousin living in Berlin
  • B. Albert's sister
  • C. A classmate in Munich
  • D. The landlady's daughter

Answer: A. Albert's cousin living in Berlin

Explanation: Elsa was Albert's cousin who lived in Berlin where her father had his business.

8. What advice did Elsa give Albert regarding passing his school exams?

  • A. Just repeat facts like a parrot in exams without needing to understand them
  • B. Leave the school immediately
  • C. Hire a personal tutor
  • D. Study only Science and Mathematics

Answer: A. Just repeat facts like a parrot in exams without needing to understand them

Explanation: Elsa assured him that anyone could pass exams by simply learning text by heart without understanding.

9. What musical instrument did Albert love to play to relax?

  • A. Violin
  • B. Piano
  • C. Flute
  • D. Guitar

Answer: A. Violin

Explanation: Albert enjoyed playing his violin until his landlady asked him to stop because of the noise.

10. Why did the landlady ask Albert to stop playing his violin?

  • A. She claimed the noise got on her nerves along with her screaming children
  • B. She was allergic to music
  • C. Albert played very poorly
  • D. It was prohibited by local law

Answer: A. She claimed the noise got on her nerves along with her screaming children

Explanation: The landlady complained that there was enough noise from her children and stopped Albert from playing violin.

11. What plan did Albert invent to leave his school legally?

  • A. Get a medical certificate declaring a nervous breakdown
  • B. Fail in all his term examinations
  • C. Run away without telling anyone
  • D. Get expelled by insulting teachers

Answer: A. Get a medical certificate declaring a nervous breakdown

Explanation: Albert wanted a medical reference stating he suffered from a nervous breakdown and needed 6 months off.

12. Who was Dr. Ernest Weil?

  • A. A newly qualified doctor recommended by Yuri
  • B. Albert's family doctor
  • C. The school physician
  • D. Elsa's uncle

Answer: A. A newly qualified doctor recommended by Yuri

Explanation: Dr. Ernest Weil had recently qualified as a medical practitioner and was a friend of Yuri.

13. Why did Dr. Ernest Weil issue the medical certificate to Albert easily?

  • A. He realized Albert was genuinely near a nervous breakdown due to school stress
  • B. Yuri paid him a huge fee
  • C. He was related to Albert
  • D. He hated the school system himself

Answer: A. He realized Albert was genuinely near a nervous breakdown due to school stress

Explanation: Dr. Weil understood that Albert's distress over school was so real that he was indeed close to a breakdown.

14. Who was Mr. Koch in the story?

  • A. The Mathematics teacher
  • B. The History teacher
  • C. The Head Teacher
  • D. The Physics teacher

Answer: A. The Mathematics teacher

Explanation: Mr. Koch was the Mathematics teacher who admired Albert's exceptional mathematical talent.

15. What remark did Mr. Koch give in Albert's reference letter?

  • A. Albert knew more math than he did and was ready to join a university
  • B. Albert was a weak student who needed extra tuition
  • C. Albert was disobedient and uncooperative
  • D. Albert should pursue music instead of science

Answer: A. Albert knew more math than he did and was ready to join a university

Explanation: Mr. Koch stated that Albert had mastered all school math and could soon teach the teacher himself.

16. What happened when Albert went to submit his medical certificate to the Head Teacher?

  • A. The Head Teacher summoned him to expel him from school first
  • B. The Head Teacher accepted the certificate gladly
  • C. The Head Teacher punished him with detention
  • D. The Head Teacher called his father

Answer: A. The Head Teacher summoned him to expel him from school first

Explanation: Before Albert could show the certificate, the Head Teacher called him to state that he was being expelled.

17. What reason did the Head Teacher give for expelling Albert?

  • A. His presence made it impossible for teachers to teach and students to learn
  • B. He failed in all examinations
  • C. He stole books from the library
  • D. He broke school furniture

Answer: A. His presence made it impossible for teachers to teach and students to learn

Explanation: The Head Teacher claimed Albert's constant rebellion disturbed the class environment completely.

18. Where was Albert planning to go after leaving school in Munich?

  • A. Milan, Italy (where his parents lived)
  • B. Berlin, Germany
  • C. London, England
  • D. Zurich, Switzerland

Answer: A. Milan, Italy (where his parents lived)

Explanation: Albert wanted to move to Milan, Italy, to join his parents and pursue higher studies in mathematics.

19. How did Albert feel while walking out of the school for the final time?

  • A. He didn't turn back to look at the school and wished goodbye only to Yuri
  • B. He cried emotionally
  • C. He thanked the Head Teacher politely
  • D. He said goodbye to all his classmates

Answer: A. He didn't turn back to look at the school and wished goodbye only to Yuri

Explanation: Albert hated the school so much that he didn't look back once and met only Yuri before leaving.

20. What subject was Albert reading at home when he wasn't studying school subjects?

  • A. Science and Geology books
  • B. History novels
  • C. Philosophy
  • D. Poetry

Answer: A. Science and Geology books

Explanation: Albert enjoyed reading science books, even though science wasn't taught well at his school grade level.

21. What was the slum condition where Albert lived in Munich like?

  • A. Squalor, dirt, domestic violence, and screaming children
  • B. Peaceful and quiet atmosphere
  • C. Clean and modern rooms
  • D. High crime rate with police raids

Answer: A. Squalor, dirt, domestic violence, and screaming children

Explanation: Albert hated his quarters due to dirt, bad food, and constant domestic violence by the landlady and her husband.

22. What did Yuri tell Albert about the students in his hostel/quarters?

  • A. Students fought duels and one was killed recently with a scar worn proudly
  • B. Students were all hard-working and polite
  • C. Students spent all time playing sports
  • D. Students were preparing for medical exams

Answer: A. Students fought duels and one was killed recently with a scar worn proudly

Explanation: Yuri shared that student life had its own violence, where duel scars were treated as badges of honor.

23. How much money did Dr. Ernest Weil charge Albert for the medical certificate?

  • A. Nothing (He only asked Albert to take Yuri out for a meal)
  • B. 50 Marks
  • C. 100 Marks
  • D. 10 Marks

Answer: A. Nothing (He only asked Albert to take Yuri out for a meal)

Explanation: Dr. Weil refused any fee and simply requested Albert to buy meal/dinner for Yuri.

24. What ironic situation happened regarding Albert's medical certificate?

  • A. He was expelled before he could even present the medical certificate
  • B. The certificate was torn by his teacher
  • C. The doctor refused to sign it at the last minute
  • D. His father burned the certificate

Answer: A. He was expelled before he could even present the medical certificate

Explanation: Ironically, Albert spent effort getting a certificate to leave, but the school expelled him anyway.

25. What main flaw of the traditional education system is highlighted in this chapter?

  • A. Rote learning and rigid rules suppress individual talent and creative thinking
  • B. Lack of sports facilities in schools
  • C. Expensive tuition fees
  • D. High difficulty level of mathematics

Answer: A. Rote learning and rigid rules suppress individual talent and creative thinking

Explanation: The story criticizes educational systems that prioritize rote memorization over critical thinking and individuality.
