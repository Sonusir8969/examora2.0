Ch-2: Human Reproduction 12 Questions

1. Spermatogenesis, the process of sperm formation, occurs within the:

  • A. Seminiferous tubules of the testis
  • B. Epididymis
  • C. Vas deferens
  • D. Prostate gland

Answer: A. Seminiferous tubules of the testis

Explanation: The seminiferous tubules contain the germinal epithelium where spermatogonia undergo meiosis to eventually form sperm.

2. The hormone primarily responsible for maintaining the endometrium during pregnancy is:

  • A. Progesterone
  • B. Estrogen alone
  • C. FSH
  • D. LH

Answer: A. Progesterone

Explanation: Progesterone, mainly secreted by the corpus luteum and later the placenta, supports and maintains the uterine lining throughout pregnancy.

3. Fertilization in humans normally takes place in the:

  • A. Ampullary region of the fallopian tube
  • B. Uterus
  • C. Ovary
  • D. Vagina

Answer: A. Ampullary region of the fallopian tube

Explanation: The ampulla, the widest part of the fallopian tube, is the usual site where the sperm meets and fertilizes the ovum.

4. The structure formed in the ovary after ovulation, which secretes progesterone, is the:

  • A. Corpus luteum
  • B. Graafian follicle
  • C. Corpus albicans
  • D. Primary follicle

Answer: A. Corpus luteum

Explanation: After the release of the ovum, the ruptured follicle transforms into the corpus luteum, which secretes progesterone.

5. The menstrual cycle in human females is primarily regulated by:

  • A. Hypothalamic, pituitary and ovarian hormones acting together
  • B. Pituitary hormones alone
  • C. Ovarian hormones alone
  • D. Thyroid hormones

Answer: A. Hypothalamic, pituitary and ovarian hormones acting together

Explanation: The menstrual cycle results from a coordinated interplay between hormones released from the hypothalamus, pituitary gland, and ovaries.

6. Implantation of the developing blastocyst normally occurs in the:

  • A. Endometrium of the uterus
  • B. Fallopian tube
  • C. Ovary
  • D. Cervix

Answer: A. Endometrium of the uterus

Explanation: The blastocyst attaches to and embeds itself within the endometrial lining of the uterus during implantation.

7. The umbilical cord functions to connect the developing fetus to the:

  • A. Placenta
  • B. Uterine wall directly
  • C. Amniotic sac only
  • D. Ovary

Answer: A. Placenta

Explanation: The umbilical cord carries blood vessels that connect the fetus to the placenta, enabling nutrient and gas exchange.

8. The general process of formation of gametes in both males and females is termed:

  • A. Gametogenesis
  • B. Fertilization
  • C. Cleavage
  • D. Parturition

Answer: A. Gametogenesis

Explanation: Gametogenesis is the collective term describing the formation of sperm (spermatogenesis) and ova (oogenesis).

9. Human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG), detected in early pregnancy tests, is secreted by the:

  • A. Developing placenta/trophoblast
  • B. Ovary
  • C. Pituitary gland
  • D. Testis

Answer: A. Developing placenta/trophoblast

Explanation: hCG is produced by the trophoblastic cells of the developing embryo shortly after implantation and helps maintain the corpus luteum.

10. Parturition, or childbirth, is triggered by hormonal and physical signals originating from the fully developed:

  • A. Fetus and placenta
  • B. Ovary alone
  • C. Uterus wall alone
  • D. Pituitary gland alone

Answer: A. Fetus and placenta

Explanation: Signals from the mature fetus and placenta initiate the mild uterine contractions that eventually lead to the process of labour.

11. The zona pellucida is a protective, transparent layer surrounding the:

  • A. Ovum (egg)
  • B. Sperm
  • C. Blastocyst wall exclusively
  • D. Uterus

Answer: A. Ovum (egg)

Explanation: The zona pellucida is a glycoprotein layer that surrounds the secondary oocyte and plays an important role during fertilization.

12. Repeated mitotic divisions of the zygote following fertilization lead to the formation of the:

  • A. Blastocyst
  • B. Corpus luteum
  • C. Follicle
  • D. Placenta directly

Answer: A. Blastocyst

Explanation: Through the process of cleavage, the zygote divides repeatedly to form a morula and eventually a blastocyst before implantation.
