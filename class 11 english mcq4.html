Ch-4: The Summer of the Beautiful White Horse 30 Questions

1. Who is the author of 'The Summer of the Beautiful White Horse'?

  • A. William Saroyan
  • B. Marga Minco
  • C. J. B. Priestley
  • D. A. R. Williams

Answer: A. William Saroyan

Explanation: The story is written by American-Armenian writer William Saroyan.

2. Who is the narrator of the story?

  • A. Aram
  • B. Mourad
  • C. Uncle Khosrove
  • D. John Byro

Answer: A. Aram

Explanation: The story is narrated from nine-year-old Aram's perspective as he recalls a childhood memory.

3. How old was Aram when the events of the story took place?

  • A. 9 years old
  • B. 13 years old
  • C. 11 years old
  • D. 7 years old

Answer: A. 9 years old

Explanation: Aram was nine years old and his cousin Mourad was thirteen.

4. To which Armenian tribe did Aram and Mourad belong?

  • A. Garoghlanian family
  • B. Assyrian family
  • C. Armenian royal family
  • D. Byro family

Answer: A. Garoghlanian family

Explanation: They belonged to the Garoghlanian tribe, known across centuries for their unshakeable honesty.

5. What was the Garoghlanian tribe famous for, even in extreme poverty?

  • A. Trust and honesty
  • B. Wealth and horse riding
  • C. Military bravery
  • D. Agricultural trade

Answer: A. Trust and honesty

Explanation: For over eleven centuries, their tribe was legendary for their strict adherence to honesty and integrity.

6. What time did Mourad wake Aram up at his window?

  • A. At 4:00 AM in the morning
  • B. At midnight
  • C. At 6:00 AM in the morning
  • D. At 2:00 AM in the night

Answer: A. At 4:00 AM in the morning

Explanation: Mourad tapped on Aram's room window at four in the morning when dawn was just breaking.

7. What was Aram's initial reaction upon seeing Mourad outside his window?

  • A. He couldn't believe his eyes because Mourad was sitting on a beautiful white horse
  • B. He thought Mourad had come to fight
  • C. He was scared that Mourad was sick
  • D. He was annoyed for being woken up early

Answer: A. He couldn't believe his eyes because Mourad was sitting on a beautiful white horse

Explanation: Aram rubbed his eyes in disbelief because his poor family could never afford a horse.

8. Why did Aram think that Mourad must have stolen the horse?

  • A. Because their tribe was too poor to buy one
  • B. Because Mourad told him he stole it
  • C. Because John Byro was chasing him
  • D. Because Mourad was known as a thief

Answer: A. Because their tribe was too poor to buy one

Explanation: Since no member of the Garoghlanian family could afford a horse, the only possibility was that Mourad had stolen it.

9. How did Aram rationalize that stealing a horse for a ride wasn't actually stealing?

  • A. Stealing for horse riding wasn't the same as stealing money unless they offered to sell it
  • B. Because they were borrowing it from a rich farmer
  • C. Because the horse belonged to their family's ancestor
  • D. Because they intended to keep it for only one hour

Answer: A. Stealing for horse riding wasn't the same as stealing money unless they offered to sell it

Explanation: Aram justified that it wouldn't be true stealing until they tried to sell the horse for commercial gain.

10. What character trait was Mourad considered to have inherited from Uncle Khosrove?

  • A. The crazy streak of the tribe
  • B. His physical strength
  • C. His love for poetry
  • D. His musical talent

Answer: A. The crazy streak of the tribe

Explanation: Mourad was considered the natural descendant of the crazy streak in their family, previously embodied by Uncle Khosrove.

11. What was Uncle Khosrove's famous catchphrase for every problem?

  • A. "It is no harm; pay no attention to it."
  • B. "Honesty is our only wealth."
  • C. "Life is short, do what you love."
  • D. "Don't worry, all will be well."

Answer: A. "It is no harm; pay no attention to it."

Explanation: Uncle Khosrove would roar "It is no harm; pay no attention to it" to stop anyone from talking.

12. What happened when Uncle Khosrove's son Arak ran to the barber shop to report their house was on fire?

  • A. Khosrove roared, "It is no harm; pay no attention to it!"
  • B. Khosrove rushed home immediately
  • C. Khosrove called the fire station
  • D. Khosrove fainted in shock

Answer: A. Khosrove roared, "It is no harm; pay no attention to it!"

Explanation: Even when told his own house was burning, Uncle Khosrove bellowed his iconic phrase to silence his son.

13. Where did Mourad and Aram take the horse to ride during the morning?

  • A. Behind their house on Walnut Avenue near orchards and irrigation ditches
  • B. In the middle of the city market
  • C. At the local school playground
  • D. On the desert highway

Answer: A. Behind their house on Walnut Avenue near orchards and irrigation ditches

Explanation: They lived at the edge of town on Walnut Avenue, surrounded by country roads, vineyards, and orchards.

14. What happened when Aram tried to ride the horse alone for the first time?

  • A. The horse ran wild into a vineyard, leaped over vines, and threw him off
  • B. The horse stood still and refused to move
  • C. Aram rode it smoothly like a expert
  • D. The horse ran straight back to its owner

Answer: A. The horse ran wild into a vineyard, leaped over vines, and threw him off

Explanation: The horse reared up, leaped over seven vines in Fetvajian's vineyard, threw Aram off, and ran away.

15. What special claim did Mourad repeatedly make about himself?

  • A. "I have a way with horses/dogs/birds."
  • B. "I am the fastest runner in town."
  • C. "I am destined to be rich."
  • D. "I know how to train wild animals."

Answer: A. "I have a way with horses/dogs/birds."

Explanation: Mourad consistently boasted that he had an understanding way with animals like horses and birds.

16. Where did the boys hide the horse during the day?

  • A. In the barn of a deserted vineyard belonging to Fetvajian
  • B. In Aram's backyard shed
  • C. In a cave in the mountains
  • D. In John Byro's stable

Answer: A. In the barn of a deserted vineyard belonging to Fetvajian

Explanation: They hid the horse in a barn at a deserted vineyard that once belonged to a farmer named Fetvajian.

17. What food did they leave for the horse in the deserted barn?

  • A. Oats and alfalfa
  • B. Fresh grass and apples
  • C. Wheat and corn
  • D. Bread and milk

Answer: A. Oats and alfalfa

Explanation: Mourad fed the horse oats and alfalfa in the barn to keep it healthy.

18. Who came to visit Aram's home in the afternoon on that day?

  • A. John Byro, an Assyrian farmer
  • B. Fetvajian, the vineyard owner
  • C. The local sheriff
  • D. Mourad's father Zorab

Answer: A. John Byro, an Assyrian farmer

Explanation: John Byro, an Assyrian farmer who had learned Armenian out of loneliness, visited Aram's mother.

19. What loss did John Byro report to Aram's mother and Uncle Khosrove?

  • A. His white horse had been stolen a month ago
  • B. His surrey carriage had been broken
  • C. His vineyard crops were ruined
  • D. His dog had run away

Answer: A. His white horse had been stolen a month ago

Explanation: John Byro grieved that his white horse costing sixty dollars had been missing for a month.

20. How much had John Byro paid for his white horse?

  • A. Sixty dollars
  • B. One hundred dollars
  • C. Fifty dollars
  • D. Twenty dollars

Answer: A. Sixty dollars

Explanation: John Byro mentioned that he had bought the horse for sixty dollars.

21. How did Uncle Khosrove respond when John Byro complained about his lost horse?

  • A. He shouted, "It's no harm! What is the loss of a horse?"
  • B. He offered to help search for it
  • C. He accused John Byro of lying
  • D. He expressed sympathy and wept

Answer: A. He shouted, "It's no harm! What is the loss of a horse?"

Explanation: Uncle Khosrove became irritated and shouted that losing a horse was nothing compared to losing homeland.

22. Why was a surrey useless to John Byro without his horse?

  • A. A surrey is a horse-drawn carriage that cannot move without a horse
  • B. The wheels were broken
  • C. It had no driver
  • D. It was stolen along with the horse

Answer: A. A surrey is a horse-drawn carriage that cannot move without a horse

Explanation: A surrey is a light four-wheeled carriage pulled by a horse.

23. What was Mourad doing when Aram ran to tell him about John Byro's visit?

  • A. Sitting under a peach tree repairing the injured wing of a robin bird
  • B. Riding the white horse
  • C. Sleeping in the barn
  • D. Feeding the dogs

Answer: A. Sitting under a peach tree repairing the injured wing of a robin bird

Explanation: Aram found Mourad gently nursing a young robin bird that had hurt its wing.

24. How long did Aram plead with Mourad to keep the horse before returning it?

  • A. At least a year until Aram learned to ride
  • B. Two years
  • C. One week
  • D. Forever

Answer: A. At least a year until Aram learned to ride

Explanation: Aram begged Mourad not to return the horse for at least a year so he could master riding it.

25. What maximum time limit did Mourad set for keeping the horse?

  • A. Six months
  • B. One year
  • C. One month
  • D. Two weeks

Answer: A. Six months

Explanation: Mourad declared that they must return the horse to its true owner in six months at the latest.

26. How long had Mourad already had the horse before he showed it to Aram?

  • A. One month
  • B. One day
  • C. One week
  • D. Two months

Answer: A. One month

Explanation: John Byro mentioned his horse was stolen a month ago, showing Mourad had kept it for a month secretly.

27. What happened when the boys unexpectedly ran into John Byro on the road to the vineyard?

  • A. John Byro inspected the horse, examined its teeth, but refused to accuse them because of their family's reputation
  • B. John Byro immediately called the police
  • C. John Byro snatched the horse back by force
  • D. The boys ran away leaving the horse behind

Answer: A. John Byro inspected the horse, examined its teeth, but refused to accuse them because of their family's reputation

Explanation: Byro looked into the horse's mouth tooth for tooth, noting it was the twin of his horse, but believed their family's fame for honesty.

28. Why didn't John Byro accuse the boys of stealing his horse even after looking at its teeth?

  • A. He trusted the Garoghlanian family's legendary honesty over his own eyes
  • B. He was afraid of Uncle Khosrove
  • C. He wasn't sure if it was his horse
  • D. The boys lied and gave a fake receipt

Answer: A. He trusted the Garoghlanian family's legendary honesty over his own eyes

Explanation: Byro said, "A suspicious man would believe his eyes instead of his heart," but he trusted their family's moral integrity.

29. When and where did the boys return the horse to John Byro?

  • A. Early next morning to John Byro's vineyard stable
  • B. In the town square at noon
  • C. They left it on the public road
  • D. They brought it directly to John Byro's house

Answer: A. Early next morning to John Byro's vineyard stable

Explanation: Touched by John Byro's faith in their family, they quietly took the horse to Byro's barn early the following morning.

30. What change did John Byro notice in his horse after it was returned?

  • A. It was stronger and better-tempered than ever before
  • B. It had become weak and lazy
  • C. It had sustained injuries on its legs
  • D. It refused to pull the surrey

Answer: A. It was stronger and better-tempered than ever before

Explanation: John Byro happily reported to Aram's mother that the horse was healthier, stronger, and better-behaved than before.
