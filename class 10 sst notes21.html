Class 10 English Exam Preparation Strategy

Key Preparation Tips:

• Prose Section: Focus on major plot points, central themes, and detailed character sketches (e.g., Lencho, Valli, Natalia, Lomov).

• Poetry Analysis: Identify rhyming schemes and literary devices (metaphor, personification, alliteration, oxymoron, irony) for extract-based questions.

• Supplementary Reader: Prepare value-based analytical questions regarding character transformations (e.g., Hari Singh, Bholi, Matilda Loisel).

• JAC & State Board Focus: Practice objective multiple-choice questions (MCQs) alongside descriptive long answer format for maximum score in board exams.
