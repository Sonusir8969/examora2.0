Ch-9: My Mother at Sixty-Six 5 Questions

1. Who is the poet of 'My Mother at Sixty-Six'?

  • A. Kamala Das
  • B. Sarojini Naidu
  • C. Adrienne Rich
  • D. Toru Dutt

Answer: A. Kamala Das

Explanation: 'My Mother at Sixty-Six' is a poem written by the Indian poet Kamala Das, reflecting on themes of ageing, mortality, and the mother-daughter bond.

2. What triggers the poet's sudden realization and fear about her mother's mortality in the poem?

  • A. A letter from her mother
  • B. Seeing her mother's pale, aged, and seemingly lifeless face while driving her to the airport
  • C. A phone call
  • D. A dream

Answer: B. Seeing her mother's pale, aged, and seemingly lifeless face while driving her to the airport

Explanation: While driving to the airport, the poet glances at her sleeping mother and is struck by her mother's pale, sagging, corpse-like appearance, which triggers a deep fear of losing her.

3. What does the imagery of 'young trees sprinting' outside the car window symbolize in contrast to the mother?

  • A. The mother's youthfulness
  • B. The vitality and continuity of life, contrasting sharply with the mother's fading energy and approaching old age
  • C. The poet's own childhood
  • D. The airport's busy environment

Answer: B. The vitality and continuity of life, contrasting sharply with the mother's fading energy and approaching old age

Explanation: The rapidly moving young trees represent life, vigor, and youth rushing past, standing in stark, poignant contrast to the poet's mother, who appears to be fading with age.

4. How does the poet try to hide her inner turmoil from her mother before boarding the flight?

  • A. By crying openly
  • B. By smiling and saying 'See you soon, Amma', masking her true fear and sadness
  • C. By ignoring her mother completely
  • D. By arguing with her mother

Answer: B. By smiling and saying 'See you soon, Amma', masking her true fear and sadness

Explanation: Despite her inner anguish and fear, the poet puts on a brave, cheerful smile and offers a customary parting phrase, hiding her true emotions from her mother.

5. What is the central theme of 'My Mother at Sixty-Six'?

  • A. The joy of travelling
  • B. The pain of watching a parent age, and the universal fear of loss and mortality
  • C. The beauty of nature
  • D. The importance of punctuality

Answer: B. The pain of watching a parent age, and the universal fear of loss and mortality

Explanation: The poem poignantly captures a daughter's realization of her mother's mortality and the deep, universal anxiety associated with ageing and the eventual loss of loved ones.
