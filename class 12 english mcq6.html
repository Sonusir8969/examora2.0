Ch-6: Poets and Pancakes 5 Questions

1. Who is the author of 'Poets and Pancakes'?

  • A. R.K. Narayan
  • B. Asokamitran
  • C. Khushwant Singh
  • D. Ruskin Bond

Answer: B. Asokamitran

Explanation: 'Poets and Pancakes' is written by Asokamitran, drawing on his personal experiences working at Gemini Studios, a prominent South Indian film studio.

2. What is Gemini Studios, the setting of the chapter?

  • A. A publishing house
  • B. A famous film studio in Madras (Chennai)
  • C. A university
  • D. A newspaper office

Answer: B. A famous film studio in Madras (Chennai)

Explanation: Gemini Studios was a well-known film production studio located in Madras, and the chapter is a satirical, anecdotal account of the people and happenings there.

3. What unusual method did the make-up department at Gemini Studios reportedly use on actors, regardless of their skin tone?

  • A. No make-up was used at all
  • B. A thick layer of poster paint was applied to give a uniform look, even on already dark-skinned actors
  • C. Only natural oils were used
  • D. Digital effects replaced make-up entirely

Answer: B. A thick layer of poster paint was applied to give a uniform look, even on already dark-skinned actors

Explanation: The chapter humorously recounts how the make-up department used a heavy layer of poster color paint on all actors' faces uniformly, regardless of their natural skin tone, following inherited outdated practices.

4. What does the author use the anecdotes in 'Poets and Pancakes' to satirize?

  • A. The government's economic policy
  • B. The superficiality, hierarchy, and idiosyncrasies of the film industry and the people within it
  • C. Agricultural practices in India
  • D. The education system

Answer: B. The superficiality, hierarchy, and idiosyncrasies of the film industry and the people within it

Explanation: Through witty, ironic anecdotes about the studio's employees—from the make-up man to the visiting poet—the author gently satirizes the vanity, pretensions, and quirks found within the film industry.

5. Which real Tamil poet is referenced in connection with a statue-related anecdote in the chapter?

  • A. Subramania Bharati
  • B. Bharathidasan
  • C. Kambar
  • D. Thiruvalluvar

Answer: A. Subramania Bharati

Explanation: The chapter includes an anecdote involving the unveiling of a statue of the great Tamil poet Subramania Bharati, used by the author to comment wryly on public figures and ceremonies.
