Ch-7: Triangles 30 Questions

1. Two figures are said to be congruent if they have the same:

  • A. Shape only
  • B. Size only
  • C. Shape and size
  • D. Area only

Answer: C. Shape and size

Explanation: Congruent figures are exact copies of each other, having identical shape and identical size.

2. Which of the following is NOT a criterion for congruence of triangles?

  • A. SAS
  • B. ASA
  • C. SSA
  • D. SSS

Answer: C. SSA

Explanation: Side-Side-Angle (SSA) or Angle-Side-Side (ASS) is not a valid congruence criterion.

3. If ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR, then side AB corresponds to:

  • A. PQ
  • B. QR
  • C. PR
  • D. QR or PR

Answer: A. PQ

Explanation: In congruent triangles, corresponding parts match the order of vertices: A↔P, B↔Q, C↔R, so AB = PQ.

4. If ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR, which of the following statements is true by CPCT?

  • A. ∠A = ∠Q
  • B. ∠B = ∠P
  • C. ∠C = ∠R
  • D. AC = PQ

Answer: C. ∠C = ∠R

Explanation: By CPCT (Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles), vertex C corresponds to vertex R, so ∠C = ∠R.

5. Angles opposite to equal sides of an isosceles triangle are:

  • A. Unequal
  • B. Equal
  • C. Complementary
  • D. Right angles

Answer: B. Equal

Explanation: Theorem 7.2 states that angles opposite to equal sides of an isosceles triangle are equal.

6. Sides opposite to equal angles of a triangle are:

  • A. Parallel
  • B. Perpendicular
  • C. Equal
  • D. Unequal

Answer: C. Equal

Explanation: Theorem 7.3 states that sides opposite to equal angles of a triangle are equal.

7. In ΔABC, if AB = AC and ∠B = 50°, find ∠C.

  • A. 50°
  • B. 80°
  • C. 100°
  • D. 130°

Answer: A. 50°

Explanation: Since AB = AC, the angles opposite to them are equal: ∠C = ∠B = 50°.

8. In ΔABC, if AB = AC and ∠A = 80°, find ∠B.

  • A. 80°
  • B. 50°
  • C. 40°
  • D. 100°

Answer: B. 50°

Explanation: ∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°. Since AB = AC, ∠B = ∠C. 80° + 2(∠B) = 180° ⇒ 2(∠B) = 100° ⇒ ∠B = 50°.

9. Each angle of an equilateral triangle measures:

  • A. 45°
  • B. 90°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 30°

Answer: C. 60°

Explanation: All sides are equal in an equilateral triangle, so all 3 angles are equal: 180° / 3 = 60°.

10. RHS congruence criterion stands for:

  • A. Right angle - Hypotenuse - Side
  • B. Right angle - Height - Side
  • C. Ratio - Height - Side
  • D. Right angle - Horizontal - Side

Answer: A. Right angle - Hypotenuse - Side

Explanation: RHS criterion applies when two right-angled triangles have an equal hypotenuse and one equal side.

11. In ΔABC and ΔDEF, AB = FD and ∠A = ∠D. To make ΔABC ≅ ΔDFE by SAS criterion, which condition is required?

  • A. BC = EF
  • B. AC = DE
  • C. AC = EF
  • D. BC = DE

Answer: B. AC = DE

Explanation: For SAS with ∠A = ∠D, the sides including ∠A (AB, AC) must equal the sides including ∠D (DF, DE). Since AB = DF, AC must equal DE.

12. In ΔPQR, if ∠R > ∠Q, then:

  • A. QR > PR
  • B. PQ > PR
  • C. PQ < PR
  • D. QR < PQ

Answer: B. PQ > PR

Explanation: In any triangle, the side opposite to the larger angle is longer. Opposite to ∠R is PQ and opposite to ∠Q is PR, so PQ > PR.

13. In any triangle, the sum of any two sides is ________ the third side.

  • A. Less than
  • B. Equal to
  • C. Greater than
  • D. Half of

Answer: C. Greater than

Explanation: Triangle Inequality Property: The sum of the lengths of any two sides of a triangle is always greater than the third side.

14. In a right-angled triangle, which side is the longest side?

  • A. Perpendicular
  • B. Base
  • C. Hypotenuse
  • D. All sides are equal

Answer: C. Hypotenuse

Explanation: The right angle (90°) is the largest angle in a right triangle, so the side opposite to it (hypotenuse) is the longest.

15. If the three sides of a triangle are 4 cm, 5 cm, and 10 cm, can such a triangle be formed?

  • A. Yes
  • B. No
  • C. Only if it is a right triangle
  • D. Cannot be determined

Answer: B. No

Explanation: Sum of 4 + 5 = 9 cm, which is NOT greater than 10 cm. Since it violates the triangle inequality rule, no triangle can be formed.

16. If ΔABC ≅ ΔLMN, then which side is equal to MN?

  • A. AB
  • B. BC
  • C. AC
  • D. None of these

Answer: B. BC

Explanation: The vertex ordering is A↔L, B↔M, C↔N. Therefore, side BC corresponds to side MN.

17. In ΔABC, if ∠A = 40° and ∠B = 60°, which is the longest side?

  • A. AB
  • B. BC
  • C. AC
  • D. AB and BC are equal

Answer: A. AB

Explanation: ∠C = 180° - (40° + 60°) = 80°. Since ∠C is the largest angle, the side opposite to it (AB) is the longest side.

18. In ΔABC, if ∠A = 40° and ∠B = 60°, which is the shortest side?

  • A. AB
  • B. BC
  • C. AC
  • D. BC and AC are equal

Answer: B. BC

Explanation: ∠A = 40° is the smallest angle, so the side opposite to it (BC) is the shortest side.

19. If ΔPQR ≅ ΔEFD, then PR is equal to:

  • A. EF
  • B. FD
  • C. ED
  • D. QR

Answer: C. ED

Explanation: In ΔPQR ≅ ΔEFD, vertex P corresponds to E and R corresponds to D. Thus, PR = ED.

20. In an isosceles triangle ABC with AB = AC, if AD is a altitude to BC, then AD:

  • A. Bisects ∠A
  • B. Bisects BC
  • C. Both A and B
  • D. Neither A nor B

Answer: C. Both A and B

Explanation: The altitude drawn to the base of an isosceles triangle acts as both the angle bisector of vertex A and the perpendicular bisector of base BC.

21. The difference between any two sides of a triangle is ________ the third side.

  • A. Greater than
  • B. Equal to
  • C. Less than
  • D. Double of

Answer: C. Less than

Explanation: By triangle inequality properties, the difference between lengths of any two sides is always less than the third side.

22. If two sides of a triangle are 6 cm and 9 cm, the third side must lie between:

  • A. 3 cm and 15 cm
  • B. 6 cm and 9 cm
  • C. 0 cm and 15 cm
  • D. 3 cm and 9 cm

Answer: A. 3 cm and 15 cm

Explanation: Third side > (9 - 6 = 3 cm) and Third side < (9 + 6 = 15 cm).

23. In ΔABC, if ∠B = ∠C = 45°, then ΔABC is a/an:

  • A. Equilateral triangle
  • B. Isosceles right-angled triangle
  • C. Scalene triangle
  • D. Obtuse triangle

Answer: B. Isosceles right-angled triangle

Explanation: ∠A = 180° - (45° + 45°) = 90°. Since it has two equal angles (45°) and one right angle (90°), it is an isosceles right triangle.

24. What full form does 'CPCT' stand for?

  • A. Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles
  • B. Congruent Parts of Common Triangles
  • C. Corresponding Points of Congruent Triangles
  • D. Central Parts of Congruent Triangles

Answer: A. Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles

Explanation: CPCT stands for Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles.

25. In ΔABC, if AB = 5 cm, BC = 6 cm, and AC = 7 cm, which angle is the largest?

  • A. ∠A
  • B. ∠B
  • C. ∠C
  • D. All angles are equal

Answer: B. ∠B

Explanation: The side AC = 7 cm is the longest side, so the angle opposite to it (∠B) is the largest angle.

26. Of all line segments drawn to a given line from a point not lying on it, which is the shortest?

  • A. Slant line
  • B. Perpendicular line segment
  • C. Parallel line
  • D. Bisecting line

Answer: B. Perpendicular line segment

Explanation: The perpendicular line segment drawn from a point to a line is shorter than any other slant line segment drawn to that line.

27. If ΔABC ≅ ΔDEF and ∠A = 30°, ∠E = 70°, find ∠C.

  • A. 70°
  • B. 80°
  • C. 30°
  • D. 100°

Answer: B. 80°

Explanation: Since ΔABC ≅ ΔDEF, ∠B = ∠E = 70°. In ΔABC, ∠C = 180° - (30° + 70°) = 80°.

28. Which criterion is used to prove two right-angled triangles congruent if their hypotenuses and one pair of legs are equal?

  • A. SAS
  • B. SSS
  • C. RHS
  • D. ASA

Answer: C. RHS

Explanation: The RHS (Right angle - Hypotenuse - Side) rule specifically applies to right-angled triangles with matching hypotenuses and one side.

29. In an isosceles triangle ABC with AB = AC, if ∠A = 100°, find ∠B.

  • A. 40°
  • B. 80°
  • C. 50°
  • D. 100°

Answer: A. 40°

Explanation: AB = AC ⇒ ∠B = ∠C. 100° + 2(∠B) = 180° ⇒ 2(∠B) = 80° ⇒ ∠B = 40°.

30. Can a triangle have side lengths 3 cm, 4 cm, and 5 cm?

  • A. Yes, it is a right triangle
  • B. No
  • C. Yes, it is an equilateral triangle
  • D. Cannot be determined

Answer: A. Yes, it is a right triangle

Explanation: 3 + 4 > 5, 3 + 5 > 4, 4 + 5 > 3 (satisfies triangle rule) and 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5² (satisfies Pythagoras theorem).
