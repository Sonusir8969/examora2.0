Chapter 2: Socialism in Europe and the Russian Revolution 30 Questions

1. Who was the ruler of Russia in 1914?

  • A. Alexander I
  • B. Nicholas II
  • C. Peter the Great
  • D. Ivan the Terrible

Answer: B. Nicholas II

Explanation: Tsar Nicholas II was the last Emperor of Russia, ruling during the turbulent years leading to the 1917 Revolution.

2. Which philosopher wrote the book 'Das Kapital'?

  • A. Lenin
  • B. Stalin
  • C. Karl Marx
  • D. Engels

Answer: C. Karl Marx

Explanation: Karl Marx, along with Friedrich Engels, wrote 'Das Kapital', which formed the basis for modern socialist and communist theories.

3. What was the name of the Russian parliament established in 1905?

  • A. Duma
  • B. Soviet
  • C. Politburo
  • D. Central Committee

Answer: A. Duma

Explanation: The Duma was established by Tsar Nicholas II after the 1905 Revolution as a consultative parliament, though its power was highly limited.

4. Which event triggered the Russian Revolution of 1905?

  • A. Execution of royal family
  • B. Bloody Sunday
  • C. World War I
  • D. October Manifesto

Answer: B. Bloody Sunday

Explanation: On 9 January 1905, peaceful protestors marching towards the Winter Palace were shot by the Tsar's troops, igniting widespread revolt.

5. Who was the leader of the Bolshevik faction of the Socialist Democratic Party?

  • A. Trotsky
  • B. Martov
  • C. Stalin
  • D. Lenin

Answer: D. Lenin

Explanation: Vladimir Lenin led the Bolsheviks (the majority faction) who believed in a disciplined party of professional revolutionaries.

6. What was the slogan of the Bolsheviks during the October Revolution?

  • A. Peace, Land, and Bread
  • B. Liberty, Equality, Fraternity
  • C. Blood and Iron
  • D. Work and Progress

Answer: A. Peace, Land, and Bread

Explanation: The Bolsheviks promised to end the war, redistribute land to peasants, and provide food to the starving city workers.

7. In which month did the Bolshevik Revolution take place according to the Russian calendar?

  • A. January
  • B. March
  • C. October
  • D. December

Answer: C. October

Explanation: The Bolshevik Revolution occurred on 25 October (Julian calendar) 1917, which corresponds to 7 November in the Gregorian calendar.

8. What was the secret police organization set up by the Bolsheviks called?

  • A. KGB
  • B. Cheka
  • C. Gestapo
  • D. FBI

Answer: B. Cheka

Explanation: The Cheka was the Bolshevik secret police force established in 1917 to suppress counter-revolutionaries and maintain state security.

9. Who led the Mensheviks, the rival faction of the Bolsheviks?

  • A. Trotsky
  • B. Lenin
  • C. Martov
  • D. Stalin

Answer: C. Martov

Explanation: Julius Martov led the Mensheviks (the minority faction), who believed in a broad-based, mass workers' party rather than an elite group.

10. Which country became the first socialist state in the world?

  • A. Germany
  • B. France
  • C. Russia
  • D. China

Answer: C. Russia

Explanation: Following the Bolshevik victory in 1917, the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic became the world's first socialist state.

11. Who was the head of the Provisional Government formed after the February Revolution?

  • A. Lenin
  • B. Kerensky
  • C. Stalin
  • D. Trotsky

Answer: B. Kerensky

Explanation: Alexander Kerensky was the leader of the Provisional Government in 1917, but his government fell to the Bolsheviks in October.

12. What was the main grievance of the Russian peasants before the revolution?

  • A. Factory closures
  • B. Lack of land
  • C. Lack of railways
  • D. Heavy military taxes

Answer: B. Lack of land

Explanation: Russian peasants were mostly poor and lacked sufficient land to grow food, making land redistribution one of their core demands.

13. Which treaty was signed by Lenin to end Russia's involvement in World War I?

  • A. Treaty of Versailles
  • B. Treaty of Brest-Litovsk
  • C. Treaty of Ghent
  • D. Treaty of Paris

Answer: B. Treaty of Brest-Litovsk

Explanation: The Treaty of Brest-Litovsk in 1918 was a peace treaty signed between Soviet Russia and the Central Powers, ending Russian participation in WWI.

14. Who was the leader of the Red Army during the Russian Civil War?

  • A. Lenin
  • B. Stalin
  • C. Trotsky
  • D. Tukhachevsky

Answer: C. Trotsky

Explanation: Leon Trotsky was the revolutionary leader who organized and led the Red Army to victory against the White Army forces.

15. What was the 'April Theses'?

  • A. A peace proposal
  • B. Lenin's demands for revolution
  • C. A book by Stalin
  • D. A new constitution

Answer: B. Lenin's demands for revolution

Explanation: In the April Theses, Lenin outlined his demands for the Bolsheviks to seize power, end the war, and transfer land to peasants.

16. What type of government did the Bolsheviks establish after 1917?

  • A. Constitutional Monarchy
  • B. Dictatorship of the Proletariat
  • C. Military Junta
  • D. Fascist State

Answer: B. Dictatorship of the Proletariat

Explanation: The Bolsheviks established a Soviet government based on the principle of dictatorship of the proletariat, ruled by the working class.

17. What did the term 'Soviet' mean during the Russian Revolution?

  • A. A political party
  • B. Council of workers and soldiers
  • C. The secret police
  • D. The royal palace

Answer: B. Council of workers and soldiers

Explanation: Soviets were grassroots councils formed by workers, soldiers, and peasants that acted as strike committees and later as centers of political power.

18. Who was the first leader of the Soviet Union after the death of Lenin?

  • A. Trotsky
  • B. Bukharin
  • C. Stalin
  • D. Molotov

Answer: C. Stalin

Explanation: Joseph Stalin consolidated power and became the absolute leader of the Soviet Union following Lenin's death in 1924.

19. What was the main driving ideology behind the Russian Revolution?

  • A. Communism
  • B. Capitalism
  • C. Feudalism
  • D. Imperialism

Answer: A. Communism

Explanation: The revolution was deeply influenced by communist ideology, which aimed to abolish private property and establish a classless society.

20. What event marked the end of the Romanov dynasty in Russia?

  • A. The February Revolution
  • B. The October Revolution
  • C. The execution of the Tsar
  • D. The Treaty of Brest-Litovsk

Answer: A. The February Revolution

Explanation: The February Revolution of 1917 forced Tsar Nicholas II to abdicate, officially bringing an end to the 300-year Romanov dynasty.

21. Which industry was heavily nationalized by the Bolsheviks?

  • A. Textiles
  • B. Railways and Banks
  • C. Agriculture
  • D. Handicrafts

Answer: B. Railways and Banks

Explanation: Bolshevik policy nationalized all major industries, large-scale factories, railways, and private banks to bring them under state control.

22. The term 'Kulaks' referred to which group in Russia?

  • A. Landless laborers
  • B. Wealthy peasants
  • C. Factory owners
  • D. Military officers

Answer: B. Wealthy peasants

Explanation: Kulaks were the prosperous peasant class who owned land and employed other peasants, and they were heavily persecuted during collectivization.

23. When did Russia officially withdraw from World War I?

  • A. 1916
  • B. 1917
  • C. 1918
  • D. 1919

Answer: C. 1918

Explanation: The Treaty of Brest-Litovsk was signed in March 1918, formally ending Russia's participation in the First World War.

24. Which party did the Bolsheviks merge with to gain power in Russia?

  • A. Socialist Revolutionaries
  • B. Mensheviks
  • C. Cadets
  • D. None, they took power alone

Answer: A. Socialist Revolutionaries

Explanation: The Bolsheviks formed a coalition government with the Left Socialist Revolutionaries after the October Revolution to bolster their support.

25. What was the policy of 'War Communism'?

  • A. Private enterprise promotion
  • B. Forced grain requisitioning
  • C. Free trade
  • D. Democratic elections

Answer: B. Forced grain requisitioning

Explanation: War Communism involved the forced requisition of grain from peasants to feed the Red Army and urban workers during the Civil War.

26. Who was the chief architect of the Soviet Union's first Five-Year Plan?

  • A. Lenin
  • B. Trotsky
  • C. Stalin
  • D. Kirov

Answer: C. Stalin

Explanation: Stalin introduced the first Five-Year Plan in 1928 to rapidly industrialize the Soviet Union and collectivize agriculture.

27. Where was the headquarters of the Communist International (Comintern) located?

  • A. Paris
  • B. London
  • C. Moscow
  • D. Berlin

Answer: C. Moscow

Explanation: The Comintern was the international organization of communist parties established in 1919 in Moscow to promote world revolution.

28. Which of the following contributed to the fall of the Tsarist regime?

  • A. World War I losses
  • B. Food shortages
  • C. Workers' strikes
  • D. All of the above

Answer: D. All of the above

Explanation: Military defeats, food scarcity, massive strikes, and general discontent all contributed to the collapse of the Tsarist autocracy in 1917.

29. How did Lenin view the state before the revolution?

  • A. A tool of the capitalist class
  • B. A representation of the people
  • C. A religious institution
  • D. A military authority

Answer: A. A tool of the capitalist class

Explanation: Lenin adhered to Marxist theory, viewing the state as an instrument of coercion used by the ruling class to suppress the proletariat.

30. What was the immediate economic impact of the 1917 Revolution?

  • A. Surplus agricultural produce
  • B. Massive industrial boom
  • C. Economic collapse and civil war
  • D. Repeal of all taxes

Answer: C. Economic collapse and civil war

Explanation: The revolution led to a severe economic crisis, industrial collapse, and a devastating civil war that lasted until the early 1920s.
