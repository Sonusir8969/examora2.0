Ch-13: Surface Areas and Volumes 30 Questions

1. The total surface area of a cuboid with length 'l', breadth 'b', and height 'h' is:

  • A. 2(lb + bh + hl)
  • B. lb + bh + hl
  • C. 2h(l + b)
  • D. lbh

Answer: A. 2(lb + bh + hl)

Explanation: Total surface area includes all 6 rectangular faces: 2(lb + bh + hl).

2. The lateral surface area of a cube with edge length 'a' is:

  • A. 6a²
  • B. 4a²
  • C. a³
  • D. 2a²

Answer: B. 4a²

Explanation: The lateral surface area excludes top and bottom faces, leaving 4 square faces: 4a².

3. The formula for the volume of a cylinder with radius 'r' and height 'h' is:

  • A. πr²h
  • B. 2πrh
  • C. ⅓πr²h
  • D. 2πr(r + h)

Answer: A. πr²h

Explanation: Volume of a right circular cylinder = base area × height = πr²h.

4. The curved surface area of a cone with radius 'r' and slant height 'l' is:

  • A. πr²h
  • B. πrl
  • C. 2πrl
  • D. πr(r + l)

Answer: B. πrl

Explanation: Curved surface area (CSA) of a cone = πrl.

5. The total surface area of a sphere of radius 'r' is:

  • A. 2πr²
  • B. 3πr²
  • C. 4πr²
  • D. ⁴/₃πr³

Answer: C. 4πr²

Explanation: The total surface area of a sphere of radius r is 4πr².

6. The curved surface area of a hemisphere of radius 'r' is:

  • A. 2πr²
  • B. 3πr²
  • C. 4πr²
  • D. πr²

Answer: A. 2πr²

Explanation: Curved surface area of a hemisphere is half of sphere's surface area = 2πr².

7. The total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius 'r' is:

  • A. 2πr²
  • B. 3πr²
  • C. 4πr²
  • D. πr²

Answer: B. 3πr²

Explanation: Total surface area = Curved surface (2πr²) + Flat base (πr²) = 3πr².

8. The volume of a sphere of radius 'r' is:

  • A. ⁴/₃πr³
  • B. ⅔πr³
  • C. 4πr²
  • D. ⅓πr²h

Answer: A. ⁴/₃πr³

Explanation: Volume of a sphere = ⁴/₃πr³.

9. If the edge of a cube is 6 cm, its volume is:

  • A. 36 cm³
  • B. 216 cm³
  • C. 144 cm³
  • D. 108 cm³

Answer: B. 216 cm³

Explanation: Volume of cube = side³ = 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 cm³.

10. The volume of a cone with radius 'r' and height 'h' is equal to what fraction of a cylinder's volume of same radius and height?

  • A. ½
  • B. ⅓
  • C. ¼
  • D. ⅔

Answer: B. ⅓

Explanation: Volume of cone = ⅓πr²h, which is ⅓ of cylinder volume (πr²h).

11. Relation between radius 'r', height 'h', and slant height 'l' of a cone is:

  • A. l² = r² + h²
  • B. l = r + h
  • C. h² = l² + r²
  • D. r² = l² + h²

Answer: A. l² = r² + h²

Explanation: By Pythagoras theorem on the right triangle inside the cone: l = √(r² + h²).

12. If the radius of a sphere is doubled, its volume becomes how many times?

  • A. 2 times
  • B. 4 times
  • C. 6 times
  • D. 8 times

Answer: D. 8 times

Explanation: Volume is proportional to r³. So (2r)³ = 8r³, i.e., 8 times.

13. If the radius of a sphere is doubled, its surface area becomes how many times?

  • A. 2 times
  • B. 4 times
  • C. 8 times
  • D. 16 times

Answer: B. 4 times

Explanation: Area is proportional to r². So (2r)² = 4r², i.e., 4 times.

14. The radius of a cylinder is 7 cm and height is 10 cm. Its curved surface area is (take π = 22/7):

  • A. 220 cm²
  • B. 440 cm²
  • C. 1540 cm²
  • D. 880 cm²

Answer: B. 440 cm²

Explanation: CSA = 2πrh = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 10 = 440 cm².

15. The volume of a cylinder with radius 7 cm and height 10 cm is:

  • A. 154 cm³
  • B. 1540 cm³
  • C. 440 cm³
  • D. 3080 cm³

Answer: B. 1540 cm³

Explanation: Volume = πr²h = (22/7) × 7 × 7 × 10 = 1540 cm³.

16. The length, breadth, and height of a room are 5 m, 4 m, and 3 m respectively. Area of 4 walls is:

  • A. 54 m²
  • B. 60 m²
  • C. 74 m²
  • D. 94 m²

Answer: A. 54 m²

Explanation: Area of 4 walls = 2h(l + b) = 2 × 3 × (5 + 4) = 6 × 9 = 54 m².

17. Volume of a hemisphere of radius 3 cm is (take π = 22/7):

  • A. 18π cm³
  • B. 36π cm³
  • C. 27π cm³
  • D. 54π cm³

Answer: A. 18π cm³

Explanation: Volume = ⅔πr³ = ⅔ × π × 3³ = ⅔ × 27π = 18π cm³.

18. The total surface area of a cube is 96 cm². The length of its edge is:

  • A. 2 cm
  • B. 4 cm
  • C. 6 cm
  • D. 8 cm

Answer: B. 4 cm

Explanation: 6a² = 96 ⇒ a² = 16 ⇒ a = 4 cm.

19. Standard unit of volume is:

  • A. m²
  • B. m³
  • C. m
  • D. cm²

Answer: B. m³

Explanation: The SI unit of volume is cubic meter (m³).

20. 1 liter is equal to how many cubic centimeters?

  • A. 100 cm³
  • B. 1000 cm³
  • C. 10000 cm³
  • D. 10 cm³

Answer: B. 1000 cm³

Explanation: 1 L = 1000 cm³ or 1 m³ = 1000 L.

21. The length of the longest rod that can be placed in a room of dimensions 10 m × 10 m × 5 m is:

  • A. 12 m
  • B. 15 m
  • C. 18 m
  • D. 20 m

Answer: B. 15 m

Explanation: Length of diagonal = √(l² + b² + h²) = √(100 + 100 + 25) = √225 = 15 m.

22. If radius of a cone is 3 cm and height is 4 cm, its slant height is:

  • A. 5 cm
  • B. 7 cm
  • C. 25 cm
  • D. 6 cm

Answer: A. 5 cm

Explanation: l = √(r² + h²) = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 cm.

23. Total surface area of a cylinder with radius 'r' and height 'h' is:

  • A. 2πrh
  • B. 2πr(r + h)
  • C. πr(r + h)
  • D. 2πr²h

Answer: B. 2πr(r + h)

Explanation: TSA = CSA + 2 × Base Area = 2πrh + 2πr² = 2πr(r + h).

24. The ratio of volumes of two spheres is 64 : 27. The ratio of their radii is:

  • A. 16 : 9
  • B. 4 : 3
  • C. 2 : 1
  • D. 8 : 3

Answer: B. 4 : 3

Explanation: V1/V2 = (r1/r2)³ = 64/27 ⇒ r1/r2 = ∛(64/27) = 4/3.

25. The diameter of a sphere is 14 cm. Its surface area is:

  • A. 154 cm²
  • B. 616 cm²
  • C. 308 cm²
  • D. 1232 cm²

Answer: B. 616 cm²

Explanation: Radius r = 7 cm. Surface Area = 4πr² = 4 × (22/7) × 49 = 616 cm².

26. A right circular cone has radius 6 cm and slant height 10 cm. Its height is:

  • A. 4 cm
  • B. 8 cm
  • C. 16 cm
  • D. 12 cm

Answer: B. 8 cm

Explanation: h = √(l² - r²) = √(100 - 36) = √64 = 8 cm.

27. Area of the base of a right circular cylinder is 154 cm² and its height is 10 cm. Its volume is:

  • A. 1540 cm³
  • B. 770 cm³
  • C. 3080 cm³
  • D. 154 cm³

Answer: A. 1540 cm³

Explanation: Volume = Base area × height = 154 × 10 = 1540 cm³.

28. Two cubes each of volume 64 cm³ are joined end to end. The surface area of resulting cuboid is:

  • A. 160 cm²
  • B. 128 cm²
  • C. 160 cm³
  • D. 80 cm²

Answer: A. 160 cm²

Explanation: Cube side = 4 cm. Cuboid dimensions: l = 8 cm, b = 4 cm, h = 4 cm. TSA = 2(8×4 + 4×4 + 4×8) = 2(32 + 16 + 32) = 160 cm².

29. The radius of a hemispherical balloon increases from 6 cm to 12 cm. The ratio of their surface areas is:

  • A. 1 : 2
  • B. 1 : 4
  • C. 1 : 8
  • D. 1 : 3

Answer: B. 1 : 4

Explanation: Ratio of areas = (r1/r2)² = (6/12)² = (1/2)² = 1 : 4.

30. Total surface area of a cone of radius 'r' and slant height 'l' is:

  • A. πr(l + r)
  • B. πr²l
  • C. 2πr(l + r)
  • D. πrl + 2πr

Answer: A. πr(l + r)

Explanation: Total surface area = Curved surface (πrl) + Base area (πr²) = πr(l + r).
