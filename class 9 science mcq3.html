Ch-3: Atoms and Molecules 10 Questions

1. Who proposed the Law of Conservation of Mass?

  • A. Joseph L. Proust
  • B. Antoine Lavoisier
  • C. John Dalton
  • D. J.J. Thomson

Answer: B. Antoine Lavoisier

Explanation: Antoine Lavoisier established the Law of Conservation of Mass, which states that mass can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction.

2. In a chemical compound like water (H₂O), the ratio of the mass of hydrogen to oxygen is always:

  • A. 1 : 8
  • B. 8 : 1
  • C. 1 : 2
  • D. 2 : 1

Answer: A. 1 : 8

Explanation: According to the Law of Constant Proportions, water always contains hydrogen and oxygen combined in a fixed mass ratio of 1:8 (2g of H to 16g of O).

3. One atomic mass unit (1 u) is defined as a mass unit equal to exactly one-twelfth the mass of one atom of:

  • A. Carbon-12
  • B. Hydrogen-1
  • C. Oxygen-16
  • D. Nitrogen-14

Answer: A. Carbon-12

Explanation: Carbon-12 isotope is taken as the standard reference for measuring atomic masses. 1 u = 1/12th the mass of one C-12 atom.

4. What is the Latin name of Sodium from which its chemical symbol 'Na' is derived?

  • A. Kalium
  • B. Natrium
  • C. Ferrum
  • D. Cuprum

Answer: B. Natrium

Explanation: The symbol Na for Sodium comes from its Latin name 'Natrium'. (Similarly, Kalium = K, Ferrum = Fe, Cuprum = Cu).

5. What is the relative molecular mass of Water (H₂O)?

  • A. 16 u
  • B. 18 u
  • C. 20 u
  • D. 32 u

Answer: B. 18 u

Explanation: Atomic mass of H = 1 u, O = 16 u. Molecular mass of H₂O = (2 × 1) + 16 = 18 u.

6. What is the chemical formula of Calcium Carbonate?

  • A. CaCO₃
  • B. Ca(OH)₂
  • C. CaCl₂
  • D. CaO

Answer: A. CaCO₃

Explanation: Calcium has a valency of +2 (Ca²⁺) and Carbonate has a valency of -2 (CO₃²⁻). Combining them gives CaCO₃.

7. What is the atomicity of Ozone (O₃)?

  • A. Monoatomic
  • B. Diatomic
  • C. Triatomic
  • D. Polyatomic (4+)

Answer: C. Triatomic

Explanation: Atomicity refers to the number of atoms constituting a molecule. Ozone consists of 3 oxygen atoms, making it triatomic.

8. What is the value of Avogadro's constant (Nₐ)?

  • A. 6.022 × 10²³
  • B. 6.022 × 10²²
  • C. 3.011 × 10²³
  • D. 1.66 × 10⁻²⁴

Answer: A. 6.022 × 10²³

Explanation: One mole of any substance contains a fixed number of particles, known as Avogadro's constant = 6.022 × 10²³ per mole.

9. Which of the following is a polyatomic ion?

  • A. Na⁺
  • B. Cl⁻
  • C. NH₄⁺
  • D. Mg²⁺

Answer: C. NH₄⁺

Explanation: Ammonium (NH₄⁺) is composed of a group of atoms carrying a net charge, making it a polyatomic ion.

10. What is the mass of 1 mole of Nitrogen gas (N₂)?

  • A. 14 g
  • B. 28 g
  • C. 7 g
  • D. 56 g

Answer: B. 28 g

Explanation: Atomic mass of N = 14 u. Molar mass of N₂ gas = 2 × 14 = 28 g/mol.
