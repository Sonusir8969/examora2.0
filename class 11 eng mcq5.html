Eng-05: Landscape of the Soul 25 Questions

1. Who is the writer of the chapter 'Landscape of the Soul'?

  • A. Nathalie Trouveroy
  • B. A.R. Williams
  • C. Gordon Cook
  • D. Nick Middleton

Answer: A. Nathalie Trouveroy

Explanation: 'Landscape of the Soul' is written by Nathalie Trouveroy, an art historian and writer.

2. Who was Wu Daozi in Chinese art history?

  • A. A famous painter of the eighth century
  • B. A Tang Emperor
  • C. A famous sculptor
  • D. A philosopher of Daoism

Answer: A. A famous painter of the eighth century

Explanation: Wu Daozi was a legendary 8th-century Chinese painter commissioned by Emperor Xuanzong.

3. Which Emperor commissioned Wu Daozi to paint a landscape for a palace wall?

  • A. Tang Emperor Xuanzong
  • B. Emperor Qin Shi Huang
  • C. Emperor Wu of Han
  • D. Emperor Kublai Khan

Answer: A. Tang Emperor Xuanzong

Explanation: Emperor Xuanzong ordered Wu Daozi to decorate a palace wall with a landscape painting.

4. What happened when Wu Daozi clapped his hands in front of the cave in his painting?

  • A. The entrance of the cave opened
  • B. The painting fell off the wall
  • C. The Emperor fell unconscious
  • D. Water started flowing from the wall

Answer: A. The entrance of the cave opened

Explanation: When the painter clapped his hands, the entrance to the cave inside his painting opened magically.

5. What happened to Wu Daozi and his painting after he entered the cave?

  • A. He entered, the cave entrance closed, and the painting vanished forever
  • B. He returned with gold coins
  • C. The Emperor captured him
  • D. The painting turned into real nature

Answer: A. He entered, the cave entrance closed, and the painting vanished forever

Explanation: The painter stepped inside, the entrance closed, and before the Emperor could move, the entire painting disappeared from the wall.

6. Why didn't a Chinese painter draw the eye of a dragon in his painting?

  • A. For fear it would fly out of the painting
  • B. Because he forgot how to draw it
  • C. Because the paint color was missing
  • D. Because the Emperor forbade him

Answer: A. For fear it would fly out of the painting

Explanation: The artist feared that adding the eye would give the dragon life, causing it to fly away from the wall.

7. Who fell in love with a blacksmith's daughter in 15th-century Antwerp?

  • A. Quinten Metsys
  • B. Wu Daozi
  • C. François Cheng
  • D. Nek Chand

Answer: A. Quinten Metsys

Explanation: Quinten Metsys, a blacksmith, fell in love with a painter's daughter in 15th-century Antwerp.

8. What was Quinten Metsys's original profession before becoming a painter?

  • A. Blacksmith
  • B. Sculptor
  • C. Carpenter
  • D. Weaver

Answer: A. Blacksmith

Explanation: Quinten Metsys was a blacksmith who changed his profession to win the acceptance of his beloved's father.

9. What did Quinten Metsys paint on the master's panel that tricked the painter?

  • A. A fly with delicate realism
  • B. A realistic dragon
  • C. A butterfly on a leaf
  • D. A portrait of the master

Answer: A. A fly with delicate realism

Explanation: Quinten secretly painted a fly on the master's easel so realistically that the master tried to swat it away.

10. What key difference between European and Chinese painting is highlighted in the text?

  • A. European painting seeks illusionistic likeness; Chinese painting aims for inner spirit
  • B. European art uses black ink; Chinese art uses oil colors
  • C. Chinese art is realistic; European art is abstract
  • D. European art has no perspective; Chinese art uses exact scale

Answer: A. European painting seeks illusionistic likeness; Chinese painting aims for inner spirit

Explanation: European art focuses on photographic realism, while Chinese art conveys essence and spiritual meaning.

11. What does the Chinese concept 'Shanshui' literally translate to?

  • A. Mountain-water
  • B. Sun-moon
  • C. Heaven-earth
  • D. River-forest

Answer: A. Mountain-water

Explanation: 'Shanshui' literally means 'mountain-water', which together represent the word 'landscape'.

12. In the Daoist concept of Shanshui, what does 'Yang' represent?

  • A. The vertical mountain, masculine, stable, warm and dry
  • B. The horizontal water, feminine, fluid, cool and moist
  • C. The unpainted empty space
  • D. The human spectator

Answer: A. The vertical mountain, masculine, stable, warm and dry

Explanation: 'Yang' reaches vertically towards Heaven, symbolizing stability, warmth, and masculine energy.

13. In the Daoist concept of Shanshui, what does 'Yin' represent?

  • A. Water, feminine, receptive, cool and moist
  • B. Mountain, masculine, warm and dry
  • C. Fire, active and bright
  • D. Air, unseen and moving

Answer: A. Water, feminine, receptive, cool and moist

Explanation: 'Yin' is horizontal, resting on the earth, representing receptive, fluid, and feminine energy.

14. What is the 'Middle Void' in Daoist philosophy?

  • A. The unpainted essential space where Yang and Yin interact
  • B. The center of a mountain peak
  • C. The deep ocean floor
  • D. The human heart

Answer: A. The unpainted essential space where Yang and Yin interact

Explanation: The Middle Void is the neutral space where interaction between Yin and Yang occurs, like breath control in Pranayama.

15. What yogic practice is compared to the 'Middle Void' in the chapter?

  • A. Pranayama (breath control)
  • B. Asanas (postures)
  • C. Dhyana (meditation)
  • D. Surya Namaskar

Answer: A. Pranayama (breath control)

Explanation: The author compares the Middle Void to Pranayama, where breath is suspended between inhalation and exhalation.

16. What role does Man play in the universe according to Daoist cosmology?

  • A. The conduit of communication between Heaven and Earth
  • B. The ruler over all animals
  • C. A passive observer with no purpose
  • D. The creator of nature

Answer: A. The conduit of communication between Heaven and Earth

Explanation: Man stands in the space between Heaven and Earth, becoming the essential medium of interaction.

17. What term was coined by French painter Jean Dubuffet in the 1940s?

  • A. Art Brut or Outsider Art
  • B. Impressionism
  • C. Abstract Surrealism
  • D. Renaissance Art

Answer: A. Art Brut or Outsider Art

Explanation: Jean Dubuffet created the concept of 'Art Brut' (raw art) created by untrained self-taught artists.

18. Who is India's most famous untutored genius in the field of 'Outsider Art'?

  • A. Nek Chand
  • B. M.F. Husain
  • C. Raja Ravi Varma
  • D. Jamini Roy

Answer: A. Nek Chand

Explanation: Nek Chand is celebrated globally for creating the magnificent Rock Garden in Chandigarh.

19. Where is the world-famous 'Rock Garden' created by Nek Chand located?

  • A. Chandigarh
  • B. New Delhi
  • C. Jaipur
  • D. Shimla

Answer: A. Chandigarh

Explanation: The Rock Garden sculpted out of stone and recycled material is situated in Chandigarh, India.

20. What materials did Nek Chand use to build his Rock Garden sculptures?

  • A. Recycled waste items, broken bangles, stones, and discarded material
  • B. Pure marble and gold leaf
  • C. Clay and wet mud
  • D. Wood and imported glass

Answer: A. Recycled waste items, broken bangles, stones, and discarded material

Explanation: Nek Chand transformed discarded trash, broken sinks, ceramic pieces, and bangles into works of art.

21. What issue of the UK-based magazine 'Raw Vision' featured Nek Chand's artwork on its cover?

  • A. 50th Issue (Spring 2005)
  • B. 25th Issue (Autumn 2000)
  • C. 1st Issue (1990)
  • D. 100th Issue (2010)

Answer: A. 50th Issue (Spring 2005)

Explanation: Raw Vision, a pioneer publication in outsider art, featured Nek Chand and his 'Women by the Waterfall' on its 50th issue cover.

22. What sculpture of Nek Chand was featured on the cover of 'Raw Vision' magazine?

  • A. Women by the Waterfall
  • B. The Dancing Girl
  • C. Dragon in the Forest
  • D. King and Queen of Stone

Answer: A. Women by the Waterfall

Explanation: The 50th issue cover displayed Nek Chand's famous sculpture 'Women by the Waterfall'.

23. What honor was conferred upon Nek Chand by UNESCO?

  • A. A five-month interactive exhibition in European museums
  • B. UNESCO World Heritage Medal
  • C. Nobel Prize in Art
  • D. Lifetime Achievement Award

Answer: A. A five-month interactive exhibition in European museums

Explanation: Swiss Commission for UNESCO honored him by organizing a 5-month exposition of his works across major cities.

24. What does the term 'Outsider Art' mean?

  • A. Art created by individuals with no formal artistic training
  • B. Art displayed outside museum buildings
  • C. Modern abstract paintings made overseas
  • D. Foreign historical paintings

Answer: A. Art created by individuals with no formal artistic training

Explanation: Outsider Art is art created by talented individuals who have received no professional or formal art training.

25. How did François Cheng describe humanity's importance in the universe?

  • A. Man is the eye of the landscape
  • B. Man is a tiny dot with no importance
  • C. Man is the master ruler of nature
  • D. Man is the destructor of beauty

Answer: A. Man is the eye of the landscape

Explanation: François Cheng quoted that man's presence is essential, describing him beautifully as "the eye of the landscape".
