Ch-7: Outcomes of Democracy 30 Questions

1. Which form of government is considered better than dictatorship or any other alternative?

  • A. Democracy
  • B. Monarchy
  • C. Military Rule
  • D. Oligarchy

Answer: A. Democracy

Explanation: Democracy promotes equality among citizens, enhances individual dignity, improves decision-making quality, and provides a method to resolve conflicts.

2. What is the most basic outcome of democracy that citizens expect from their government?

  • A. Accountable, responsive, and legitimate government
  • B. High economic growth rate
  • C. Complete eradication of poverty
  • D. Equal distribution of wealth

Answer: A. Accountable, responsive, and legitimate government

Explanation: Democracy produces a government that is accountable to citizens, responsive to their needs and expectations, and legally legitimate.

3. What term is used to describe a decision-making process in democracy where procedures laid down by law are followed?

  • A. Transparency
  • B. Dictatorship
  • C. Bureaucracy
  • D. Arbitrary Rule

Answer: A. Transparency

Explanation: Transparency means that citizens have the right and the means to examine the process of decision-making in a democracy.

4. Why is decision-making in a democracy often delayed compared to a dictatorship?

  • A. It follows deliberation, negotiation, and established procedures
  • B. Democratic leaders are inefficient
  • C. Decisions are taken only by public referendum
  • D. Courts stop every government law

Answer: A. It follows deliberation, negotiation, and established procedures

Explanation: Democracies base decisions on deliberation and negotiation, which takes time but leads to decisions that are more acceptable to people and more effective.

5. Which of the following is a key feature of a legitimate government?

  • A. It is elected by the people through free and fair elections
  • B. It rules through military power
  • C. It does not allow opposition parties
  • D. It works without constitutional limitations

Answer: A. It is elected by the people through free and fair elections

Explanation: A democratic government is a legitimate government because it is the people's own government chosen through regular elections.

6. On comparing economic growth rates between 1950 and 2000, which regime type had a slightly higher rate of economic growth?

  • A. Dictatorships
  • B. Democracies
  • C. Constitutional Monarchies
  • D. Federal States

Answer: A. Dictatorships

Explanation: Historically, non-democratic regimes had a slightly higher rate of economic growth between 1950 and 2000, although the difference was negligible in poor countries.

7. Economic development in a country depends on which of the following factors?

  • A. Population size, global situation, and cooperation from other countries
  • B. Only the political system chosen
  • C. Religion followed by the majority
  • D. Military expenditure of the state

Answer: A. Population size, global situation, and cooperation from other countries

Explanation: Economic development depends on multiple factors including population, global position, international trade, and economic priorities set by the country.

8. In which aspect do democracies usually lag behind non-democratic systems?

  • A. Speed of decision-making
  • B. Respecting individual freedom
  • C. Ensuring political equality
  • D. Accommodating social diversity

Answer: A. Speed of decision-making

Explanation: Dictatorships can make decisions very fast because they do not have to bother about consultation in assemblies or care about majorities.

9. Democracy stands much superior to any other form of government in promoting:

  • A. Dignity and freedom of the individual
  • B. Rapid industrial production
  • C. Complete economic equality
  • D. Total elimination of political debates

Answer: A. Dignity and freedom of the individual

Explanation: Every individual wants to receive respect from fellow beings, and democracy strongly recognizes and legally guarantees the dignity and freedom of citizens.

10. In South Asia, public support for democracy is strongest in which of the following countries?

  • A. All countries of South Asia (India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka)
  • B. Only in military-ruled states
  • C. Only in developed island nations
  • D. Support for democracy is non-existent in South Asia

Answer: A. All countries of South Asia (India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka)

Explanation: Surveys show widespread support for democracy across all South Asian countries, as people prefer being ruled by representatives elected by them.

11. Which country in South Asia experiences a severe problem where more than half of its population lives in poverty?

  • A. Bangladesh
  • B. Sri Lanka
  • C. Bhutan
  • D. Maldives

Answer: A. Bangladesh

Explanation: In Bangladesh, more than half of the population lives in poverty, highlighting that political equality does not automatically end economic inequality.

12. Democracy guarantees political equality by giving every adult citizen:

  • A. One vote with equal value
  • B. Equal income and wealth
  • C. Free government employment
  • D. Ownership of land

Answer: A. One vote with equal value

Explanation: Democracies are based on political equality, where all adults have equal voting rights ('one person, one vote, one value').

13. What condition must be met so that majority rule in a democracy does not become oppressive majority community rule?

  • A. Every citizen must have a chance of being in majority at some point in time
  • B. The majority community should permanently rule the nation
  • C. Minority groups should be barred from contesting elections
  • D. Rule by majority should mean rule by religious majority

Answer: A. Every citizen must have a chance of being in majority at some point in time

Explanation: Rule by majority means rule by majority opinion on specific issues, not permanent rule by a majority religious, linguistic, or racial community.

14. What is a unique test and continuous feature of a successful democracy?

  • A. As people get some benefits, they ask for more and produce more expectations
  • B. People stop expressing complaints against the government
  • C. Elections are conducted without political parties
  • D. All economic inequalities disappear completely

Answer: A. As people get some benefits, they ask for more and produce more expectations

Explanation: The fact that people are complaining and raising expectations is itself testimony to the success of democracy, as citizens become conscious of their rights.

15. How does democracy help in handling social differences and conflicts?

  • A. By developing procedures and mechanisms to negotiate differences peacefully
  • B. By suppressing minority voices completely
  • C. By ignoring cultural differences
  • D. By enforcing uniform customs on all citizens

Answer: A. By developing procedures and mechanisms to negotiate differences peacefully

Explanation: Democracy provides legal and political mechanisms to reduce tensions and resolve social conflicts through peaceful dialogue.

16. Which of the following is often criticized about democratic governments in practice?

  • A. They are often not attentive to the needs and demands of all citizens and fall short of perfection
  • B. They completely deny citizens the right to vote
  • C. They do not hold regular elections
  • D. They eliminate opposition parties

Answer: A. They are often not attentive to the needs and demands of all citizens and fall short of perfection

Explanation: Democracies are often criticized for corruption, slow action, and failing to fulfill all expectations of the electorate, though they remain legitimate systems.

17. Respect and equal treatment for women are necessary ingredients of a democratic society because:

  • A. Democracy recognizes individual dignity and legal equality for all citizens regardless of gender
  • B. Women form the majority of voters in every democracy
  • C. Non-democratic countries completely ban women from public life
  • D. It is mandatory only under international law

Answer: A. Democracy recognizes individual dignity and legal equality for all citizens regardless of gender

Explanation: Democratic principles make it easier for women and marginalized groups to struggle against long-standing inequalities and discrimination.

18. What is the fundamental difference between a democratic and a non-democratic government regarding public opinion?

  • A. Democratic government relies on public opinion, whereas non-democratic government does not care about public opinion
  • B. Non-democratic governments always conduct public opinion polls
  • C. Democratic governments suppress public opinion
  • D. Both governments give identical importance to public opinion

Answer: A. Democratic government relies on public opinion, whereas non-democratic government does not care about public opinion

Explanation: Democratic rulers must stay sensitive to public opinion to win elections, whereas dictators do not depend on public approval.

19. In a democracy, what transforms people from status of subjects into that of citizens?

  • A. Right to vote and realization that their vote matters
  • B. Receiving free goods from the government
  • C. High economic growth
  • D. Strict laws enforced by police

Answer: A. Right to vote and realization that their vote matters

Explanation: Voting rights empower individuals, giving them a stake in governance and turning passive subjects into active citizens.

20. What is the relationship between political democracy and economic inequality in practice?

  • A. Political democracy does not guarantee the removal of economic inequalities
  • B. Political democracy immediately removes all income disparities
  • C. Dictatorships always ensure equal wealth distribution
  • D. Democracies increase poverty systematically

Answer: A. Political democracy does not guarantee the removal of economic inequalities

Explanation: While democracies bring political equality through voting rights, economic inequalities between the rich and poor often continue to grow.

21. Which type of government is considered 'people's own government'?

  • A. Democratic government
  • B. Monarchical government
  • C. Military dictatorship
  • D. Oligarchical government

Answer: A. Democratic government

Explanation: Democracy is called 'people's own government' because representatives are chosen directly or indirectly by the citizens.

22. Why are complaints by citizens treated as a sign of success in a democracy?

  • A. It shows that citizens have developed awareness and the courage to critically evaluate power
  • B. It proves that the government is completely failing
  • C. It leads to continuous changing of governments every month
  • D. It indicates that citizens dislike democracy

Answer: A. It shows that citizens have developed awareness and the courage to critically evaluate power

Explanation: Public complaints demonstrate that citizens are conscious of their rights and do not blindly accept authority.

23. What guarantees that democratic decisions are accepted by citizens as right and effective?

  • A. Decisions are based on norms, procedures, and consultation
  • B. Decisions are issued directly by military orders
  • C. Decisions are taken secretly by leaders
  • D. Decisions are made without any public debate

Answer: A. Decisions are based on norms, procedures, and consultation

Explanation: Because democratic decisions involve consultation and follow set procedures, citizens are more likely to accept them as fair.

24. What is a key weakness in the actual functioning of democratic governments regarding information sharing?

  • A. They do not always share information fully with citizens
  • B. They share all secret national security data publicly
  • C. They ban newspapers and television networks
  • D. They refuse to hold parliamentary sessions

Answer: A. They do not always share information fully with citizens

Explanation: Despite claims of transparency, democratic governments in practice often fail to share complete information with the public on key matters.

25. Which aspect of democracy provides a basis for claims against caste-based and gender-based discrimination?

  • A. Moral and legal recognition of equality and dignity
  • B. Rapid economic development policies
  • C. Creation of armed forces
  • D. Centralized administration systems

Answer: A. Moral and legal recognition of equality and dignity

Explanation: Democratic constitutions legally guarantee equality, giving disadvantaged groups a ground to fight against social injustices.

26. In terms of reducing economic inequalities, democratic regimes have shown:

  • A. A mixed record with growing wealth gaps in many countries
  • B. Complete success in abolishing poverty
  • C. Total failure compared to dictatorships
  • D. Immediate equal distribution of land and property

Answer: A. A mixed record with growing wealth gaps in many countries

Explanation: Evidence shows that democracies have not been very successful in reducing economic inequalities, as top income shares continue to rise in many nations.

27. Which condition is necessary for a democracy to accommodate social diversity successfully?

  • A. The majority must work together with the minority community
  • B. The majority must rule forcefully over minorities
  • C. Minority groups must give up their culture
  • D. Political parties based on religion must be banned completely

Answer: A. The majority must work together with the minority community

Explanation: Democracy remains a democracy only as long as the majority works in coordination with minorities so that governments represent general views.

28. What makes a democratic government legitimate even if it is slow or less efficient?

  • A. It is elected and sanctioned by the people
  • B. It spends less money on administration
  • C. It does not levy taxes
  • D. It works faster than military rulers

Answer: A. It is elected and sanctioned by the people

Explanation: Legitimacy comes from popular consent through elections, making democracy desirable despite administrative delays.

29. What is a primary expectation of citizens from a democratic government regarding corruption?

  • A. That it should be free of corruption and responsive to people
  • B. That it should legalise bribery
  • C. That corruption is acceptable as long as work is done
  • D. That only senior officers should be audited

Answer: A. That it should be free of corruption and responsive to people

Explanation: Citizens expect democratic governments to be honest, clean, and accountable, though corruption remains a common grievance in practice.

30. Why is democracy considered a principle as well as an ongoing process?

  • A. Because fulfilling one goal leads to new demands and higher expectations
  • B. Because democratic constitutions change every year
  • C. Because elections happen every month
  • D. Because it guarantees instant solutions to all social problems

Answer: A. Because fulfilling one goal leads to new demands and higher expectations

Explanation: Democracy is an ongoing process because as citizens experience democratic freedoms, they demand further improvements in governance and society.
