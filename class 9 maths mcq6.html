Ch-6: Lines and Angles 30 Questions

1. Two angles whose sum is 90° are called:

  • A. Supplementary angles
  • B. Complementary angles
  • C. Adjacent angles
  • D. Reflex angles

Answer: B. Complementary angles

Explanation: By definition, if the sum of two angles is 90°, they are called complementary angles.

2. Two angles whose sum is 180° are called:

  • A. Complementary angles
  • B. Vertically opposite angles
  • C. Supplementary angles
  • D. Acute angles

Answer: C. Supplementary angles

Explanation: When the sum of two angles equals 180°, they form a supplementary pair.

3. Find the complement of an angle measuring 35°.

  • A. 55°
  • B. 145°
  • C. 65°
  • D. 45°

Answer: A. 55°

Explanation: Complementary angle = 90° - 35° = 55°.

4. Find the supplement of an angle measuring 110°.

  • A. 70°
  • B. 80°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 250°

Answer: A. 70°

Explanation: Supplementary angle = 180° - 110° = 70°.

5. An angle which is greater than 180° but less than 360° is called a:

  • A. Obtuse angle
  • B. Straight angle
  • C. Reflex angle
  • D. Complete angle

Answer: C. Reflex angle

Explanation: Angles lying strictly between 180° and 360° are classified as reflex angles.

6. If two intersecting lines form vertically opposite angles, then these angles are always:

  • A. Supplementary
  • B. Equal
  • C. Complementary
  • D. Unequal

Answer: B. Equal

Explanation: According to Theorem 6.1, if two lines intersect each other, then the vertically opposite angles are equal.

7. If a ray stands on a straight line, the sum of two adjacent angles so formed is equal to:

  • A. 90°
  • B. 180°
  • C. 270°
  • D. 360°

Answer: B. 180°

Explanation: This is Axiom 6.1 (Linear Pair Axiom): If a ray stands on a line, the sum of the two adjacent angles is 180°.

8. In a linear pair of angles, if one angle measures 65°, find the measure of the other angle.

  • A. 115°
  • B. 25°
  • C. 125°
  • D. 105°

Answer: A. 115°

Explanation: Sum of linear pair = 180°. Other angle = 180° - 65° = 115°.

9. If two lines are parallel to a third line, then they are ________ to each other.

  • A. Perpendicular
  • B. Parallel
  • C. Intersecting
  • D. Coincident

Answer: B. Parallel

Explanation: Lines which are parallel to the same line are parallel to each other (Theorem 6.6).

10. If a transversal intersects two parallel lines, then alternate interior angles are:

  • A. Supplementary
  • B. Complementary
  • C. Equal
  • D. Unequal

Answer: C. Equal

Explanation: When a transversal cuts two parallel lines, each pair of alternate interior angles is equal.

11. If a transversal intersects two parallel lines, the interior angles on the same side of the transversal are:

  • A. Equal
  • B. Complementary
  • C. Supplementary
  • D. Reflex

Answer: C. Supplementary

Explanation: Interior angles on the same side of a transversal (consecutive interior angles) sum up to 180° (supplementary).

12. The sum of all interior angles of a triangle is always equal to:

  • A. 90°
  • B. 180°
  • C. 270°
  • D. 360°

Answer: B. 180°

Explanation: Angle Sum Property of a Triangle: The sum of the three interior angles of any triangle is 180°.

13. An exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of its two:

  • A. Adjacent interior angles
  • B. Opposite interior angles
  • C. Exterior angles
  • D. Complementary angles

Answer: B. Opposite interior angles

Explanation: Exterior Angle Theorem states that an exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of its two interior opposite angles.

14. If the two interior opposite angles of a triangle are 45° and 65°, find its exterior angle.

  • A. 110°
  • B. 70°
  • C. 100°
  • D. 120°

Answer: A. 110°

Explanation: Exterior angle = Sum of opposite interior angles = 45° + 65° = 110°.

15. If two complementary angles are in the ratio 2 : 3, find the angles.

  • A. 36° and 54°
  • B. 40° and 60°
  • C. 30° and 60°
  • D. 20° and 70°

Answer: A. 36° and 54°

Explanation: Let angles be 2x and 3x. 2x + 3x = 90° ⇒ 5x = 90° ⇒ x = 18°. Angles are 2(18°) = 36° and 3(18°) = 54°.

16. If two supplementary angles are in the ratio 4 : 5, find the larger angle.

  • A. 80°
  • B. 100°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 110°

Answer: B. 100°

Explanation: 4x + 5x = 180° ⇒ 9x = 180° ⇒ x = 20°. Larger angle = 5x = 5(20°) = 100°.

17. An angle is equal to its supplement. What is the measure of this angle?

  • A. 45°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 180°

Answer: C. 90°

Explanation: Let the angle be x. x + x = 180° ⇒ 2x = 180° ⇒ x = 90°.

18. An angle is equal to its complement. What is its measure?

  • A. 30°
  • B. 45°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 60°

Answer: B. 45°

Explanation: Let the angle be x. x + x = 90° ⇒ 2x = 90° ⇒ x = 45°.

19. If three angles at a point on a straight line are in ratio 1 : 2 : 3, what is the largest angle?

  • A. 30°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 120°

Answer: C. 90°

Explanation: x + 2x + 3x = 180° ⇒ 6x = 180° ⇒ x = 30°. Largest angle = 3x = 3(30°) = 90°.

20. The measure of an angle which is 30° less than its supplement is:

  • A. 75°
  • B. 105°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 45°

Answer: A. 75°

Explanation: Let angle be x. Supplement = x + 30°. x + (x + 30°) = 180° ⇒ 2x = 150° ⇒ x = 75°.

21. In ΔABC, if ∠A = 70° and ∠B = 50°, find the measure of ∠C.

  • A. 50°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 70°
  • D. 80°

Answer: B. 60°

Explanation: ∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180° ⇒ 70° + 50° + ∠C = 180° ⇒ ∠C = 180° - 120° = 60°.

22. If the angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4, find the smallest angle.

  • A. 20°
  • B. 40°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 80°

Answer: B. 40°

Explanation: 2x + 3x + 4x = 180° ⇒ 9x = 180° ⇒ x = 20°. Smallest angle = 2x = 2(20°) = 40°.

23. A triangle can have how many right angles?

  • A. At most one
  • B. Two
  • C. Three
  • D. None

Answer: A. At most one

Explanation: If a triangle had two right angles (90° + 90° = 180°), the third angle would be 0°, which is impossible.

24. What is the sum of all angles formed around a single point?

  • A. 180°
  • B. 270°
  • C. 360°
  • D. 90°

Answer: C. 360°

Explanation: The sum of all angles completely surrounding a point equals 360° (a complete angle).

25. If two lines intersect such that one of the angles is 90°, then the other three angles are each equal to:

  • A. 45°
  • B. 90°
  • C. 180°
  • D. 60°

Answer: B. 90°

Explanation: Vertically opposite angle is 90°, and linear pair adjacent angles are 180° - 90° = 90°. All 4 angles are 90°.

26. If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, the bisectors of any pair of alternate interior angles are:

  • A. Perpendicular
  • B. Parallel
  • C. Intersecting
  • D. Coincident

Answer: B. Parallel

Explanation: The bisectors of alternate interior angles form equal alternate angles with the transversal, making them parallel.

27. If an angle is 14° more than its complement, find the angle.

  • A. 38°
  • B. 52°
  • C. 48°
  • D. 42°

Answer: B. 52°

Explanation: Let angle = x. x + (x - 14°) = 90° ⇒ 2x = 104° ⇒ x = 52°.

28. What is the value of reflex angle of 120°?

  • A. 60°
  • B. 240°
  • C. 180°
  • D. 300°

Answer: B. 240°

Explanation: Reflex angle = 360° - Given Angle = 360° - 120° = 240°.

29. In a right-angled triangle, if one acute angle is 35°, the other acute angle is:

  • A. 45°
  • B. 55°
  • C. 65°
  • D. 35°

Answer: B. 55°

Explanation: The two acute angles in a right-angled triangle are complementary: 90° - 35° = 55°.

30. If side BC of ΔABC is produced to D such that exterior ∠ACD = 120° and ∠B = 70°, then ∠A is:

  • A. 50°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 70°
  • D. 120°

Answer: A. 50°

Explanation: Exterior angle = ∠A + ∠B ⇒ 120° = ∠A + 70° ⇒ ∠A = 120° - 70° = 50°.
