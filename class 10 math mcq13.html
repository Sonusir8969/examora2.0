Ch-13: Statistics 30 Questions

1. The empirical relationship between the three measures of central tendency is:

  • A. 3 Median = Mode + 2 Mean
  • B. Mode = 3 Mean - 2 Median
  • C. Mean = 3 Median - 2 Mode
  • D. 2 Median = Mode + 3 Mean

Answer: A. 3 Median = Mode + 2 Mean

Explanation: Standard empirical formula connecting measures of central tendency is 3 Median = Mode + 2 Mean.

2. Class mark of a class interval is given by:

  • A. Upper limit - Lower limit
  • B. (Upper limit + Lower limit) / 2
  • C. Upper limit / 2
  • D. Lower limit / 2

Answer: B. (Upper limit + Lower limit) / 2

Explanation: Class mark represents the mid-point of a class interval: (Upper class limit + Lower class limit) / 2.

3. For a frequency distribution, the mean is calculated using formula Σf_i x_i / Σf_i. This method is called:

  • A. Assumed Mean Method
  • B. Step-Deviation Method
  • C. Direct Method
  • D. Grouped Method

Answer: C. Direct Method

Explanation: Finding mean directly by taking the ratio of sum of product of frequencies and class marks to total frequency is the Direct Method.

4. The mode of a grouped frequency distribution can be determined graphically from a:

  • A. Histogram
  • B. Frequency Polygon
  • C. Ogive
  • D. Pie Chart

Answer: A. Histogram

Explanation: The highest rectangle in a histogram corresponds to the modal class, from which mode can be located graphically.

5. The median of a grouped data can be found graphically by drawing:

  • A. Histograms
  • B. Ogives
  • C. Bar graphs
  • D. Pie charts

Answer: B. Ogives

Explanation: Median corresponds to the x-coordinate of the point of intersection of 'less than' and 'more than' cumulative frequency curves (ogives).

6. If mean = 20 and median = 22, then mode is equal to:

  • A. 20
  • B. 26
  • C. 24
  • D. 22

Answer: B. 26

Explanation: Mode = 3 Median - 2 Mean = 3(22) - 2(20) = 66 - 40 = 26.

7. Sum of deviations of observations from their mean is always equal to:

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. -1
  • D. Mean

Answer: A. 0

Explanation: Mathematical property of arithmetic mean: Σ(x_i - Mean) = 0.

8. The class size of interval 10 - 25 is:

  • A. 10
  • B. 15
  • C. 25
  • D. 17.5

Answer: B. 15

Explanation: Class size = Upper limit - Lower limit = 25 - 10 = 15.

9. Which measure of central tendency is affected most by extreme values?

  • A. Mean
  • B. Median
  • C. Mode
  • D. Range

Answer: A. Mean

Explanation: Mean accounts for the numerical value of every single observation, making it highly sensitive to extreme values (outliers).

10. The mean of first 10 natural numbers is:

  • A. 5
  • B. 5.5
  • C. 6
  • D. 10

Answer: B. 5.5

Explanation: Sum of first 10 natural numbers = 10 × 11 / 2 = 55. Mean = 55 / 10 = 5.5.

11. In the formula Mean = a + (Σf_i d_i / Σf_i), 'a' represents:

  • A. Class size
  • B. Assumed mean
  • C. Frequency
  • D. Cumulative frequency

Answer: B. Assumed mean

Explanation: In Assumed Mean Method, 'a' denotes the assumed mean chosen usually from mid-values of x_i.

12. Cumulative frequency table is useful in determining the:

  • A. Mean
  • B. Median
  • C. Mode
  • D. Range

Answer: B. Median

Explanation: Cumulative frequencies are required to locate the position N/2 to identify the median class.

13. The mode of the data 2, 3, 4, 3, 5, 3, 1, 2, 3, 4 is:

  • A. 2
  • B. 3
  • C. 4
  • D. 5

Answer: B. 3

Explanation: 3 occurs most frequently (4 times) in the given data set.

14. If the mode of a distribution is 8 and mean is 8, then median is:

  • A. 8
  • B. 16
  • C. 24
  • D. 4

Answer: A. 8

Explanation: 3 Median = Mode + 2 Mean = 8 + 2(8) = 24 ⇒ Median = 8.

15. The median of numbers 4, 1, 8, 3, 6, 9, 2 is:

  • A. 3
  • B. 4
  • C. 6
  • D. 5

Answer: B. 4

Explanation: Arranging in ascending order: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9. Middle value (4th position) is 4.

16. Constructing a cumulative frequency table is useful in determining:

  • A. Mean
  • B. Median
  • C. Mode
  • D. All of these

Answer: B. Median

Explanation: Cumulative frequency distribution is constructed specifically to compute the median.

17. While computing mean of grouped data, we assume that the frequencies are:

  • A. Evenly distributed over all classes
  • B. Centred at class marks of classes
  • C. Concentrated at lower limits
  • D. Concentrated at upper limits

Answer: B. Centred at class marks of classes

Explanation: In grouped data calculation, each class interval is represented by its mid-point (class mark).

18. If mean of x, x+3, x+6, x+9, and x+12 is 10, then x is:

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 3
  • D. 4

Answer: D. 4

Explanation: Sum = 5x + 30. Mean = (5x + 30)/5 = x + 6 = 10 ⇒ x = 4.

19. In Step-Deviation Method, u_i is calculated as:

  • A. (x_i - a) / h
  • B. (a - x_i) / h
  • C. h × (x_i - a)
  • D. x_i - a

Answer: A. (x_i - a) / h

Explanation: Standard definition: u_i = d_i / h = (x_i - a) / h, where h is class height.

20. In modal class formula, f_1 represents:

  • A. Frequency of modal class
  • B. Frequency of class preceding modal class
  • C. Frequency of class succeeding modal class
  • D. Total frequency

Answer: A. Frequency of modal class

Explanation: In Mode = l + [(f_1 - f_0) / (2f_1 - f_0 - f_2)] × h, f_1 is frequency of modal class.

21. The upper limit of modal class for data: [0-10: 5, 10-20: 8, 20-30: 12, 30-40: 6] is:

  • A. 20
  • B. 30
  • C. 40
  • D. 10

Answer: B. 30

Explanation: Maximum frequency is 12, corresponding to class interval 20 - 30. Its upper limit is 30.

22. If the mean of first n natural numbers is 15, then n is equal to:

  • A. 15
  • B. 29
  • C. 30
  • D. 28

Answer: B. 29

Explanation: Mean = (n + 1)/2 = 15 ⇒ n + 1 = 30 ⇒ n = 29.

23. Which of the following is NOT a measure of central tendency?

  • A. Mean
  • B. Median
  • C. Mode
  • D. Standard Deviation

Answer: D. Standard Deviation

Explanation: Standard Deviation is a measure of dispersion, whereas Mean, Median, and Mode measure central tendency.

24. The mean of 5 observations is 12. If one observation is excluded, mean becomes 10. Excluded number is:

  • A. 20
  • B. 18
  • C. 15
  • D. 22

Answer: A. 20

Explanation: Sum of 5 observations = 5 × 12 = 60. Sum of 4 observations = 4 × 10 = 40. Excluded value = 60 - 40 = 20.

25. The algebraic sum of deviations of a set of n values from their mean is:

  • A. n
  • B. 0
  • C. 1
  • D. n/2

Answer: B. 0

Explanation: The sum of differences between each observation and arithmetic mean is always zero.

26. Median class is the class interval whose cumulative frequency is just greater than or equal to:

  • A. N
  • B. N / 2
  • C. N / 4
  • D. 2N

Answer: B. N / 2

Explanation: By definition, median class is identified by looking for cumulative frequency that is just ≥ N/2.

27. If each observation of a data set is multiplied by 3, its mean will:

  • A. Remain same
  • B. Become 3 times
  • C. Increase by 3
  • D. Become 1/3rd

Answer: B. Become 3 times

Explanation: Multiplying every observation in a set by a constant k multiplies the mean by k.

28. For symmetrical distribution, relation between mean, median, and mode is:

  • A. Mean = Median = Mode
  • B. Mean > Median > Mode
  • C. Mean < Median < Mode
  • D. Mean + Mode = Median

Answer: A. Mean = Median = Mode

Explanation: In a perfectly symmetrical distribution, Mean, Median, and Mode coincide at the same central value.

29. What is the class mark of interval 40 - 50?

  • A. 40
  • B. 45
  • C. 50
  • D. 10

Answer: B. 45

Explanation: Class mark = (40 + 50) / 2 = 90 / 2 = 45.

30. Point of intersection of 'less than' and 'more than' ogives corresponds to x-axis coordinate value of:

  • A. Mean
  • B. Median
  • C. Mode
  • D. Class size

Answer: B. Median

Explanation: The x-value corresponding to intersection point of both cumulative frequency curves gives the median.
