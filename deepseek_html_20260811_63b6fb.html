Rewards are earned through legitimate activities on our platform:

  • Cashback: 1-5% cashback on eligible transactions (varies by partner)
  • Referral Bonus: $10 for each referred friend who signs up and completes KYC
  • Loyalty Rewards: Quarterly loyalty bonuses based on account activity
  • Promotional: Special promotions and seasonal rewards

⚠️ Rewards are not guaranteed returns. All rewards are subject to eligibility criteria and business rules. This is not an investment scheme. No fixed returns are promised.
