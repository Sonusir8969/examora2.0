Ch-1: A Letter to God 30 Questions

1. Who is the author of the story 'A Letter to God'?

  • A. G.L. Fuentes
  • B. J.K. Rowling
  • C. William Shakespeare
  • D. Roald Dahl

Answer: A. G.L. Fuentes

Explanation: Gregorio López y Fuentes (G.L. Fuentes) is the author of 'A Letter to God'.

2. Where was Lencho's house situated?

  • A. At the top of a low hill
  • B. In the middle of a city
  • C. At the bottom of a cliff
  • D. Inside a thick forest

Answer: A. At the top of a low hill

Explanation: Lencho's house was sitting on the crest (top) of a low hill in the valley.

3. What was Lencho's field expecting before the rain?

  • A. A good harvest
  • B. Firewood
  • C. New seeds
  • D. Chemical fertilizers

Answer: A. A good harvest

Explanation: The field of ripe corn promised a good harvest, provided it got a downpour or at least a shower.

4. What did Lencho compare the large raindrops to?

  • A. New coins
  • B. Pearls
  • C. Silver coins
  • D. Diamonds

Answer: A. New coins

Explanation: Lencho compared the raindrops to new coins—the big drops were 10 cent pieces and small ones 5 cent pieces.

5. Which crop was growing in Lencho's fields?

  • A. Corn
  • B. Wheat
  • C. Rice
  • D. Barley

Answer: A. Corn

Explanation: Lencho grew ripe corn dotted with flowers in his field.

6. What totally destroyed Lencho's crop?

  • A. Hailstorm
  • B. Flood
  • C. Drought
  • D. Locust attack

Answer: A. Hailstorm

Explanation: A sudden strong wind began to blow, bringing heavy hailstones that completely ruined the crops.

7. The hailstones resembled which of the following?

  • A. Silver coins
  • B. Gold coins
  • C. Diamonds
  • D. Copper coins

Answer: A. Silver coins

Explanation: The text states that the frozen hailstones truly did resemble new silver coins.

8. What did Lencho compare the devastation caused by the hailstorm to?

  • A. A plague of locusts
  • B. A wild cyclone
  • C. An earthquake
  • D. A volcanic eruption

Answer: A. A plague of locusts

Explanation: Lencho remarked that even a plague of locusts would have left more than what the hailstorm left.

9. What was the single hope left in the hearts of Lencho's family?

  • A. Help from God
  • B. Help from the government
  • C. Help from neighbors
  • D. Taking a bank loan

Answer: A. Help from God

Explanation: Their only remaining hope was divine assistance—"Help from God".

10. How much money did Lencho ask God for?

  • A. 100 pesos
  • B. 70 pesos
  • C. 500 pesos
  • D. 1000 pesos

Answer: A. 100 pesos

Explanation: Lencho wrote a letter to God requesting 100 pesos to sow his field again and live until the next harvest.

11. Who read the letter written by Lencho?

  • A. The Postmaster
  • B. The Postman
  • C. Lencho's wife
  • D. The Mayor

Answer: A. The Postmaster

Explanation: First the postman saw it and brought it laughing to his boss, the postmaster, who opened and read it.

12. What was the postmaster's immediate reaction upon seeing Lencho's letter?

  • A. He laughed heartily
  • B. He became angry
  • C. He began to cry
  • D. He ignored it completely

Answer: A. He laughed heartily

Explanation: The postmaster broke out laughing when he saw a letter addressed directly to "God".

13. How would you describe the personality of the postmaster?

  • A. Fat, amiable, and kind-hearted
  • B. Strict and arrogant
  • C. Greedy and selfish
  • D. Uneducated and rude

Answer: A. Fat, amiable, and kind-hearted

Explanation: The postmaster is described in the text as a "fat, amiable fellow" who possessed deep empathy.

14. Why did the postmaster decide to respond to Lencho's letter?

  • A. To preserve Lencho's unshakable faith in God
  • B. To mock Lencho's simplicity
  • C. To ask Lencho for a bribe
  • D. To make fun of the villagers

Answer: A. To preserve Lencho's unshakable faith in God

Explanation: In order not to shake the writer's deep faith in God, the postmaster decided to answer the letter.

15. How much money was the postmaster able to collect for Lencho?

  • A. 70 pesos
  • B. 100 pesos
  • C. 50 pesos
  • D. 75 pesos

Answer: A. 70 pesos

Explanation: Despite his best efforts, the postmaster could collect only a little more than half—70 pesos.

16. Who contributed to collect money for Lencho?

  • A. The postmaster and post office employees
  • B. The village chief and farmers
  • C. Lencho's extended relatives
  • D. Local government officers

Answer: A. The postmaster and post office employees

Explanation: The postmaster gave part of his salary and asked his friends and employees to contribute as an act of charity.

17. How did Lencho feel when he counted the money in the envelope?

  • A. Angry
  • B. Surprised
  • C. Delighted
  • D. Grateful

Answer: A. Angry

Explanation: Lencho showed not the slightest surprise upon seeing the money, but he became angry when he counted only 70 pesos.

18. Why was Lencho angry after counting the money?

  • A. The amount was less than what he requested
  • B. The notes were torn and old
  • C. The envelope was opened
  • D. It was delivered late

Answer: A. The amount was less than what he requested

Explanation: He had requested 100 pesos, but found only 70 pesos inside, believing God could not make a mistake.

19. What phrase did Lencho use for the post office employees in his second letter?

  • A. A bunch of crooks
  • B. Unhelpful peasants
  • C. Selfish people
  • D. Helpers of God

Answer: A. A bunch of crooks

Explanation: Lencho called them a "bunch of crooks", suspecting they stole 30 pesos from his total 100 pesos.

20. What did Lencho request God in his second letter regarding the remaining money?

  • A. To send it not through the mail
  • B. To double the total amount
  • C. To give it to his children
  • D. To deposit it in a bank

Answer: A. To send it not through the mail

Explanation: He asked God to send the remaining 30 pesos directly, but not through mail because post office employees were crooks.

21. Lencho compares his sons and himself to an ox. What does "an ox of a man" mean?

  • A. A hard-working person
  • B. An uneducated person
  • C. A foolish person
  • D. A slow and lazy worker

Answer: A. A hard-working person

Explanation: "An ox of a man" is a metaphor used for Lencho, meaning he worked extremely hard like an animal in the fields.

22. What was the central emotion/faith demonstrated by Lencho in the story?

  • A. Unshakable faith in God
  • B. Faith in human generosity
  • C. Trust in government officials
  • D. Belief in nature's kindness

Answer: A. Unshakable faith in God

Explanation: The main theme of the story is Lencho's absolute, innocent, and blind faith in God.

23. How did the field look after the hailstorm stopped?

  • A. Covered with salt
  • B. Covered with snow
  • C. Covered with green leaves
  • D. Covered with water

Answer: A. Covered with salt

Explanation: The field looked completely white, as if covered with salt.

24. What ironical situation occurs at the end of the story?

  • A. The helpers who donated money were called crooks by Lencho
  • B. Lencho received more money than he asked for
  • C. The postmaster stole Lencho's money
  • D. Lencho's crop suddenly regrew

Answer: A. The helpers who donated money were called crooks by Lencho

Explanation: The irony lies in the fact that the very people who collected money out of goodwill were labeled "a bunch of crooks".

25. On what day of the week did Lencho go to the post office to mail his first letter?

  • A. Sunday
  • B. Monday
  • C. Saturday
  • D. Friday

Answer: A. Sunday

Explanation: Lencho began to write the letter at daybreak on Sunday.

26. How did Lencho affix the stamp to his envelope?

  • A. With a blow of his fist
  • B. Using glue from the desk
  • C. Using tape
  • D. With a stapler

Answer: A. With a blow of his fist

Explanation: Lencho licked the stamp and then affixed it to the envelope with a blow of his fist.

27. What signed name was on the letter Lencho received back?

  • A. God
  • B. Postmaster
  • C. Post office
  • D. Anonymous

Answer: A. God

Explanation: The postmaster signed the letter with a single word: "God", so that Lencho's faith remained intact.

28. In which direction did Lencho look for rain clouds in the morning?

  • A. North-East
  • B. South-West
  • C. North-West
  • D. South-East

Answer: A. North-East

Explanation: Lencho looked toward the north-east sky, expecting huge mountains of rain clouds to approach.

29. What was Lencho's wife doing when he mentioned the rain?

  • A. Preparing supper
  • B. Washing clothes
  • C. Feeding cattle
  • D. Sleeping

Answer: A. Preparing supper

Explanation: Lencho's wife was preparing supper when he spoke about getting water/rain.

30. What type of conflict is primary in the story 'A Letter to God'?

  • A. Conflict between nature and man, and between humans themselves
  • B. Conflict between two different countries
  • C. Conflict between rich and poor classes
  • D. Conflict between science and religion

Answer: A. Conflict between nature and man, and between humans themselves

Explanation: The story highlights man vs nature (hailstorm ruining crops) and human vs human (lack of trust between Lencho and post office staff).
