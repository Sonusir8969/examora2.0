Suppl-6: The Ghat of the Only World 25 Questions

1. Who is the author of 'The Ghat of the Only World'?

  • A. Amitav Ghosh
  • B. Agha Shahid Ali
  • C. Vikram Seth
  • D. Khushwant Singh

Answer: A. Amitav Ghosh

Explanation: The chapter is a biographical homage written by renowned author Amitav Ghosh in memory of his friend, poet Agha Shahid Ali.

2. Who was Agha Shahid Ali?

  • A. A celebrated Kashmiri-American poet
  • B. A famous Indian politician
  • C. A renowned doctor in Brooklyn
  • D. A painter from Delhi

Answer: A. A celebrated Kashmiri-American poet

Explanation: Shahid was a well-known poet who wrote in English and hailed from Kashmir.

3. What disease was Agha Shahid Ali suffering from?

  • A. Brain cancer (Malignant brain tumour)
  • B. Tuberculosis
  • C. Heart disease
  • D. Kidney failure

Answer: A. Brain cancer (Malignant brain tumour)

Explanation: Shahid suffered from a malignant brain tumor that ultimately led to his death.

4. What promise did Shahid ask Amitav Ghosh to fulfill before his death?

  • A. To write something about him after he passes away
  • B. To publish his unreleased poetry books
  • C. To visit Kashmir on his behalf
  • D. To take care of his family

Answer: A. To write something about him after he passes away

Explanation: Shahid specifically requested Amitav to write about him so that he wouldn't be forgotten after his death.

5. Where was Shahid living during his treatment in the United States?

  • A. Brooklyn, New York
  • B. Chicago
  • C. Washington D.C.
  • D. Los Angeles

Answer: A. Brooklyn, New York

Explanation: Shahid lived in an apartment in Brooklyn, New York, during his last days.

6. On what date did Shahid first mention his approaching death to Amitav Ghosh?

  • A. April 25, 2001
  • B. May 10, 2000
  • C. December 8, 2001
  • D. January 1, 2002

Answer: A. April 25, 2001

Explanation: On April 25, 2001, Shahid spoke to Amitav about his memory blackouts and mentioned that he had little time left.

7. What was Shahid's attitude toward his terminal illness and impending death?

  • A. Cheerful, vibrant, and facing it with stoic grace and joy
  • B. Depressed, weeping, and bitter
  • C. Angry and blameful towards doctors
  • D. Isolated and refusing to speak to anyone

Answer: A. Cheerful, vibrant, and facing it with stoic grace and joy

Explanation: Shahid never let his illness diminish his enthusiasm for life, social gatherings, food, and music.

8. Which famous Indian singer's music was Shahid immensely fond of?

  • A. Begum Akhtar
  • B. Lata Mangeshkar
  • C. Asha Bhosle
  • D. Kishori Amonkar

Answer: A. Begum Akhtar

Explanation: Shahid was a great admirer of Ghazal singer Begum Akhtar and had met her during his youth in India.

9. Which culinary delicacy did Shahid love cooking and serving to his friends?

  • A. Kashmiri Wazwan and Rogan Josh
  • B. Hyderabadi Biryani
  • C. South Indian Dosa
  • D. Bengali Fish Curry

Answer: A. Kashmiri Wazwan and Rogan Josh

Explanation: Shahid was an enthusiastic cook who loved preparing traditional Kashmiri cuisine like Rogan Josh for guests.

10. What specific ingredient smell in cooking made Shahid instantly happy?

  • A. Kashmiri Rogan Josh with proper spices and ghee
  • B. Freshly brewed coffee
  • C. Baked bread
  • D. Roasted garlic

Answer: A. Kashmiri Rogan Josh with proper spices and ghee

Explanation: He took food very seriously and could judge the exact stage of cooking just by the aroma.

11. Where did Shahid wish to pass away initially?

  • A. Kashmir
  • B. New York
  • C. New Delhi
  • D. London

Answer: A. Kashmir

Explanation: Shahid expressed a strong desire to go back to Kashmir to die, though logistical and political issues prevented it.

12. Why was Shahid unable to return to Kashmir in his final days?

  • A. Due to administrative difficulties, political turmoil, and medical conditions
  • B. His family refused to go
  • C. He lost his passport
  • D. He changed his mind completely

Answer: A. Due to administrative difficulties, political turmoil, and medical conditions

Explanation: Feared complications and instability in the region made it impractical for him to travel while terminally ill.

13. Where was Shahid buried after his death?

  • A. Northampton, Massachusetts
  • B. Srinagar, Kashmir
  • C. Brooklyn, New York
  • D. New Delhi, India

Answer: A. Northampton, Massachusetts

Explanation: Shahid was laid to rest at Northampton, Massachusetts, near his family members' place.

14. What form of English poetry did Shahid help popularize in America?

  • A. The Ghazal written in English
  • B. Sonnets
  • C. Haiku
  • D. Free verse ballads

Answer: A. The Ghazal written in English

Explanation: Shahid was famous for adapting the traditional Urdu/Persian Ghazal form into English poetry masterfully.

15. What was Shahid's view on the political situation in Kashmir?

  • A. He was deeply pained by the violence but firmly believed in secularism and peace
  • B. He supported militant groups
  • C. He was completely indifferent to politics
  • D. He advocated for religious division

Answer: A. He was deeply pained by the violence but firmly believed in secularism and peace

Explanation: Shahid mourned the tragedy of Kashmir without taking narrow fanatical sides, valuing inclusivity and harmony.

16. What was the title of Shahid's poetry collection published in 2001, from which the chapter's title is derived?

  • A. The Country Without a Post Office
  • B. Rooms Are Never Finished
  • C. The Ghat of the Only World
  • D. A Nostalgist's Map of America

Answer: A. The Country Without a Post Office

Explanation: While 'The Country Without a Post Office' is one of his most famous works, the phrase 'The Ghat of the Only World' comes from his poem in 'Rooms Are Never Finished'.

17. How did Amitav Ghosh record his interactions with Shahid after Shahid asked him to write about him?

  • A. By keeping a meticulous daily diary of every phone call and conversation
  • B. By recording audio tapes secretly
  • C. By making video blogs
  • D. By asking Shahid's doctor for notes

Answer: A. By keeping a meticulous daily diary of every phone call and conversation

Explanation: From April 25 onwards, Amitav Ghosh noted down every phone call and meeting details in a written journal.

18. What kind of atmosphere always prevailed in Shahid's apartment?

  • A. Festive, filled with friends, laughter, food, and poetry recitations
  • B. Quiet and gloomy like a hospital room
  • C. Tense and argumentative
  • D. Dark and strictly closed to visitors

Answer: A. Festive, filled with friends, laughter, food, and poetry recitations

Explanation: Shahid loved company; his living room was constantly alive with poets, students, writers, and friends.

19. Which university did Shahid teach at as a professor of creative writing?

  • A. University of Utah / Massachusetts
  • B. Harvard University
  • C. Oxford University
  • D. Delhi University

Answer: A. University of Utah / Massachusetts

Explanation: Shahid taught at various American institutions including the University of Utah and University of Massachusetts Amherst.

20. What date did Agha Shahid Ali pass away?

  • A. December 8, 2001
  • B. November 15, 2001
  • C. April 25, 2001
  • D. January 10, 2002

Answer: A. December 8, 2001

Explanation: Shahid passed away peacefully in his sleep on December 8, 2001.

21. What feeling did Amitav Ghosh experience after Shahid's death?

  • A. A profound void/emptiness, yet enriched by the friendship
  • B. Relief that the suffering was over
  • C. Anger at medical science
  • D. Indifference

Answer: A. A profound void/emptiness, yet enriched by the friendship

Explanation: Ghosh felt a huge void in his life, but felt grateful to have known such a vibrant soul.

22. What does the word 'Ghat' mean in the context of the title?

  • A. A threshold or passage between life and death / bank of a river
  • B. A high mountain peak
  • C. A valley in Kashmir
  • D. A place of celebration

Answer: A. A threshold or passage between life and death / bank of a river

Explanation: 'Ghat' symbolizes the transition edge or riverbank leading to the next realm after death.

23. How did Shahid respond when a security guard at Barcelona airport asked about his occupation?

  • A. He replied, "I am a poet, and I write poetry."
  • B. He said he was a doctor
  • C. He remained silent
  • D. He said he was a tourist

Answer: A. He replied, "I am a poet, and I write poetry."

Explanation: Shahid proudly asserted his identity as a poet when questioned during airport security checks.

24. What quality of Shahid made him stand out among contemporary poets?

  • A. His love for life, music, secular ethos, and refusal to let sorrow conquer joy
  • B. His strict religious orthodoxy
  • C. His isolation from society
  • D. His commercial focus on making money

Answer: A. His love for life, music, secular ethos, and refusal to let sorrow conquer joy

Explanation: Shahid combined artistic brilliance with warmth, humor, hospitality, and a deep appreciation for culture.

25. What type of prose piece is 'The Ghat of the Only World'?

  • A. A memoir / biographical tribute
  • B. A fictional short story
  • C. A travelogue
  • D. A political essay

Answer: A. A memoir / biographical tribute

Explanation: It is a non-fictional memoir honoring the memory and legacy of a beloved friend and artist.
