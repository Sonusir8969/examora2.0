Ch-8: Motion 10 Questions

1. Which of the following physical quantities is a vector quantity?

  • A. Distance
  • B. Speed
  • C. Displacement
  • D. Time

Answer: C. Displacement

Explanation: Displacement has both magnitude and direction, making it a vector quantity, whereas distance and speed have only magnitude (scalars).

2. What is the SI unit of acceleration?

  • A. m/s
  • B. m/s²
  • C. m·s
  • D. km/h

Answer: B. m/s²

Explanation: Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity per unit time. Its SI unit is meters per second squared (m/s² or m·s⁻²).

3. If an object moves along a circular path of radius r and completes one full round, its displacement is:

  • A. 2πr
  • B. πr
  • C. Zero
  • D. 2r

Answer: C. Zero

Explanation: Since the initial position and final position are identical after completing one full round, the net displacement is zero (though distance is 2πr).

4. Slope of a velocity-time graph gives which physical quantity?

  • A. Distance
  • B. Displacement
  • C. Speed
  • D. Acceleration

Answer: D. Acceleration

Explanation: The slope of a velocity-time graph represents Δv / Δt, which is acceleration. (The area under it gives displacement).

5. Which of the following equations represents the second equation of motion?

  • A. v = u + at
  • B. s = ut + ½ at²
  • C. v² - u² = 2as
  • D. v = s / t

Answer: B. s = ut + ½ at²

Explanation: The 1st equation is v = u + at, the 2nd is s = ut + ½ at² (position-time relation), and the 3rd is v² = u² + 2as.

6. What type of motion is executed by an athlete running at a constant speed along a circular track?

  • A. Uniform Linear Motion
  • B. Accelerated Motion
  • C. Non-uniform Speed Motion
  • D. Retarded Motion

Answer: B. Accelerated Motion

Explanation: Even at constant speed, the direction of motion continuously changes at every point in circular motion, meaning velocity changes—making it accelerated motion.

7. To convert a speed from km/h to m/s, we multiply it by:

  • A. 18 / 5
  • B. 5 / 18
  • C. 10 / 36
  • D. 1000 / 60

Answer: B. 5 / 18

Explanation: 1 km/h = 1000 m / 3600 s = 5 / 18 m/s. Therefore, multiply km/h by 5/18 to get m/s.

8. The area under a speed-time (or velocity-time) graph represents:

  • A. Acceleration
  • B. Distance / Displacement
  • C. Velocity
  • D. Time taken

Answer: B. Distance / Displacement

Explanation: Area = speed × time = distance. The bounded area under a velocity-time graph equals total distance/displacement covered.

9. When a body travels equal distances in equal intervals of time, its motion is said to be:

  • A. Non-uniform
  • B. Uniform
  • C. Accelerated
  • D. Retarded

Answer: B. Uniform

Explanation: By definition, covering equal distances in equal intervals of time (no matter how small the time interval) defines uniform motion.

10. A train starts from rest and attains a velocity of 72 km/h in 5 minutes. Assuming uniform acceleration, what is its acceleration?

  • A. 1/15 m/s²
  • B. 2/15 m/s²
  • C. 0.2 m/s²
  • D. 4 m/s²

Answer: A. 1/15 m/s²

Explanation: u = 0, v = 72 × (5/18) = 20 m/s, t = 5 × 60 = 300 s. Acceleration a = (v - u)/t = (20 - 0)/300 = 1/15 m/s² (≈ 0.067 m/s²).
