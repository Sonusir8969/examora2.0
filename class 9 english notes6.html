NCERT Solutions for Class 9 English Chapter 6 My Childhood

NCERT Solutions for Class 9 English Beehive Chapter 6 My Childhood covers an excerpt from A.P.J. Abdul Kalam's autobiography Wings of Fire, describing his early life in Rameswaram, his family, friendships, and important questions and answers.

Overview of Topics & Subtopics (Click to Jump)

  • 1. About the Author and Setting
  • 2. Key Incidents
  • 3. Theme & Message

1. About the Author and Setting

Short Note:

• This chapter is an excerpt from 'Wings of Fire', the autobiography of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, who later became a renowned scientist and the President of India.

• Kalam grew up in a modest, close-knit family in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, where his father was a boat owner and a man of great wisdom despite limited formal education.

• He describes his childhood friendships with Ramanadha Sastry, Aravindan and Sivaprakasan, which crossed religious lines and taught him early lessons in secularism and unity.

2. Key Incidents

Short Note:

• Kalam recalls helping his cousin Samsuddin distribute newspapers to earn his own pocket money, showing his early sense of responsibility and independence.

• He describes an incident where a new teacher seated him separately because he was a Muslim sitting beside a Brahmin boy, which upset both families, until a more broad-minded teacher, Sivasubramania Iyer, corrected the situation and promoted harmony.

• His interactions with his science teacher and his fascination with the flight of birds sparked his early interest in aeronautics.

3. Theme & Message

Short Note:

• The chapter highlights the values of hard work, humility, communal harmony and the influence of good mentors and family members in shaping a person's character and ambitions.

• It shows how a modest upbringing, combined with strong values and curiosity, can lay the foundation for great achievements later in life.

Reading With Insight (Textbook Questions)

Q1: Why did Kalam's father decide to build a wooden boat, and what does this reveal about him?
Answer: Kalam's father built a boat to rent it to a fisherman friend for a small share of the daily catch, showing his practical wisdom, resourcefulness and willingness to help others even with limited resources.
Q2: How did the incident with the new teacher affect Kalam, and how was it resolved?
Answer: The new teacher's discriminatory act of separating Kalam from his Brahmin friend based on religion deeply upset both boys and their families; however, the issue was resolved when the more sensible teacher, Sivasubramania Iyer, intervened, questioned the narrow-mindedness of such discrimination, and restored harmony between the students.
Q3: What lesson did Kalam learn from his friendships during childhood?
Answer: Kalam learned that friendship and human values transcend religious and social boundaries, as his closest childhood friends belonged to different religious backgrounds, and this experience shaped his lifelong belief in secularism and unity.

Extract-Based / Short Answer Questions

Q4: What role did Kalam's family play in shaping his values?
Answer: Kalam's family, especially his father's wisdom and simplicity and his mother's compassion, instilled in him strong moral values, humility and a sense of duty that stayed with him throughout his life.
Q5: How did Kalam's early fascination with birds hint at his future career?
Answer: Kalam's childhood curiosity about how birds flew and his admiration for their movement foreshadowed his later career as an aerospace scientist and his key role in India's missile and space programmes.
