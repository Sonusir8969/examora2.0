Ch-5: Thermodynamics 30 Questions

1. The First Law of Thermodynamics is a statement of:

  • A. Conservation of energy
  • B. Conservation of mass
  • C. Conservation of momentum
  • D. Law of definite proportions

Answer: A. Conservation of energy

Explanation: The First Law states that energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed from one form to another (ΔU = q + w).

2. A system which can exchange neither energy nor matter with its surroundings is called:

  • A. An isolated system
  • B. A closed system
  • C. An open system
  • D. An adiabatic system

Answer: A. An isolated system

Explanation: An isolated system has boundary walls that prevent both matter transfer and heat exchange with surroundings.

3. Which of the following is an intensive property?

  • A. Temperature
  • B. Mass
  • C. Volume
  • D. Enthalpy

Answer: A. Temperature

Explanation: Intensive properties are independent of the amount of substance present in the system.

4. For a process occurring at constant pressure, heat exchanged (q_p) is equal to:

  • A. ΔH
  • B. ΔU
  • C. ΔG
  • D. ΔS

Answer: A. ΔH

Explanation: Enthalpy change (ΔH) represents the heat absorbed or evolved by a system at constant pressure.

5. The mathematical expression for the relation between ΔH and ΔU for ideal gases is:

  • A. ΔH = ΔU + Δn_g RT
  • B. ΔH = ΔU - Δn_g RT
  • C. ΔU = ΔH + Δn_g RT
  • D. ΔH = ΔU / RT

Answer: A. ΔH = ΔU + Δn_g RT

Explanation: For chemical reactions involving gases, ΔH = ΔU + Δ(PV) = ΔU + Δn_g RT where Δn_g is the change in moles of gaseous species.

6. For an isothermal expansion of an ideal gas, internal energy change (ΔU) is:

  • A. Zero
  • B. Positive
  • C. Negative
  • D. Infinite

Answer: A. Zero

Explanation: Internal energy of an ideal gas depends solely on temperature (T). Since ΔT = 0 in an isothermal process, ΔU = 0.

7. A reaction is always spontaneous at all temperatures if:

  • A. ΔH < 0 and ΔS > 0
  • B. ΔH > 0 and ΔS < 0
  • C. ΔH > 0 and ΔS > 0
  • D. ΔH < 0 and ΔS < 0

Answer: A. ΔH < 0 and ΔS > 0

Explanation: In ΔG = ΔH - TΔS, if ΔH is negative and ΔS is positive, ΔG will remain negative at any temperature.

8. Hess's Law of constant heat summation is based on:

  • A. First Law of Thermodynamics
  • B. Second Law of Thermodynamics
  • C. Third Law of Thermodynamics
  • D. Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics

Answer: A. First Law of Thermodynamics

Explanation: Hess's Law is a consequence of the conservation of energy, stating that overall enthalpy change is independent of the pathway taken.

9. The standard enthalpy of formation (Δ_f H°) of an element in its reference standard state is:

  • A. Zero
  • B. Positive
  • C. Negative
  • D. Dependent on pressure

Answer: A. Zero

Explanation: By convention, standard enthalpy of formation of an element in its most stable physical state at 1 bar and 298 K is taken as zero.

10. The unit of entropy (S) is:

  • A. J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹
  • B. J mol⁻¹
  • C. kJ mol⁻¹
  • D. J K mol⁻¹

Answer: A. J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹

Explanation: Entropy is defined as heat divided by absolute temperature (S = q/T), giving units of J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹.

11. In an adiabatic process, which of the following is true?

  • A. q = 0
  • B. ΔT = 0
  • C. w = 0
  • D. ΔP = 0

Answer: A. q = 0

Explanation: An adiabatic process occurs with no heat transfer between system and surroundings (q = 0).

12. The Third Law of Thermodynamics states that the entropy of a perfectly crystalline substance approaches zero as temperature approaches:

  • A. Absolute Zero (0 K)
  • B. 0 °C
  • C. 273 K
  • D. 100 °C

Answer: A. Absolute Zero (0 K)

Explanation: At 0 Kelvin, molecular motion completely ceases in a perfect crystal, causing entropy to drop to zero.

13. Which of the following state functions is used to measure degree of randomness or disorder?

  • A. Entropy
  • B. Enthalpy
  • C. Internal energy
  • D. Work done

Answer: A. Entropy

Explanation: Entropy (S) directly quantifies thermal disorder and chaotic motion within a thermodynamic system.

14. For a spontaneous process at constant temperature and pressure, Gibbs free energy change (ΔG) must be:

  • A. Negative (< 0)
  • B. Positive (> 0)
  • C. Zero (= 0)
  • D. Infinite

Answer: A. Negative (< 0)

Explanation: A process occurs spontaneously only if Gibbs free energy decreases (ΔG < 0).

15. Work done in a free expansion of an ideal gas in vacuum is:

  • A. Zero
  • B. Maximum
  • C. Positive
  • D. Negative

Answer: A. Zero

Explanation: Free expansion occurs against external pressure P_ext = 0, so work done w = -P_ext ΔV = 0.

16. The condition for equilibrium in a reversible reaction in terms of ΔG is:

  • A. ΔG = 0
  • B. ΔG < 0
  • C. ΔG > 0
  • D. ΔG = 1

Answer: A. ΔG = 0

Explanation: When a system reaches thermodynamic equilibrium, there is no driving force, hence ΔG = 0.

17. Which of the following is NOT a state function?

  • A. Heat (q)
  • B. Enthalpy (H)
  • C. Entropy (S)
  • D. Internal Energy (U)

Answer: A. Heat (q)

Explanation: Heat (q) and Work (w) are path functions because their values depend on the path taken between states.

18. Heat capacity at constant volume is denoted by C_v. The relation C_p - C_v for 1 mole of an ideal gas is:

  • A. R
  • B. 2R
  • C. R / 2
  • D. Zero

Answer: A. R

Explanation: Mayer's relation states C_p - C_v = R for one mole of an ideal gas.

19. In an endothermic reaction, enthalpy change (ΔH) is always:

  • A. Positive
  • B. Negative
  • C. Zero
  • D. Equal to ΔS

Answer: A. Positive

Explanation: Endothermic processes absorb heat from surroundings, increasing the enthalpy of products over reactants (ΔH > 0).

20. The change in entropy when ice melts to form liquid water at 0 °C is:

  • A. Positive
  • B. Negative
  • C. Zero
  • D. Undefined

Answer: A. Positive

Explanation: Transition from a rigid crystalline solid to a fluid liquid increases molecular mobility and disorder (ΔS > 0).

21. Bomb calorimeter is used to measure heat change at:

  • A. Constant volume (q_v)
  • B. Constant pressure (q_p)
  • C. Constant temperature
  • D. Variable volume

Answer: A. Constant volume (q_v)

Explanation: A bomb calorimeter has a rigid, sealed steel vessel maintaining constant volume during combustion reactions (q_v = ΔU).

22. Bond dissociation energy is always:

  • A. Positive
  • B. Negative
  • C. Zero
  • D. Dependent on temperature only

Answer: A. Positive

Explanation: Breaking chemical bonds requires energy input, making bond dissociation an endothermic process (ΔH > 0).

23. Which statement represents the Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics?

  • A. Defines concept of temperature and thermal equilibrium
  • B. Conservation of energy
  • C. Concept of entropy
  • D. Absolute zero entropy

Answer: A. Defines concept of temperature and thermal equilibrium

Explanation: If two bodies are in thermal equilibrium with a third body, they are in thermal equilibrium with each other.

24. Standard state conditions for thermodynamic measurements are:

  • A. 1 bar pressure and specified temperature (usually 298.15 K)
  • B. 1 atm pressure and 0 K
  • C. 100 bar pressure and 273 K
  • D. 1 bar pressure and 0 °C

Answer: A. 1 bar pressure and specified temperature (usually 298.15 K)

Explanation: The IUPAC standard state specifies a pressure of 1 bar at 298.15 K (25 °C).

25. An extensive property among the following is:

  • A. Volume
  • B. Density
  • C. Pressure
  • D. Refractive index

Answer: A. Volume

Explanation: Extensive properties depend on the mass or quantity of matter present in the system.

26. In an exothermic reaction, heat is evolved. Therefore, ΔH is:

  • A. Negative
  • B. Positive
  • C. Zero
  • D. Equal to ΔU + RT

Answer: A. Negative

Explanation: Exothermic reactions release energy into the surroundings, causing system enthalpy to decrease (ΔH < 0).

27. Relation between standard free energy change (ΔG°) and equilibrium constant (K) is:

  • A. ΔG° = -RT ln K
  • B. ΔG° = RT ln K
  • C. ΔG° = -RT log K
  • D. ΔG° = RT / K

Answer: A. ΔG° = -RT ln K

Explanation: Thermodynamically, standard free energy change is linked to equilibrium constant via ΔG° = -RT ln K = -2.303 RT log K.

28. Thermochemistry is mainly concerned with the study of:

  • A. Heat changes during chemical reactions
  • B. Rates of reaction
  • C. Movement of ions
  • D. Atomic structures

Answer: A. Heat changes during chemical reactions

Explanation: Thermochemistry deals with the heat energy absorbed or released during chemical transformations.

29. The unit of molar heat capacity is:

  • A. J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹
  • B. J K⁻¹
  • C. J mol⁻¹
  • D. kJ K⁻¹

Answer: A. J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹

Explanation: Molar heat capacity is the amount of heat required to raise the temperature of 1 mole of substance by 1 Kelvin.

30. For the sublimation of solid iodine (I₂ (s) → I₂ (g)), the signs of ΔH and ΔS are respectively:

  • A. Positive and Positive
  • B. Negative and Negative
  • C. Positive and Negative
  • D. Negative and Positive

Answer: A. Positive and Positive

Explanation: Sublimation requires heat absorption (ΔH > 0) and increases spatial disorder going from solid to gas (ΔS > 0).
