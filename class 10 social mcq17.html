Ch-17: Consumer Rights 30 Questions

1. In which year was the Consumer Protection Act (COPRA) enacted in India?

  • A. 1986
  • B. 1991
  • C. 1975
  • D. 2005

Answer: A. 1986

Explanation: The Indian Parliament enacted the Consumer Protection Act (COPRA) in 1986 to safeguard and promote consumer interests.

2. When is National Consumer Day celebrated in India every year?

  • A. 24th December
  • B. 15th March
  • C. 26th January
  • D. 2nd October

Answer: A. 24th December

Explanation: On 24th December 1986, the Consumer Protection Act was signed into law, which is why 24th December is observed as National Consumer Day.

3. World Consumer Rights Day is celebrated internationally on which date?

  • A. 15th March
  • B. 24th December
  • C. 10th December
  • D. 1st May

Answer: A. 15th March

Explanation: World Consumer Rights Day is observed globally every year on March 15.

4. Which certification mark guarantees quality standards for industrial manufactured products in India?

  • A. ISI Mark
  • B. AGMARK
  • C. Hallmark
  • D. FPO Mark

Answer: A. ISI Mark

Explanation: The ISI mark issued by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) certifies safety and quality standards for manufactured industrial goods.

5. Which certification mark is used to ensure quality and purity of agricultural products like ghee, honey, and spices?

  • A. AGMARK
  • B. ISI Mark
  • C. Hallmark
  • D. ISO Certification

Answer: A. AGMARK

Explanation: AGMARK is a quality certification mark applied to agricultural commodities in India.

6. Which quality certification mark is used for certifying gold and silver jewelry in India?

  • A. Hallmark
  • B. AGMARK
  • C. ISI Mark
  • D. FSSAI

Answer: A. Hallmark

Explanation: Hallmark certifies the purity and fineness of precious metals like gold and silver items sold in India.

7. Which consumer right empowers citizens to obtain information about ingredients, price, manufacturing date, and expiry date of a product?

  • A. Right to Information
  • B. Right to Safety
  • C. Right to Choose
  • D. Right to Representation

Answer: A. Right to Information

Explanation: The Right to Information guarantees that consumers are informed about details regarding the goods and services they purchase.

8. Under which Act enacted in 2005 can Indian citizens seek information about the functions and functioning of government departments?

  • A. Right to Information (RTI) Act
  • B. Consumer Protection Act
  • C. Essential Commodities Act
  • D. Monopolies Restrictive Trade Practices Act

Answer: A. Right to Information (RTI) Act

Explanation: The Right to Information (RTI) Act, passed in October 2005, allows citizens to request information from public authorities.

9. Which consumer right ensures that consumers are protected against products and services hazardous to life and property?

  • A. Right to Safety
  • B. Right to Seek Redressal
  • C. Right to Consumer Education
  • D. Right to Choose

Answer: A. Right to Safety

Explanation: The Right to Safety requires that goods like pressure cookers or electrical appliances meet safety standard requirements.

10. If a consumer is forced to purchase a specific product along with another product against their wish, which right is violated?

  • A. Right to Choose
  • B. Right to Safety
  • C. Right to Information
  • D. Right to Redressal

Answer: A. Right to Choose

Explanation: The Right to Choose ensures that any consumer has the freedom to select a product or service without compulsion.

11. Under COPRA, a three-tier quasi-judicial machinery was set up at which three levels?

  • A. District, State, and National levels
  • B. Village, Block, and District levels
  • C. State, National, and International levels
  • D. Municipal, High Court, and Supreme Court levels

Answer: A. District, State, and National levels

Explanation: COPRA established a three-tier consumer dispute redressal mechanism comprising District Commission, State Commission, and National Commission.

12. What is the popular slogan created by the government to spread awareness among consumers in India?

  • A. Jago Grahak Jago
  • B. Satyamev Jayate
  • C. Swachh Bharat Abhiyan
  • D. Digital India

Answer: A. Jago Grahak Jago

Explanation: 'Jago Grahak Jago' (Wake up, Consumer, Wake up) is a multimedia awareness campaign launched by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs.

13. Which of the following document is mandatory to present as proof when filing a complaint in a consumer court?

  • A. Cash Memo / Purchase Receipt
  • B. Ration Card
  • C. Driving License
  • D. Aadhaar Card

Answer: A. Cash Memo / Purchase Receipt

Explanation: A cash memo serves as legal proof of purchase when seeking compensation in consumer courts.

14. What right allows consumers to seek compensation against unfair trade practices or exploitation?

  • A. Right to Seek Redressal
  • B. Right to Information
  • C. Right to Safety
  • D. Right to Association

Answer: A. Right to Seek Redressal

Explanation: Right to Seek Redressal ensures consumers can get fair settlements, product replacements, or money refunds for damaged products.

15. What international organisation set up guidelines for consumer protection in 1985?

  • A. United Nations (UN)
  • B. World Bank
  • C. World Trade Organisation (WTO)
  • D. International Court of Justice

Answer: A. United Nations (UN)

Explanation: In 1985, the United Nations adopted the UN Guidelines for Consumer Protection to assist countries in protecting consumer rights.

16. What global umbrella organization links consumer advocacy groups across more than 100 countries?

  • A. Consumers International
  • B. Amnesty International
  • C. Transparency International
  • D. Greenpeace

Answer: A. Consumers International

Explanation: Consumers International is the global federation of consumer groups that leads international consumer awareness.

17. Which of the following is an example of an unfair trade practice by sellers?

  • A. Adulteration and under-weighing of goods
  • B. Providing valid cash memo receipts
  • C. Displaying correct MRP on goods
  • D. Selling certified ISI marked appliances

Answer: A. Adulteration and under-weighing of goods

Explanation: Adulteration, selling expired goods, false claims, and short weighing are forms of unfair trade practices.

18. What does MRP printed on a packaged commodity stand for?

  • A. Maximum Retail Price
  • B. Minimum Retail Price
  • C. Mandatory Retail Payment
  • D. Market Rate Price

Answer: A. Maximum Retail Price

Explanation: MRP stands for Maximum Retail Price, which is the highest price that can be charged to a consumer for that product.

19. Charging more than the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) printed on a product is considered:

  • A. An illegal and punishable offense under Consumer Protection Law
  • B. A legal business practice
  • C. An optional surcharge
  • D. Standard taxation policy

Answer: A. An illegal and punishable offense under Consumer Protection Law

Explanation: Sellers are prohibited by law from selling any packaged good at a price higher than the printed MRP.

20. What non-governmental organization in India works toward educating and guiding consumers on how to file cases in consumer courts?

  • A. Consumer Protection Councils (Grahak Panchayat)
  • B. Election Commission
  • C. NITI Aayog
  • D. Bar Council of India

Answer: A. Consumer Protection Councils (Grahak Panchayat)

Explanation: Consumer Protection Councils assist consumers in filing complaints and raising public awareness about consumer rights.

21. The agency responsible for standardizing products in India is called:

  • A. Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)
  • B. Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
  • C. Security and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
  • D. Food Corporation of India (FCI)

Answer: A. Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)

Explanation: BIS is the national standards body of India responsible for harmonious development of standardization and quality marking.

22. Which consumer right ensures that consumers acquire the knowledge and skill to be an informed consumer throughout life?

  • A. Right to Consumer Education
  • B. Right to Representation
  • C. Right to Safety
  • D. Right to Choice

Answer: A. Right to Consumer Education

Explanation: The Right to Consumer Education focuses on making citizens aware of their rights, legal remedies, and responsibilities as buyers.

23. If a consumer is dissatisfied with the decision of the District Consumer Commission, where can they appeal next?

  • A. State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission
  • B. National Consumer Commission
  • C. Supreme Court of India
  • D. High Court directly

Answer: A. State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission

Explanation: Appeals against orders of a District Commission can be filed at the State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

24. What happens when a consumer files a case in a consumer forum without an advocate?

  • A. Consumers can represent their own case without hiring a lawyer
  • B. The case is automatically rejected
  • C. The consumer is fined
  • D. Court proceedings are canceled

Answer: A. Consumers can represent their own case without hiring a lawyer

Explanation: Consumer forums are designed to be accessible and straightforward, allowing consumers to present their claims directly.

25. Consumer Protection Act was amended and updated comprehensively in which year to include e-commerce transactions?

  • A. 2019
  • B. 2000
  • C. 2010
  • D. 1995

Answer: A. 2019

Explanation: The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 replaced the 1986 Act, introducing rules for e-commerce, central protection authorities, and mediation.

26. Adding harmful or substandard substances to food products to increase weight or volume is known as:

  • A. Adulteration
  • B. Standardization
  • C. Diversification
  • D. Monetization

Answer: A. Adulteration

Explanation: Food adulteration involves adding unsafe or inferior substances to food products, posing risks to health.

27. Which logo ensures food safety compliance on food packages in India?

  • A. FSSAI
  • B. ISO
  • C. Eco-mark
  • D. Woolmark

Answer: A. FSSAI

Explanation: The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) license number and logo certify food safety standards on food packages.

28. What certification mark is provided for environment-friendly products in India?

  • A. Eco-mark
  • B. Green-mark
  • C. Earth-mark
  • D. Bio-mark

Answer: A. Eco-mark

Explanation: The Eco-mark scheme identifies consumer products that meet specified environmental criteria along with quality standards.

29. Who is considered a consumer under the law?

  • A. A person who buys goods or services for personal use or consumption
  • B. A trader buying goods for resale
  • C. A manufacturer buying raw materials for factory production
  • D. A wholesaler selling goods to retailers

Answer: A. A person who buys goods or services for personal use or consumption

Explanation: A consumer is an end-user who purchases products or services for personal consumption rather than commercial resale.

30. Why is consumer awareness important in a market economy?

  • A. It prevents exploitation by traders and promotes safe, ethical market practices
  • B. It helps sellers charge higher prices
  • C. It eliminates the need for laws and courts
  • D. It stops consumers from buying imported goods

Answer: A. It prevents exploitation by traders and promotes safe, ethical market practices

Explanation: Well-informed consumers are aware of their rights and duties, protecting themselves from market malpractices and exploitation.
