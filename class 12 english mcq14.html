Ch-14: The Third Level 5 Questions

1. Who is the author of 'The Third Level'?

  • A. Jack Finney
  • B. Isaac Asimov
  • C. Ray Bradbury
  • D. H.G. Wells

Answer: A. Jack Finney

Explanation: 'The Third Level' is a short story written by the American author Jack Finney, blending elements of fantasy and psychological escapism.

2. What mysterious discovery does Charley, the narrator, claim to make at Grand Central Station?

  • A. A secret exit to the street
  • B. A hidden third level of the station that leads back in time to the year 1894
  • C. An underground shopping mall
  • D. A new subway line

Answer: B. A hidden third level of the station that leads back in time to the year 1894

Explanation: Charley claims to have stumbled upon a mysterious third level at Grand Central Station, different from the known two levels, which somehow led him back to the year 1894.

3. What does Charley initially believe is the psychological reason behind his supposed discovery of the third level?

  • A. It was purely a coincidence with no meaning
  • B. It might be a form of escapism from the fears and anxieties of modern life, like the atomic bomb and wars
  • C. He was time travelling for entertainment
  • D. He wanted to become rich

Answer: B. It might be a form of escapism from the fears and anxieties of modern life, like the atomic bomb and wars

Explanation: Charley initially rationalizes his strange discovery of the third level as a possible psychological escape mechanism, a way of retreating from the stresses and insecurities of the modern, anxiety-ridden world.

4. What evidence does Charley's friend Sam provide that supports the idea that the third level and time travel are real?

  • A. A newspaper clipping
  • B. An old letter from Sam, dated in 1894, along with a rare, correctly dated old stamp Charley finds later
  • C. A photograph
  • D. A recorded phone message

Answer: B. An old letter from Sam, dated in 1894, along with a rare, correctly dated old stamp Charley finds later

Explanation: The story's twist involves Charley discovering an old letter from his friend Sam, seemingly written from 1894, and later a rare 1894-dated postage stamp bought by his father, suggesting Sam actually did escape to the past.

5. What is a central theme explored in 'The Third Level'?

  • A. The mechanics of railway systems
  • B. Escapism from the pressures of contemporary life through fantasy and nostalgia for a simpler past
  • C. The importance of punctuality
  • D. Advances in scientific technology

Answer: B. Escapism from the pressures of contemporary life through fantasy and nostalgia for a simpler past

Explanation: The story explores humanity's desire to escape modern anxieties by retreating into nostalgic fantasies of a simpler, calmer past era, represented by the imagined journey to 1894.
