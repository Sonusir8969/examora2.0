NCERT Solutions for Class 11 English Ch-3: Discovering Tut: the Saga Continues

'Discovering Tut: the Saga Continues' by A.R. Williams is an informative article about the ongoing scientific investigation into the mysterious life and death of the ancient Egyptian pharaoh Tutankhamun, using modern forensic technology like CT scans.

Overview of Topics & Subtopics

  • Discovery of Tutankhamun's Tomb by Howard Carter
  • Modern Forensic Investigation Using CT Scan Technology
  • Theories Regarding the Cause of Tutankhamun's Death
  • Insights into Ancient Egyptian Mummification Practices

Important Questions and Answers

Q1: Who discovered Tutankhamun's tomb, and when was it found?
Answer: The tomb of the young pharaoh Tutankhamun was discovered by British archaeologist Howard Carter in 1922 in the Valley of the Kings, Egypt, and it was remarkable for being found almost completely intact, filled with numerous treasures.
Q2: What modern technology was used by scientists to investigate Tutankhamun's mummy, and why?
Answer: Scientists used computed tomography (CT) scan technology to examine Tutankhamun's mummy non-invasively, creating over 1,700 detailed digital images of his body, allowing them to investigate his physical condition and possible causes of death without physically damaging the fragile mummy.
Q3: What were some of the earlier theories regarding the cause of Tutankhamun's death?
Answer: Earlier theories, based on limited X-ray evidence, suggested that Tutankhamun might have died from a blow to the head, possibly indicating murder, since a bone fragment was found loose inside his skull.
Q4: What did the CT scan reveal about the cause of Tutankhamun's death?
Answer: The CT scan team concluded that the skull fragment was likely a result of the mummification process itself rather than a fatal head injury, and they instead found evidence of a badly broken leg, which might have caused a fatal infection, suggesting he may have died from complications of this injury rather than being murdered.
Q5: What broader significance does this ongoing scientific investigation hold?
Answer: This ongoing investigation demonstrates how modern forensic science and technology can be combined with archaeology to reinterpret and gain deeper insights into ancient historical mysteries, continually reshaping our understanding of the past.
