Ch-10: Circles 30 Questions

1. How many tangents can a circle have at maximum?

  • A. One
  • B. Two
  • C. Zero
  • D. Infinitely many

Answer: D. Infinitely many

Explanation: A circle consists of infinitely many points, and a tangent can be drawn at each point.

2. A line intersecting a circle in two distinct points is called a:

  • A. Tangent
  • B. Secant
  • C. Chord
  • D. Radius

Answer: B. Secant

Explanation: A line that intersects a circle at two points is called a secant.

3. A tangent to a circle intersects it in _____ point(s).

  • A. Exactly one
  • B. Two
  • C. Three
  • D. No

Answer: A. Exactly one

Explanation: By definition, a tangent touches the circle at exactly one point.

4. The angle between a tangent to a circle and the radius through the point of contact is:

  • A. 45°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 180°

Answer: C. 90°

Explanation: The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.

5. From a point Q, the length of the tangent to a circle is 24 cm and the distance of Q from the centre is 25 cm. The radius of the circle is:

  • A. 7 cm
  • B. 12 cm
  • C. 15 cm
  • D. 24.5 cm

Answer: A. 7 cm

Explanation: Radius = √(25² - 24²) = √(625 - 576) = √49 = 7 cm.

6. The maximum number of parallel tangents a circle can have on opposite sides of a diameter is:

  • A. 1
  • B. 2
  • C. 4
  • D. Infinite

Answer: B. 2

Explanation: At the ends of a diameter, a circle can have at most two parallel tangents.

7. If TP and TQ are two tangents to a circle with centre O so that ∠POQ = 110°, then ∠PTQ is equal to:

  • A. 60°
  • B. 70°
  • C. 80°
  • D. 90°

Answer: B. 70°

Explanation: In quadrilateral OPTQ, ∠OPT = ∠OQT = 90°. Thus, ∠PTQ = 180° - ∠POQ = 180° - 110° = 70°.

8. If tangents PA and PB from a point P to a circle with centre O are inclined to each other at an angle of 80°, then ∠POA is equal to:

  • A. 50°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 70°
  • D. 80°

Answer: A. 50°

Explanation: ∠APB = 80° ⇒ ∠AOB = 180° - 80° = 100°. Line OP bisects ∠AOB, so ∠POA = 100° / 2 = 50°.

9. The lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are:

  • A. Unqual
  • B. Equal
  • C. Parallel
  • D. Perpendicular

Answer: B. Equal

Explanation: Theorem 10.2 states that lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal.

10. Two concentric circles are of radii 5 cm and 3 cm. The length of the chord of the larger circle which touches the smaller circle is:

  • A. 8 cm
  • B. 10 cm
  • C. 4 cm
  • D. 6 cm

Answer: A. 8 cm

Explanation: Half length of chord = √(5² - 3²) = √(25 - 9) = √16 = 4 cm. Full chord length = 2 × 4 = 8 cm.

11. Number of tangents that can be drawn from a point inside a circle is:

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. 2
  • D. Infinite

Answer: A. 0

Explanation: No tangent can be drawn to a circle from a point lying inside it.

12. Number of tangents that can be drawn to a circle from a point outside it is:

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. 2
  • D. 3

Answer: C. 2

Explanation: Exactly two tangents can be drawn to a circle from an external point.

13. A quadrilateral ABCD is drawn to circumscribe a circle. Then AB + CD is equal to:

  • A. BC + AD
  • B. AC + BD
  • C. AD + BD
  • D. AB + BC

Answer: A. BC + AD

Explanation: For a quadrilateral circumscribing a circle, sum of opposite sides are equal: AB + CD = AD + BC.

14. If a parallelogram circumscribes a circle, then it is a:

  • A. Square
  • B. Rectangle
  • C. Rhombus
  • D. Trapezium

Answer: C. Rhombus

Explanation: A parallelogram circumscribing a circle has all sides equal, making it a rhombus.

15. The common point of a tangent and the circle is called:

  • A. Centre
  • B. Point of contact
  • C. Secant
  • D. Origin

Answer: B. Point of contact

Explanation: The single point where the tangent touches the circle is called the point of contact.

16. Distance between two parallel tangents of a circle of radius 4 cm is:

  • A. 4 cm
  • B. 8 cm
  • C. 16 cm
  • D. 2 cm

Answer: B. 8 cm

Explanation: Distance between two parallel tangents equals the diameter = 2 × 4 cm = 8 cm.

17. If radii of two concentric circles are 6 cm and 10 cm, length of chord of larger circle touching smaller circle is:

  • A. 8 cm
  • B. 12 cm
  • C. 16 cm
  • D. 20 cm

Answer: C. 16 cm

Explanation: Half chord = √(10² - 6²) = √(100 - 36) = √64 = 8 cm. Total chord length = 2 × 8 = 16 cm.

18. In figure, if PA and PB are tangents to a circle with centre O such that ∠APB = 50°, then ∠AOB is:

  • A. 130°
  • B. 100°
  • C. 50°
  • D. 150°

Answer: A. 130°

Explanation: ∠AOB + ∠APB = 180° ⇒ ∠AOB = 180° - 50° = 130°.

19. If angle between two tangents drawn from P to circle with radius r and centre O is 60°, then length of OP is:

  • A. r
  • B. 2r
  • C. r√3
  • D. r/2

Answer: B. 2r

Explanation: sin 30° = r / OP ⇒ 1/2 = r / OP ⇒ OP = 2r.

20. The angle between two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle is _____ to angle subtended by line-segment joining points of contact at centre.

  • A. Equal
  • B. Complementary
  • C. Supplementary
  • D. Half

Answer: C. Supplementary

Explanation: The sum of these two angles is 180°, which makes them supplementary.

21. Line segment joining centre to point of contact of tangent is always _____ to tangent.

  • A. Parallel
  • B. Perpendicular
  • C. Equal
  • D. Coincident

Answer: B. Perpendicular

Explanation: Standard Theorem 10.1: Radius is perpendicular to tangent at point of contact.

22. If length of tangent from point A at distance 5 cm from centre is 4 cm, radius of circle is:

  • A. 3 cm
  • B. 4 cm
  • C. 5 cm
  • D. 9 cm

Answer: A. 3 cm

Explanation: r = √(5² - 4²) = √(25 - 16) = √9 = 3 cm.

23. Total number of tangents that can be drawn to a circle from a point on its circumference is:

  • A. 0
  • B. 1
  • C. 2
  • D. Infinite

Answer: B. 1

Explanation: Exactly one tangent passes through a specific point lying on the circle.

24. In a right triangle ABC right angled at B, BC = 6 cm and AB = 8 cm. A circle is inscribed in ΔABC. Radius of circle is:

  • A. 1 cm
  • B. 2 cm
  • C. 3 cm
  • D. 4 cm

Answer: B. 2 cm

Explanation: Hypotenuse AC = √(6² + 8²) = 10 cm. Inradius r = (a + b - c)/2 = (6 + 8 - 10)/2 = 4/2 = 2 cm.

25. If PT is tangent to circle with centre O and radius 6 cm such that PT = 8 cm, then OP is:

  • A. 10 cm
  • B. 12 cm
  • C. 14 cm
  • D. 7 cm

Answer: A. 10 cm

Explanation: OP = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 cm.

26. Concentric circles are circles having the same:

  • A. Radius
  • B. Diameter
  • C. Centre
  • D. Circumference

Answer: C. Centre

Explanation: Circles having same centre but different radii are called concentric circles.

27. If PA and PB are tangents from P to a circle with centre O and ∠AOB = 130°, then ∠APB is:

  • A. 40°
  • B. 50°
  • C. 60°
  • D. 70°

Answer: B. 50°

Explanation: ∠APB = 180° - 130° = 50°.

28. A line touching a circle at one point only is called a:

  • A. Secant
  • B. Tangent
  • C. Chord
  • D. Segment

Answer: B. Tangent

Explanation: Definition of tangent: A straight line touching a circle at exactly one point.

29. Tangents drawn at the ends of a diameter of a circle are:

  • A. Perpendicular
  • B. Parallel
  • C. Intersecting
  • D. Equal in length

Answer: B. Parallel

Explanation: Both tangents are perpendicular to the diameter, so alternate interior angles are 90°, making them parallel.

30. If length of a tangent from a point P to a circle of radius 9 cm is 12 cm, distance of P from centre is:

  • A. 13 cm
  • B. 15 cm
  • C. 21 cm
  • D. 11 cm

Answer: B. 15 cm

Explanation: Distance = √(9² + 12²) = √(81 + 144) = √225 = 15 cm.
