Ch-9: Madam Rides the Bus 30 Questions

1. Who is the author of 'Madam Rides the Bus'?

  • A. Vallikkannan
  • B. Gavin Maxwell
  • C. Arup Kumar Datta
  • D. Lokesh Abrol

Answer: A. Vallikkannan

Explanation: 'Madam Rides the Bus' was written by Tamil writer Vallikkannan and translated into English by K.S. Sundaram.

2. What was Valli's full name?

  • A. Valliammai
  • B. Vallidevi
  • C. Valliamman
  • D. Vallikanya

Answer: A. Valliammai

Explanation: Valli's full name was Valliammai, and she was an eight-year-old girl.

3. How old was Valli?

  • A. 8 years old
  • B. 10 years old
  • C. 12 years old
  • D. 6 years old

Answer: A. 8 years old

Explanation: Valli was an eight-year-old girl who was curious about the outside world.

4. What was Valli's favorite pastime?

  • A. Standing in the front doorway of her house watching the street
  • B. Playing with children of her own age
  • C. Reading storybooks in her room
  • D. Going to the market with her mother

Answer: A. Standing in the front doorway of her house watching the street

Explanation: Valli had no playmates of her own age on her street, so standing in the front doorway was her favorite pastime.

5. What was Valli's deepest and strongest desire?

  • A. To ride on the bus that traveled between her village and town
  • B. To buy new clothes and toys
  • C. To move to a big city
  • D. To go to school in the nearby town

Answer: A. To ride on the bus that traveled between her village and town

Explanation: Valli's overwhelming desire was to take a ride on the bus, even if just once.

6. What was the distance from Valli's village to the nearest town?

  • A. 6 miles
  • B. 10 miles
  • C. 4 miles
  • D. 12 miles

Answer: A. 6 miles

Explanation: The distance from her village to the nearest town was six miles.

7. How much was the one-way bus fare to the town?

  • A. 30 paise
  • B. 50 paise
  • C. 1 rupee
  • D. 20 paise

Answer: A. 30 paise

Explanation: The bus fare was thirty paise one way, which Valli overheard was "almost nothing at all" to a rich person.

8. How long did the bus journey take from the village to the town?

  • A. 45 minutes
  • B. 30 minutes
  • C. 1 hour
  • D. 20 minutes

Answer: A. 45 minutes

Explanation: The trip to the town took forty-five minutes.

9. How did Valli gather information about the bus journey?

  • A. By listening to neighbors and asking careful questions
  • B. By reading a timetable at the bus station
  • C. By asking the bus conductor directly beforehand
  • D. From her school teachers

Answer: A. By listening to neighbors and asking careful questions

Explanation: Valli listened attentively to conversations between her neighbors who used the bus and asked discreet questions.

10. How much money did Valli need in total for a round trip on the bus?

  • A. 60 paise
  • B. 30 paise
  • C. 1 rupee
  • D. 90 paise

Answer: A. 60 paise

Explanation: She needed 30 paise for the journey to the town and another 30 paise for the return journey, totaling 60 paise.

11. How did Valli manage to save sixty paise for the bus fare?

  • A. By resisting every temptation to buy peppermints, toys, balloons, and rides on the merry-go-round
  • B. By borrowing money from her mother
  • C. By selling old books
  • D. By doing chores for neighbors

Answer: A. By resisting every temptation to buy peppermints, toys, balloons, and rides on the merry-go-round

Explanation: Valli painstakingly saved every stray coin and stifled all her childish desires to collect sixty paise.

12. What time did Valli plan her bus trip to ensure her mother wouldn't notice?

  • A. On the 1:00 PM bus during her mother's afternoon nap time
  • B. Early in the morning at 6:00 AM
  • C. Late in the evening after dinner
  • D. During school hours

Answer: A. On the 1:00 PM bus during her mother's afternoon nap time

Explanation: Her mother slept every day from about one to four, giving Valli three hours of undisturbed time.

13. Why did the bus conductor call Valli 'Madam'?

  • A. Because Valli acted like a self-reliant, confident grown-up and refused help
  • B. Because Valli was wearing adult clothes
  • C. To mock and tease her maliciously
  • D. Because he mistook her for a wealthy passenger

Answer: A. Because Valli acted like a self-reliant, confident grown-up and refused help

Explanation: The conductor was a jolly person and playfully called her 'Madam' because of her independent and dignified attitude.

14. Why did Valli stand up on her seat during the bus ride?

  • A. A canvas blind covered the lower part of the window, blocking her view
  • B. There were no vacant seats available
  • C. She wanted to dance inside the bus
  • D. The seat was wet and dirty

Answer: A. A canvas blind covered the lower part of the window, blocking her view

Explanation: The lower part of her window was covered with a canvas blind, so she stood up on the seat to look over the blind.

15. What scenery did Valli enjoy through the bus window on her way to town?

  • A. A canal, palm trees, grasslands, mountains, and blue sky
  • B. Dense dark forests and waterfalls
  • C. Tall concrete skyscrapers and factories
  • D. Crowded shopping malls and markets

Answer: A. A canal, palm trees, grasslands, mountains, and blue sky

Explanation: On one side was a canal flanked by palm trees, grassland, distant mountains, and on the other was a deep ditch and green fields.

16. Why was Valli annoyed by the elderly woman who sat next to her?

  • A. The woman chewed betel nut, had ugly earholes, and treated Valli like a child
  • B. The woman tried to take Valli's money
  • C. The woman pushed her off the seat
  • D. The woman was singing loudly

Answer: A. The woman chewed betel nut, had ugly earholes, and treated Valli like a child

Explanation: Valli found the elderly woman repulsive with big ear holes and betel juice about to spill, and disliked her probing questions.

17. What amusing sight made Valli clap her hands with laughter?

  • A. A young cow running fast right in the middle of the road in front of the bus
  • B. A monkey riding a bicycle
  • C. A dog chasing the bus wheels
  • D. The conductor making funny faces

Answer: A. A young cow running fast right in the middle of the road in front of the bus

Explanation: A cow with its tail held high ran right in front of the bus. The more the driver honked, the faster and crazier it ran, delighting Valli.

18. Why didn't Valli get off the bus when it reached the town destination?

  • A. She was afraid of getting lost and only intended to take the return ride
  • B. She forgot her ticket inside the bus
  • C. The conductor ordered her to stay seated
  • D. It started raining heavily outside

Answer: A. She was afraid of getting lost and only intended to take the return ride

Explanation: Valli was too scared to wander around the town alone and planned to go back home on the same bus.

19. What gesture of kindness did the conductor offer Valli at the town bus stop?

  • A. He offered to buy her a cold drink from a nearby stall with his own money
  • B. He offered her a free ticket for the return journey
  • C. He gave her a toy doll as a gift
  • D. He offered to introduce her to his family

Answer: A. He offered to buy her a cold drink from a nearby stall with his own money

Explanation: The conductor kindly offered to buy her a cold drink since she wouldn't get off, but Valli politely declined.

20. What tragic sight did Valli see on her return journey to the village?

  • A. The same young cow lying dead by the roadside
  • B. An injured dog on the street
  • C. A broken-down bus blocking the road
  • D. A house on fire in the village

Answer: A. The same young cow lying dead by the roadside

Explanation: On her way back, Valli saw the lovable cow lying lifeless by the roadside, hit by a fast vehicle.

21. How did seeing the dead cow impact Valli's mood?

  • A. She became deeply saddened, quiet, and lost all interest in looking outside
  • B. She became terrified and started crying out loud
  • C. She ignored it and continued laughing
  • D. She asked the conductor to stop the bus immediately

Answer: A. She became deeply saddened, quiet, and lost all interest in looking outside

Explanation: The memory of the dead cow haunted Valli, dampening her enthusiasm and ruining her joy for the rest of the ride.

22. What life lesson did Valli encounter through the experience with the cow?

  • A. The mysterious and fragile nature of life and death
  • B. That animals should not be allowed on roads
  • C. That bus drivers drive too fast
  • D. That traveling alone is always fun

Answer: A. The mysterious and fragile nature of life and death

Explanation: The stark contrast between the lively running cow and its lifeless body taught Valli about the harsh reality of death.

23. What time did Valli reach back home from her bus journey?

  • A. 2:45 PM
  • B. 3:30 PM
  • C. 1:45 PM
  • D. 4:00 PM

Answer: A. 2:45 PM

Explanation: Valli took the 1:00 PM bus, reached town at 1:45 PM, and arrived back at her village by 2:45 PM.

24. Who was at Valli's house talking to her mother when she returned?

  • A. Her aunt from South Street
  • B. A village shopkeeper
  • C. Her school teacher
  • D. A neighbor woman

Answer: A. Her aunt from South Street

Explanation: When Valli entered her house, she found her mother awake and chatting with one of her aunts from South Street.

25. What description did the aunt give regarding Valli's personality?

  • A. A real 'chit of a girl' who pokes her nose into matters like a grown lady
  • B. A shy and obedient quiet child
  • C. A foolish girl who knows nothing
  • D. A naughty child who always causes trouble

Answer: A. A real 'chit of a girl' who pokes her nose into matters like a grown lady

Explanation: Her aunt described Valli as a 'chit of a girl' who speaks like a grown woman taking part in conversations.

26. Why did Valli smile secretly to herself at the end of the story?

  • A. She knew about her secret bus journey which her mother and aunt were completely unaware of
  • B. She was remembering a funny joke
  • C. She had hidden extra money in her pocket
  • D. She was making fun of her aunt's attire

Answer: A. She knew about her secret bus journey which her mother and aunt were completely unaware of

Explanation: Valli smiled because her mother and aunt were discussing things happening outside without realizing Valli had just returned from town.

27. What character traits best define Valli in this story?

  • A. Courageous, self-reliant, meticulous planner, and observant
  • B. Foolish, disobedient, and irresponsible
  • C. Cowardly, shy, and dependent on others
  • D. Careless, talkative, and wasteful

Answer: A. Courageous, self-reliant, meticulous planner, and observant

Explanation: Valli demonstrates remarkable maturity, meticulous planning, restraint, courage, and keen powers of observation.

28. What was the color of the new bus Valli traveled on?

  • A. White with green stripes
  • B. Red with yellow stripes
  • C. Blue with white stripes
  • D. Yellow with red stripes

Answer: A. White with green stripes

Explanation: The bus was brand new, painted a gleaming white with some green stripes along the sides.

29. Who translated 'Madam Rides the Bus' into English from Tamil?

  • A. K.S. Sundaram
  • B. R.K. Narayan
  • C. Kamala Das
  • D. Mulk Raj Anand

Answer: A. K.S. Sundaram

Explanation: The story was translated from Tamil into English by K.S. Sundaram.

30. What was the conductor's attitude towards Valli throughout the journey?

  • A. Helpful, humorous, respectful, and friendly
  • B. Rude, strict, and impatient
  • C. Careless, ignoreful, and indifferent
  • D. Angry and demanding extra money

Answer: A. Helpful, humorous, respectful, and friendly

Explanation: The conductor was a cheerful person who respected Valli's self-confidence and humorously addressed her as 'Madam'.
