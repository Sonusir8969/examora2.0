Class 10 Social Science Board Exam Strategy

Key Board Preparation Tips:

• History: Focus heavily on 'Nationalism in India' for source-based questions, long answers, and historical map identification (INC Sessions, Satyagraha centers).

• Geography: Practice map marking for dams, major crop producers, nuclear/thermal power plants, sea ports, and international airports.

• Political Science: Focus on comparative case studies (Belgium vs Sri Lanka), 3-tier Federalism features, national & regional parties, and political reforms.

• Economics: Master key indicators (BMI, Per Capita Income, HDI), formal vs informal credit sources, double coincidence of wants, and MNC globalisation impacts.
