Ch-12: Heron's Formula 30 Questions

1. Heron's formula for the area of a triangle with side lengths a, b, and c is:

  • A. √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)]
  • B. s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)
  • C. ½ × base × height
  • D. √[(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)]

Answer: A. √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)]

Explanation: Heron's formula states that Area = √[s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)], where s is the semi-perimeter.

2. In Heron's formula, 's' represents the:

  • A. Perimeter
  • B. Semi-perimeter
  • C. Square of perimeter
  • D. Side length

Answer: B. Semi-perimeter

Explanation: 's' stands for semi-perimeter, which is equal to (a + b + c) / 2.

3. If the sides of a triangle are 3 cm, 4 cm, and 5 cm, its semi-perimeter (s) is:

  • A. 12 cm
  • B. 6 cm
  • C. 8 cm
  • D. 10 cm

Answer: B. 6 cm

Explanation: s = (3 + 4 + 5) / 2 = 12 / 2 = 6 cm.

4. The area of a triangle with sides 3 cm, 4 cm, and 5 cm is:

  • A. 6 cm²
  • B. 12 cm²
  • C. 15 cm²
  • D. 20 cm²

Answer: A. 6 cm²

Explanation: s = 6; Area = √[6 × (6-3) × (6-4) × (6-5)] = √[6 × 3 × 2 × 1] = √36 = 6 cm².

5. The area of an equilateral triangle with side 'a' is given by:

  • A. (√3 / 2) a²
  • B. (√3 / 4) a²
  • C. (√3 / 4) a
  • D. (1 / 2) a²

Answer: B. (√3 / 4) a²

Explanation: Using Heron's formula or standard trigonometry, the area of an equilateral triangle of side 'a' is (√3 / 4)a².

6. What is the perimeter of an equilateral triangle of side 10 cm?

  • A. 20 cm
  • B. 30 cm
  • C. 15 cm
  • D. 40 cm

Answer: B. 30 cm

Explanation: Perimeter = 3 × side = 3 × 10 = 30 cm.

7. The area of an equilateral triangle with side 8 cm is:

  • A. 16√3 cm²
  • B. 32√3 cm²
  • C. 64√3 cm²
  • D. 8√3 cm²

Answer: A. 16√3 cm²

Explanation: Area = (√3 / 4) × 8² = (√3 / 4) × 64 = 16√3 cm².

8. An isosceles triangle has equal sides of 5 cm each and a base of 6 cm. Its area is:

  • A. 12 cm²
  • B. 24 cm²
  • C. 18 cm²
  • D. 15 cm²

Answer: A. 12 cm²

Explanation: s = (5 + 5 + 6) / 2 = 8 cm. Area = √[8 × (8-5) × (8-5) × (8-6)] = √[8 × 3 × 3 × 2] = √144 = 12 cm².

9. If the perimeter of a triangle is 60 cm, then its semi-perimeter is:

  • A. 120 cm
  • B. 30 cm
  • C. 20 cm
  • D. 15 cm

Answer: B. 30 cm

Explanation: Semi-perimeter s = Perimeter / 2 = 60 / 2 = 30 cm.

10. The perimeter of a triangular field is 450 m and its sides are in the ratio 13 : 12 : 5. Its smallest side is:

  • A. 75 m
  • B. 150 m
  • C. 180 m
  • D. 90 m

Answer: A. 75 m

Explanation: 13x + 12x + 5x = 450 ⇒ 30x = 450 ⇒ x = 15. Smallest side = 5 × 15 = 75 m.

11. The area of a triangle having base 12 cm and height 5 cm is:

  • A. 60 cm²
  • B. 30 cm²
  • C. 15 cm²
  • D. 45 cm²

Answer: B. 30 cm²

Explanation: Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 12 × 5 = 30 cm².

12. If each side of an equilateral triangle is doubled, its area becomes how many times?

  • A. 2 times
  • B. 3 times
  • C. 4 times
  • D. 8 times

Answer: C. 4 times

Explanation: Area is proportional to (side)². If side becomes 2a, Area becomes (2)² = 4 times.

13. The area of a right-angled triangle with base 8 cm and hypotenuse 10 cm is:

  • A. 24 cm²
  • B. 40 cm²
  • C. 48 cm²
  • D. 80 cm²

Answer: A. 24 cm²

Explanation: Height = √(10² - 8²) = √(100 - 64) = √36 = 6 cm. Area = ½ × 8 × 6 = 24 cm².

14. If the perimeter of an equilateral triangle is 180 cm, its area is:

  • A. 900√3 cm²
  • B. 1800√3 cm²
  • C. 3600√3 cm²
  • D. 450√3 cm²

Answer: A. 900√3 cm²

Explanation: Side = 180 / 3 = 60 cm. Area = (√3 / 4) × 60² = (√3 / 4) × 3600 = 900√3 cm².

15. Heron was a famous mathematician born in:

  • A. India
  • B. Alexandria (Egypt)
  • C. Greece
  • D. Rome

Answer: B. Alexandria (Egypt)

Explanation: Heron was born in Alexandria in Egypt.

16. The sides of a triangle are in the ratio 3 : 5 : 7 and its perimeter is 300 m. Its area is:

  • A. 1500√3 m²
  • B. 3000 m²
  • C. 750√3 m²
  • D. 1200√3 m²

Answer: A. 1500√3 m²

Explanation: 3x+5x+7x = 300 ⇒ 15x = 300 ⇒ x = 20. Sides are 60m, 100m, 140m. s = 150m. Area = √[150 × 90 × 50 × 10] = 1500√3 m².

17. An isosceles right triangle has area 200 cm². The length of its hypotenuse is:

  • A. 20 cm
  • B. 20√2 cm
  • C. 40 cm
  • D. 10√2 cm

Answer: B. 20√2 cm

Explanation: Area = ½ × x² = 200 ⇒ x² = 400 ⇒ x = 20. Hypotenuse = √(20² + 20²) = √800 = 20√2 cm.

18. The height corresponding to the longest side of a triangle with sides 13 cm, 14 cm, and 15 cm is:

  • A. 11.2 cm
  • B. 12 cm
  • C. 10 cm
  • D. 8.4 cm

Answer: A. 11.2 cm

Explanation: s = 21. Area = √[21 × 8 × 7 × 6] = 84 cm². Longest side = 15. Area = ½ × 15 × h = 84 ⇒ h = 168 / 15 = 11.2 cm.

19. If each side of a triangle is halved, its area becomes:

  • A. One-half
  • B. One-fourth
  • C. One-third
  • D. One-eighth

Answer: B. One-fourth

Explanation: Scaling sides by 1/2 changes the area by (1/2)² = 1/4 of the original area.

20. In an isosceles triangle, the length of two equal sides is 13 cm each and perimeter is 36 cm. The area is:

  • A. 60 cm²
  • B. 30 cm²
  • C. 120 cm²
  • D. 90 cm²

Answer: A. 60 cm²

Explanation: Third side = 36 - 26 = 10 cm. s = 18. Area = √[18 × 5 × 5 × 8] = √3600 = 60 cm².

21. The side lengths of a rhombus are 10 cm and one diagonal is 12 cm. The area of the rhombus is:

  • A. 96 cm²
  • B. 48 cm²
  • C. 120 cm²
  • D. 192 cm²

Answer: A. 96 cm²

Explanation: Diagonal bisects it into two triangles with sides 10, 10, 12. Area of 1 triangle = 48 cm². Total area = 2 × 48 = 96 cm².

22. The sides of a triangle are 11 cm, 12 cm, and 13 cm. Its semi-perimeter is:

  • A. 36 cm
  • B. 18 cm
  • C. 24 cm
  • D. 12 cm

Answer: B. 18 cm

Explanation: s = (11 + 12 + 13) / 2 = 36 / 2 = 18 cm.

23. If the area of an equilateral triangle is 16√3 cm², its perimeter is:

  • A. 24 cm
  • B. 12 cm
  • C. 48 cm
  • D. 36 cm

Answer: A. 24 cm

Explanation: (√3 / 4) a² = 16√3 ⇒ a² = 64 ⇒ a = 8 cm. Perimeter = 3 × 8 = 24 cm.

24. The area of a triangle with sides 8 cm, 11 cm, and perimeter 32 cm is:

  • A. 8√30 cm²
  • B. 16√30 cm²
  • C. 4√30 cm²
  • D. 12√30 cm²

Answer: A. 8√30 cm²

Explanation: Third side = 32 - 19 = 13 cm. s = 16. Area = √[16 × 8 × 5 × 3] = √1920 = 8√30 cm².

25. Unit of area in SI system is:

  • A. m
  • B. m²
  • C. m³
  • D. cm

Answer: B. m²

Explanation: The SI unit of area is square meter (m²).

26. If a triangle has sides a, b, c and (s - a) = 5 cm, (s - b) = 10 cm, (s - c) = 1 cm, then semi-perimeter s is:

  • A. 16 cm
  • B. 32 cm
  • C. 20 cm
  • D. 18 cm

Answer: A. 16 cm

Explanation: Adding (s-a)+(s-b)+(s-c) = 3s - (a+b+c) = 3s - 2s = s. Thus s = 5 + 10 + 1 = 16 cm.

27. Using data from Q26, the area of the triangle is:

  • A. 20√2 cm²
  • B. 20√10 cm²
  • C. 40 cm²
  • D. 10√5 cm²

Answer: B. 20√10 cm²

Explanation: Area = √[16 × 5 × 10 × 1] = √800 = 20√2 cm² (Wait, √800 = √(400×2) = 20√2 cm²).

28. The altitude of an equilateral triangle of side 12 cm is:

  • A. 6√3 cm
  • B. 12√3 cm
  • C. 3√3 cm
  • D. 6 cm

Answer: A. 6√3 cm

Explanation: Altitude of equilateral triangle = (√3 / 2) × side = (√3 / 2) × 12 = 6√3 cm.

29. Can Heron's formula be used to find the area of a quadrilateral?

  • A. Yes, by dividing it into two triangles
  • B. No, never
  • C. Only if it is a square
  • D. Only if it is a circle

Answer: A. Yes, by dividing it into two triangles

Explanation: Any quadrilateral can be divided into two triangles by drawing a diagonal, and Heron's formula can be applied to both.

30. If the area of a right-angled isosceles triangle is 32 cm², the length of its hypotenuse is:

  • A. 8√2 cm
  • B. 8 cm
  • C. 16 cm
  • D. 4√2 cm

Answer: A. 8√2 cm

Explanation: ½ × x² = 32 ⇒ x² = 64 ⇒ x = 8. Hypotenuse = √(8² + 8²) = √128 = 8√2 cm.
