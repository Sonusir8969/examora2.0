Eng-01: The Portrait of a Lady 25 Questions

1. Who is the author of the story 'The Portrait of a Lady'?

  • A. Khushwant Singh
  • B. Ruskin Bond
  • C. Vikram Seth
  • D. A.R. Williams

Answer: A. Khushwant Singh

Explanation: The story 'The Portrait of a Lady' is an autobiographical account written by Khushwant Singh about his relationship with his grandmother.

2. How did the author's grandfather appear in the portrait hanging above the mantelpiece?

  • A. Wearing a big turban and loose-fitting clothes
  • B. Wearing a modern suit and tie
  • C. Wearing a traditional dhoti kurta
  • D. Wearing a uniform with medals

Answer: A. Wearing a big turban and loose-fitting clothes

Explanation: The portrait showed the grandfather with a big turban, loose clothes, and a long white beard that covered the best part of his chest.

3. What was the grandmother's daily routine while waking up the author for school in the village?

  • A. Sing morning prayers in a monotonous sing-song manner
  • B. Read storybooks aloud
  • C. Cook lavish breakfasts
  • D. Exercise in the courtyard

Answer: A. Sing morning prayers in a monotonous sing-song manner

Explanation: She said her morning prayer in a monotonous sing-song while she bathed and dressed the author, hoping he would listen and learn it by heart.

4. What did the grandmother feed the village dogs with?

  • A. Stale chapatis
  • B. Fresh bread crumbs
  • C. Biscuits and milk
  • D. Bones and meat

Answer: A. Stale chapatis

Explanation: The grandmother carried several stale chapatis with her for the village dogs while returning from the village school and temple.

5. Where was the author's village school located?

  • A. Attached to a temple
  • B. Next to a river bank
  • C. In the middle of a market
  • D. Inside a government complex

Answer: A. Attached to a temple

Explanation: The village school was attached to a temple where the priest taught the children the alphabet and the morning prayer.

6. What was the turning point in the friendship between the author and his grandmother?

  • A. When they moved to the city to live with his parents
  • B. When the author went to the university
  • C. When the author went abroad for higher studies
  • D. When the grandfather passed away

Answer: A. When they moved to the city to live with his parents

Explanation: Shifting to the city was the turning point in their friendship as the author started going to an English school in a motor bus, and they saw less of each other.

7. How did the author travel to his English school in the city?

  • A. In a motor bus
  • B. On a bicycle
  • C. Walking with grandmother
  • D. In a horse carriage

Answer: A. In a motor bus

Explanation: In the city, the author went to an English medium school in a motor bus, so the grandmother could no longer accompany him.

8. Why was the grandmother unhappy with the author's city school education?

  • A. There was no teaching about God and scriptures
  • B. The fees were too expensive
  • C. The school was too far from home
  • D. She did not like English grammar

Answer: A. There was no teaching about God and scriptures

Explanation: She was distressed that there was no teaching about God and the scriptures in the English school.

9. What was grandmother's view regarding music lessons taught at the city school?

  • A. It had lewd associations and was fit only for harlots and beggars
  • B. It was essential for overall development
  • C. It was good for spiritual healing
  • D. It was a waste of time for boys

Answer: A. It had lewd associations and was fit only for harlots and beggars

Explanation: To her, music had lewd associations and was the monopoly of harlots and beggars, not meant for gentlefolk.

10. When the author went to university, what change occurred in their relationship?

  • A. The common link of friendship was snapped as he got a separate room
  • B. They stopped talking completely
  • C. Grandmother moved back to the village
  • D. They became closer than before

Answer: A. The common link of friendship was snapped as he got a separate room

Explanation: When the author was given a room of his own, the last common link of their friendship (sharing a room) was snapped.

11. How did the grandmother spend her time in the city after the common link of friendship was broken?

  • A. Spinning wheel, reciting prayers, and feeding sparrows
  • B. Watching television and reading books
  • C. Visiting neighbors and social work
  • D. Sleeping and gardening

Answer: A. Spinning wheel, reciting prayers, and feeding sparrows

Explanation: From sunrise to sunset she sat by her wheel spinning, reciting prayers, and in the afternoon relaxed to feed the sparrows.

12. Which time of the day was described as the 'happiest half-hour of the day' for the grandmother?

  • A. While feeding the sparrows in the afternoon
  • B. During morning prayers
  • C. When the author returned from school
  • D. While spinning the wheel

Answer: A. While feeding the sparrows in the afternoon

Explanation: Feeding hundreds of little sparrows bits of bread in the verandah was the happiest half-hour of the day for her.

13. How long was the author away for higher studies abroad?

  • A. 5 years
  • B. 3 years
  • C. 7 years
  • D. 2 years

Answer: A. 5 years

Explanation: The author went abroad for higher studies and was expected to be away for five years.

14. What was grandmother's gesture when she came to see off the author at the railway station?

  • A. She kissed his forehead silently in prayer
  • B. She cried bitterly
  • C. She gave him advice on studying hard
  • D. She gave him a holy book

Answer: A. She kissed his forehead silently in prayer

Explanation: She showed no emotion, her lips moved in prayer, and she silently kissed the author's forehead before he departed.

15. What unusual thing did the grandmother do on the evening of the author's return from abroad?

  • A. Collected women of the neighborhood and beat an old drum singing of warriors' homecoming
  • B. Went to the temple to offer special prayers
  • C. Prepared a big feast for the whole family
  • D. Slept early without talking to anyone

Answer: A. Collected women of the neighborhood and beat an old drum singing of warriors' homecoming

Explanation: She collected the women of the neighborhood, got an old dilapidated drum, and sang for hours celebrating her grandson's return.

16. What was the immediate result of the grandmother's over-exertion during the celebration?

  • A. She was taken ill with a mild fever
  • B. She lost her voice
  • C. She broke her hand
  • D. She fainted instantly

Answer: A. She was taken ill with a mild fever

Explanation: The next morning she was taken ill. The doctor said it was a mild fever, but she knew her end was near.

17. What did the grandmother claim just before her death?

  • A. That her end was near and she didn't want to waste time talking
  • B. That she wanted to visit the village temple
  • C. That she wanted to eat sweets
  • D. That she was completely healthy

Answer: A. That her end was near and she didn't want to waste time talking

Explanation: She stated that since she had omitted to pray in the last hours of her life, she was not going to waste any more time talking to family members.

18. How did the grandmother die peacefully in her bed?

  • A. Praying and counting her rosary beads
  • B. Holding her grandson's hand
  • C. While drinking water
  • D. While talking to her family

Answer: A. Praying and counting her rosary beads

Explanation: Even before anyone could suspect, her lips stopped moving and the rosary fell from her lifeless fingers as she passed away peacefully.

19. How did the sparrows express their grief over the death of the grandmother?

  • A. Sat silently around her body without chirping
  • B. Flew wildly around the courtyard making noise
  • C. Ate all the bread crumbs quickly
  • D. Didn't show up at all

Answer: A. Sat silently around her body without chirping

Explanation: Thousands of sparrows sat scattered on the floor in complete silence; there was no chirping at all.

20. What did the sparrows do when the author's mother threw bread crumbs to them after grandmother's death?

  • A. Took no notice of the bread crumbs
  • B. Fought over the crumbs
  • C. Ate them silently
  • D. Carried them away in their beaks

Answer: A. Took no notice of the bread crumbs

Explanation: The sparrows took no notice of the bread thrown by the mother and flew away quietly when the grandmother's corpse was carried off.

21. How was the grandmother's physical appearance described in her old age?

  • A. Short, fat, slightly bent, with a criss-cross of wrinkles
  • B. Tall, slim, and perfectly upright
  • C. Short, thin, and agile
  • D. Tall, muscular, and active

Answer: A. Short, fat, slightly bent, with a criss-cross of wrinkles

Explanation: She was short and fat and slightly bent, with wrinkles running from everywhere to everywhere on her face.

22. What clothing did the grandmother always wear in the house?

  • A. Spotless white clothes
  • B. Brightly colored silk sarees
  • C. Simple dark blue suits
  • D. Plain cotton yellow attire

Answer: A. Spotless white clothes

Explanation: She hobbled about the house in spotless white with one hand resting on her waist to balance her stoop.

23. What phrase did the author use to describe the peaceful appearance of his grandmother?

  • A. An expanse of pure white serenity
  • B. A mountain of calm strength
  • C. A ray of golden sunshine
  • D. An ocean of silent love

Answer: A. An expanse of pure white serenity

Explanation: The author compared her peaceful white-clad look to 'an expanse of pure white serenity breathing peace and contentment'.

24. What did the grandmother do inside the temple while the author was studying at school?

  • A. Read scriptures
  • B. Cleaned the temple premises
  • C. Prepared food for priests
  • D. Chanted songs with other women

Answer: A. Read scriptures

Explanation: While the children sat in rows learning the alphabet, grandmother sat inside reading scriptures.

25. What was done with the bread crumbs left by the sparrows the next morning?

  • A. Swept into the dustbin by the sweeper
  • B. Eaten by stray dogs
  • C. Fed to the cows
  • D. Collected and saved for later

Answer: A. Swept into the dustbin by the sweeper

Explanation: Next morning the sweeper swept the unused bread crumbs into the dustbin as the sparrows hadn't touched them.
