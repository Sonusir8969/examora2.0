Ch-10: Lifelines of National Economy 30 Questions

1. Which organization constructs and maintains the National Highways in India?

  • A. NHAI (National Highways Authority of India)
  • B. PWD (Public Works Department)
  • C. CPWD (Central Public Works Department)
  • D. BRO (Border Roads Organisation)

Answer: A. NHAI (National Highways Authority of India)

Explanation: The Golden Quadrilateral and major National Highways are constructed and maintained by the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI).

2. Which authority constructs and maintains roads in the border areas of India?

  • A. Border Roads Organisation (BRO)
  • B. CPWD
  • C. Zila Parishad
  • D. NHAI

Answer: A. Border Roads Organisation (BRO)

Explanation: Established in 1960, BRO constructs and maintains roads in the strategically sensitive border areas of northern and north-eastern India.

3. What is the main objective of the Golden Quadrilateral Super Highways?

  • A. Reduce time and distance between India's major mega cities
  • B. Connect rural villages with urban markets
  • C. Transport heavy raw materials only to ports
  • D. Connect border posts to state capitals

Answer: A. Reduce time and distance between India's major mega cities

Explanation: The main objective of the 6-lane Golden Quadrilateral project is to reduce travel time and distance between Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata.

4. Which two extreme locations are connected by the North-South Corridor?

  • A. Srinagar and Kanyakumari
  • B. Silchar and Porbandar
  • C. Delhi and Chennai
  • D. Amritsar and Kanyakumari

Answer: A. Srinagar and Kanyakumari

Explanation: The North-South corridor links Srinagar (Jammu & Kashmir) with Kanyakumari (Tamil Nadu).

5. Which two places are connected by the East-West Corridor?

  • A. Silchar and Porbandar
  • B. Srinagar and Kanyakumari
  • C. Kolkata and Mumbai
  • D. Guwahati and Ahmedabad

Answer: A. Silchar and Porbandar

Explanation: The East-West corridor connects Silchar in Assam to Porbandar in Gujarat.

6. Which scheme provides special central funds to link every rural village in India to a major town by an all-weather road?

  • A. Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY)
  • B. MGNREGA
  • C. Swarnajayanti Gram Swarozgar Yojana
  • D. Smart Cities Mission

Answer: A. Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana (PMGSY)

Explanation: Under PMGSY, special provisions are made so that every village is connected to a major town by all-weather motorable roads.

7. What is the primary mode of transportation for freight and passengers in India?

  • A. Railways
  • B. Roadways
  • C. Waterways
  • D. Airways

Answer: A. Railways

Explanation: Indian Railways is the principal mode of transportation for long-distance travel and bulk freight transport across the country.

8. Which region in India has a dense network of railways due to level land, high population density, and rich agricultural resources?

  • A. Northern Plains
  • B. Himalayan Region
  • C. Peninsular Plateau
  • D. Thar Desert

Answer: A. Northern Plains

Explanation: The vast level land, high population density, and agricultural wealth make Northern Plains ideal for dense railway networks.

9. Which major railway development along the west coast has facilitated movement of passengers and goods, despite challenges like terrain and landslides?

  • A. Konkan Railway
  • B. Metro Railway
  • C. Northern Railway
  • D. Eastern Railway

Answer: A. Konkan Railway

Explanation: The development of the Konkan Railway along the western coast has connected important economic zones despite rugged terrain.

10. What is the main advantage of pipeline transport network?

  • A. Minimal trans-shipment losses and delays
  • B. Very low initial laying cost
  • C. Flexibility to change routes daily
  • D. Suitable for carrying all passenger traffic

Answer: A. Minimal trans-shipment losses and delays

Explanation: While initial construction costs are high, running costs are low and pipeline transport virtually eliminates trans-shipment losses and delays.

11. Which pipeline network connects the gas fields of Gujarat to Jagdishpur in Uttar Pradesh via Madhya Pradesh?

  • A. HVJ (Hazira-Vaidpur-Jagdishpur) Pipeline
  • B. Salaya-Koyali Pipeline
  • C. Naharkatia-Barauni Pipeline
  • D. Mumbai High-Ankleshwar Pipeline

Answer: A. HVJ (Hazira-Vaidpur-Jagdishpur) Pipeline

Explanation: The HVJ gas pipeline links Hazira in Gujarat with Jagdishpur in UP, running through Vijaipur in MP.

12. What is the cheapest mode of transport for carrying heavy and bulky goods over long distances?

  • A. Waterways
  • B. Roadways
  • C. Airways
  • D. Railways

Answer: A. Waterways

Explanation: Waterways are fuel-efficient, eco-friendly, and the cheapest mode of transport for bulky cargo.

13. Which inland waterway connects Prayagraj and Haldia over the Ganga river (National Waterway No. 1)?

  • A. Ganga river between Prayagraj and Haldia (1620 km)
  • B. Brahmaputra river between Sadiya and Dhubri
  • C. West Coast Canal in Kerala
  • D. Godavari and Krishna stretch

Answer: A. Ganga river between Prayagraj and Haldia (1620 km)

Explanation: National Waterway No. 1 (NW-1) spans 1,620 km along the Ganga river from Prayagraj to Haldia.

14. Which major sea port was developed immediately after independence to ease the volume of trade on the Mumbai port following partition?

  • A. Kandla (Deendayal Port)
  • B. Marmagao
  • C. Jawaharlal Nehru Port
  • D. Kochi Port

Answer: A. Kandla (Deendayal Port)

Explanation: Kandla in Kutch (Gujarat) was the first port developed after Independence to compensate for the loss of Karachi port after partition.

15. Kandla port in Gujarat is a tidal port also renamed as:

  • A. Deendayal Port
  • B. Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port
  • C. Kamarajar Port
  • D. V.O. Chidambaranar Port

Answer: A. Deendayal Port

Explanation: Kandla Port has been officially renamed Deendayal Port.

16. Which premier iron ore exporting port of India accounts for about fifty percent of India's iron ore exports?

  • A. Marmagao Port (Goa)
  • B. Visakhapatnam
  • C. Paradip
  • D. Mangaluru

Answer: A. Marmagao Port (Goa)

Explanation: Marmagao Port located in Goa is the country's premier iron ore exporting port.

17. Which is the deepest landlocked and well-protected sea port on the eastern coast of India?

  • A. Visakhapatnam
  • B. Chennai
  • C. Paradip
  • D. Tuticorin

Answer: A. Visakhapatnam

Explanation: Visakhapatnam is the deepest landlocked and well-protected port situated in Andhra Pradesh.

18. Which port was developed as a subsidiary port to relieve pressure on the Kolkata port?

  • A. Haldia Port
  • B. Paradip Port
  • C. Dhamra Port
  • D. Chittagong Port

Answer: A. Haldia Port

Explanation: Haldia port was established as a satellite port to lighten the heavy burden on Kolkata port.

19. When was air transport nationalised in India?

  • A. 1953
  • B. 1947
  • C. 1965
  • D. 1971

Answer: A. 1953

Explanation: Air transport in India was nationalized in the year 1953.

20. Which public enterprise provides helicopter services to Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC) for offshore operations and to inaccessible remote areas?

  • A. Pawan Hans Helicopters Ltd.
  • B. Air India
  • C. Alliance Air
  • D. Indian Coast Guard Aviation

Answer: A. Pawan Hans Helicopters Ltd.

Explanation: Pawan Hans Helicopters Ltd. provides air services to ONGC in off-shore exploration and remote north-eastern areas.

21. Indian Postal network is the largest in the world. First class mail includes:

  • A. Cards and envelopes
  • B. Book packets and newspapers
  • C. Registered periodicals
  • D. Parcels and heavy goods

Answer: A. Cards and envelopes

Explanation: Cards and envelopes are considered first-class mail and are airlifted between stations covering both land and air routes.

22. To facilitate quick delivery of mails in large towns and cities, how many mail channels have been introduced recently?

  • A. 6 channels
  • B. 4 channels
  • C. 8 channels
  • D. 10 channels

Answer: A. 6 channels

Explanation: Six mail channels (Rajdhani Channel, Metro Channel, Green Channel, Business Channel, Bulk Mail Channel, Periodical Channel) were set up for fast delivery.

23. STD stands for:

  • A. Subscriber Trunk Dialling
  • B. Standard Trunk Dialling
  • C. System Telephone Network
  • D. Subscriber Telephone Department

Answer: A. Subscriber Trunk Dialling

Explanation: STD refers to Subscriber Trunk Dialling, which allows direct long-distance calling across telecom circles.

24. What is trade between two or more countries called?

  • A. International Trade
  • B. Local Trade
  • C. Internal Trade
  • D. Domestic Trade

Answer: A. International Trade

Explanation: Trade that takes place across borders between two or more countries is defined as International Trade.

25. When the value of exports exceeds the value of imports, it is called a/an:

  • A. Favourable balance of trade
  • B. Unfavourable balance of trade
  • C. Balanced trade
  • D. Trade deficit

Answer: A. Favourable balance of trade

Explanation: If export value is higher than import value, a country enjoys a favorable balance of trade.

26. If the value of imports is greater than the value of exports, it is termed as:

  • A. Unfavourable balance of trade
  • B. Favourable balance of trade
  • C. Surplus trade
  • D. Equal trade

Answer: A. Unfavourable balance of trade

Explanation: When a nation pays more for imports than it earns through exports, the trade balance becomes unfavorable.

27. Which sector in India acts as a major invisible export and source of foreign exchange, growing significantly over the last three decades?

  • A. Tourism industry
  • B. Agriculture
  • C. Mining
  • D. Heavy engineering

Answer: A. Tourism industry

Explanation: Tourism brings in significant foreign exchange, promotes national integration, and supports local handicrafts and services.

28. Why is international trade considered an 'economic barometers' for a country?

  • A. It reflects the economic prosperity and financial strength of a country
  • B. It determines total crop yield
  • C. It calculates exact population growth rate
  • D. It measures rainfall variability

Answer: A. It reflects the economic prosperity and financial strength of a country

Explanation: High volume of international trade indicates strong economic health and competitiveness globally.

29. Which artificial sea port in India is second only to Mumbai in terms of volume of traffic and trade?

  • A. Chennai Port
  • B. Kolkata Port
  • C. Kochi Port
  • D. Vishakhapatnam Port

Answer: A. Chennai Port

Explanation: Chennai is one of the oldest artificial ports in India and ranks right behind Mumbai in cargo trade volume.

30. Raja Sansi International Airport is located in which city?

  • A. Amritsar
  • B. Delhi
  • C. Ahmedabad
  • D. Chandigarh

Answer: A. Amritsar

Explanation: Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport (formerly known as Raja Sansi Airport) is located in Amritsar, Punjab.
