Ch-5: The Hundred Dresses – I 30 Questions

1. Who is the author of 'The Hundred Dresses – I'?

  • A. Eleanor Estes
  • B. L.M. Montgomery
  • C. Roald Dahl
  • D. Lois Lowry

Answer: A. Eleanor Estes

Explanation: 'The Hundred Dresses' was written by American children's author Eleanor Estes.

2. Who was the quiet girl who sat in Room 13?

  • A. Wanda Petronski
  • B. Peggy
  • C. Maddie
  • D. Cecile

Answer: A. Wanda Petronski

Explanation: Wanda Petronski was a quiet Polish girl who sat in Room 13.

3. In which seat did Wanda usually sit in Room 13?

  • A. Next to the last seat in the last row
  • B. The very front row near the teacher
  • C. In the middle of the room
  • D. Next to Peggy's desk

Answer: A. Next to the last seat in the last row

Explanation: Wanda sat in the seat next to the last seat in the back row of Room 13.

4. Why did Wanda sit in the back row of the classroom?

  • A. Her feet were usually caked with dry mud from Boggins Heights
  • B. She was forced by the teacher
  • C. She liked sitting with noisy boys
  • D. She could not see the blackboard from the front

Answer: A. Her feet were usually caked with dry mud from Boggins Heights

Explanation: She came from Boggins Heights and her shoes were often caked with mud, so she sat where rough boys sat.

5. From where did Wanda Petronski walk to school every day?

  • A. Boggins Heights
  • B. Oliver Street
  • C. Maple Avenue
  • D. Country Road

Answer: A. Boggins Heights

Explanation: Wanda lived in Boggins Heights, a poor neighborhood on the outskirts of the town.

6. Who was the most popular girl in Room 13?

  • A. Peggy
  • B. Maddie
  • C. Wanda
  • D. Cecile

Answer: A. Peggy

Explanation: Peggy was the most popular girl in school; she was pretty, rich, and had beautiful clothes.

7. Who was Peggy's best friend?

  • A. Maddie
  • B. Wanda
  • C. Cecile
  • D. Miss Mason

Answer: A. Maddie

Explanation: Maddie was Peggy's closest friend and spent most of her time with her.

8. What dress did Wanda wear to school every day?

  • A. A faded blue dress that didn't hang right
  • B. A bright red cotton dress
  • C. A green silk dress with a red sash
  • D. A yellow wool tunic

Answer: A. A faded blue dress that didn't hang right

Explanation: Wanda always wore a faded blue dress that was clean, but looked unironed and faded.

9. How many dresses did Wanda claim to have lined up in her closet?

  • A. A hundred
  • B. Fifty
  • C. Sixty
  • D. Twenty

Answer: A. A hundred

Explanation: Whenever asked, Wanda claimed she had a hundred dresses all lined up in her closet.

10. How many pairs of shoes did Wanda claim to have?

  • A. Sixty pairs
  • B. Ten pairs
  • C. Fifty pairs
  • D. A hundred pairs

Answer: A. Sixty pairs

Explanation: Wanda claimed she had sixty pairs of shoes, all lined up in her closet too.

11. Why did Peggy and Maddie wait for Wanda before school?

  • A. To have fun and mock her about her hundred dresses
  • B. To walk to school together as friends
  • C. To borrow her notes
  • D. To share lunch with her

Answer: A. To have fun and mock her about her hundred dresses

Explanation: They waited at the corner of Oliver Street to make fun of her claims before going into school.

12. Why was Maddie uncomfortable when Peggy teased Wanda?

  • A. Because Maddie herself was poor and wore hand-me-down clothes
  • B. Because Wanda was her cousin
  • C. Because she hated Peggy
  • D. Because the teacher would punish them

Answer: A. Because Maddie herself was poor and wore hand-me-down clothes

Explanation: Maddie was poor too, wearing clothes given by others (which her mother re-trimmed), so she feared becoming the next target.

13. What did Maddie wish to write in a secret note to Peggy?

  • A. Asking Peggy to stop teasing Wanda Petronski
  • B. Asking Peggy to lend her money
  • C. Inviting Peggy to her house
  • D. Complaining about Miss Mason

Answer: A. Asking Peggy to stop teasing Wanda Petronski

Explanation: Maddie started writing a note to Peggy to ask her to stop teasing Wanda, but tore it up in fear of losing Peggy's friendship.

14. Why did Maddie tear up the note she started writing to Peggy?

  • A. She feared Peggy and other girls would make fun of her instead
  • B. She ran out of ink
  • C. She changed her mind about Wanda
  • D. Peggy noticed her writing

Answer: A. She feared Peggy and other girls would make fun of her instead

Explanation: She pictured herself as the new target standing in front of the girls being questioned about her clothes.

15. What drawing competition was organized in the school for girls?

  • A. Designing dresses
  • B. Designing motorboats
  • C. Painting landscapes
  • D. Drawing portraits

Answer: A. Designing dresses

Explanation: For girls, the contest consisted of designing dresses, while for boys, it was designing motorboats.

16. What contest was held for the boys in the class?

  • A. Designing motorboats
  • B. Drawing cars
  • C. Designing airplanes
  • D. Painting flags

Answer: A. Designing motorboats

Explanation: The boys' contest was to submit sketches and designs for motorboats.

17. Who did everyone expect to win the drawing contest for girls?

  • A. Peggy
  • B. Maddie
  • C. Wanda
  • D. Cecile

Answer: A. Peggy

Explanation: Everyone believed Peggy would win because she drew better than anyone else in Room 13.

18. Who won the drawing contest for girls?

  • A. Wanda Petronski
  • B. Peggy
  • C. Maddie
  • D. Cecile

Answer: A. Wanda Petronski

Explanation: Miss Mason announced that Wanda Petronski was the winner of the girls' drawing contest.

19. How many drawings of dresses did Wanda submit for the contest?

  • A. A hundred drawings
  • B. Ten drawings
  • C. Fifty drawings
  • D. Sixty drawings

Answer: A. A hundred drawings

Explanation: Wanda submitted a hundred individual drawings, all unique and beautifully colored.

20. Who was the teacher in Room 13?

  • A. Miss Mason
  • B. Mrs. Kuperus
  • C. Miss Petronski
  • D. Mrs. Thomas

Answer: A. Miss Mason

Explanation: Miss Mason was the class teacher of Room 13.

21. Who won the contest among the boys?

  • A. Jack Beggles
  • B. Thomas Smith
  • C. Jake Petronski
  • D. John Miller

Answer: A. Jack Beggles

Explanation: Miss Mason announced that Jack Beggles won the contest for the boys with his design for a motorboat.

22. Why was Wanda absent on the day the contest winners were announced?

  • A. Her family was preparing to move to a big city
  • B. She was sick with fever
  • C. She had missed the school bus
  • D. She was celebrating her birthday

Answer: A. Her family was preparing to move to a big city

Explanation: Wanda had been absent for days because her family decided to move to a large city due to discrimination.

23. How did the students react when they saw Wanda's drawings displayed in Room 13?

  • A. They gasped in admiration and applauded
  • B. They laughed at her drawings
  • C. They ignored them completely
  • D. They complained to the teacher

Answer: A. They gasped in admiration and applauded

Explanation: Every student gasped in awe, admired the gorgeous colors, and even the boys whistled in appreciation.

24. What specific drawing did Peggy recognize and admire among Wanda's hundred sketches?

  • A. A blue dress and a green dress with a red sash
  • B. A yellow dress with lace
  • C. A purple gown with silver stars
  • D. A white wedding dress

Answer: A. A blue dress and a green dress with a red sash

Explanation: Peggy pointed out the green dress with the red sash and the blue dress Wanda used to describe to them.

25. What did Peggy realize about Wanda after seeing her artwork?

  • A. Wanda was immensely talented at drawing
  • B. Wanda had cheated in the competition
  • C. Wanda bought those drawings
  • D. Wanda copied Peggy's style

Answer: A. Wanda was immensely talented at drawing

Explanation: Peggy admitted in awe that Wanda really knew how to draw and was a true artist.

26. What nationality was Wanda Petronski?

  • A. Polish
  • B. German
  • C. Russian
  • D. Italian

Answer: A. Polish

Explanation: Wanda Petronski was a Polish immigrant living in America.

27. Why was Wanda's name considered funny or strange by her classmates?

  • A. It was long and difficult for native American children to pronounce
  • B. It sounded like an animal's name
  • C. It had an odd translation
  • D. It was very short

Answer: A. It was long and difficult for native American children to pronounce

Explanation: Her foreign Polish surname sounded uncommon compared to names like Thomas, Smith, or Allen.

28. What was Peggy's nature regarding animals and smaller children?

  • A. She was kind and protected small children from bullies
  • B. She was cruel to everyone
  • C. She ignored animals
  • D. She picked fights with everyone

Answer: A. She was kind and protected small children from bullies

Explanation: Peggy wasn't inherently mean; she cried for hours if an animal was mistreated and protected young kids.

29. Why didn't Peggy think she was being cruel to Wanda?

  • A. She thought Wanda was lying about having a hundred dresses
  • B. She knew Wanda liked the teasing
  • C. She was asked by Miss Mason to test Wanda
  • D. Wanda encouraged her

Answer: A. She thought Wanda was lying about having a hundred dresses

Explanation: Peggy argued that Wanda brought it upon herself by telling an obvious lie about owning a hundred dresses.

30. What central theme does 'The Hundred Dresses – I' reflect?

  • A. Bullying, social discrimination, and empathy
  • B. Importance of winning sports
  • C. Secrets of successful teaching
  • D. Benefits of living in big cities

Answer: A. Bullying, social discrimination, and empathy

Explanation: The story deals with peer pressure, prejudice based on background/poverty, and the need for compassion.
