Ch-18: On the Face of It 4 Questions

1. What literary form is 'On the Face of It'?

  • A. A short story
  • B. A one-act play
  • C. A poem
  • D. A letter

Answer: B. A one-act play

Explanation: 'On the Face of It' is a one-act play written by Susan Hill, exploring themes of self-acceptance and overcoming isolation.

2. Who is Derry, one of the main characters in the play?

  • A. An elderly gardener
  • B. A teenage boy who is self-conscious and withdrawn due to a disfiguring facial burn scar
  • C. A visiting doctor
  • D. Mr. Lamb's nephew

Answer: B. A teenage boy who is self-conscious and withdrawn due to a disfiguring facial burn scar

Explanation: Derry is a young boy deeply affected psychologically by a facial disfigurement from an accident, which has made him bitter, withdrawn, and hyper-aware of others' reactions to his appearance.

3. Who is Mr. Lamb, and what role does he play in Derry's life?

  • A. Derry's strict schoolteacher
  • B. An elderly, unconventional man with an artificial leg who helps Derry gain a more positive, accepting outlook on life
  • C. A stranger who bullies Derry
  • D. Derry's father

Answer: B. An elderly, unconventional man with an artificial leg who helps Derry gain a more positive, accepting outlook on life

Explanation: Mr. Lamb is an eccentric elderly man, himself missing a leg, who befriends the isolated Derry and, through his optimistic philosophy on life, helps him begin to move past his self-pity and fear of others' judgment.

4. What central theme does 'On the Face of It' explore?

  • A. The importance of physical beauty
  • B. Overcoming self-pity, isolation, and prejudice to embrace life fully despite physical differences
  • C. The dangers of trespassing
  • D. Career planning for teenagers

Answer: B. Overcoming self-pity, isolation, and prejudice to embrace life fully despite physical differences

Explanation: The play conveys a powerful message about moving beyond self-imposed isolation and society's prejudice regarding physical appearance, encouraging genuine engagement with and appreciation of life.
