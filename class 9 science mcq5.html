Ch-5: The Fundamental Unit of Life 10 Questions

1. Who discovered cells first in 1665 while observing a thin slice of cork?

  • A. Robert Brown
  • B. Robert Hooke
  • C. Leeuwenhoek
  • D. Purkinje

Answer: B. Robert Hooke

Explanation: Robert Hooke observed cork cells under a simple microscope in 1665 and termed them 'cell' (meaning little rooms).

2. Which cell organelle is known as the "Powerhouse of the Cell"?

  • A. Lysosome
  • B. Golgi Apparatus
  • C. Mitochondrion
  • D. Endoplasmic Reticulum

Answer: C. Mitochondrion

Explanation: Mitochondria generate cellular energy in the form of ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) molecules, earning them the name powerhouse of the cell.

3. Which organelle is called the "Suicide Bag" of a cell?

  • A. Ribosome
  • B. Lysosome
  • C. Vacuole
  • D. Plastid

Answer: B. Lysosome

Explanation: Lysosomes contain powerful digestive enzymes. If the cell gets damaged, lysosomes may burst and digest their own cell.

4. What is the primary chemical component of a plant cell wall?

  • A. Protein
  • B. Lipid
  • C. Cellulose
  • D. Starch

Answer: C. Cellulose

Explanation: The plant cell wall is mainly composed of cellulose, a complex carbohydrate providing structural strength to plants.

5. Which organelle plays a crucial role in protein synthesis?

  • A. Mitochondria
  • B. Ribosome
  • C. Vacuole
  • D. Smooth Endoplasmic Reticulum

Answer: B. Ribosome

Explanation: Ribosomes are the cellular sites where amino acids are assembled into proteins.

6. Who discovered the nucleus in the cell in 1831?

  • A. Robert Hooke
  • B. Robert Brown
  • C. Schleiden
  • D. Schwann

Answer: B. Robert Brown

Explanation: Robert Brown discovered and named the nucleus inside living cells in 1831.

7. A plant cell shrinks when placed in a hypertonic solution due to loss of water. This process is called:

  • A. Endosmosis
  • B. Plasmolysis
  • C. Diffusion
  • D. Imbibition

Answer: B. Plasmolysis

Explanation: When a living plant cell loses water through osmosis in a concentrated solution, the cell contents shrink away from the cell wall (plasmolysis).

8. Kitchen of the plant cell is:

  • A. Mitochondria
  • B. Chloroplast
  • C. Endoplasmic Reticulum
  • D. Chromoplast

Answer: B. Chloroplast

Explanation: Chloroplasts contain green chlorophyll pigments that prepare food via photosynthesis, making them the kitchen of plant cells.

9. Which of the following organelles contain their own DNA and ribosomes?

  • A. Mitochondria and Plastids
  • B. Lysosomes and Vacuoles
  • C. Golgi apparatus and ER
  • D. Ribosomes and Nucleus

Answer: A. Mitochondria and Plastids

Explanation: Both mitochondria and plastids have their own independent DNA and ribosomes, enabling them to produce some of their own proteins.

10. Chromosomes are made up of:

  • A. DNA only
  • B. Protein only
  • C. DNA and Protein
  • D. RNA and Lipid

Answer: C. DNA and Protein

Explanation: Chromosomes consist of double-stranded DNA wrapped tightly around histone proteins.
