Ch-8: Going Places 6 Questions

1. Who is the author of the short story 'Going Places'?

  • A. A.R. Barton
  • B. William Douglas
  • C. Selma Lagerlof
  • D. Kalki

Answer: A. A.R. Barton

Explanation: 'Going Places' is a short story written by A.R. Barton, exploring themes of adolescence, class, and escapist fantasy.

2. What does Sophie, the protagonist, dream of becoming in the future?

  • A. A teacher
  • B. A boutique owner or fashion designer, or perhaps an actress
  • C. A football player
  • D. A doctor

Answer: B. A boutique owner or fashion designer, or perhaps an actress

Explanation: Sophie has grand, somewhat unrealistic dreams of owning a boutique, becoming a fashion designer, or even an actress, despite her working-class background.

3. Who is Danny Casey, whom Sophie claims to have met?

  • A. Her cousin
  • B. A famous Irish football player Sophie fantasizes about and pretends to have encountered
  • C. Her school teacher
  • D. Her father's employer

Answer: B. A famous Irish football player Sophie fantasizes about and pretends to have encountered

Explanation: Danny Casey is a real footballer Sophie admires, but her claim of meeting him personally and getting his autograph is revealed to be an imaginative fantasy rather than reality.

4. Who is Jansie in the story?

  • A. Sophie's younger sister
  • B. Sophie's practical, down-to-earth friend who has already started working
  • C. Sophie's teacher
  • D. Danny Casey's manager

Answer: B. Sophie's practical, down-to-earth friend who has already started working

Explanation: Jansie is Sophie's realistic and grounded friend who has already left school to work in a biscuit factory, contrasting with Sophie's dreamy, unrealistic aspirations.

5. Who is Geoff, and how does he behave towards Sophie?

  • A. Sophie's father, who is very affectionate
  • B. Sophie's elder brother, who is reserved, works at a garage, and often goes out alone without confiding in the family
  • C. Sophie's football coach
  • D. A neighbor who dislikes Sophie

Answer: B. Sophie's elder brother, who is reserved, works at a garage, and often goes out alone without confiding in the family

Explanation: Geoff, Sophie's older brother, works at a garage and has taken to going out alone secretively, remaining emotionally distant from his family, which increases Sophie's fascination with using him in her fantasies.

6. What is the central theme explored in 'Going Places'?

  • A. The excitement of professional football
  • B. The gap between illusion and harsh reality, especially for working-class youth seeking escape through fantasy
  • C. The importance of family businesses
  • D. The benefits of higher education

Answer: B. The gap between illusion and harsh reality, especially for working-class youth seeking escape through fantasy

Explanation: The story explores how economic and social constraints lead Sophie to escape into fantasy and self-deception, highlighting the poignant gap between her dreams and her real, limited circumstances.
