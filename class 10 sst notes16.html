NCERT Solutions for Class 10 Civics Chapter 5 covers How to Assess Democracy's Outcomes, Accountable, Responsive and Legitimate Government, Economic Growth and Reduction of Inequality, Accommodation of Social Diversity, and Dignity and Freedom of Citizens.

1. How Do We Assess Democracy's Outcomes?

Short Note:

• Why Democracy is Better: Promotes equality among citizens, enhances dignity of the individual, improves quality of decision-making, provides a method to resolve conflicts, and allows room to correct mistakes.

• Expectation vs Reality: Democracy is often seen as a superior form of government in principle, but in practice, its outcomes depend on active citizen participation and institutional strength.

2. Accountable, Responsive & Legitimate Government

Short Note:

• Accountable Government: Citizens have the right to choose their rulers and exercise control over them through free and fair elections.

• Responsive Government: Expected to take care of the needs and demands of the people, following proper procedures and transparency.

• Legitimate Government: A government that is elected by the people and rules according to constitutional norms. It is "people's own government."

3. Economic Growth, Development & Inequality

Short Note:

• Economic Growth: Dictatorships have slightly higher economic growth rates historically compared to democracies, but democracy ensures equitable distribution and human development.

• Reduction of Inequality & Poverty: Democracies are based on political equality, but economic disparities often persist, requiring proactive welfare policies by governments.

4. Accommodation of Diversity & Dignity of Citizens

Short Note:

• Accommodation of Social Diversity: Democracy develops procedures to conduct competition peacefully among social groups (e.g., Belgium model).

• Dignity & Freedom: Democracy stands much superior to any other form of government in promoting dignity and freedom of the individual, especially for women and historically marginalized communities.

Intext & Conceptual Questions

Q1: What is meant by 'Transparency' in a democracy?
1. Transparency means that decision-making processes are open and follow established rules and laws.
2. A citizen has the right and means to examine the process of decision-making in government.
3. Mechanisms like the Right to Information (RTI) act strengthen transparency in democratic systems.
Q2: Why is democratic government considered a 'Legitimate Government'?
1. A democratic government is elected by citizens through periodic, free, and fair elections.
2. It derives its authority and legitimacy directly from the consent of the governed.
3. Even if it is slow or less efficient, it remains the people's own government.

Textbook Chapter End Exercise Questions

Q1: How does democracy produce an accountable, responsive, and legitimate government?
Answer:
1. Accountable: Citizens can hold rulers accountable through periodic elections and question decisions in parliament and media.
2. Responsive: Democracy forms public opinion and pushes governments to respond to public demands and needs.
3. Legitimate: It functions based on constitutional laws and enjoys popular support because rulers are chosen by voters.
Q2: What are the conditions under which democracies accommodate social diversities?
Answer:
1. Democracy must not be seen as mere rule by majority opinion; majority must work with minority groups.
2. Majority rule should not become rule by a majority community in terms of religion, language, or race.
3. Every citizen must have a chance of being in majority at some point in time through political processes.
Q3: Industrialized democracies face challenges, yet democracy is superior to other forms of government. Give reasons.
Answer:
1. Promotes fundamental equality among all citizens without discrimination.
2. Safeguards individual freedom, expression, and human rights.
3. Provides peaceful and institutionalized mechanisms to resolve conflicts.
4. Allows scope for self-correction and constitutional amendment when errors occur.
