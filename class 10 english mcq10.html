Ch-10: The Sermon at Benares 30 Questions

1. What was Gautama Buddha's early name before he attained enlightenment?

  • A. Siddhartha Gautama
  • B. Siddhartha Vardhamana
  • C. Ashoka Gautama
  • D. Rahul Gautama

Answer: A. Siddhartha Gautama

Explanation: Gautama Buddha was born as a prince named Siddhartha Gautama in northern India.

2. At what age was Prince Siddhartha sent away for schooling in Hindu sacred scriptures?

  • A. Twelve years old
  • B. Ten years old
  • C. Fifteen years old
  • D. Eight years old

Answer: A. Twelve years old

Explanation: At twelve, Siddhartha was sent away for schooling in the Hindu sacred scriptures.

3. At what age did Siddhartha return home and marry a princess?

  • A. Sixteen years old
  • B. Eighteen years old
  • C. Twenty years old
  • D. Twenty-five years old

Answer: A. Sixteen years old

Explanation: Four years later, at the age of sixteen, he returned home and married a princess.

4. How long was Siddhartha shielded from the sufferings of the world in his royal palace?

  • A. For about ten years
  • B. For about five years
  • C. For about fifteen years
  • D. For about twenty years

Answer: A. For about ten years

Explanation: For about ten years, the prince lived a royal life protected from world sorrow.

5. Which four sights did Prince Siddhartha see that moved him to renounce his royal life?

  • A. A sick man, an aged man, a funeral procession, and a begging monk
  • B. A blind man, a wounded soldier, a dead animal, and a hermit
  • C. An old woman, a poor child, a funeral, and a scholar
  • D. A beggar, a sick child, a burning house, and a priest

Answer: A. A sick man, an aged man, a funeral procession, and a begging monk

Explanation: Out hunting at age twenty-five, he witnessed a sick man, an aged man, a funeral procession, and a monk begging for alms.

6. How many years did Siddhartha wander in search of enlightenment?

  • A. Seven years
  • B. Five years
  • C. Ten years
  • D. Twelve years

Answer: A. Seven years

Explanation: Siddhartha wandered for seven years before sitting down under a peepal tree.

7. Under what kind of tree did Siddhartha sit until he attained enlightenment?

  • A. A peepal tree
  • B. A banyan tree
  • C. A mango tree
  • D. A neem tree

Answer: A. A peepal tree

Explanation: He sat under a fig/peepal tree, vowing to stay until enlightenment came.

8. What name was given to the tree under which Siddhartha attained enlightenment?

  • A. Bodhi Tree (Tree of Wisdom)
  • B. Sacred Banyan
  • C. Nirvana Tree
  • D. Kalpavriksha

Answer: A. Bodhi Tree (Tree of Wisdom)

Explanation: He renamed the tree the Bodhi Tree, meaning the Tree of Wisdom.

9. What does the title 'Buddha' mean?

  • A. The Awakened or The Enlightened
  • B. The Great Scholar
  • C. The Royal Prince
  • D. The Holy Teacher

Answer: A. The Awakened or The Enlightened

Explanation: After attaining enlightenment, Siddhartha became known as the Buddha ('The Awakened' or 'The Enlightened').

10. Where did Buddha preach his very first sermon?

  • A. Benares (Varanasi)
  • B. Bodh Gaya
  • C. Lumbini
  • D. Sarnath

Answer: A. Benares (Varanasi)

Explanation: The Buddha preached his first sermon at the city of Benares, most holy of dipping places on the river Ganges.

11. What tragedy had struck Kisa Gotami in the chapter?

  • A. Her only son had died
  • B. Her husband had left her
  • C. Her home had been destroyed
  • D. She had lost all her wealth

Answer: A. Her only son had died

Explanation: Kisa Gotami had an only son, and he died, causing her extreme grief.

12. What did Kisa Gotami do in her grief carrying her dead child?

  • A. She went from neighbor to neighbor asking for medicine to bring him back to life
  • B. She went straight to the cremation ground
  • C. She locked herself inside her house
  • D. She went to the king to demand justice

Answer: A. She went from neighbor to neighbor asking for medicine to bring him back to life

Explanation: In her grief, she carried her dead child to all her neighbors, asking them for medicine.

13. Who directed Kisa Gotami to go to Sakyamuni Buddha?

  • A. A man she met in her neighborhood
  • B. Her husband
  • C. A wise physician
  • D. A village priest

Answer: A. A man she met in her neighborhood

Explanation: At length, a man replied that he could not give her medicine, but knew a physician—Sakyamuni Buddha.

14. What item did Buddha ask Kisa Gotami to procure as a cure?

  • A. A handful of mustard seeds
  • B. A cup of holy water
  • C. A pinch of sacred ash
  • D. A lotus flower from the river

Answer: A. A handful of mustard seeds

Explanation: Buddha requested Kisa Gotami to bring him a handful of mustard seeds.

15. What strict condition did Buddha place on obtaining the mustard seeds?

  • A. They must come from a house where no one has lost a child, husband, parent, or friend
  • B. They must be purchased from the central market before sunrise
  • C. They must be grown in her own garden
  • D. They must be given by a royal priest

Answer: A. They must come from a house where no one has lost a child, husband, parent, or friend

Explanation: Buddha stipulated that the mustard seeds must come from a family where no beloved one had died.

16. Why was Kisa Gotami unable to get mustard seeds from any household?

  • A. Every household had lost a beloved family member to death
  • B. People refused to help her
  • C. Mustard seeds were unavailable in the city
  • D. Nobody understood her request

Answer: A. Every household had lost a beloved family member to death

Explanation: Every house she visited had witnessed the death of a parent, child, partner, or friend.

17. Where did Kisa Gotami sit down weary and hopeless after searching all day?

  • A. By the wayside watching the lights of the city
  • B. At the door of the Buddha's monastery
  • C. Under the Bodhi tree
  • D. On the bank of the river Ganges

Answer: A. By the wayside watching the lights of the city

Explanation: Weary and hopeless, she sat down at the wayside watching the city lights flicker up and extinguish.

18. What realization came to Kisa Gotami as she watched the city lights extinguish?

  • A. That human lives flicker up and are extinguished just like the city lights, making death universal
  • B. That she needed to buy lamps for her home
  • C. That the city was unsafe at night
  • D. That she should leave Benares immediately

Answer: A. That human lives flicker up and are extinguished just like the city lights, making death universal

Explanation: She realized that fate of mortals is like city lights that flicker and die out; death is common to all.

19. What comparison did Buddha use to describe the mortality of human lives?

  • A. Ripe fruits early in danger of falling, and earthen vessels made by a potter
  • B. Leaves falling from autumn trees
  • C. Water flowing into the ocean
  • D. Sparks flying from a bonfire

Answer: A. Ripe fruits early in danger of falling, and earthen vessels made by a potter

Explanation: Buddha compared human life to ripe fruits in danger of falling and earthen vessels that break at last.

20. According to Buddha, can weeping or grieving bring a dead person back to life?

  • A. No, weeping increases suffering and harms one's health without saving the dead
  • B. Yes, deep sorrow moves the gods
  • C. Yes, if done for long enough
  • D. No, but it makes the dead person happy

Answer: A. No, weeping increases suffering and harms one's health without saving the dead

Explanation: Buddha taught that weeping and grieving only make the body sick and pale, never bringing back the deceased.

21. How can a person obtain peace of mind according to Buddha's sermon?

  • A. By drawing out the arrow of lamentation, complaint, and grief
  • B. By offering sacrifices at temples
  • C. By isolating oneself from society
  • D. By acquiring vast knowledge

Answer: A. By drawing out the arrow of lamentation, complaint, and grief

Explanation: He who seeks peace should draw out the arrow of lamentation, complaint, and grief to become composed and blessed.

22. What happens to a person who overcomes all sorrow?

  • A. He becomes free from sorrow and attains true peace
  • B. He becomes immortal physically
  • C. He becomes a king
  • D. He forgets his past completely

Answer: A. He becomes free from sorrow and attains true peace

Explanation: Buddha said that he who has overcome all sorrow will become free from sorrow and be blessed.

23. What lesson did Buddha want Kisa Gotami to understand through the exercise of finding mustard seeds?

  • A. Death is inevitable and universal to all living beings
  • B. Mustard seeds have healing powers
  • C. People in the city are unhelpful
  • D. Grief can cure illness

Answer: A. Death is inevitable and universal to all living beings

Explanation: Buddha wanted her to realize that grief is personal, but death is an inescapable reality shared by every family.

24. What river flows near Benares where Buddha gave his sermon?

  • A. River Ganges
  • B. River Yamuna
  • C. River Godavari
  • D. River Narmada

Answer: A. River Ganges

Explanation: Benares is described as the most holy of dipping places on the River Ganges.

25. What was Siddhartha Gautama's son's name or royal title referenced in historical accounts of Buddha?

  • A. Rahula
  • B. Anand
  • C. Devadatta
  • D. Sujata

Answer: A. Rahula

Explanation: Prince Siddhartha had a son named Rahula before leaving his royal palace.

26. Which word in the sermon means 'a formal talk on a religious or moral subject'?

  • A. Sermon
  • B. Scripture
  • C. Lamentation
  • D. Desolation

Answer: A. Sermon

Explanation: A sermon is a talk or discourse given on a religious or moral topic.

27. How does the text describe the nature of human life in this world?

  • A. Troubled, brief, and combined with pain
  • B. Long, peaceful, and joyous
  • C. Permanent and unchanging
  • D. Completely predictable and simple

Answer: A. Troubled, brief, and combined with pain

Explanation: Buddha stated, "The life of mortals in this world is troubled and brief and combined with pain."

28. What term is used to describe human beings subject to death?

  • A. Mortals
  • B. Immortals
  • C. Saints
  • D. Ascetics

Answer: A. Mortals

Explanation: Human beings who must die are referred to as 'mortals' in Buddha's sermon.

29. Who compiled/adapted 'The Sermon at Benares' in the NCERT First Flight textbook?

  • A. Betty Renshaw (from Values and Voices)
  • B. Gavin Maxwell
  • C. Vallikkannan
  • D. Arup Kumar Datta

Answer: A. Betty Renshaw (from Values and Voices)

Explanation: The chapter is adapted from Betty Renshaw's compilation 'Values and Voices: A College Reader'.

30. What path leads to immortality according to the lesson's philosophical theme?

  • A. Surrendering selfishness and realizing the truth of existence
  • B. Accumulating wealth and power
  • C. Living in isolation in the wilderness
  • D. Performing complex rituals repeatedly

Answer: A. Surrendering selfishness and realizing the truth of existence

Explanation: Surrendering selfishness leads to immortality and wisdom as one moves beyond individual attachment and grief.
