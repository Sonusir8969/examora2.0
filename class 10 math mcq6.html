Ch-6: Triangles 30 Questions

1. All circles are always:

  • A. Congruent
  • B. Similar
  • C. Equal in area
  • D. Neither similar nor congruent

Answer: B. Similar

Explanation: All circles have the exact same shape regardless of their size, making them similar.

2. All squares are:

  • A. Congruent
  • B. Similar
  • C. Equal in perimeter
  • D. None of these

Answer: B. Similar

Explanation: All squares have corresponding angles equal to 90° and their side lengths are proportional, so they are similar.

3. All _______ triangles are similar.

  • A. Isosceles
  • B. Scalene
  • C. Equilateral
  • D. Right-angled

Answer: C. Equilateral

Explanation: Every equilateral triangle has internal angles measuring 60°, satisfying the similarity criterion.

4. Basic Proportionality Theorem is also known as:

  • A. Pythagoras Theorem
  • B. Thales Theorem
  • C. Euclid's Lemma
  • D. Apollonius Theorem

Answer: B. Thales Theorem

Explanation: Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT) was introduced by the Greek mathematician Thales.

5. In ΔABC, DE || BC such that AD = 1.5 cm, DB = 3 cm, and AE = 1 cm. What is EC?

  • A. 1.5 cm
  • B. 2 cm
  • C. 2.5 cm
  • D. 3 cm

Answer: B. 2 cm

Explanation: By BPT, AD/DB = AE/EC ⇒ 1.5/3 = 1/EC ⇒ 1/2 = 1/EC ⇒ EC = 2 cm.

6. If two triangles are similar, then their corresponding sides are:

  • A. Equal
  • B. Proportional
  • C. Parallel
  • D. Perpendicular

Answer: B. Proportional

Explanation: By definition, two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and corresponding sides are in the same ratio (proportional).

7. If ΔABC ~ ΔPQR, then which of the following is true?

  • A. AB/PQ = BC/QR = AC/PR
  • B. AB/QR = BC/PR = AC/PQ
  • C. AB/PR = BC/PQ = AC/QR
  • D. AB = PQ

Answer: A. AB/PQ = BC/QR = AC/PR

Explanation: In similar triangles, the ratio of corresponding sides is equal: AB/PQ = BC/QR = AC/PR.

8. Which of the following is NOT a criterion for similarity of two triangles?

  • A. AAA
  • B. SAS
  • C. SSS
  • D. RHS

Answer: D. RHS

Explanation: AAA, SAS, and SSS are criteria for similarity. RHS is primarily a congruence criterion.

9. In ΔABC, DE || BC. If AD/DB = 3/5 and AC = 5.6 cm, then AE is equal to:

  • A. 2.1 cm
  • B. 3.1 cm
  • C. 1.8 cm
  • D. 2.4 cm

Answer: A. 2.1 cm

Explanation: AD/AB = AE/AC ⇒ 3/(3+5) = AE/5.6 ⇒ 3/8 = AE/5.6 ⇒ AE = (3 × 5.6) / 8 = 2.1 cm.

10. If ΔABC ~ ΔDEF such that AB = 1.2 cm and DE = 1.4 cm, the ratio of areas of ΔABC and ΔDEF is:

  • A. 6 : 7
  • B. 36 : 49
  • C. 16 : 25
  • D. 9 : 16

Answer: B. 36 : 49

Explanation: Ratio of areas of similar triangles is equal to square of ratio of corresponding sides = (1.2/1.4)² = (6/7)² = 36/49.

11. In a right triangle ABC, right angled at B, if AC = 10 cm and AB = 6 cm, then BC is:

  • A. 8 cm
  • B. 7 cm
  • C. 9 cm
  • D. 4 cm

Answer: A. 8 cm

Explanation: By Pythagoras Theorem, AC² = AB² + BC² ⇒ 100 = 36 + BC² ⇒ BC² = 64 ⇒ BC = 8 cm.

12. The length of hypotenuse of an isosceles right-angled triangle whose one side is 4 cm long is:

  • A. 4 cm
  • B. 4√2 cm
  • C. 8 cm
  • D. 2√2 cm

Answer: B. 4√2 cm

Explanation: Hypotenuse = √(4² + 4²) = √32 = 4√2 cm.

13. In ΔABC, AB = 6√3 cm, AC = 12 cm, and BC = 6 cm. The angle B is:

  • A. 120°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 90°
  • D. 45°

Answer: C. 90°

Explanation: AB² = 108, BC² = 36, AC² = 144. Since AB² + BC² = 108 + 36 = 144 = AC², ∠B = 90° by converse of Pythagoras Theorem.

14. The line segment joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and equal to ____ of it.

  • A. Double
  • B. Half
  • C. One-third
  • D. Equal

Answer: B. Half

Explanation: By Midpoint Theorem, the segment joining midpoints of two sides is parallel to third side and half in length.

15. Diagonals of a trapezium ABCD with AB || DC intersect each other at O. If AB = 2 CD, then ratio of areas of ΔAOB and ΔCOD is:

  • A. 1 : 4
  • B. 4 : 1
  • C. 2 : 1
  • D. 1 : 2

Answer: B. 4 : 1

Explanation: ΔAOB ~ ΔCOD. Ratio of areas = (AB/CD)² = (2CD/CD)² = 4/1 = 4 : 1.

16. If a vertical pole of length 6 m casts a shadow 4 m long on ground, and at the same time a tower casts a shadow 28 m long, height of tower is:

  • A. 40 m
  • B. 42 m
  • C. 38 m
  • D. 50 m

Answer: B. 42 m

Explanation: Height of pole / Shadow of pole = Height of tower / Shadow of tower ⇒ 6/4 = h/28 ⇒ h = (6 × 28) / 4 = 42 m.

17. Altitude of an equilateral triangle of side 8 cm is:

  • A. 4√3 cm
  • B. 2√3 cm
  • C. 8√3 cm
  • D. 4 cm

Answer: A. 4√3 cm

Explanation: Height of equilateral triangle = (√3 / 2) × side = (√3 / 2) × 8 = 4√3 cm.

18. Areas of two similar triangles are in the ratio 9 : 25. The ratio of their corresponding sides is:

  • A. 81 : 625
  • B. 3 : 5
  • C. 5 : 3
  • D. 9 : 25

Answer: B. 3 : 5

Explanation: Ratio of sides = √(Ratio of areas) = √(9/25) = 3/5 = 3 : 5.

19. In ΔABC, ∠A = 90° and AD ⊥ BC. Then AD² is equal to:

  • A. BD × CD
  • B. AB × AC
  • C. BC × CD
  • D. AB × BD

Answer: A. BD × CD

Explanation: By similarity theorem in right-angled triangles, altitude to hypotenuse satisfies AD² = BD × CD.

20. Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are:

  • A. Equal
  • B. Unequal
  • C. Complementary
  • D. Supplementary

Answer: A. Equal

Explanation: Equiangular triangles have equal corresponding angles and are always similar by AAA similarity criterion.

21. If side of an equilateral triangle is 'a', its area is given by:

  • A. (√3/4) a²
  • B. (√3/2) a²
  • C. (1/2) a²
  • D. √3 a²

Answer: A. (√3/4) a²

Explanation: Standard formula for area of an equilateral triangle with side 'a' is (√3/4) a².

22. Perimeters of two similar triangles ΔABC and ΔPQR are 36 cm and 24 cm respectively. If PQ = 10 cm, then AB is:

  • A. 15 cm
  • B. 12 cm
  • C. 18 cm
  • D. 20 cm

Answer: A. 15 cm

Explanation: Ratio of perimeters = Ratio of corresponding sides ⇒ 36/24 = AB/10 ⇒ 3/2 = AB/10 ⇒ AB = 15 cm.

23. In a square ABCD, length of diagonal AC is 10 cm. The side of square is:

  • A. 5 cm
  • B. 5√2 cm
  • C. 10√2 cm
  • D. 5/2 cm

Answer: B. 5√2 cm

Explanation: Diagonal = side × √2 ⇒ 10 = side × √2 ⇒ side = 10/√2 = 5√2 cm.

24. If ΔABC ~ ΔDEF and ∠A = 47°, ∠E = 83°, then ∠C is equal to:

  • A. 50°
  • B. 60°
  • C. 70°
  • D. 80°

Answer: A. 50°

Explanation: Since ΔABC ~ ΔDEF, ∠B = ∠E = 83°. In ΔABC, ∠C = 180° - (47° + 83°) = 180° - 130° = 50°.

25. A guy wire attached to a vertical pole of height 18 m is 24 m long and has a stake attached to other end. How far from base of pole should stake be driven?

  • A. 6√7 m
  • B. 12 m
  • C. 6√5 m
  • D. 18 m

Answer: A. 6√7 m

Explanation: Distance = √(24² - 18²) = √(576 - 324) = √252 = 6√7 m.

26. Corresponding medians of two similar triangles are in ratio 4 : 9. Ratio of areas of these triangles is:

  • A. 2 : 3
  • B. 16 : 81
  • C. 81 : 16
  • D. 4 : 9

Answer: B. 16 : 81

Explanation: Ratio of areas of similar triangles is equal to square of ratio of corresponding medians = (4/9)² = 16/81.

27. In a rhombus ABCD, side AB = 10 cm and diagonal AC = 12 cm. Length of other diagonal BD is:

  • A. 16 cm
  • B. 8 cm
  • C. 20 cm
  • D. 12 cm

Answer: A. 16 cm

Explanation: Diagonals bisect at right angles. Half diagonal OA = 6 cm. OB = √(10² - 6²) = 8 cm. BD = 2 × OB = 16 cm.

28. If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is _____ to the third side.

  • A. Perpendicular
  • B. Parallel
  • C. Equal
  • D. Coincident

Answer: B. Parallel

Explanation: This statement is the Converse of Basic Proportionality Theorem (Converse of Thales Theorem).

29. In ΔABC, D and E are points on AB and AC such that DE || BC. If AD = x, DB = x - 2, AE = x + 2, EC = x - 1, then x =

  • A. 4
  • B. 3
  • C. 2
  • D. 1

Answer: A. 4

Explanation: By BPT, x / (x - 2) = (x + 2) / (x - 1) ⇒ x(x - 1) = (x - 2)(x + 2) ⇒ x² - x = x² - 4 ⇒ -x = -4 ⇒ x = 4.

30. Triangle ABC is an isosceles triangle right-angled at C. Then AB² is equal to:

  • A. 2 AC²
  • B. AC²
  • C. 3 AC²
  • D. 4 AC²

Answer: A. 2 AC²

Explanation: In right-angled ΔABC at C, AC = BC. By Pythagoras Theorem: AB² = AC² + BC² = AC² + AC² = 2 AC².
