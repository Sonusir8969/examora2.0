NCERT Solutions for Class 11 English Ch-2: We're Not Afraid to Die...

'We're Not Afraid to Die... if We Can All Be Together' by Gordon Cook and Alan East recounts a real-life adventure of a family's perilous sea voyage aboard the yacht Wavewalker, and how they survived a life-threatening storm through courage and teamwork.

Overview of Topics & Subtopics

  • The Family's Voyage Plan Aboard the Wavewalker
  • The Deadly Storm and the Damage to the Ship
  • The Family's Courage, Teamwork, and Survival Efforts
  • Themes of Adventure, Determination, and Family Bonding

Important Questions and Answers

Q1: What was the purpose and route of the author's family voyage on the Wavewalker?
Answer: The author's family, along with a crew, undertook an ambitious round-the-world voyage on their sailing boat Wavewalker, planning to sail via some of the most dangerous waters, including a stop at the Antarctic Ocean, before continuing towards Australia.
Q2: What disaster struck the ship near Antarctica?
Answer: While sailing through the dangerous waters near Antarctica, the ship was struck by three massive, back-to-back waves during a violent storm, which severely damaged the ship, flooded the cabins, injured crew members, and threatened to sink the vessel.
Q3: How did the author's son Jonathan and other family members contribute to the survival effort?
Answer: Thirteen-year-old Jonathan bravely assisted in critical repair work, including plugging holes and pumping out water, despite his own injuries; the entire family and crew worked tirelessly together in shifts to keep the ship afloat and steer it safely to the nearest port.
Q4: What does the phrase 'We're not afraid to die...' from the story's title signify?
Answer: The phrase, spoken by the young daughter Sue, signifies the family's deep bond and willingness to face death together rather than being separated, expressing the ultimate value of family unity, courage, and togetherness even in the face of mortal danger.
Q5: What central lesson does this account convey?
Answer: This account conveys that human courage, resourcefulness, and teamwork, particularly the unbreakable strength of family bonds, can help overcome even the most life-threatening natural disasters and adversities.
