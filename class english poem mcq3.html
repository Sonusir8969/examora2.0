Poem-03: The Voice of the Rain 25 Questions

1. Who is the poet of the poem 'The Voice of the Rain'?

  • A. Walt Whitman
  • B. Ted Hughes
  • C. Shirley Toulson
  • D. Markus Natten

Answer: A. Walt Whitman

Explanation: 'The Voice of the Rain' is a famous poem written by the American poet Walt Whitman.

2. Who are the two speakers in the poem?

  • A. The Poet and the Rain
  • B. The Poet and the Earth
  • C. The Rain and the Sky
  • D. The Sun and the Sea

Answer: A. The Poet and the Rain

Explanation: The poem takes the form of a dialogue between the poet and the rain.

3. What question did the poet ask the rain?

  • A. Who art thou? (Who are you?)
  • B. Where are you going?
  • C. Why are you falling?
  • D. When will you stop?

Answer: A. Who art thou? (Who are you?)

Explanation: The poet asks the soft-falling shower: "And who art thou? said I to the soft-falling shower..."

4. How did the rain answer the poet's question?

  • A. I am the Poem of Earth
  • B. I am the Daughter of Heaven
  • C. I am the Queen of Clouds
  • D. I am the Gift of Nature

Answer: A. I am the Poem of Earth

Explanation: The rain translates its response as: "I am the Poem of Earth, said the voice of the rain."

5. From where does the rain originate or rise?

  • A. Impalpable out of the land and the bottomless sea
  • B. From the mountain tops
  • C. From the trees in forest
  • D. Directly from outer space

Answer: A. Impalpable out of the land and the bottomless sea

Explanation: The rain rises in the form of vapor silently (impalpable) from land and sea towards heaven.

6. What does the word 'Impalpable' mean in the poem?

  • A. Something that cannot be touched or felt physical
  • B. Heavy and dense
  • C. Extremely loud
  • D. Bright and colorful

Answer: A. Something that cannot be touched or felt physical

Explanation: 'Impalpable' refers to water vapor rising invisibly without being felt or touched.

7. Where does the water vapor go after rising up?

  • A. Upwards to heaven (sky), where it changes form
  • B. Into underground rivers
  • C. Into another planet
  • D. It disappears permanently

Answer: A. Upwards to heaven (sky), where it changes form

Explanation: Vapor ascends to heaven, condenses into clouds, and changes shape while remaining water in essence.

8. What function does rain perform when it falls back to earth?

  • A. It laves (washes) drought, atomies, and dust-layers
  • B. It destroys crops and plants
  • C. It turns soil into rock
  • D. It causes only floods

Answer: A. It laves (washes) drought, atomies, and dust-layers

Explanation: The rain descends to wash away dry areas, tiny particles (atomies), and dust on the earth.

9. What does the word 'lave' mean?

  • A. Wash / Bathe
  • B. Love
  • C. Leave
  • D. Burn

Answer: A. Wash / Bathe

Explanation: 'Lave' is an old poetic word meaning to wash or bathe.

10. What would happen to seeds without rain?

  • A. They would remain hidden and unborn
  • B. They would grow into huge trees quickly
  • C. They would fly away in wind
  • D. They would turn into stone

Answer: A. They would remain hidden and unborn

Explanation: The poem mentions: "...and all that in them without me were seeds only, latent, unborn."

11. What figure of speech gives the rain a voice to speak?

  • A. Personification
  • B. Simile
  • C. Oxymoron
  • D. Alliteration

Answer: A. Personification

Explanation: Giving human capability of speech to a non-human entity like rain is Personification.

12. What figure of speech is "I am the Poem of Earth"?

  • A. Metaphor
  • B. Simile
  • C. Personification
  • D. Hyperbole

Answer: A. Metaphor

Explanation: Direct comparison between Rain and Poem without using 'like' or 'as' makes it a Metaphor.

13. What parallel is drawn in the last two lines of the poem?

  • A. Between rain and a song / music
  • B. Between rain and thunder
  • C. Between earth and moon
  • D. Between poet and painter

Answer: A. Between rain and a song / music

Explanation: The poet compares the water cycle of rain to a song originating from a singer and returning with love.

14. What cycle is described in the poem?

  • A. Water cycle in nature
  • B. Life and death cycle
  • C. Carbon cycle
  • D. Season change cycle

Answer: A. Water cycle in nature

Explanation: The scientific process of evaporation, condensation, and precipitation is beautifully described.

15. What does the rain continuously give back to its own origin?

  • A. Life, purity, and beauty
  • B. Salt and minerals
  • C. Cold air
  • D. Darkness

Answer: A. Life, purity, and beauty

Explanation: "And give back life to my own origin, and make pure and beautify it."

16. What is meant by "reck'd or unreck'd"?

  • A. Whether cared for or uncared for / noticed or unnoticed
  • B. Destroyed or saved
  • C. Heavy or light
  • D. Wet or dry

Answer: A. Whether cared for or uncared for / noticed or unnoticed

Explanation: 'Reck'd or unreck'd' means whether people appreciate and notice its work or not, rain fulfills its duty.

17. Why are the last two lines placed inside brackets/parentheses?

  • A. Because they are the poet's own commentary comparing song to rain
  • B. They were written by another author
  • C. They contain grammatical errors
  • D. They are extra optional lines

Answer: A. Because they are the poet's own commentary comparing song to rain

Explanation: The poet puts his observation about music/song in brackets separate from the rain's direct speech.

18. What does "soft-falling shower" describe?

  • A. Gentle drizzle of rain
  • B. Heavy hailstorm
  • C. Snowfall
  • D. Violent storm

Answer: A. Gentle drizzle of rain

Explanation: Soft-falling shower refers to gentle, quiet drops of rain falling from the sky.

19. What figure of speech is used in "soft-falling shower"?

  • A. Alliteration
  • B. Simile
  • C. Irony
  • D. Hyperbole

Answer: A. Alliteration

Explanation: The repetition of the initial consonant 's' sound in "soft-falling shower" is Alliteration.

20. What quality of rain is highlighted by the phrase "Eternal I rise"?

  • A. Never-ending nature of water cycle
  • B. Short lifespan
  • C. Unpredictable arrival
  • D. Destruction caused by rain

Answer: A. Never-ending nature of water cycle

Explanation: 'Eternal' signifies that the process of rising as vapor and falling as rain goes on forever.

21. How does the poem describe the origin point of a song?

  • A. Issuing from its birth-place (heart of the singer)
  • B. Coming from radio
  • C. Coming from birds
  • D. From wind blowing in trees

Answer: A. Issuing from its birth-place (heart of the singer)

Explanation: A song originates from the soul/heart of its creator, travels around, and returns with love.

22. Which word in the poem means 'hidden' or 'inactive'?

  • A. Latent
  • B. Atomies
  • C. Lave
  • D. Impalpable

Answer: A. Latent

Explanation: 'Latent' means present but dormant, waiting for moisture to sprout.

23. What form does rain take in heaven before descending?

  • A. Vaguely form'd (Clouds)
  • B. Ice cubes
  • C. Rainbow
  • D. Lightning

Answer: A. Vaguely form'd (Clouds)

Explanation: "Upward to heaven, whence, vaguely form'd, altogether chang'd, and yet the same."

24. What is the tone of the rain in the poem?

  • A. Proud, gentle, and nurturing
  • B. Angry and destructive
  • C. Sad and weeping
  • D. Indifferent

Answer: A. Proud, gentle, and nurturing

Explanation: Rain proudly describes its essential role in nurturing and beautifying the earth.

25. What is the central theme of 'The Voice of the Rain'?

  • A. The cyclical, life-giving, and purifying role of rain in nature
  • B. The danger of severe rainfall
  • C. The importance of writing poetry
  • D. The scientific structure of atmosphere

Answer: A. The cyclical, life-giving, and purifying role of rain in nature

Explanation: The poem celebrates the eternal water cycle and rain's indispensable service to the Earth.
