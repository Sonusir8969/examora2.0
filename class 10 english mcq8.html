Ch-8: Mijbil the Otter 30 Questions

1. Who is the author of 'Mijbil the Otter'?

  • A. Gavin Maxwell
  • B. Arup Kumar Datta
  • C. Lokesh Abrol
  • D. Lucio Rodrigues

Answer: A. Gavin Maxwell

Explanation: The story 'Mijbil the Otter' is an excerpt written by Gavin Maxwell.

2. Where did Gavin Maxwell travel in the early months of 1956?

  • A. Southern Iraq
  • B. Northern India
  • C. Southern Egypt
  • D. Eastern Turkey

Answer: A. Southern Iraq

Explanation: Maxwell traveled to Southern Iraq in the early months of 1956.

3. Why did Maxwell decide to keep an otter instead of a dog?

  • A. His dog Camus had died and he felt Camus's place could not be filled easily by another dog
  • B. Otters were cheaper to buy in Iraq
  • C. He wanted to train an exotic animal
  • D. Dogs were banned in Camusfearna

Answer: A. His dog Camus had died and he felt Camus's place could not be filled easily by another dog

Explanation: After losing his dog Camus, Maxwell felt lonely and thought of keeping an otter at Camusfearna, ringed by water.

4. Where did Maxwell get the idea that keeping an otter as a pet was suitable?

  • A. His residence at Camusfearna was surrounded by water
  • B. A friend suggested it to him
  • C. He saw one in a circus
  • D. He read about it in a magazine

Answer: A. His residence at Camusfearna was surrounded by water

Explanation: Camusfearna was surrounded by water, making it an ideal environment for an otter.

5. Who suggested that Maxwell get an otter from the Tigris marshes?

  • A. His friend
  • B. An Arab merchant
  • C. The British Consulate official
  • D. A local fisherman

Answer: A. His friend

Explanation: His friend suggested that he could get an otter in the Tigris marshes where they were common as pets among Arabs.

6. Where did Maxwell collect his mail and find the otter waiting for him?

  • A. At the Consulate General in Basra
  • B. At the airport in Baghdad
  • C. At the post office in Camusfearna
  • D. At a market in London

Answer: A. At the Consulate General in Basra

Explanation: He collected his mail at the Consulate General in Basra, where two Arabs handed him a sack containing the otter.

7. How was the otter brought to Maxwell's bedroom?

  • A. In a sack brought by two Arabs with a note from his friend
  • B. In a wooden cage
  • C. In a cardboard box
  • D. Carried on a leash

Answer: A. In a sack brought by two Arabs with a note from his friend

Explanation: Two Arabs were squatting on the floor with a sack that squirmed, containing the otter sent by his friend.

8. What scientific race or name was given to Maxwell's otter species?

  • A. Lutrogale perspicillata maxwelli
  • B. Panthera uncia maxwelli
  • C. Canis lupus maxwelli
  • D. Felis catus maxwelli

Answer: A. Lutrogale perspicillata maxwelli

Explanation: Zoologists later named the otter's race 'Lutrogale perspicillata maxwelli' or Maxwell's otter.

9. How did Mijbil behave on his first night with Maxwell?

  • A. Aloof and indifferent, sleeping on the floor far away
  • B. Extremely friendly and slept on the bed
  • C. Wildly aggressive and biting
  • D. Constantly crying and howling

Answer: A. Aloof and indifferent, sleeping on the floor far away

Explanation: For the first twenty-four hours, Mijbil was neither hostile nor friendly; he remained aloof and indifferent.

10. On the second night, where did Mijbil go to sleep?

  • A. On Maxwell's bed, tucked in the crook of his knees
  • B. Inside the bathroom sink
  • C. In the travel box
  • D. Under the sofa

Answer: A. On Maxwell's bed, tucked in the crook of his knees

Explanation: On the second night, Mijbil came onto Maxwell's bed and slept in the crook of his knees until morning.

11. What happened when Maxwell took Mijbil to the bathroom for the first time?

  • A. Mijbil went wild with joy in the bathtub water
  • B. Mijbil refused to enter and hid under the door
  • C. Mijbil tried to drink all the tap water
  • D. Mijbil fell asleep on the mat

Answer: A. Mijbil went wild with joy in the bathtub water

Explanation: Mijbil went wild with joy in the water for half an hour, plunging, rolling, and splashing.

12. According to Maxwell, what is an otter's view on static water?

  • A. Static water is a waste and must be splashed or manipulated until it flows
  • B. Static water is ideal for swimming
  • C. Static water is dangerous and to be avoided
  • D. Static water is good for drinking only

Answer: A. Static water is a waste and must be splashed or manipulated until it flows

Explanation: To an otter, static water is a waste; it must be provoked to move and splash.

13. What surprising trick did Mijbil learn to do on his own in the bathroom?

  • A. Turn on the water tap till it produced a full flow
  • B. Wash his own toys
  • C. Open the door lock with his teeth
  • D. Turn off the light switch

Answer: A. Turn on the water tap till it produced a full flow

Explanation: Mijbil escaped to the bathroom and managed to turn the tap on with his paws.

14. What game did Mijbil invent using a broken suitcase and a ping-pong ball?

  • A. Rolling the ball up the sloping lid and rushing to catch it before it fell off
  • B. Throwing the ball into the bathtub
  • C. Bouncing the ball against the bedroom wall
  • D. Hiding the ball inside the suitcase pockets

Answer: A. Rolling the ball up the sloping lid and rushing to catch it before it fell off

Explanation: Mijbil placed the ball on the high end of the sloping suitcase lid and ran to ambush it as it rolled down.

15. Why did British Airways refuse to transport Mijbil from Iraq to London?

  • A. They had strict rules against carrying animals on flights
  • B. Mijbil did not have a valid health certificate
  • C. The ticket price was too high
  • D. They feared otters would damage the seats

Answer: A. They had strict rules against carrying animals on flights

Explanation: British Airways would not fly animals, so Maxwell booked a flight on another airline to Paris and then to London.

16. What constraint did the airline put regarding transporting Mijbil?

  • A. He had to be packed into a square box no more than 18 inches high to be kept on the floor
  • B. He had to travel in a special cage in the pilot's cabin
  • C. He had to be sedated throughout the flight
  • D. He had to be kept in a waterproof bag in the luggage hold

Answer: A. He had to be packed into a square box no more than 18 inches high to be kept on the floor

Explanation: The airline insisted that Mijbil be packed into a box not more than eighteen inches square.

17. What dreadful incident happened when Maxwell checked the box before heading to the airport?

  • A. Mijbil had torn the zinc lining and was covered in blood
  • B. Mijbil had escaped into the streets
  • C. The box was completely destroyed and empty
  • D. Mijbil was unconscious

Answer: A. Mijbil had torn the zinc lining and was covered in blood

Explanation: Mijbil tried to rip open the box's metal lining, injuring himself and leaving blood trickling through the air holes.

18. How did the air hostess respond when Maxwell informed her about Mijbil on the airplane?

  • A. She was extremely kind and suggested keeping Mijbil on his knees
  • B. She demanded that Mijbil be placed in the cargo section
  • C. She panicked and called the pilot
  • D. She offered to hold Mijbil herself

Answer: A. She was extremely kind and suggested keeping Mijbil on his knees

Explanation: The air hostess was exceptionally sympathetic and advised Maxwell to keep the otter on his lap.

19. What commotion occurred when Mijbil bounded out of the box during the flight?

  • A. A woman stood up screaming 'A rat! A rat!' and passengers panicked
  • B. The otter jumped into the cockpit
  • C. All the luggage fell off the overhead racks
  • D. The plane had to make an emergency landing

Answer: A. A woman stood up screaming 'A rat! A rat!' and passengers panicked

Explanation: Mijbil disappeared down the aisle, causing a woman to scream 'A rat! A rat!' in panic.

20. Where did Maxwell find Mijbil after searching the plane aisle?

  • A. He returned directly to Maxwell's lap, chittering with affection
  • B. Hiding under the pilot's seat
  • C. Inside another passenger's coat pocket
  • D. Locked in the plane galley

Answer: A. He returned directly to Maxwell's lap, chittering with affection

Explanation: Mijbil bounded back onto Maxwell's knee and rubbed his face against Maxwell's neck.

21. How long did Maxwell and Mijbil stay together in London?

  • A. For nearly a month
  • B. For two weeks
  • C. For six months
  • D. For a year

Answer: A. For nearly a month

Explanation: Mijbil and Maxwell remained in London for nearly a month.

22. What habit did Mijbil develop while walking along the street near a primary school in London?

  • A. Galloping along the low wall of the school yard to distract pupils and teachers
  • B. Barking at school children
  • C. Hiding behind the lampposts
  • D. Trying to enter the school gate

Answer: A. Galloping along the low wall of the school yard to distract pupils and teachers

Explanation: Mijbil would jump onto the 30-yard-long low brick wall and gallop along it, attracting the attention of children inside.

23. Why were Londoners continuously guessing what kind of animal Mijbil was?

  • A. Because otters were uncommon and unfamiliar pets in London
  • B. Because Mijbil wore a mask
  • C. Because Mijbil was unusually large
  • D. Because Mijbil was dyed in bright colors

Answer: A. Because otters were uncommon and unfamiliar pets in London

Explanation: Average Londoners had rarely seen an otter, leading to various wild guesses about its identity.

24. Which of the following was NOT one of the guesses Londoners made about Mijbil?

  • A. A baby dinosaur
  • B. A baby seal, a squirrel, or a walrus
  • C. A beaver, a leopard, or a hippo
  • D. A badger or a mongoose

Answer: A. A baby dinosaur

Explanation: Londoners guessed Mijbil was a seal, walrus, squirrel, beaver, leopard, or badger, but never a dinosaur.

25. What question asked by a laborer digging a drain was awarded the highest score by Maxwell?

  • A. "Here, Mister, what’s that supposed to be?"
  • B. "Is that a wild rat?"
  • C. "Can it swim?"
  • D. "Where did you buy that animal?"

Answer: A. "Here, Mister, what’s that supposed to be?"

Explanation: A laborer dropped his tools, stared, and finally blurted out, "Here, Mister, what's that supposed to be?", winning top points in Maxwell's view.

26. How did Mijbil behave with toys like marbles, rubber balls, and terrapin shells?

  • A. He spent hours juggling them on his back with his paws without dropping them
  • B. He chewed and destroyed them instantly
  • C. He buried them in the garden
  • D. He threw them at Maxwell

Answer: A. He spent hours juggling them on his back with his paws without dropping them

Explanation: Mijbil loved juggling small objects like marbles on his flat belly while lying on his back.

27. What term did Maxwell use to describe the intense emotional bond formed between human and pet in the chapter?

  • A. Otter thraldom
  • B. Animal instinct
  • C. Pet obsession
  • D. Creature love

Answer: A. Otter thraldom

Explanation: Maxwell mentioned that "otter thraldom" is a spell binding anyone who has ever owned an otter.

28. What was the name of Gavin Maxwell's house in Scotland?

  • A. Camusfearna
  • B. Boggins Heights
  • C. Dhekiabari
  • D. Basra Villa

Answer: A. Camusfearna

Explanation: Gavin Maxwell lived at Camusfearna in the West Highlands of Scotland.

29. How did Maxwell clean the mud off Mijbil when he first got him?

  • A. By bathing him repeatedly for days until his true coat color emerged
  • B. By brushing his fur with a wire comb
  • C. By wiping him with dry towels
  • D. By letting him swim in the river

Answer: A. By bathing him repeatedly for days until his true coat color emerged

Explanation: It took a month of continuous washing to remove the mud coating and reveal Mijbil's soft chocolate-brown coat.

30. What core quality of the air hostess won Maxwell's deep gratitude during the difficult flight?

  • A. Her profound empathy, composure, and kindness
  • B. Her strict adherence to flight regulations
  • C. Her skill in recapturing escaped animals
  • D. Her speed in serving meals

Answer: A. Her profound empathy, composure, and kindness

Explanation: Maxwell referred to the air hostess as "the very queen of her kind" for her compassionate understanding.
